Morgan Mireles (born 21 October 2005), known as Burnette, is a former Ninzard politician who served as the 2nd District General of Catsland from July 2022 to January 2023 and was the Preservation Party's nominee for General of Parry in December 2025. Hot-tempered, quick and unfiltered, she was known as the “sigma female.”1,2,3,4

One of Cat's earliest loyalists, she ran Catsland for the Disco Party in the first months of the new presidency and lost it in January 2023 to Meikaqu, whom Cat herself had put up against her. Later that year she broke with Cat and was stalked by Cat's followers for it; she joined ViaTea's Liberation Movement and helped other Ninzardians get out. Cat's ledger shows her refusing $5,136 of the money offered to her.5,1,6

Apollos backed her as the Preservates' nominee in Parry, where she took 51 of 130 votes (39.2%) against PoG, the best result a Preservate had yet recorded in a race for the district's generalship. In 2026 she was one of Parry's party operatives in the Preservate Apparatus and finished second in the district's snap election for Legislature XI. She left Ninzard on 1 July 2026.3,4,7,8,9

Early years and Hybriden

Mireles was born on 21 October 2005. She came up through Ninzard's earlier years before finding in Cat what she called the “common sense solution,” and she became one of Cat's earliest loyalists.1

When Raffaele rebuilt the District of Hybriden from September 2020, he trained its members in what he called the “Dark Arts” of psychological warfare: that hacking depends more on social intelligence than on technical skill, and how to trick enemies into fighting each other. Cat learned from him, and so did other Hybridians, Burnette among them. Some of those Hybridians would later join Cat's Meowsalute Movement and others would resist it.10 Burnette would do both.

General of Catsland (2022–2023)

By 2022 Burnette was a member of the Disco Party. In June of that year Cat had the Disconics, Burnette among them, quietly work to push the party's leader, Vesper, out of the leadership; when he agreed to a new leadership vote, believing it would be fair, they all voted for Cat.11

When Cat became President on 20 July 2022, Burnette succeeded her as General of Catsland. Her term covered the rest of 2022 and was marked by conflict inside the district. Many members objected to Cat's crackdowns, the Katastoles, which they believed went beyond what a president should do, and several grew afraid of Catsland's atmosphere. Laser was one of the first to reject Cat's actions and leave the district.2

In the presidential election of January 2023 Burnette endorsed Cat.12 Cat, however, preferred to keep her followers divided and herself above them, and she had Meikaqu, who had left the Disco Party for the Preservation Party, run against Burnette for Catsland.13 Cat and Vice President Snowby endorsed Meikaqu; Governor Roland endorsed Burnette. Meikaqu won 42 of the 65 votes to Burnette's 23 (35.4%), becoming the only Preservate ever elected in Ninzardia.5

Break with Cat (2023–2025)

Cat grew increasingly obsessive about Burnette. In 2023, while Burnette was still a minor, Cat made sexual advances toward her, which Burnette rebuffed. She distanced herself from Cat, and Cat and her followers began to stalk her.1 Her defection was the turning point for Sev, one of Cat's drivers. At the start of 2024 Cat ordered him to stalk Burnette; instead he showed himself to her and told her who had sent him. She threatened him and fought him at first, but as he kept appearing without hostility she came to trust him. Cat reassigned him, and by April he too had broken with her.1 Burnette was one of several Ninzards Cat had stalked, with Sev and later Ploutos.14

By the end of 2023 Burnette was unsettled by what she was hearing, among it the burning of Raffaele's house and accounts of Cat's advances toward others. She turned, reluctantly, to the Liberation Movement that ViaTea had founded that December, and became one of its leading members. With Kain, and with the permission of Governor Adelynn, she helped the “lost” Ninzardians who wanted out of Cat's faction move to Nightwork.1,15 In early 2024 she, Spinzle, Kain and ViaTea reached out to former President Apollos, who treated them with suspicion. By the second half of the year he and Spinzle were working together again, and the movement had an outlet outside Ninzard: until Cat's fall, Apollos, Spinzle and ViaTea helped Ninzards clean up their digital presence and cut their ties with the community.16

Cat's ledger of payments from March 2022 to April 2024, which records 68 Ninzards, lists Burnette 31st. She accepted $302 and refused $5,136, seventeen times as much.17,6 After Cat's fall, the Catx3 Dossier drew on Burnette's testimony, with that of others in Cat's circle, to confirm Cat's long-term plans and Cat's own admission that she had grown used to emotional chaos.18

Parry campaign (2025)

Burnette and ViaTea had been friends, but they fell out by the end of 2024. By late 2025 Burnette was a Preservate. When ViaTea launched the Liberation Party in November 2025, Burnette took to adding her to group chats to taunt her, and ViaTea, insulted, leaked Burnette's full real name. Burnette found the whole thing amusing.15

Nomination

In the Parry snap election of October 2025 the Preservate nominee, Phalth, finished second with a third of the vote. Some Preservates read Boygho's failure to win a majority as a good sign; others saw fatigue with the party's leadership in an Orie district. President Apollos agreed with the second reading and pushed for Burnette to be the party's next nominee in Parry. She was a controversial figure in the party, hot-tempered and known for her edgy, vulgar and unfiltered way of speaking, but she held principles that matched the Preservates', especially those of the Pragma Flank. Her relationship with Apollos was complicated, yet he saw her as the “necessary option” and a chance to try something new. Most Preservates were indifferent to the choice, while the party establishment grew wary of where Apollos was taking the party.3

In the Dreadnought Debate of 12 November 2025, over whether to expand the Preservate Apparatus, Burnette surprised the party by siding with Aurase and Grace, who wanted to keep the party's structure as it was.3

Campaign against PoG

Boygho dropped out of the December race, leaving a two-way contest between Burnette and PoG. Both were seen as “hype” candidates. Burnette's temper, quick logic and personality resonated strongly with the party's female base and brought the Preservates an enthusiasm they had not seen before, especially among voters who wanted energy and would not vote for a boring candidate. But PoG, one of the community's brightest trolls, proved just as exciting.19

Burnette was in the expanded Apparatus when Apollos presented it with his GCM Matrix on 16 December, a quadrant for mapping the community's recurring crises. Much of the Apparatus's newer membership reacted badly, and Burnette answered with a meme: “i ain't reading all that / i'm happy for u tho / or sorry that happened.” Later in the debate she told him he was inventing calculus to justify being a Discord moderator. Apollos replied that it was nothing complex and that he would draw it out for her, then posted a deliberately crude Microsoft Paint drawing of the quadrant, to the party's amusement.20

The day before the vote Burnette admitted that she had thought she was doing well with Parry's silent majority, until she saw fifty people gather in a voice call just to watch PoG speak and others posting “aura” videos of her.4

Result

Voting began on 22 December 2025. Parry was the last race to be called, still within the first day: PoG won 79 of the 130 votes (60.8%) to Burnette's 51 (39.2%), the only coalition candidate elected anywhere in a Preservate landslide. It was the highest share any Preservate candidate had won in a race for General of Parry, and Apollos had been right that she had a fair chance, but he had underestimated PoG's campaign. PoG's fervent following and the “aura” edits that circulated of her gave her a “girlboss” image; Burnette had a similar air, as the “sigma female,” but not the same emotional or technical backing.4

Burnette accepted her defeat gracefully. When Vice President Spinzle asked what had happened in Parry, she answered simply: “anais lefebvre.” In her victory address that day, the party's Boss, Adelynn, spoke to her by name.4

「To Burnette: The loss feels heavy. But please, do not let this extinguish your fire. You stepped into the arena when it would have been easier to stay on the sidelines. You brought an energy, a wit, and a passion to this campaign that we desperately needed. A vote count does not define your worth to this party or this community. You are vital to us, and I am incredibly proud of how you fought.」

— Adelynn, Boss of the Preservation Party, 22 December 2025

Preservate Apparatus (2026)

Burnette stayed in the Apparatus through the winter. On 2 February 2026, when Kaimachi told the room that Spinzle had named her the party's “brainrot wrangler,” Burnette's verdict on Spinzle was that she was “a ruthless bastard.”21 When the Apparatus was reorganized on 24 February under Adelynn, Burnette became one of Parry's two party operatives, with Kaimachi, both in Apollos's Pragma Flank.7

In March 2026, after Apollos was assaulted on his university campus, Spinzle used a bluff to force a written confession from one of his attackers. Grace objected to her methods; Al Commando defended them with Aceiron's own phrase, “play stupid games, win stupid prizes,” and Burnette told him it was the smartest thing he had ever said.22

During the debate over Part I of the Charter, which was agreed on 7 March, Aceiron called ViaTea a hypocrite for having leaked Burnette's real name, and asked what the Charter's “absolute protection from doxxing” could even mean in Ninzard. ViaTea answered with a message from Burnette herself.23

「it means you dont leak personally identifiable information. its not a difficult concept to grasp.」

— Burnette, forwarded by ViaTea in the Charter debate, March 2026

In the snap election for Parry's seat in Legislature XI at the end of March, held under approval voting, Burnette won the approval of 65 of 122 voters (53.3%), finishing second behind Kaimachi, who took the seat with 74 (60.7%), and ahead of Phalth and the Orie BadSpirit.8 When KatDoesMuch, newly elected for Waffle, joined the Apparatus, Burnette had a welcome for her.24

Burnette: @Kat(NotC*t) save yourself girl. leave while you can

KatDoesMuch: @Burnette your a … boomer and your saying shit like: “A sigma female is loyal, assertive, and independent. She doesn't follow trends or care what other people think about her.” dont even talk to me

When Woofer, the new Boss, restructured the Apparatus on 2 April, Kaimachi became Parry's coordinator and Burnette stayed on as one of its two party operatives, now with Aurase.25 Liver, the traditionalist Catholic Representative for Scope, dismissed her as a “try-hard tomboy” who had given up traditional femininity for an aggressive, foul-mouthed manner in order to survive in Parry.26

Departure (2026)

When Kaimachi challenged PoG for Parry in June 2026, the race was expected to be as close as PoG's contest with Burnette six months earlier; Kaimachi won it by 66 votes to 64.27 On 1 July 2026, the day Apollos's presidency ended and Adelynn and Grace said their farewells, Burnette was among the veteran members who left Ninzard, with Danbi, Ixan, Yunin and others.9
