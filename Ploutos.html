Niccolò Tagliaferro (born 14 February 2005), known as Ploutos, is an Italian-American administrator of the District of Main, a co-founder of the cybersecurity group Channeling-4 (C-4), and a Representative for Main in Legislatures XI and XII. The youngest member of the Tagliaferro Family, he is the younger brother of the Underground governor Spikester, whose downfall he helped bring about and carried as a burden for years afterward.1,2

Ploutos rose from a moderator of Sacraday to one of Main's most respected administrators under General Woofer, with whom he later began a relationship known in the community as “NicNat.” He led Legislature XI during the drafting of the Charter and served in the Senior Leadership of the Preservate Apparatus. Well-liked across party lines, he is known for answering the community's cruelty with grace.3,4

Family and the Underground

Ploutos was born on 14 February 2005, the youngest of the Tagliaferro Family that the hustler Paolo (“Tag”) brought into the pre-Ninzard Braykout community, alongside Paolo's cousins Giada (OffBeat) and Niccolò's older brother Davide (Spikester).1 When their father suffered an incapacitating stroke and the family faced a $50,000 medical debt, Spikester began selling drugs through his post as Governor of Nightwork-Scope, keeping the young Niccolò insulated from it with a story about “crypto investments.”5

As Spikester's dealings drew the dangerous Quorey into the family's orbit, a fourteen-year-old Ploutos — frightened by his brother's late-night pacing — quietly messaged Vice President Hokan.6 He later helped the leadership end his brother's rule: with his assistance, Seandry planted a RAT on Spikester's computer and gathered the evidence that was handed to the police.2 When Davide was arrested in February 2020, he turned on his brother; Niccolò lowered his head and wept, and carried a deep guilt and sadness over his part in it.2 That August, Ploutos was among the four Californians — with Seandry, Hackman and Advy — who founded Channeling-4.7

Main, Hybriden and return

In May 2021 General Danbi, broadening Main's staff across party lines, appointed Ploutos among its highest administrators.8 The following March, General Geo stripped his permissions and then banned him for using the word “Geodude,” after which Raffaele welcomed him into the Italian cohort of Hybriden, where he served as an administrator for about a year until President Catx3 eliminated the district.9,3 Woofer then invited him back to Main.3

In the December 2023 election Ploutos ran against Woofer for General of Main as a moderate Orie of the Original Party, following his former leader Raffaele; he was well-liked in the district, and the Main Staff Team briefly split over his candidacy. He lost with 81 votes to Woofer's 100 (44.8%) — her closest race to that point. Rather than hold the challenge against him, Woofer reappointed him an administrator, valuing a dissenting voice, and Ploutos accepted and grew a newfound respect for the young general.10,3

Through the Catx3 years Ploutos remained part of C-4. In December 2023 he drove Adelynn through the exurban dark to extract the laundering victim Tierra from one of Cat's houses, waiting with the engine running and sprinting to carry the girl to the car when the meeting turned dangerous; Cat later learned of his role and had him stalked.11,12 He sat on Kay's information-containment team in the final phase of the investigation.13

The Vigil Incident

In January 2026, after a despairing late-night tirade, Kain fell silent, and the community spiraled into speculation. Ploutos — who had moved to the Los Angeles area in 2023 to attend the same university as Hackman — recognized the danger at once, having watched a similar spiral in his brother. He and Hackman drove to Kain's apartment, found him unconscious from an overdose, placed him in the recovery position and called emergency services; their decision bridged the digital and physical worlds and prevented a tragedy.14

Administrator of Main

Under General Woofer, Ploutos served as an administrator of the District of Main alongside PluPlu, beneath Head of Administrators Seandry. His presence helped cement the Italian character of the district, though he kept his distance from the Bellucci cohort's theatrics.3 In the 2025 presidential election he was among the prominent Main staff who backed Apollos, helping carry the district.15

In the debate over admitting Kaimachi to the Preservate Apparatus, Ploutos sided with the integrationists in a single line: “Why kill the flame when we can use it to warm the house?”16 In March 2026 he and Woofer began dating, and on 26 March they told the community they were together — a revelation the community dubbed “NicNat.” Woofer was noted to be a “tsundere” around him, and the two spent their free time playing video games.17

Legislature XI and the Charter

In the March 2026 snap election Ploutos was elected to replace Adelynn in Main, winning 201 of 237 votes (84.8%), the highest share of that cycle.18,19 Legislature XI appointed him its Leader, succeeding Shuffle; the separate title was abolished on 11 May 2026 when the Charter made the Vice President, Spinzle, Leader of the Legislature by office.20 When Woofer became Boss of the Preservate Apparatus on 2 April, Ploutos joined its Senior Leadership with Seandry, Spinzle and Apollos.21

During the Charter debates Ploutos became a steady, theological voice against the chamber's cruelty. When Power of Greed's broadcast cast the fifteen-year-old Woofer as a “complicit survivor” of Cat's circle, he dismissed the “dark anime plotline” and called the community back to reality.22 When Lless attacked him over his brother Davide and his family's “bloodline,” Ploutos granted every word about Davide and said he welcomed the guilt if it moved him to hand out bandages rather than throw stones.4 He also volunteered for the Ninzard Career Initiative and the May Day Résumé Bounty, reviewing members' job applications with Apollos, Hackman, Advy, Poka and Idodo.17,23

Ploutos was among the thirty who gathered at the Holloway residence as the Charter was finalized, arriving late with Woofer, and it was he who posted that the Charter had passed Legislature XI by a vote of 13–1–1.24 After it was ratified over pineapple pizza, Minister Poka's joking call to impeach Apollos moved Ploutos to file articles of impeachment “for crimes against humanity,” though he changed his own vote to “No” partway through.25 On the death of Soda in May 2026 he urged the community to extend grace rather than seek out the footage.26

In the June 2026 Summer Cycle he was re-elected to Legislature XII with 221 votes (85.0%), and under President Poka he sat among Main's representatives with Advy, Seandry and Syn.27,28 That August he was among the Apparatus members who urged the Capital to intervene in the Salon War in Parry, a plea President Poka overruled.29
