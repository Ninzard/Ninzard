The Unighted Party was a small political party of the Nightian Faction during the Golden Era of the Ninzard Project. Its best-known member, Clayton, won Nightwork-Volley for the party in December 2016 and was its only representative in Legislature VII.1,2 The Grass Party, founded by Power of Greed, later formed out of it.
