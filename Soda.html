Lucca Heckel (c. 2001 – 18 May 2026), known online as Soda, was an American internet personality and politician within the Ninzard Project.1,2 A foundational member from the 2012 Broader Project of Zarden (BPZ) era, Heckel was part of the original "Solorado Cohort" and the close-knit "Edith Gang" alongside longtime leaders Spinzle, Pieban, and Snowby.2,3

Heckel served as the 7th District General of Scope from July 2022 to January 2023.4 His tenure was characterized by volatile administrative disputes, fierce conflict with the Ninzardia leadership, and the strain of managing unpopular expansionist mandates, culminating in his electoral defeat by Lyman in January 2023.5,6

Following years of untreated mental illness—including schizophrenia and dissociative identity disorder—Heckel became increasingly isolated, ultimately falling under the influence of the extremist Black Sun Fraternity (BSF).1,7 On 18 May 2026, Heckel died by suicide during a live video broadcast in Scope, an event that profoundly affected the community, prompted the legislative recall of Representative Lless, and catalyzed reforms surrounding community mental health and crisis intervention.1,8

Early life and Solorado (2012–2018)

Heckel joined the community in 2012 at approximately eleven years of age during the pre-foundation BPZ era.2,9 He settled in the District of Solorado, becoming a core figure of the "Solorado Cohort."3 Contemporaries described the early Heckel as an energetic, loyal, and technically inquisitive youth with an extensive knowledge of automobiles and mechanics.10

Heckel remained integrated within the "Edith Gang," the primary social circle surrounding Spinzle (Edith Arden).3,4 Following the deaths of Tiny2 and Holidae in mid-2018, the District of Solorado was formally abolished by the Legislature on 8 July 2018 to halt community collapse.11 Heckel, alongside other Solorado refugees, was transferred into the Nightian Faction, taking up residence in the District of Scope.11

District General of Scope (2022–2023)

Following the July 2022 elections, in which Spinzle vacated the Scope generalship upon her election as Governor of Nightwork, Heckel stood as the Original Party candidate for District General of Scope.4 Bolstered by his long-standing ties to Spinzle, Heckel defeated opposition candidate Yim with 88.9% of the vote, assuming office on 10 July 2022.4

Heckel's administration faced immediate difficulties. While Spinzle had governed Scope as a protected, low-activity sanctuary, the wider Original Party establishment—led by Hokan and Xorbit—pressured Heckel to implement aggressive expansion policies.5 Scopians resisted the influx of unvetted members, viewing it as a violation of the district's traditional culture.5

Concurrently, Heckel engaged in public disputes with Ninzardia Governor Roland and the "Gang of Five," who publicly criticized Heckel's moderation as incompetent and erratic.5,12 Under mounting pressure, Heckel displayed severe emotional volatility, making unhinged statements and graphic threats in public channels.5 Spinzle's refusal to remove Heckel from office strained her own administration, drawing accusations of favoritism from the Capital and fracturing her relationship with longtime friend Kendall.13

On 2 January 2023, on the eve of the general election, Heckel abruptly attempted to resign.5 Governor Spinzle appointed Caleb Jensen as acting General for twenty-four hours to maintain order, but the party apparatus required Heckel to remain on the ballot.5 On 3 January 2023, Heckel was defeated by Preservation Party candidate Lyman, securing only 20 of 58 votes (34.5%).5

Later activities and radicalization (2023–2026)

Following his electoral defeat, Heckel became increasingly alienated from mainstream server politics.1,5 In November 2024, the Edith Gang permanently dissolved following the fallout from Spinzle's "Dead Man's Switch" confrontation with President Catx3; Heckel joined Kendall and Pieban in severing all ties with Spinzle.14

During the February 2026 "Samson Coup," Heckel was exploited as an insider asset by Holy Iron Flank leader NGin.15 Leveraging his remaining administrative privileges in the Original Party server, Heckel granted NGin unauthorized permissions to seize the server infrastructure in exchange for financial consideration, unaware that Raffaele Bellucci had already detected the plot.15 Following the neutralization of the coup, Heckel's standing among the Orie leadership was completely destroyed.15

In his final months, Heckel fell under the influence of Torin Graves (Scalen), leader of the esoteric neo-fascist Black Sun Fraternity (BSF).7 Graves exploited Heckel's escalating psychiatric breakdown—characterized by severe paranoia, schizophrenia, and a French-speaking dissociative alter named "Carter"—to manipulate him into self-destructive behavior.1,7 Despite extensive efforts by Waffle members Lesty and Matcha to de-escalate Heckel's distress through French-language communication, Heckel severed contact with them in May 2026, viewing all external support as hostile surveillance.1

Death and memorial

At 02:33 PST on the morning of 18 May 2026, Heckel broadcast his suicide via a live video stream in a Scope voice channel before twelve members.1,16 The footage was immediately clipped and weaponized by BSF affiliates to cause distress across the server.1,16

Minister of Justice Kay instituted an immediate zero-tolerance directive, mandating permanent, unappealable bans for any user distributing or requesting the recording, while the Capital deployed crisis resources across the network.16 When Scopian Representative Lless publicly celebrated Heckel's death, General Hyperio initiated an emergency recall vote under Section 13 of the Charter, resulting in Lless's decisive removal from Legislature XI by an 85% majority.8

On 23 May 2026, an official memorial service was conducted on a restricted Stage channel in Scope, organized by President Apollos, Vice President Spinzle, and Minister of Health & Privacy Idodo.10 The event featured speeches by Spinzle, Caleb Jensen, Durand, AJ, Matcha, Liver, Raffaele, and Idodo, alongside a coordinated community-wide period of silence.10 By consensus of the district, Heckel's memorialized Discord account was retained in Scope under restricted permissions.10
