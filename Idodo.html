Jonah Daniel McIntyre (born July 12, 2001), commonly known by the online handle Idodo, is an American political figure within the Ninzard Project. A veteran member of the Class of 2012 and the Solorado cohort, McIntyre served as the 3rd District General of Solorado (2016–2017) and later as the 1st Minister of Health & Privacy (2025–2026) under President Reuben Park.

Throughout his fourteen-year involvement, McIntyre was noted for his procedural centrism, support for personal privacy, and institutional focus on psychological well-being. During the 2025–2026 restoration period, he served briefly as Capital Adjudicator before leading the newly created Ministry of Health & Privacy (MoHP), where he spearheaded the Ninzard Career Initiative and managed community-wide mental health resources in the wake of the suicide of former General Liam Higgins. McIntyre stepped down from public life alongside the rest of the second Apollos administration on July 1, 2026.

Early involvement (2012–2016)

Jonah McIntyre joined the Broader Project of Zarden in late 2012 as an early member of the Solorado Legion, associating with regional leaders Gabriel Locklear and Fabricio Fragoso ("Faber"). Having experienced disruption during his youth due to his parents' divorce, McIntyre developed a pronounced emphasis on order, stability, and organizational tidiness.

In December 2013, McIntyre was appointed as one of Solorado's delegates to the Ninzard Legislature by General Locklear. During the tenure of Legislature II in early 2014, McIntyre aligned with regional leaders against the controversial No Swearing Act (NSA) introduced by Vice President Stephen Baek ("Kain"). Emulating Locklear, McIntyre deliberately broke the profanity restriction as a protest action; he was temporarily banned until pardoned by Capital Adjudicator JackManJay. Following the recall of Kain and Locklear's elevation to the Vice Presidency in February 2014, McIntyre remained aligned with the Resistance Party, advocating for decentralized authority and the mitigation of factional conflict.

Solorado generalship and the Golden Era (2016–2018)

During the July 2016 elections following the Ninzard Civil War, McIntyre broke ranks with the Resistance establishment to run for District General of Solorado on the populist Original Party ticket. In an upset outcome, McIntyre defeated the incumbent, Fragoso, becoming the 3rd District General of Solorado.

Shortly after taking office, McIntyre emerged as an outspoken internal critic of Sacraday General Judah over the latter's recurring use of racial slurs and disruption of legislative sessions. McIntyre joined Vice President Isaiah Choi ("Hokan"), Capital Adjudicator Malcolm McCluskey ("MMC"), and Ninzardia Governor Locklear in petitioning President Park for Judah's removal, clashing directly with Park over executive tolerance of interpersonal toxicity. McIntyre was re-elected to a second six-month term as District General in December 2016. In June 2017, following a prolonged period of political fatigue and friction with local organizers, McIntyre was defeated in his bid for a third term by Edith Arden ("Spinzle"), who ran as a moderate candidate under the Resistance banner.

Factional politics and Catx3 harassment (2018–2025)

Following his generalship, McIntyre remained active as an independent voice. During the 2020 presidential election, he was selected by Revamp Party nominee Julia Yoo ("Czulia") as her vice-presidential running mate, presenting a centrist, anti-expansionist platform in opposition to the incumbent Apollos–Helen ticket. In the July 2022 presidential election, McIntyre ran as the Original Party's nominee, placing fourth with 202 out of 490 votes (41.2%).

During the presidency of Catherine Avalon ("Catx3"), McIntyre was targeted after refusing financial overtures from the Capital. In 2022, Avalon attempted to bribe McIntyre with $280 USD; McIntyre declined the offer entirely. On July 12, 2024—his twenty-third birthday—Avalon orchestrated the killing of McIntyre's dog, which was left hung on his front porch. Days later, Avalon executed a swatting attack against McIntyre's family residence. These incidents led McIntyre to take a temporary hiatus from Discord and join the counter-intelligence cell managed by Capitalia General Mikayla Holloway ("Kay").

On August 18, 2024, McIntyre traveled to Los Angeles for an in-person meeting with community members, including Grace Coolahan, Natalia Whitaker ("Woofer"), and Stephen Baek. When Avalon physically confronted the group inside an urban laser tag arena, McIntyre assisted in restraining Avalon after she initiated a physical altercation, successfully de-escalating the encounter prior to venue security intervening. McIntyre subsequently provided sworn testimony and corroborating evidence for the Catx3 Dossier.

Second Apollos administration (2025–2026)

Capital Adjudication and executive delegation

Following the resignation of Catherine Avalon in August 2025, Reuben Park re-entered the presidency on September 22, 2025, appointing McIntyre as Capital Adjudicator. Hours after taking office, Park and Vice President Arden announced an immediate executive hiatus, formally delegating administrative operations to McIntyre.

Facing planned raids and hostile trolling from members of the Original Party led by former Governor Caleb Jensen, McIntyre enacted an immediate communication lockdown on the central public servers. On the evening of September 23, 2025, McIntyre convened an unprecedented private conference with all five seated District Generals, persuading ideological rivals to de-escalate tensions and avoid retaliatory actions. McIntyre successfully stabilized the transition until the executive return.

Ministry of Health & Privacy

On November 3, 2025, following a major Cabinet reorganization, the office of Capital Adjudicator was partitioned into the Ministry of Justice (assigned to Mikayla Holloway) and the Ministry of Health & Privacy (MoHP), to which McIntyre was appointed. In this role, McIntyre was charged with overseeing user welfare, mitigating burnout, and safeguarding personally identifiable information (PII).

In March 2026, McIntyre, in collaboration with the Ministry of Activity under Alessia Zorzi ("Poka"), launched the Ninzard Career Initiative (NCI). The initiative provided free resume workshops, technical formatting assistance, and simulated job interviews conducted in an auxiliary server to assist members in seeking offline employment. On May 1, 2026 (International Workers' Day), the program received private financial underwriting from Raffaele Bellucci, who offered direct micro-grants to members submitting verified resumes.

The Holloway Summit

On May 2, 2026, McIntyre was one of thirty leaders present at the Holloway residence in Los Angeles for the "Holloway Summit." During the armed arrival of Ethan O'Neal ("NGin") and members of the Holy Iron Flank, McIntyre physically intervened to disarm and restrain O'Neal after the latter initiated an assault against a fellow attendee. Following the subsequent spiritual reconciliation and de-escalation led by Malachi Holloway, McIntyre participated in the collective peace accord and attended church services alongside former political adversaries the following morning.

Crisis management and resignation

On May 18, 2026, former District General Liam Higgins ("Soda") died by suicide on a live video stream within a Scope voice channel. With President Park and Vice President Arden offline in Canada, McIntyre assumed lead crisis management responsibilities. He immediately opened private intake channels in the community's auxiliary server, published national and international mental health hotline resources, and coordinated real-time intervention for the twelve witnesses present during the broadcast.

On May 23, 2026, McIntyre organized and addressed the closed memorial service held for Higgins in the Scope stage channel, delivering the concluding address on adolescent isolation. On July 1, 2026, having completed his term, McIntyre officially resigned from the Capital Administration alongside President Park, Vice President Arden, and Minister Holloway. He was succeeded as Minister of Health & Privacy on July 2, 2026, by Katerina Johnson.
