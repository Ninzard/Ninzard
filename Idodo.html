Jonah Daniel McIntyre (born 12 July 2001), known as Idodo, is an American Ninzard politician who served as the 3rd District General of Solorado from 2016 to 2017 and as the inaugural Minister of Health & Privacy from 3 November 2025 to 1 July 2026.1,2

A veteran member of the Class of 2012 and the Solorado cohort, McIntyre was characterized across his fourteen-year tenure by an emphasis on procedural order, personal privacy, and institutional stability.3,4 Initially aligned with Gabriel's Resistance Party, he broke ranks during the 2016 realignment to join the Original Party, unseating incumbent General Faber in Solorado.1 During the presidency of Catx3, McIntyre became a target of physical and digital retaliation after rejecting Capital bribery, suffering a swatting attack and the targeted killing of his pet dog in July 2024.5,6

Following the fall of the Cat regime in 2025, McIntyre joined President Apollos's Pragma Flank within the Preservation Party. He briefly served as Capital Adjudicator before assuming the newly partitioned Ministry of Health & Privacy (MoHP), where he established the Ninzard Career Initiative (NCI) and directed community crisis interventions following the death of former General Soda.2,7,8 He retired from public administration alongside Apollos on 1 July 2026.9

Early life and Solorado Legion (2012–2016)

McIntyre joined the Broader Project of Zarden in late 2012 as an original member of the Solorado Legion. Following his parents' divorce during his youth, McIntyre experienced heightened anxiety surrounding disorder, which manifested as an acute personal and political preference for structural tidiness and predictability.3 In primary school he was regarded by peers as mature and deliberate.10

In December 2013, McIntyre was appointed by General Gabriel Locklear as one of Solorado's legislative representatives.3 During the tenure of Legislature II in early 2014, McIntyre protested the controversial No Swearing Act (NSA) introduced by Vice President Kain. Following Locklear's lead, McIntyre deliberately violated the profanity policy and received a permanent ban; he was subsequently pardoned by Capital Adjudicator JackManJay.4 He remained a core organizer within the Resistance Party throughout the 2014–2016 factional conflicts.3

District General of Solorado (2016–2017)

In the July 2016 elections following the Ninzard Civil War, McIntyre unexpectedly ran on the populist Original Party ticket, defeating incumbent Resistance General Faber to become the 3rd District General of Solorado.1

Shortly after taking office, McIntyre emerged as an outspoken critic of Sacraday General Judah over the latter's recurring use of racial slurs and disruption of legislative sessions. McIntyre joined Vice President Hokan, Capital Adjudicator MMC, and Governor Locklear in demanding Judah's removal, clashing directly with President Apollos over executive tolerance of interpersonal toxicity.11 McIntyre was re-elected to a second term in December 2016.12 In June 2017, following prolonged friction with Locklear and Faber's regional political machine, McIntyre was defeated in his bid for a third term by Spinzle.13

Factional politics and Catx3 targeting (2018–2025)

McIntyre ran for Vice President on Julia Yoo's (Czulia) Revamp Party ticket in the 2020 presidential election, advocating for decentralization and an end to the Apollos–Helen administration's expansionism; the ticket placed second with 28.8% of the vote.14 In the July 2022 presidential election, McIntyre stood as the Original Party's nominee, placing fourth with 202 out of 490 votes (41.2%).15 In December 2023, he contested the District Generalship of Waffle, narrowly losing to Matcha.16

During the presidency of Catherine Avalon (Catx3), McIntyre refused financial overtures from the Capital, rejecting an attempted bribe of $280 USD.6 On 12 July 2024—his twenty-third birthday—Avalon orchestrated the killing of McIntyre's dog, which was discovered hung on his front porch. Days later, Avalon executed a swatting attack against McIntyre's family home.5

On 18 August 2024, McIntyre attended an in-person gathering in Sherman Oaks, California. When Avalon confronted the attendees inside an urban laser tag facility, McIntyre physically intervened alongside Kain, restraining Avalon after she assaulted Woofer and Laser, and de-escalating the altercation prior to venue security intervening.17 McIntyre subsequently joined General Kay's counter-intelligence cell, providing sworn testimony for the Catx3 Dossier.18

Apollos II administration (2025–2026)

Capital Adjudicator

Following the collapse of the Cat regime, Apollos was elected to a second presidency on 20 September 2025 and appointed McIntyre as Capital Adjudicator on 22 September.2 Hours after taking office, Apollos and Vice President Spinzle embarked on an executive hiatus, formally delegating administrative operations to McIntyre.2

Facing planned raids and hostile trolling from members of the Original Party led by former Governor Caleb Jensen, McIntyre enacted an immediate communication lockdown on the central public servers.2 On the evening of 23 September 2025, McIntyre convened a private conference with all five seated District Generals, successfully persuading ideological rivals—including Rudolf and Mel—to de-escalate tensions and avoid retaliatory actions, stabilizing the transition.2

Minister of Health & Privacy

On 3 November 2025, the office of Capital Adjudicator was partitioned into the Ministry of Justice (assigned to Kay) and the Ministry of Health & Privacy (MoHP), with McIntyre appointed to lead the latter.19 He was tasked with overseeing user welfare, safeguarding personally identifiable information (PII), and mitigating burnout.2

In March 2026, McIntyre, in collaboration with Minister of Activity Poka, launched the Ninzard Career Initiative (NCI). The program provided resume evaluations, portfolio formatting, and simulated job interviews in an auxiliary server to assist members in obtaining offline employment.7 On 1 May 2026 (International Workers' Day), the program received private financial underwriting from Raffaele Bellucci, who funded direct micro-grants for participants submitting verified resumes.20

The Holloway Summit

On 2 May 2026, McIntyre was one of thirty leaders present at the Holloway residence in Los Angeles for the "Holloway Summit." When NGin and members of the Holy Iron Flank arrived armed with blunt weapons, McIntyre physically intervened alongside Sev to disarm and restrain NGin after he kicked a member in the head.21 Following the subsequent de-escalation and prayer led by Malachi Holloway, McIntyre participated in the collective peace accord and attended church services alongside former political adversaries the following morning.21,22

Crisis management and resignation

On 18 May 2026, former General Liam Higgins (Soda) died by suicide on a live video stream within a Scope voice channel.8 With President Apollos and Vice President Spinzle offline in Canada, McIntyre assumed lead crisis management responsibilities. He immediately opened private intake channels in the community's auxiliary server, published national and international mental health hotline resources, and coordinated real-time intervention for the twelve witnesses present during the broadcast.8

Liam had a family. He had friends who loved him. He was carrying a profound amount of pain. … If you were in that voice channel this morning, or if you've seen the video, or if today's news is just bringing you to a dark place, please don't sit alone in your room with it. … We're here.

— Minister Idodo, 18 May 2026

On 23 May 2026, McIntyre addressed the closed memorial service held for Higgins in Scope, delivering the concluding address on adolescent isolation.23 On 1 July 2026, having completed his term, McIntyre officially resigned from the Capital Administration alongside Apollos, Spinzle, and Kay.9 He was succeeded as Minister of Health & Privacy on 2 July 2026 by Katdsm.24

Personal life and style

McIntyre resides in Colorado. Shaped by his parents' divorce, his approach to governance emphasized tidiness, procedural predictability, and conflict de-escalation.3 Politically, he evolved from a Resistance-aligned legalist to a moderate Original Party figure, before concluding his career in the Pragma Flank of the Preservation Party.1,2,3
