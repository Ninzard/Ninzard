Stephen Baek (born 1 April 2002), known as Kain, is a Ninzard politician and troll who served as the 2nd Vice President of the Ninzard Project from 1 January to 14 February 2014, under Antareze, and as the first Boss of the Original Party, then called the Tomato Party, until Apollos succeeded him in July 2015.1 He was twice General of Parry, as its 2nd general from 2016 to 2019 and its 5th from 2019 to 2022, and now represents the district in the Legislature.2,3,4

Remembered as Ninzard's “King of Trolls,” Kain blew up Syn's Minecraft fortress in the Han River Incident, led Parry out from under Syn's control as the server BobaBitcoin, and, as the alt account OneAgainstAll, deleted the District of Volley in a single day in 2022.5,6,7,8,9 He was one of the six who exposed Cat's crimes in August 2025.10 His long feud with Aceiron ended in March 2026, when, knowing he would be arrested, he stayed with his injured rival in his Los Angeles apartment and called for help.11,12

Early life

Baek was born on 1 April 2002 and lives in Los Angeles, where he has studied at Santa Monica College.9,13 His family is Korean, with relatives in South Korea, and he and Syn often traded insults in Korean.14,13 His cousin Jay Baek, known as Brutus, was a fellow Orie and troublemaker who became General of Main.15,14,16

Early career (2014–2016)

Kain served as the 2nd Vice President of the Ninzard Project under President Antareze from 1 January to 14 February 2014, at the age of eleven, between Splatter and Gabriel. He was also the first Boss of the Original Party, which began as the Tomato Party, until Apollos succeeded him on 11 July 2015.1 His early years left him with a reputation for destructiveness that outlasted them: in 2017 the Legislature still turned him down for office “due to his past.”17

First generalship of Parry (2016–2019)

Election

Transferred to Nightwork, the former vice president was elected General of Nightwork-Parry for the Original Party in the 2016 district general elections, succeeding DegreeZ. Apollos had repaired his reputation by featuring him in many of his videos.18,1 He was re-elected in June and December 2017.19,20 When Apollos nominated him for Capital Adjudicator in 2017, the Legislature rejected him as untrustworthy, which offended him; he voted against Raffaele, the eventual choice, and taunted him with slurs, calling him a “con artist” for his hacking and scams.17,21

The Han River Incident

Finding Syn's obsession with order “pretentious,” Kain spent three days in 2017 secretly tunnelling beneath Syn's Minecraft fortress and lining its foundations with TNT, then lit the charge while Syn was streaming a tour of his finished base. Syn vowed to bury him under the Han River; Kain laughed until he wheezed. The episode cemented his status as a troll king, and the slang it produced became the foundation of Parry's culture.5,22

Tensions in Nightwork

In 2018 Kain, Brutus and their friends clashed with Governor DegreeZ over moderation, echoed Hyperio's rhetoric against Nightwork's legitimacy and bullied some Nightwork members they knew in real life.23 He mocked Syn by spreading photos of him through the chats and tried to have him hacked, until Apollos ordered him to stop.24 Backed by Governor Spikester, whom he supported, he kept Parry in the snap elections of December 2018, but narrowly lost it to Atria, an independent, in January 2019.25,26

Second generalship of Parry (2019–2022)

Return

Determined to win back his “crown,” Kain ran against General Helen in December 2019 and narrowly defeated her after a heated contest, becoming the 5th General of Parry.2 In 2020 he joined in mocking Xorbit when Xorbit's presidential campaign collapsed amid accusations from women.27

BobaBitcoin

On 1 September 2020 Kain and Brutus, now General of Main, defied Governor Syn's lockdown of Nightwork by inviting about two dozen new members. Syn stripped their permissions, but after the cousins and the governor cursed at each other in Korean on a call, Apollos sided with Kain and restored them.14 When Syn kept his grip on the old Parry server, Kain declared that “도토리's got our back,” and Parry's members left to found a new server under his control, BobaBitcoin.6 Easily re-elected that October, he kept Syn out of the district but promised there would be “no war,” and made BobaBitcoin the only district members could freely join and leave.28

In January 2021 a drug-fuelled night in the grounds of a Los Angeles elementary school ended with a police citation, and the community mocked him for it relentlessly.29 He was re-elected narrowly in May 2021, against ScottyAte of the Grass Party, and again in August.30,31 He was among the most important Paleories, the populist Ories who wanted the party to return to its Tomato Party roots, and he defended Mel's staff role in BobaBitcoin against Syn's demand that she keep to one district.16 In January 2022 he lost BobaBitcoin to Aurase, the only Nightian ever elected for the Poo Party.32 With the rest of the Orie establishment, he was incensed at Apollos for backing Snowby against his own vice president.33

OneAgainstAll (2022)

In the chaos of 2022 Kain operated a troll account, OneAgainstAll, which those who trusted him gave permissions across Nightwork and which he used to delete chats and ban members; his confidants kept quiet to protect their own reputations.7 Mel, who found him funny, made him an administrator of Volley, and on 5 May 2022 he banned the whole district and deleted every channel.8 Cat tried to pay him to cause chaos; he took five dollars and told her he would do it for free.34,35 OneAgainstAll was even nominated for president that summer under the Grass Party banner, though most voters knew who was behind it and would not elect an alt.36,37 When Syn learned the truth after the election, he raged at Kain in Korean; Kain laughed and pulled Apollos into the call.38

Under Cat (2023–2025)

In December 2023 Kain was the Original Party's candidate for Deputy Governor of Nightwork, as running mate to ViaTea, General of Waffle. Their campaign turned the race into a close contest, but Adelynn and Grace won re-election with 53.9% of the vote.39 With Burnette he helped “lost” Ninzardians join Nightwork with Adelynn's permission, and in early 2024 he, Spinzle, Burnette and ViaTea reached out to former President Apollos.40,41

When the party's leadership opened in June 2024, Kain appeared from nowhere to challenge Aceiron for it, declaring himself the “OG Orie leader” come to reclaim his “rightful place.” On 9 June he released a diss track against Aceiron that swung momentum his way, but Aceiron's allies obtained a compromising recording of him, which Aceiron circulated before the vote, and won the leadership.42,43,44

On 18 August 2024, at a laser tag arena in Sherman Oaks, Kain was among a group of members who found themselves confronted by Cat, who had followed them there. He mocked her by repeatedly disabling her vest, and when she seized Woofer by the throat, he and 7orB forced her to let go.45 In 2025 he joined the Sactown Pound, a cell that spent a month hunting Cat and Meikaqu in Sacramento, treating the whole affair as “content” and then as “a wild vacation.”46,47,48 On 19 August 2025 he was one of the six, with Kay, Spinzle, Apollos, 7orB and ViaTea, who exposed the full extent of Cat's crimes, and he worked on the Cat Files until he withdrew on 3 November.10,37 Her ledger records the five dollars he accepted and $6,832 he refused.35

The Capital and the “gayest breakup” (2025)

Kain campaigned hard for Apollos's comeback, insulting Aceiron and clashing so childishly with Syn that Grace asked them both to be quiet.49 After the victory he and ViaTea were the first members of the new Capital.50 On 24 October 2025, however, Apollos told him he was “unfit” to lead in the current environment and endorsed the Preservate Phalth in the Parry snap election. Kain ran anyway as an independent to spite him, and finished last on 28 October with 29 of 130 votes (22.3%), behind Boygho and Phalth.51,52 During the campaign he confronted Aceiron in a Parry voice call at 3 a.m.; a recording of their profane argument spread, Parryans revived his old diss track, and Aceiron left the district before the vote.53

His public rampage after the defeat ended his alliance with the president: on 3 November Apollos removed his and ViaTea's Capital permissions, in what Aceiron dubbed “the gayest breakup.” Kain left the Preservation Party and joined ViaTea's Liberation Party, sitting in the secondary leadership of the Liberator–Orie Coalition that winter.50,54,55 Elected on 27 December 2025 as one of the representatives to the Charter forum that became Legislature XI, he and the troll Rickard led the “Low EE” insurgency there, flooding debate with absurd images of dogs to make serious discussion impossible.56

2026

Crisis and return

In January 2026, after a despairing late-night tirade against several of the community's leaders, Kain fell silent; Ploutos and Hackman drove to his apartment, and he was hospitalized after a near-fatal overdose and treated for his mental health.57,58 Apollos, who felt guilty about their break, appealed to Adelynn as Boss of the Preservation Party, and on 23 February 2026 Kain and ViaTea rejoined the Preservate Apparatus at its lowest rank. His first message asked Apollos where the toilets were that he needed to scrub.59 In the debate on the Charter he argued that a rigid, written code would only show trolls where its edges lay, and that moderation should be left to each district; Adelynn declared him “entirely correct.”60

Feud with Aceiron

In February Kain humiliated Aceiron with an AI clone of his voice reading erotic fan fiction, tricking him into playing it on his own stream and then spreading it among his fellow students in Sacramento.61 Aceiron retaliated by exposing Kain's addiction to his college and his family in South Korea, which cut him off from their support. Kain answered by having members harass Aceiron's sister and, on 26 March, by releasing rats into the house Aceiron shared with his ailing mother.13

On 29 March Aceiron drove to Los Angeles and forced his way into Kain's apartment. When an armed visitor arrived, Aceiron suffered a panic attack, collapsed and struck his head. Kain sent ViaTea away, stayed with his bleeding rival and called for help, knowing that the police would find the drugs in his home; he was arrested on felony drug charges.11 He spent his 24th birthday, 1 April, in custody, as trolls from all five districts gathered to mark it in his absence; the Capital, with Raffaele's help, helped pay for his defence lawyer.9,12 Kaimachi took his seat in Legislature XI.62,63

Recovery

Woofer named him an Honorary Veteran of the Preservate Apparatus in April.64 Fresh from thirty days in a residential rehabilitation centre, he was among the guests at the Holloway Summit on 2 May 2026, where he and Aceiron exchanged a quiet nod.65,66 After the Charter's ratification he met Apollos, Syn, Raffaele and Aceiron in Seattle, and appeared healthy and sober in the photograph the five took together.67,68 That summer Parry elected him its representative with 91 votes (70.0%), as a Preservate, and in August he and Mélanie, Parry's other representative, tried to help General Kaimachi keep the district from imploding during the Salon War.3,4,69

Legacy

Kain is remembered as Ninzard's troll king. Parry's dialect, which grew up under his generalships and those of his successors, owes several of its words to him, from the Han River Incident to his gym photos and their talk of “vascular” veins, and to his habit of citing his 130 IQ just before doing something self-destructive.22 The archive offers his return as proof of what the community could forgive: when he survived an overdose, Aceiron mocked him, but the Apparatus welcomed him back.70
