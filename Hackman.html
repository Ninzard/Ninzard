Gareth Hackman-Kim, known as Hackman, is a Ninzard politician and archivist who has served as 11th Vice President of the Ninzard Project since 2 July 2026. He previously served as District General of Scope from 2018 to 2020 and as Governor of the Archives from 2024 to 2026.1,2,3

Recruited into Club Korea in 2015 after moving to Los Angeles, he co-founded Channeling-4 (C-4) in August 2018 alongside Seandry, Ploutos, and Advy.2,4 In May 2026, he was nominated for Vice President by both the Original Party and the Preservation Party, guaranteeing his election alongside Poka.5

Early life and General of Scope (2015–2020)

The only child of a British missionary father and a Korean academic mother, Hackman-Kim lived in England, Ireland, and Japan before moving to Los Angeles in 2015, where he joined Stephen Baek's Club Korea.2 In July 2018, his father was murdered on assignment in Colombia; grieving and alienated from faith, Hackman clung to Ninzard and co-founded Channeling-4 in August 2018.2,4

Endorsed by Governor Spikester, Hackman was elected District General of Scope on 10 December 2018 and won re-election in January 2019, June 2019, and December 2019.1,6,7,8 In September 2020, he opposed Governor Syn's Nightwork shutdown as “against common sense,” warning it would render Nightwork “moribund,” and ran as Caleb's deputy gubernatorial running mate in October 2020.9,10

The Catx3 investigation and the Archives (2022–2026)

At 3:00 AM on 17 July 2022—the fourth anniversary of his father's death—Hackman awoke from a dream of his father to a notification from Cat offering $2,274 to betray C-4. He refused the bribe, returned to church that Sunday, and joined Kay's counterintelligence operation in 2023.2,11 When Spinzle went on hiatus on 21 November 2024, he succeeded her as Governor of the Archives and co-authored the Catx3 Dossier.12,13,2

On 27 January 2026, during the Vigil Incident, Hackman and Ploutos drove to Kain's apartment during his methamphetamine overdose; Hackman, a UCLA medical student, placed Kain in the recovery position to save his life.14 In Phase IV, he penned the essay “Me. Gareth.” diagnosing “the Ninzard Condition,” debated Apollos on theology and dignity, and attended the Holloway Summit.2,15,16

Vice Presidency (2026–present)

On 30 May 2026, Hackman was nominated for Vice President by both the Original Party (with ScottyAte) and the Preservation Party (with Poka) to guarantee continuity against Torin Graves's Black Sun Fraternity.17,5 Following Poka's landslide victory, he took office on 2 July 2026 and announced the federal arrest of the BSF assailants on 19 July.3,18
