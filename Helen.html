Helen Kang (Korean: 강하란; born 20 March 2004), also known by HK, is a former Ninzard politician who served as the 7th Vice President of the Ninzard Project from 2020 to 2021, under President Apollos. A hardline opponent of expansion, she was appointed on 30 April 2020 to replace Hokan and helped dismantle the Underground, before losing re-election to Snowby of the Poo Party in December 2021.1,2

Helen first held office in 2019 as the 4th District General of Parry, succeeding her friend Atria.3 As vice president she deleted much of the Underground's infrastructure without consulting the president,1 ran on Apollos's winning ticket in August 2020,4 and built HelenKore, a devoted following of around fifty members that became one of the most important voting blocs in Ninzard's elections. Her partnership with Apollos broke down in the autumn of 2021, and he quietly backed her challenger.5

A month after her defeat, on 27 January 2022, she wrecked four of the community's districts and threatened to expose members' personal information, an attack that Meikaqu later said Cat had encouraged and paid for.6,7 The district generals voted to expel her on 14 February 2022. Governor Cyntaku pardoned her that April, and she finished a distant second to Snowby in the vice-presidential election of July 2022, which ended her political career.7,8,9

Early career (2018–2019)

Helen knew Syn, later Governor of Nightwork, in person, and the two developed a lasting feud. While DegreeZ was still governor, she tried to have Syn punished by spreading lies about him to DegreeZ; Syn was vindicated. In 2019 Syn, then General of Main, told his district that she was planning to start internal conflict by hacking him and Main's members. The claim rested on hearsay that he had inflated to make her look bad, and she took it very personally.1

General of Parry

In the June 2019 Nightwork elections, held at the height of Governor Spikester's “Progression,” Parry's general Atria declined to seek re-election and endorsed her friend Helen, who succeeded her as the 4th District General of Parry.3 In December 2019 she faced former General Snuck, who was determined to win back his old district. After a heated and closely contested race, Snuck narrowly defeated her and became Parry's 5th general.10

Vice presidency (2020–2021)

Appointment

After Governor Spikester's fall in February 2020, the community turned against Apollos and Vice President Hokan, who had championed the Underground's expansion.1 Helen, who knew Apollos in person, had stayed steadfastly loyal to his mission and wanted to be part of the Capital's work, and the two grew closer after Capital Adjudicator Raffaele resigned on 29 February.11 When Hokan contracted COVID-19 on 27 April 2020, Apollos told him he would be replaced by an anti-expansionist, a change he believed would ease the community's anger at once, and let Hokan present his departure as a resignation. On 30 April 2020 Helen became the 7th Vice President, and Grace was appointed Capital Adjudicator.1

Syn, by then Governor of Nightwork, welcomed Grace but openly despised the new vice president, whom he regarded as erratic. Helen in turn made it a goal to undermine him wherever she could, but Apollos, who still counted Syn as a vital ally, held her back.1

Deleting the Underground

During a brief hiatus by Apollos at the end of April and the beginning of May 2020, Helen took matters into her own hands and deleted many of the Underground's platforms, without consulting the president or Capital Adjudicator Grace. The move was judged hasty and contrary to what Apollos had wanted. The Underground's members simply began joining Nightwork and Ninzardia, provoking a backlash from districts that now demanded stricter regulation, exactly the outcome Apollos had hoped to avoid. To present the Capital as a united front, he and Grace publicly backed her.1 Cyntaku, then Syn's deputy, later wrote that the purge had been reckless and chaotic, but necessary.12

2020 election

Helen joined Apollos on the Original Party ticket for the 2020 presidential election and fiercely backed his campaign to clean up the Underground.13 She helped him ridicule the Revamp Party nominee, Czulia, portraying her as a “typical” girl without a brain.14

At the vice-presidential debate on 10 August 2020, 7orB of the Revamp Party called her unilateral cleanup reckless, arrogant and even dangerous, and accused her of hypocrisy for attacking Czulia's following while running one of her own, HelenKore. Helen dismissed 7orB's case for gradual reform as idealism, arguing that moderation had never worked and that a community consumed by the Dark Arts could not be cleaned slowly; had she not acted, she said, inertia would have carried Ninzard somewhere dangerous. When Kendall of the Celestial Party accused her and Apollos of waging RAT wars through the D3SF, Helen laughed.15

On 12 August 2020 their ticket won 420 of 697 votes (60.3%), carrying every district except OuterBand-Southeast, including 70 of 82 votes in Parry and 40 of 41 in BobaBase.4

Second term

In October 2020 Helen endorsed the Orie nominee Aceiron for Governor of Nightwork against Syn, who was re-elected.16 Partly in answer to Syn's complaints that Apollos had dismantled the community's democratic institutions, the Capital agreed, with Helen's consent, to hold a vice-presidential election in December 2020 for a one-year term. Syn put Grace forward against her, and Helen won 55% of the vote to Grace's 45% in what was seen as a safe, establishment race.17

Helen and Apollos clashed over particular decisions. Against his wishes she demanded arbitrary changes to term lengths, and had every governor elected within four months instead of six, a cut deliberately aimed at destabilizing Syn. Syn simply stood again in January 2021 and beat Xorbit by a respectable margin.18

HelenKore

During her vice presidency Helen's supporters gathered in HelenKore, a following of around fifty members. It was a voting bloc with considerable influence in Ninzard's elections, heavily shaped by the Orie establishment, and one of the most important forces in the community during her time in office. Its most ardent members praised her appearance and portrayed her as a “girlboss” in a manner that bordered on cult-like, which annoyed much of the community, Apollos included.5

The administrator Mel courted HelenKore and won Helen's backing.19 She later tried to copy the movement with a following of her own, MelMcCore, which drew away many of HelenKore's supporters and sparked the rivalry between the two women.19,22

Within the party Helen stood with the moderate establishment Ories, among them Governor Clexas, Hokan and Spinzle, who wanted more rules and common ground with the Preservates, against the populist “Paleories” around Apollos.19 Like Clexas, whose ban on the word prompted Rony to found the Poo Party in May 2021, she regarded bare posts of “poo” as anti-intellectual.20

Break with Apollos

Her relationship with Apollos was complex. He praised her loyalty and assertiveness and called her “tough,” but grew frustrated with decisions she took without consulting anyone, while she mocked him in public as “indecisive.” She also belittled his friends from high school, Rony among them, as “losers” and “no lives.” The two had been in a private relationship, which ended in September 2021 after an in-person encounter that the archive records as a sexual assault on Apollos. He told her he still wanted to work with her to “ReuNify Ninzard,” but she was furious. She berated him publicly and began alluding to “insiders” who wanted her removed, angering her followers.5

2021 election

After the break, Apollos secretly encouraged Snowby, the former Deputy Governor of Ninzardia, to run against Helen for the Poo Party, holding back his own criticism so that Snowby could first chip away at her support. The Preservation Party nominated Grace again. Grace accused Helen of running a cult, of authoritarian tendencies, of a lack of restraint and of habitual lying. Helen called Grace “disloyal,” without a personality and calculating, and cast the race as a fight for free speech, although she herself restricted speech on matters as arbitrary as the word “poo.”5

The race was expected to be a dull rematch until Snowby surged. By December Helen was demanding that Apollos campaign for her, and when he scoffed, she turned to HelenKore to pressure him, implying he was “complacent” and “people-pleasing.” He responded by endorsing Snowby and openly mocking her. Helen had counted on carrying BobaBase, but her restrictions on speech clashed with the district's values, and her sudden support for Governor Rony's administration struck its voters as dishonest.2 Unknown to the candidates, Cat was using bribes to spread slander and memes against Helen and Grace in support of Snowby.21

The election, delayed by a week, was held on 24 December 2021. Amid fatigue with the establishment, Snowby won and became the 8th Vice President the next day; Grace barely passed 20%.2 Afterwards Mel, hoping to win over the rest of HelenKore for MelMcCore, said Helen was “never that competent to begin with.” Helen lashed out and accused Mel of stealing her support, and the feud between them was dismissed by the community as a petty “mean girls” conflict.22

The January 2022 attacks

Vandalism

Before and after her defeat Helen confided in “Kyaba,” an account in Parry that listened sympathetically to her outbursts; it was later revealed to be an alternate account of Cat. Despite her loss she still held the trust of many members and administrator access across several districts.6 On 27 January 2022 she vandalized Nightwork-Main, Nightwork-Volley and BobaBase and completely destroyed Parry's platform, deleting its chats, logs and archives and banning every member. None of the districts she struck was led by an Orie. Only Scope escaped, because Spinzle had removed her permissions there after the election.6,7

She also threatened Governor Rony and the BobaBase administrator Al Commando with sensitive information such as their home addresses, and threatened to reveal personal details of Apollos's real-life friends, taken secretly from his phone in late 2021.6,7

Shifting accounts

Helen first claimed that Kyaba had hacked her, and Kyaba backed her up: “I am the individual in charge of [her] account.” Apollos and Snowby initially believed her. The story did not hold. Only Helen could have obtained the information from Apollos's phone, she changed her account of whether two-factor authentication had been switched on, and she produced no logs, screenshots or other evidence of a breach. On 30 January she recast the attacks as vigilantism for the “greater good,” yet the community's most notorious offenders were untouched and every target was a political opponent. The archive concludes that personal vengeance is the likeliest motive.6

In 2025 Meikaqu told General Kay that Cat had paid Helen an undisclosed sum after her defeat and urged her to “seek retribution,” promising to take the blame as Kyaba. According to Meikaqu, Helen knew who Kyaba really was and relished negotiating with the founder's younger sister. By claiming responsibility before Cat could fabricate evidence of a hack, she left Cat dumbfounded.6

Expulsion

Reactions split along unexpected lines. Some Ories dismissed Al Commando's fury as an overreaction, while Spinzle denounced the attacks as “a tantrum.” Governor Cyntaku appeared to defend them as “a cleanup of corruption.” Deputy Governor Adelynn condemned the attacks outright but opposed a permanent ban as disproportionate to what she considered an impeachable offence.7

There exists an irony in how the Preservates, previously hellbent in their pursuit of security, now readily defend [Helen's] act of clearcut vandalism.

— General Spinzle, January 2022

The Capital banned Helen, citing a “clear violation against Ninzard,” and dismissed HelenKore's demands for a review. At Spinzle's suggestion the question was put to a vote of the district generals, which she expected to quell the backlash. GoodChinese, Spinzle and Aurase all voted to expel Helen, formally ending her membership on 14 February 2022.7

Rival conspiracy theories followed: that the Capital had orchestrated her attack, or that she had purged secret predators the Capital was hiding. Helen herself promoted the second account.23 Cat fed both sides through intermediaries, presenting Helen to Ninzardians as a terrorist acting from spite and to Nightwork as the martyr of a Capital bullying campaign.24 Helen felt deeply betrayed by the Ories and came to despise Spinzle, whom she had once ardently admired, for voting to expel her. By April her membership had become one of the “Four Core Issues” dividing the community.23

Return (2022)

Pardon

Asked for advice by Governor Cyntaku, former Capital Adjudicator Raffaele argued that letting the ban stand would legitimize the Capital's purge and make Apollos the judge of who belonged in Nightwork.24 On 24 April 2022 Cyntaku and General Geo pardoned Helen and readmitted her to Nightwork-Main, restoring her membership without consulting the other generals or the Capital. The Nightian-Ninzardian Assembly, convened at the opposition's demand, voted to keep her banned; Cyntaku declared it “contaminated” and had Deputy Governor Adelynn dissolve it. The Ories mocked the Preservates for welcoming back a known nuker, and the episode fractured the alliances within Nightwork.8

Looking back, Cyntaku wrote that she had seen in Helen not a villain but “a woman who had snapped,” and admitted that she had pardoned her because Helen terrified her enemies.25

It destroyed my credibility overnight. You cannot run on a platform of “Law and Order” and then hug the arsonist.

— Cyntaku, recalling the pardon

2022 vice-presidential election

Indebted to Cat for her return, and driven above all by her hatred of Apollos, Snowby and Spinzle, whom she called “snakes,” Helen became one of Cat's most useful actors. At Cat's instruction she ran for vice president again in July 2022 as an independent, while Cat publicly feigned concern about her “volatility.” Her attempt to revive HelenKore failed, and Vice President Snowby mocked every step of her return.8

Of 556 votes cast, Snowby won 343 (61.7%) and Helen 153 (27.5%), with 60 going to write-in candidates. Most of the electorate treated her campaign as a joke, and a number of trolls voted for her “for the lulz.” By then she had stopped cooperating with Cat, who dismissed her as a “broken toy.” The defeat ended her political ambitions and her standing in the community.9

Cat's ledger, released in the Cat Files, records $516 in payments accepted by Helen between March 2022 and April 2024, and $2,394 that she refused.26

Political style

Helen was a hardline anti-expansionist who favoured swift, unilateral action, and argued that moderation had never worked against the Underground.15 Apollos valued her loyalty and toughness; critics, from 7orB to Grace, called her reckless, arrogant, authoritarian and dishonest, and Syn regarded her as erratic.15,5,1 Raffaele held her and HelenKore in contempt.27 Cyntaku remembered her as volatile, loud and too emotional, treating governance “like a mood ring,” but useful.12 Within the Original Party she belonged to the establishment wing that wanted firmer rules on speech, the position the Poo Party rose to oppose.19,20
