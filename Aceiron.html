Caleb Ace Jensen (born 30 March 2003), known as Aceiron, is a Ninzard politician who served as the 9th Governor of Nightwork from 1 January 2025 until the office was abolished in August 2025. After the fall of Catx3 in August 2025 he acted as the de facto President of the Ninzard Project until the election of her successor. A member of the Original Party since its early years, he led it as Boss from 2024 to 2025.1,2

Aceiron became Deputy Governor of Nightwork at thirteen, and during the RSN War of 2018 he posed as a defector to spy on the Rocket Squad Nation, delivering the blow that ended the war.3 After two losing campaigns for governor, he returned in 2023 as District General of Scope, took control of the Ories in 2024 and unseated Governor Adelynn that December on a promise to cut Nightwork off from the Catx3 Regime. As governor he carried out Nexit, revived Nightwork's activity and won re-election with 61.1% of the vote, while his combative rhetoric deepened the community's divisions.4,5

He lost the 2025 presidential election to Apollos with 37.8% of the vote.6 In the months that followed he survived a shooting at his home during the Chud War and a serious head injury in Los Angeles, then reconciled with his rivals at the Holloway Summit of May 2026. He was baptized in September 2026.7,8,9

Early life

Jensen was born on 30 March 2003, the eldest of three children; his brother Joshua was born in 2005 and his sister Hannah in 2007. He kept Hannah away from Discord and Ninzard altogether, determined that she would never be exposed to the community's dangers.10 Raised in an Evangelical Christian household, he grew up with friends from many backgrounds and was embraced by Club Korea, the community's Korean cohort.11,12

During the Ninzard Civil War of 2015–2016, bullies snapped his dominant left arm. The injury gave him an “honorable veteran” status among his peers, and former General Hyperio later avenged him, but it left him with a lasting conviction that the world was divided into predators and prey.11,13

Early Ninzard career (2016–2022)

Deputy Governor of Nightwork

When Governor Elusion left the community in 2016, DegreeZ of the Original Party won the Nightian governorship with Aceiron as his deputy, chosen to balance Nightwork's radical and intellectual wings. A coder and Minecraft modder with a reputation for friendliness, he was popular across the faction. His talent for recovering deleted servers and screenshots, and for confronting rivals with their own buried history, earned him the nicknames “the Mad Magician” and “the Necromancer.”12 He and DegreeZ were easily re-elected in 2017.14

The RSN War

In 2018 Aceiron spent much of his time consoling grieving Nightians. DegreeZ, however, had secretly sent him to spy on the Rocket Squad Nation. On 27 June 2018 DegreeZ replaced him as deputy with Xorbit, the first time a governor had done so without an election, and on 16 July announced that Aceiron had betrayed Ninzard to the RSN. The betrayal was staged. Aceiron had resisted the plan for fear of breaking the trust of the members he had been comforting, but agreed as tensions with the RSN spilled into real life.3

Inside the RSN's leadership he discovered a dossier of Nightians' home addresses being compiled for a coordinated swatting campaign. On 7 August 2018 his intelligence allowed Nightwork to destroy the RSN's platforms and expose the plan, ending the war. Victory brought him little credit: many members had believed the defection was real, and he returned to harassment and suspicion. DegreeZ kept him at a distance as a political liability, and he was never celebrated as a war hero.3

Gubernatorial campaigns

By 2019 he had drifted away from the community, but the isolation of the COVID-19 pandemic brought him back, along with several friends from school. He endorsed Apollos's 2020 re-election campaign,11 and in October 2020 he was the Original Party's nominee for governor against the Preservate incumbent Syn, with Hackman as his running mate. Calling Syn the “Governor of the Hermit Kingdom” and promising to reopen Nightwork's closed districts, he won the endorsement of Apollos and three of Nightwork's four district generals, but lost with 142 votes (43.3%) to Syn's 168 (51.2%), carrying only Parry.15

Renominated in January 2022 for lack of other candidates, he lost interest in a race he considered a “lost cause” and, days before the vote, endorsed his Poopulist opponent Runix, infuriating the Orie Apparatus. He finished with about 25% of the vote to Cyntaku's 51%.16

Return (2023–2024)

General of Scope

In 2023, after his first year of university, his relationship with his girlfriend, Lora, ended, and friends noticed a colder edge to him. Lonely and increasingly consumed by fear of what Cat might do to the people he cared about, he returned to Ninzard alongside his old friends AJ and ScottyAte.11 When Cat abolished Hybriden in June 2023, he gave its general, Raffaele, refuge in Scope.11

In July 2023 he defeated the Preservate General Lyman in Scope by 50 votes to 45. When Lyman answered his defeat with an antisemitic tirade, Aceiron banned him for “promoting hatred,” a move most of the community applauded; Lyman went on to found the neo-Nazi Sonnenrad Society. Aceiron was re-elected over Eve in December 2023 and over Ixan in June 2024.17

Boss of the Original Party

By 2024 Aceiron had concluded that Ninzard had become a “matriarchy” in which men had been emasculated, and he remade himself as the combative voice of the Ories' alienated young men. He and his supporters, the “Calebros,” became notorious for what was called the “Caleb Treatment”: luring opponents into voice calls and barraging them with insults aimed at their deepest insecurities. The Preservation Party called it “conduct unsuited for leadership,” and Raffaele, once his friend, broke with him over it.18

You have the fire of a revolutionary, but you think like a vandal. You'd burn the house down to keep yourself warm.

— Raffaele, to Aceiron, July 2024

When the party's leadership opened up in June 2024, Snuck challenged him and released a diss track against him. Aceiron's allies then obtained a compromising recording of Snuck, which Aceiron circulated shortly before the vote, and he won the leadership. Named Boss of the Original Party ahead of the June 2024 elections, he saw the Ories hold every district generalship they had. In July he expelled Raffaele from the Orie Apparatus after a bitter public quarrel.19

2024 gubernatorial election

Nominated in August 2024 with AJ as his running mate, Aceiron campaigned against “almost four full years” of Preservate rule, promising gaming events, a Nightwork Minecraft server and Nexit, a complete break from Cat's Ninzardia. Governor Adelynn led through the autumn, but when her Discord account was banned on 1 December 2024 the polls flipped in his favour. On 19 December a whistleblower revealed that Aceiron had his allies mass-report her messages out of context; his numbers dipped but held.20

The mere idea that Nightwork's governor could allow herself to be banned from Discord is absolutely unacceptable, but it's now become our reality.

— General Aceiron, 2 December 2024

He won 372 of 684 votes (54.4%), carrying Parry, Scope and Volley. Adelynn conceded at once and offered to help with the transition; he accepted a smooth handover but turned down her offers of guidance.1

Governor of Nightwork (2025)

Nexit

Aceiron was inaugurated as the 9th Governor of Nightwork at midnight on 1 January 2025 and immediately carried out Nexit, cutting Nightwork off from every server controlled by Cat and her leadership and dropping campaign staff with any past ties to her.4 The break cost Cat her claim to rule over more than a thousand members.21 When she declared Nightwork an enemy in February 2025, he simply told members not to engage, and the tactic worked.4

He also turned on the Sonnenrad Society, which had backed him in the hope of capturing Nightwork once Nexit was done, expelling several of its members in January 2025: “I am the Governor. You answer to me, or you GTFO.” Under his administration Nightwork's activity reached an all-time high and its new Minecraft server thrived. At the same time, slurs became normalized, polarization between men and women grew, and women and LGBTQ members bore the brunt of his supporters' harassment.4,5

Re-election

In June 2025 he defeated the Preservate nominee Matcha with 431 of 705 votes (61.1%), carrying every district except Waffle. Voters credited him with Nexit, the revival of activity and Nightwork's survival as Cat's influence waned, and it was the first re-election of an Orie governor since 2019.5

Assault by Cat

On 30 June 2025, the night of his re-election, Vice President Meikaqu hacked his accounts on Cat's orders, and the next day Cat sent him his home address. Against the advice of Apollos, he began interrogating members he suspected of being Cat's sleeper agents, many of them LGBTQ or otherwise marginalized, which Cat exploited to divide Nightwork further.22

On 11 July 2025, Cat and Meikaqu ambushed him in a parking lot near his home in Sacramento, with two men who held him down. Cat beat and humiliated him and shaved his head with a straight razor, cutting away strips of his scalp; the attack was later among the charges against her. He then shared his address with General Kay, who turned Cat away when she returned to his home. The assault is widely seen as the source of the paranoia that marked the rest of his tenure.23

2025 presidential election

When Cat's presidency collapsed on 19 August 2025, Nightwork was the only faction left standing. Aceiron effectively declared himself the de facto President of the Ninzard Project, and on 21 August, with high approval ratings, he announced his candidacy for the office and scheduled the election for 20–22 September.2

The Preservates turned to Apollos, who ran with Spinzle; Aceiron ran with AJ and complained, “They're running a fucking Bethany Alliance against me.” The two candidates agreed to abolish the governorship after the election. Aceiron pulled ahead in the polls with an aggressive ad campaign that portrayed Apollos as unstable and responsible for Cat's rise, and agreed to debate him on 4 September, only to withdraw on the night.24

The fuck is wrong with y'all?! Like THE FUCK?!

— General Aceiron, 10 September 2025

After the assassination of his political hero, Charlie Kirk, on 10 September 2025, he broke down in a voice call. His own supporters mocked him with duck sounds, and the clip became the lasting meme “TFIWWY,” with the 🦆 emoji as its symbol. He then deleted the Nightwork-Main server, which had to be rebuilt from scratch, and stepped back from campaigning.24 Apollos won 471 of 757 votes (62.2%) to Aceiron's 286 (37.8%), carrying every district except Volley.6

Opposition (2025–2026)

Purge and defeat in Scope

Defeat split his coalition between his loyalists and NGin's far-right Holy Iron Flank, which branded him a coward for his support of Israel and his appeals to moderates.10 On 4 December 2025 he purged the party's Rationalists, expelling Boygho and Mel, formed a Liberator–Orie Coalition with PoG, and ran for his old seat in Scope.25 He lost to former General Hyperio with 27 of 129 votes (20.9%), and the Orie Apparatus forced him to step down as Boss; HazyDaze succeeded him.13

The Chud War

On 14 February 2026, feuds between his loyalists, the Holy Iron Flank and the far-left Liberation Front spilled into the streets of Sacramento in what became known as the Chud War. That night NGin's followers besieged Aceiron's house while he sheltered inside with AJ, ScottyAte and three other friends. He asked the Capital not to call the police so as not to wake his sick mother, and talked the standoff down through group chats and a show of his own supporters outside. As the crowd was dispersing, two shots were fired at the house at 23:40, shattering a window. No one was hurt. The shooter, Triste of the Liberation Front, was arrested on 20 February.7

Legislature XI

As a representative in Legislature XI, which drafted the Charter of the Ninzard Project, he opposed its revised version until Raffaele, AJ and ScottyAte persuaded him to vote for it, and Part I passed with the support of all fifteen representatives.26 Ahead of the 2026 election, which Poka won, he told the Ories he was no longer interested in the presidency.27

Injury in Los Angeles

In late March 2026 Aceiron discovered that Kain had released rats into the house he shared with his mother, and drove south to confront him. On 29 March, after a car accident on the way, he forced his way into Kain's apartment in Los Angeles. When an armed visitor drew a gun on him, he suffered a panic attack, collapsed and struck his head. Kain stayed with him and called for help, knowing it meant his own arrest on drug charges. Aceiron spent four days in hospital with a fractured rib, a concussion and a head wound that needed fourteen staples, and was diagnosed with post-traumatic stress disorder and panic disorder. His seat in the Legislature was filled in a snap election.28

The Holloway Summit and faith

On 2 May 2026, five weeks after his injury, Aceiron arrived as the last of thirty guests at the Holloway Summit. There he embraced NGin, who had called for his death during the Chud War, in a moment remembered as the end of the community's years of hostility; Kain, just out of rehab, greeted him with a nod. Apollos afterwards invited him and Raffaele into the Preservate Apparatus.8

Aceiron had stopped attending church by 2023.11 In hospital he prayed again, and through the summer of 2026 he attended a Presbyterian church near his mother's home, quietly questioning its pastor every week. He was baptized on 20 September 2026 with his mother, brother and sister present. He has resisted attempts to frame it as a redemption story, saying that his obsessive–compulsive disorder and his bad days remain.9

Political style

Aceiron's politics centred on activity. He argued that Ninzard survived on events, competition and even conflict, and dismissed Preservate safety-first governance as a “nanny-state” that had sterilized the community's soul, a view the Ories' Rationalist Flank carried forward.18,10 His coalition ranged from activity-minded Rationalists and mainstream conservatives to manosphere figures and, uneasily, a far right he alternately tolerated and purged.10 Supporters credited him with Nexit and Nightwork's revival; critics pointed to the normalization of slurs, the harassment of women and LGBTQ members, and his intolerance of dissent.5

Later accounts describe his hyper-masculine persona as a mask for insecurity and for years of untreated trauma and obsessive–compulsive disorder, which he had refused to acknowledge until his hospitalization in 2026.27,29
