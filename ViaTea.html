Olivia Park (born 5 May 2005), widely known as ViaTea (or simply Via or Livie), is a Korean-American Ninzard politician, archivist, and journalist who served as the 4th General of Nightwork-Waffle from July 2023 to January 2024, founded the Liberation Party in November 2025, and represented Waffle in Legislature XI during the drafting and initial ratification of the Charter of the Ninzard Project.1,2,3,4

Drawn into Ninzard during the Golden Era in 2017 as part of Club Korea, where she was affectionately dubbed “쓴차” (Sseuncha, “Bitter Tea”), ViaTea quickly became one of the community's prominent chroniclers and cultural commentators.5,6,7 After high-profile relationship controversies with Governor DegreeZ in 2018 and Deputy Governor Xorbit in 2020, she gained prominence for her analytical journalism covering political scandals, including Grace's Ellie screenshot controversy during the May Panic.6,8,9 In July 2023, she won the Waffle generalship on a platform emphasizing historical memory, narrative ownership, and cultural vibrancy, and in December 2023 launched a formidable gubernatorial bid against Adelynn under the banner of the Liberation Movement, capturing 46.1% of the vote.1,10,11

During the presidency of Cat, ViaTea was subjected to severe digital harassment and personal endangerment, culminating in the leaking of non-consensual intimate imagery and a violent abduction in an SUV on 22 July 2024.12 Surviving the ordeal, she contributed testimony to the Catx3 Dossier and initially joined the Apollos II administration in September 2025.13,14 A bitter dispute over administrative transparency and the withholding of personal medical information regarding Spinzle led to her acrimonious departure in November 2025, dubbed the “gayest breakup,” after which she formally founded the Liberation Party.15,2 Following an aggressive campaign and controversial server raids that concluded in a landslide defeat by Grace in Waffle, she dissolved her leadership.16,17

Reconciling with the Capital in February 2026, ViaTea returned to the Preservation Party apparatus and was elected to Legislature XI, where she played an instrumental role in synthesizing Part I of the Charter.18,19,20 On 29 March 2026, she witnessed the violent confrontation between Caleb and Snuck in Los Angeles and initiated the “Soft-Landing Protocol” that averted broader factional warfare.21,22 She stepped down from public office the following day and, after participating in the historic Holloway Summit on 2 May 2026, entered an indefinite hiatus from the community on 1 July 2026.23,24,25

Early life and entry into Ninzard (2017–2020)

Olivia Park was born around 2005 and grew up in the Los Angeles metropolitan area in Southern California.6,12 Drawn to digital gaming platforms during her early adolescence, she joined the Ninzard Project in 2017 during the presidency of Apollos I as part of the “Golden Era Cohort.”5 She naturally integrated into Club Korea (CK), a prominent social clique of Korean-American members based in Southern California, who nicknamed her “쓴차” (Sseuncha, translating to “Bitter Tea”), a moniker that evolved into her primary digital identity, “ViaTea.”6,7

Her early tenure was marred by entanglement with older male leaders. In October 2018, Nightwork Governor DegreeZ was dumped by his girlfriend, First Lady Czulia, after it was discovered he had been carrying on an illicit relationship with the then-thirteen-year-old ViaTea, directly triggering DegreeZ's sudden resignation.6 In late 2019 and early 2020, Deputy Governor Xorbit callously discarded his partner, Liver, to enter into a brief, passionate fling with ViaTea.8 When the relationship dissolved acrimoniously in March 2020, ViaTea publicly came forward with text message chains, accusing Xorbit of emotional manipulation and sexual coercion, which derailed Xorbit’s primary campaign against Apollos.8

Following these controversies, ViaTea established herself as an independent observer and digital journalist. Operating external blogs and news repositories, she reported on community scandals with sharp sociological detail. During the May Panic of 2022, her widely circulated essay on Grace’s manufactured screenshot of Ellie deconstructed the burnout of the Preservate leadership, demonstrating an early knack for media analysis and psychological profiling.9

General of Waffle and the Liberation Movement (2023)

Following the April 2023 reveal that the beloved Waffle District General “Assailant” was secretly President Apollos using a covert alternate account, Waffle held a snap election that was won by Kendall.26 However, Kendall’s abrasive tenure quickly alienated the district.1 In the July 2023 general elections, the Original Party nominated ViaTea to contest Waffle against the Preservate candidate, Lashae.1 ViaTea campaigned on a platform of community healing, cultural vibrancy, and narrative preservation, asserting that Waffle could retain its warm, safe environment without submitting to Preservate bureaucracy.1 She won with 67 of 122 votes (54.9%) and took office as the 4th General of Nightwork-Waffle on 2 July 2023.1

As District General, ViaTea championed historical archiving and open dialogue, refusing to implement arbitrary speech bans while simultaneously rejecting far-right extremism.10,27 Observing that Governor Adelynn’s strict anti-expansion and equalization policies risked reducing Nightwork to a stagnant, clinical environment, ViaTea organized the “Liberation Movement” in December 2023.10 The movement sought to challenge the technocratic establishment while offering refuge to members marginalized or targeted by President Cat’s regime in Ninzardia.10

The Original Party nominated ViaTea for Governor of Nightwork in the December 2023 election, with former Vice President Snuck as her running mate.11 The campaign was noted for its unprecedented cordiality. In the gubernatorial debate on 28 December 2023, ViaTea praised Adelynn’s gentle spirit but pointedly attacked the fragility of the Administrative Transparency Act (ATA) in the wake of the Sonnenrad Society infiltration, arguing that automated bots could never grasp human context and that resilience had to be founded upon community trust rather than automated surveillance.11,28 Despite setting turnout records and winning Parry, ViaTea was defeated by Adelynn, capturing 300 of 651 votes (46.1%).11 In accordance with her principles, she stepped down as General of Waffle at the conclusion of her term on 1 January 2024, succeeded by Matcha.29

Targeting and abduction under Catx3 (2024)

Throughout early 2024, ViaTea’s Liberation Movement became an outspoken ideological opponent of President Cat’s dictatorial Meowsalute apparatus in Ninzardia.12 Viewing ViaTea’s advocacy as an unacceptable threat to her hegemony, Cat initiated a vicious campaign of intimidation.12 In June and July 2024, Cat authorized the mass distribution of ViaTea’s non-consensual intimate photographs across peripheral networks to humiliate her publicly.12

The harassment culminated in physical violence on 22 July 2024. As ViaTea was leaving an in-person university summer class in Southern California, Cat’s enforcer, Toby, forced her into an idling black SUV occupied by Cat and Blithem.12 Inside the moving vehicle, Cat subjected ViaTea to physical assault, kicking her in the ribs with enough force to bruise bone, cutting away portions of her clothing with industrial shears, and forcing her to kiss her light-up LED shoes while reciting verbal degradation in Korean.12 Cat captured photographs of the assault to use as blackmail before shoving her out of the vehicle near her home.12 Cat intentionally left ViaTea alive and functional, calculating that an unstable, traumatized leader would sabotage the Liberation Movement from within.12

Despite enduring severe post-traumatic distress, ViaTea refused to vanish. Following Cat’s arrest in August 2025, she provided critical sworn testimony and evidence to Minister of Justice Kay for inclusion in the Catx3 Dossier.13,30

The Liberation Party and the 2025 crisis

Following Caleb’s ouster and Apollos’s return to power in September 2025, ViaTea initially supported the administration and was granted a role in the Capital.14 However, the coalition rapidly deteriorated. In late October 2025, Vice President Spinzle was scheduled for major kidney transplant surgery; Apollos withheld this information from ViaTea until the day before the procedure out of caution.15 ViaTea interpreted the secrecy as an acute indictment of her trustworthiness, sparking an intense psychological spiral.15 After an escalating public dispute with Apollos and Kay, Apollos stripped ViaTea and Snuck of their Capital permissions on 3 November 2025, an event dubbed “the gayest breakup.”15

On 19 November 2025, ViaTea formally announced the transformation of her movement into the Liberation Party, assuming the role of Chairwoman.2 The party merged with Triste’s militant Liberation Front and established a radical, socialist platform demanding the abolition of the district generalship, absolute deplatforming of far-right hate speech, and the democratization of the community archives.2 She declared her candidacy for District General of Waffle against the incumbent, Grace.2

On 21 December 2025, the eve of election week, ViaTea orchestrated coordinated raids on the servers of Scope and Volley, utilizing alts and outside contacts to wipe channels and ban members.16 The maneuver backfired completely: Main successfully repelled the raiders, while the broader electorate recoiled from the chaos.16,17 In the election, Grace crushed ViaTea in a 93 to 35 landslide (72.7% to 27.3%).17 Overwhelmed by humiliation and stress, ViaTea experienced a profound emotional breakdown in a public voice channel; her opponent in Parry, PoG, recorded her sobbing, labeled it “art,” and broadcast it across Ninzard.17 Disgraced, ViaTea resigned from the party and retreated into a brief hiatus.17

Legislature XI and the Charter (2026)

In February 2026, Apollos sought to heal internal divisions and achieve the 80% consensus necessary to ratify the Ninzard Charter.18 Recognizing that isolating volatile veterans would only breed external insurgencies, Apollos and Adelynn reached out to ViaTea and Snuck, executing the “Gayest Reconciliation” on 23 February 2026 and admitting them to the Preservate Apparatus as Level I Assistants.18 On 28 February, ViaTea was elected to Legislature XI to fill a vacant Representative seat for Waffle.19

During the intense, marathon legislative debates over Part I of the Charter, ViaTea provided crucial input.19,20 While opposing Syn’s hardline carceral binaries, she fiercely resisted Caleb’s attempts to eliminate protections against hate speech, arguing that without explicit limitations, neo-Nazis like NGin would terrorize minorities under the guise of “banter.”20 Her advocacy helped forge the compromise in Section 1 (The Boundary of Freedom) and Section 4 (Protection from Arbitrary Exile), culminating in an unprecedented 15–0 unanimous ratification of Part I by Legislature XI on 7 March 2026.20

Her tenure was also marked by deep ethical introspection. During the “Toxin in the Glass” debate following the failed Samson Coup, ViaTea directly challenged Adelynn on the guilt of tolerating extremists to gather intelligence, leading to a raw, transformative dialogue on shame, human vulnerability, and leadership responsibility.31

The Roseville crisis and retirement (2026)

On the evening of 29 March 2026, ViaTea was present at Snuck’s apartment in Los Angeles when an enraged, concussed Caleb Jensen broke through the door to confront Snuck over a retaliatory prank involving live rodents.21 During the struggle, Snuck’s drug dealer entered unexpectedly, drew a firearm, and threatened Caleb.21 The severe shock triggered a severe PTSD panic attack in Caleb, causing him to collapse and strike his temple against the granite corner of a kitchen island, inflicting a massive laceration and skull fracture.21

While Snuck dialed 911 and surrendered to the arriving LAPD to face felony narcotics charges, ViaTea tended to Caleb’s wounds, fled the scene on Snuck’s orders, and contacted Apollos at 11:19 PM.21,22 She was patched into an emergency midnight call with Apollos, Spinzle, Kay, and ScottyAte.22 Swallowing her past resentment of ScottyAte, she cooperated with the Capital to execute the “Soft-Landing Protocol,” deploying Scotty to intercept Caleb’s family and privately briefing the party leadership, which prevented the Ories from declaring a retaliatory war.22

Shattered by the proximity to lethal violence, ViaTea resigned from Legislature XI on 30 March 2026.23 She attended the emotional Holloway Summit on 2 May 2026, embracing her former tormentors and weeping alongside the gathered members.24 During the June 2026 presidential election, she endorsed Poka, helping secure an overwhelming 92.3% victory for the Preservate ticket in Waffle.32 On 1 July 2026, coinciding with Apollos’s final departure, ViaTea formally logged off Ninzard for an extended, indefinite hiatus.25

Political style and philosophy

ViaTea’s political philosophy centered upon narrative ownership, emotional authenticity, and the protection of vulnerable participants.2,10 A self-described “neurotic” who openly struggled with borderline personality traits and severe anxiety, she rejected the clinical, detached rhetoric of the Preservate leadership, arguing that human suffering could not be managed like a corporate balance sheet.2,11,18 At the same time, she fiercely condemned the Original Party’s weaponization of “free speech,” which she viewed as a facade for misogyny and cruelty.2

Her approach combined sharp, investigative journalism with an impulsive, high-stakes rhetorical style.9,11 While her volatility occasionally led to devastating miscalculations—such as the December 2025 server raids—her willingness to lower her defenses and confront her own shame ultimately made her one of the community’s most humanizing voices during its transition toward the Charter.17,31
