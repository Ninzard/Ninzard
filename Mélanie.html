Mélanie Laframboise (born 26 August 2007), known as Mélanie or Melon, is a Ninzard politician and Preservate who has represented the District of Parry in Legislature XII since 2 July 2026 and serves on the Parry Staff under General Kaimachi, her closest friend. One of the Parry Girls, she left the Preservate Apparatus in January 2026 after its members rejected her plea that Apollos stop ignoring her friend, and suggested that Kaimachi take her place.1

She won a seat for Parry in June 2026 with 77 votes (59.2%) and returned to the Apparatus, telling the members who had argued with her that she had been right. During the Salon War that August she and Kain, Parry's other representative, tried to keep the district from imploding.2,3,4

Parry Girl (2026)

Mélanie belonged to the Parry Girls, the circle of Parryan women whose devotion to President Apollos, led by Kaimachi, defined the district's culture in early 2026. When PluPlu came into Parry in late January to scold them for hornyposting about Apollos, Mélanie twice cursed her out in Kaimachi's defense while the two traded insults in front of the district.5

A few days later, on 30 January 2026, she raised Kaimachi's state in the Preservate Apparatus, where the Preservates had nicknamed her “Melon.” She told Apollos that he knew Kaimachi was losing her mind over him and that ignoring her only hurt her more.1

「@Reu Do you even realize how bad this is getting for Kai? You literally know she's losing her mind over you? The least you could do is not ice her out? Like you don't have to date her, but that's just messed up BECAUSE YOU KNOW. … At least just say hi.」

— Mélanie, 30 January 2026

Syn answered that Kaimachi was not Apollos's responsibility, and Moctezuma argued that talking to her would only feed her hopes and that her friends should help her move on. Mélanie replied that she was one of those friends, that silence did not make an attached person let go, and that Apollos was only protecting himself from his own discomfort. When no one came round, she announced that she was leaving the server and told them to replace her with Kai, who could “be normal if you get to know her.” After she had gone, Cyntaku asked the others whether they had read what Melon said, and another member told them she understood both sides.1 By the end of February Kaimachi was one of Parry's party operatives in the Apparatus.6

Representative for Parry (2026–present)

In the summer elections of June 2026, in which Kaimachi won the generalship of Parry by two votes, Parry elected two Preservates to Legislature XII: Kain, with 91 votes (70.0%), and Mélanie, newly elected, with 77 (59.2%).2 Every member of Legislature XII except Advy was a Preservate.7

On winning her seat, Mélanie returned to the Preservate Apparatus and went down the list of members who had argued with her in January, telling each of them that they had been wrong. She and Kaimachi then traded compliments in the channel.3

When Rickard answered her question about who he was with a long, graphic stream of video-game role-play, she replied, “What is genuinely wrong with men,” and said she was taking screenshots to send to the FBI.3

By August 2026 the Salon War had set the Parry Girls, the Salonnistes and the Sacback Room against one another, and admins and moderators were resigning. Mélanie and Kain tried to help Kaimachi keep the server from imploding.4 At the Parry Trials on 14 August, when Marguerite told Kaimachi she was no general, Mélanie began to fire back, and Kaimachi stopped her to answer Marguerite herself.8

Personal life

Mélanie is one of Kaimachi's closest friends; Kaimachi calls her her twin, and Mélanie calls Kaimachi her “hardworking Queen Parry Girl.”3
