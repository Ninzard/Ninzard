The Grass Party was a small political party of the Ninzard Project founded by Power of Greed (PoG), which formed out of the Unighted Party. Its name stood both for “grassroots” campaigns that gave a voice to ordinary members and for the need to “touch grass”: PoG believed the community could only last if it found romance and passion outside Discord.1 Known as the “silent” party, it held Volley through Lesty from 2020 to 2021, twice challenged Snuck for Parry with ScottyAte, and was dissolved by 2025.2,3,4
