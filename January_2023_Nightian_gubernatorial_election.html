The January 2023 Nightian gubernatorial election was held on 3 January 2023 to choose the Governor of Nightwork. Former Deputy Governor Adelynn of the Preservation Party, with Grace as her running mate, unseated Governor Spinzle of the Original Party and her deputy governor, Atria, by 279 votes to 276, three votes out of 555. Adelynn carried Main, Scope and Volley; Spinzle carried Parry and Waffle.1,2

It came at the end of what the archive calls perhaps the most chaotic election season in the Ninzard Project. The Ories held three of Nightwork's five districts, but rapid, unregulated growth had left the community deeply polarized. Adelynn, a moderate distrusted by much of her own party, ran on an equalization policy to rein in that growth, while Spinzle defended her hands-off approach. The leak of Spinzle's private study of members' mental health, known as Studygate, hurt her shortly before the vote, and former President Apollos endorsed Adelynn.3,4,5,6

Adelynn's administration began the same day, and at Grace's insistence she also became Boss of the Preservation Party. President Cat, who had expected Spinzle to win, at once offered Adelynn and Grace thousands of dollars for their cooperation; both refused. The Preservates also took three of the five district generalships and the Ories none, and the result was read as the end of the Original Party's old expansionist establishment.5,7,1,8,3

Background

Spinzle had governed Nightwork since July 2022, when she unseated the Preservate governor Cyntaku.9,5 Her attempt to give Nightwork a written constitution stalled amid the polarization of late 2022, but she put through a set of targeted reforms, “The Ethics,” meant to blunt the influence of President Cat.10 By the end of her term the Original Party controlled Main, Parry and Scope and the Preservates only Volley, and Nightwork's severely unregulated growth had made the community chaotic and polarized.4

Cat, who hated Spinzle, set out to wear down her administration from within.11 With Raffaele she kept the districts in a state of manufactured crisis while persuading Apollos that Spinzle's procedural approach was failing to keep the peace, and Apollos came to distrust Spinzle's judgment, though not her motives. Spinzle's refusal to remove Soda, a childhood friend, as General of Scope disappointed the Scopians who had voted for her and drove Kennie, another old friend and Soda's fiercest defender, to begin drifting toward Cat.12 Deputy Governor Atria, frightened by threats that Cat and Raffaele sent her from burner accounts, asked for lockdowns and mass bans; Spinzle judged the threats to be bluffs and refused, and by the end of 2022 Atria was secretly passing administration details to Cat.13

Nominations

Preservation Party

After Cyntaku's defeat the Preservate Apparatus wanted new leadership. Adelynn, the former deputy governor, was the obvious choice, but many in the party doubted her commitment because of her moderation and her rigid adherence to her values; she had defied some of Cyntaku's actions and had even considered leaving Cyntaku's re-election ticket. As a compromise the party made Grace its Boss, but after Grace lost the November 2022 snap election for General of Main to the Orie Sundaze, it would not make her its nominee for governor.5

Adelynn weighed her run carefully before announcing it, and chose Grace as her running mate, which made theirs a comparatively moderate ticket. Grace backed her nomination, but the party's older, more traditional members opposed it and formed the Axiom Flank, a group of radical isolationists; Adelynn's reformed Preservates came to be called the Logos Flank.5

Original Party

Spinzle ran for re-election with Atria, her deputy governor, as her running mate.2

Campaign

Issues

Adelynn proposed an equalization policy to control the sudden growth of Nightwork and promised to “cleanse” it of corruption, authoritarianism and polarization. Under her the party turned away from the authoritarianism of the Syn and Cyntaku years toward a more reformist, controlled approach, still in contrast with Spinzle's strict institutional non-involvement.5 Spinzle had made it her priority to safeguard the freedoms Nightwork had lost under Syn and Cyntaku and to avoid excessive bureaucracy.1

Studygate

As governor, Spinzle had run a study of whether Ninzard helped or harmed its members' mental health, comparing the words new and old members used in answering questions such as “How are you feeling?” in July and again in December 2022. She found that the old members' mental health had worsened and the new members' improved, but she judged the study uninteresting and never published it. Shortly before the election someone working for Cat leaked it. The answers were anonymous, yet their wording made some members identifiable, minors among them, and Nightians accused Spinzle of “experimenting on children”; she responded sarcastically.6

Meikaqu defended Spinzle on grounds of necessity rather than ethics, arguing that the findings helped ground policy in logic. Adelynn agreed that the charges were exaggerated but criticized Spinzle for recklessness with sensitive information and for trying to “over-intellectualize” matters that called for empathy.6

Debate

On 30 December 2022 the four candidates met in the Nightwork gubernatorial debate. Asked about gender equality in elections, since all four were women, they downplayed it: Grace called the question itself absurd, and Atria showed no interest in it. Spinzle defended leaving district expansion to the generals, while Adelynn accused her of submitting to their will without exercising authority of her own, and of inadvertently promoting antisemitic content. Adelynn warned that the rapid growth of Parry, then called BobaBitcoin, and of Main could turn governor's races into contests for a single district. Atria argued for non-intervention; Grace accused the administration of refusing to take responsibility and called for more intervention.2

Spinzle: 「I would suggest that these are simply normal electoral dynamics. If Nightwork-Volley's constituents desire the same rate of growth as Parry and Main, they'd vote for an expansionist candidate. It's a matter of democratic decision rather than dysfunction necessitating the governor's micromanagement.」

Adelynn: 「… In my opinion, this is not acceptable. An electorate problem that jeopardizes the fairness and democratic norms that Nightwork upholds is a pressing concern. If equalization and efforts to balance the members are beneficial, I believe they should be implemented.」

— The Nightwork gubernatorial debate, 30 December 2022

Endorsements

Former President Apollos's endorsement of Adelynn surprised Spinzle: he pointed to her mismanagement of the sudden influx of new members and argued that Nightwork needed effective leadership.1 Rudolf, running for General of Parry as an independent, also endorsed Adelynn.14 In Waffle, where he was still known only as General Assailant, Apollos kept silent about the race.15 Privately, Cat preferred Adelynn, whom she took for a “naive” puppet she could use, but she expected Spinzle, who still had a considerable loyal following, to be re-elected.7,1

Results

Of the 555 votes cast, Adelynn won 279 (50.3%) and Spinzle 276 (49.7%). Spinzle ran ahead of her party's nominees for general in every district. The archive describes a split community, wary of a return to the radical Preservate vision of Cyntaku and Geo but uneasy about the Ories' mass expansion, which had changed the community's culture; in the end the freedoms Spinzle defended felt less urgent to voters than the chaos around them.1

Main

Main, the largest district, gave Adelynn 109 of its 188 votes, carried by her and Grace's reputation for reliability among Mainians; most of Spinzle's support there came from moderate, establishment-minded Ories wary of the radical Preservates of the past.1 On the same ballot Woofer, a little-known Preservate administrator, unseated the Orie general Sundaze by 113 votes to 75.16

Parry

Parry was Spinzle's strongest district, 116 votes to 60, and she ran far ahead of its Orie general, Xorbit. Parryans disliked the Ories' aggressive expansionism, but most held a longer-standing grudge against the Preservate governments of Syn and Cyntaku, and the Regex Rebellion was still vividly remembered.1 The independent Rudolf won the generalship with 71 votes to Xorbit's 59 and 46 for the Preservate Aurase.14

Scope

Spinzle's home district, which had historically backed her almost unanimously, voted for Adelynn by 30 votes to 28 on an unusually low turnout: only 58 of about 75 eligible members voted. Many Scopians had turned against the Ories over expansionism and the ineptitude of their district's Orie leadership, yet Spinzle stayed personally popular, and Scope welcomed her back as an elder stateswoman after her defeat.1 The Preservate Lyman won the generalship with 30 votes to 20 for the incumbent Orie, Soda, and 8 for the independent Cosmo.17

Volley

Volley gave Adelynn her best result, 48 votes to 19 (71.6%); Volleyans strongly supported the Preservates and deeply distrusted expansionist management.1 General Molly was re-elected over Mel by 57 votes to 10.18

Waffle

Waffle went to Spinzle by 34 votes to 32. Wafflers respected her for sponsoring the revival of their district, but Studygate and her party's links to members facing disturbing allegations damaged her standing.1 Apollos, as General Assailant, was re-elected with 44 votes to 12 for the Orie Idodo and 10 for the Preservate Lashae.15

Concurrent elections

The district general elections were held the same day. The Preservates won Main, Scope and Volley and independents won Parry and Waffle; the Ories, who had held three generalships, won none. Molly and Apollos were the only incumbent generals re-elected.8,18,15

In the presidential election, President Cat of the Disco Party was re-elected over former Vice President Hokan of the Original Party, taking 342 of Nightwork's 555 votes and 537 of 806 in all (66.6%). Vice President Snowby was re-elected over Xorbit with 386 of Nightwork's votes and 601 in all (74.6%), at the time the most votes anyone had received in the Project.19,20 Humiliated, Hokan quietly left the community without a word.8

Aftermath

Cat was pleasantly surprised by Spinzle's defeat, having expected her to win, and judged Adelynn a “weak leader” and “fresh meat” whose administration she could dismantle from within. Right after the election she offered the governor-elect and Grace thousands of dollars for their cooperation; both refused, as did a Main staff member she then tried to bribe.1

At Grace's demand Adelynn immediately became Boss of the Preservates.5 Her administration began on 3 January 2023, promising clear policies, transparency and an end to the chaos of 2022.7 On the day of her victory Spinzle suggested that she clean up Nightwork by attrition rather than with a jarring shift; Adelynn disagreed at first but moderated her plans, and her willingness to compromise won her the cooperation of four of the five generals.21

With Sundaze, Xorbit, Soda and Spinzle all defeated, the Original Party fell into shambles.8 The archive treats the election as the end of its old establishment: radical expansionism became a taboo ideology, and many expected the party to dissolve.3 Six months later, in the July 2023 election, Adelynn was re-elected over Kennie with 75% of the vote.22
