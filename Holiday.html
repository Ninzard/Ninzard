Holly Dacia Merriday (2 December 2001 – June 2018), known as Holiday, was a Coloradan member of the Ninzard Project who served as the 3rd Governor of Ninzardia from 1 October 2017 to 31 March 2018. One of the childhood friends who came into the project with Abby's Solorado cohort in 2012, she was elected governor for the Resistance Party in September 2017 with Spinzle as her running mate, winning in a landslide after President Apollos broke with his own party to endorse her.1,2

Her governorship was overshadowed by self-doubt and by the feud between Apollos and her online boyfriend Tiny2, the founder of Gold Road. In February 2018 she withdrew from the community almost entirely, and she did not run again.3 When Tiny2 died in May 2018, Alaric revealed that she had known of his death and kept silent, and much of the community blamed her for it.4 Isolated and depressed, she died by suicide in June 2018, at the age of sixteen. Her death was announced to the community on 18 June, and the exact time she died was never established.5 The two deaths ended the Golden Era, hastened the closing of Sacraday and Solorado, and left a burden of blame and guilt that the community carried for years.6,7,8

Early life

Holiday grew up in Colorado, where she, Spinzle, Snowby and Kennie formed a small circle of friends. She was the most popular of the four and the one most at ease with other students. Much was made of her looks, and she came to tie her sense of worth to her appearance.9

Snowby's older sister Abby noticed that her sister's friends were being targeted by bullies. When Eliza recruited Abby in 2012 with the promise of a structured, moderated safe haven, Abby brought the whole Colorado cohort into the project as the Solorado legion, and Holiday came with them.1 By 2017 she belonged to the Resistance Party, which Gabriel and Faber had founded in April 2014.10,2

Tiny2 and Gold Road (2015–2017)

In June 2015, as the Nightian War came to an end, Tiny2 publicly mocked Spinzle's critique of President Antareze's use of remote access trojans. Holiday, who had noticed how outspoken he was, reached out to him out of curiosity. Their conversation was the first of many, and it grew into an online romantic relationship.11

After Apollos's election in 2016, Tiny2 led a number of older members out of the community and founded Gold Road, a smaller server built around a close-knit membership and democratic transfers of power. As Gold Road declined in the summer of 2017 and its members drifted back to Ninzard, Tiny2 turned his resentment on Apollos.12 Apollos, who regularly received screenshots of Tiny2 and his friends insulting him, came to regard Holiday with suspicion, but she gave no sign of ill will toward him and kept out of the quarrel. When Tiny2 went on insulting and threatening Apollos after the two had tried to make amends in September 2017, she asked him to stop, which angered him.12

Governor of Ninzardia (2017–2018)

2017 election

When Governor Meikaqu declined to seek re-election in September 2017, the Resistance Party nominated Holiday, a friend of both Snowby and Spinzle and one of its strongest choices against the dominant Original Party. She chose Spinzle, then General of Solorado, as her running mate. Tiny2 pressed for her candidacy, in part out of hatred for Apollos.2,12 Snowby, who had decided not to run herself, had fallen out with Holiday over several personal arguments and helped the Ories rally against her. The Ories nominated Judah, who chose Brutus as his running mate.2

Apollos stayed silent through the early stages of the race. Then, in the middle of September, he endorsed Holiday and Spinzle against his own party's ticket, shocking the Ories and angering Judah and Brutus, while members of the Resistance Party welcomed his stand against one-party rule. Holiday defeated Judah in a landslide, although Judah still carried Sacraday, and she took office as the 3rd Governor of Ninzardia on 1 October 2017. She and Apollos became friends.2 In the same election Elsy, also of the Resistance Party, succeeded Judah as General of Sacraday, and Faber succeeded Spinzle in Solorado.2

Tenure

Holiday governed as a moderate, promoting legionism and the freedom of the legions; Faber, running to succeed her, later placed himself toward the center as she had.13 Her term coincided with rising tension across the community, as mental-health struggles spread among its young members and Tiny2 began to suffer breakdowns.14

In Sacraday, Elsy's tirades turned into calls for war against Apollos's “dictatorship.” After the Resistance Party removed her from its ranks, she began harassing Holiday and Spinzle, calling them traitors. In February 2018, under mounting pressure from Sacraday's members, Spinzle called a snap election as a referendum on Elsy's rule, and on 14 February Rudolf won it for the Original Party. Holiday remained silent after his victory, which some Ories took as disapproval.15

Withdrawal

In February 2018 Holiday grew noticeably quiet, and members began to ask where she was. She was distancing herself because of personal struggles and her conflicts with Tiny2, and she had come to feel deeply inferior to Meikaqu, believing that she had destroyed the peaceful, good-natured Ninzardia her predecessor had built. Convinced that she was a source of conflict, and afraid that even Snowby, her real-life friend, disliked her, she withdrew from Ninzard almost entirely. Spinzle kept Ninzardia running behind the scenes.3

In March 2018 Holiday announced that she would not seek re-election and endorsed Faber.3 Many wondered whether she and Tiny2 would leave for Gold Road, but the two had been growing apart. Faber won the election and succeeded her on 1 April 2018.13

Death of Tiny2 (May 2018)

Tiny2 lived with sickle cell anemia, which caused him constant pain, kept him indoors and left him lonely; in the months before his death he complained of exhaustion and publicly questioned why he was alive. Holiday had been his main source of emotional support. As she became more socially active online, his insecurity turned into jealousy and attempts to control whom she spoke to, especially other boys. Wanting to ground herself in the real world, she proposed that they spend a week apart. His mental state then declined quickly, and his last messages were sent on 18 May 2018.4

News of his death reached Holiday, Alaric and DegreeZ, the Governor of Nightwork, who had shared a private group chat with him. DegreeZ chose to keep it confidential to avoid unrest in Gold Road. On 25 May Alaric made it public, calling DegreeZ spineless and accusing Holiday of guilt and moral responsibility for her silence. He had a hidden grudge against her: she had muted him on Gold Road for disruptive behavior, including racial slurs, and had turned down his advances.4

Alaric's revelation unjustly cast Holiday as the villain of Tiny2's story. Accused of causing his death, she came to believe that she had killed him. Many people reached out to her, Apollos among them, but she did not reply.4 She turned to Snowby and Spinzle. Snowby could not accept that Holiday had kept Tiny2's death a secret; Holiday lashed out at her, and the two never spoke again. Spinzle, who had been visiting her often, was left as her only support.4,16

Gold Road, now run mostly by Alaric, became a platform for hatred of Holiday. Apollos took it apart, entering with an alternate account, deleting its chats and removing every member, and on 1 June 2018 he stripped Alaric of his permissions as General of Scope for blaming her for Tiny2's death. Holiday, who had already been planning to leave Gold Road because of the hatred she was receiving, greeted its end with relief.4,17

Death (June 2018)

As her depression deepened, Holiday isolated herself despite Spinzle's efforts to comfort her, and she stopped answering messages altogether.4 In mid-June 2018 she was found dead in a bathtub. She had died by suicide, at the age of sixteen. Spinzle, who called her Day, announced her death to the community on 18 June in three words: “Day is dead.”5

Time of death

The archive records that it was never clear exactly when she died.5 No one had seen or heard from her for days before she was found, and the circumstances defeated the usual ways of estimating how long a person has been dead:

  • Body cooling. Estimates usually rest on a body cooling predictably toward the temperature of the room, as in the Henssge nomogram. Water conducts heat some twenty to twenty-five times better than air, and warm bathwater would at first have kept her body warm and delayed the usual cooling curve. As the water itself cooled toward room temperature, the rate of heat loss kept changing, so the standard formulas based on core temperature could not be relied on.
  • Lividity. Because she had lost a great deal of blood into the water, too little remained in her circulation to settle into the lowest parts of her body. Lividity, which ordinarily helps place a death within a window of roughly two to twelve hours according to whether it still blanches under pressure, would have been faint, patchy or absent.
  • Immersion. Long immersion wrinkles, softens and loosens the skin and distorts the early markers of decomposition, while a closed, humid bathroom speeds bacterial breakdown and hides the usual signs of decay in air.

Without timestamped digital activity, such as the moment her phone or computer was last used, a medical examiner could have offered only a broad window, typically a range of twenty-four to seventy-two hours, or recorded the time of death as undetermined. Her death is therefore dated only to June 2018. The one fixed date the archive gives is 18 June, the day it became known.

Aftermath

Blame

The deaths of Tiny2 and Holiday set members against one another. Alaric and others blamed Apollos; conspiracy theories claimed that he had told both of them to take their own lives, and critics accused him of having “killed the community.” Returning from a break, Apollos condemned those looking for someone to blame as wicked, repulsive people who dishonored Holiday's death. The statement only enraged his critics further, and at one point Ninzards confronted him in person, assaulted him and called him a murderer.5 Abby, Snowby's older sister and once his confidante, accused him of guilt as well.18

Faber, who had known Holiday personally, spent more than a week consoling the community's mourners before he felt unable to go on. When Rudolf publicly declared that Faber's negligence over Gold Road had led to her death, Rudolf was condemned on all sides, but Sacradians already tired of the governor used the charge against him all the same. Faber announced his resignation on 30 June, then took two weeks off instead, and Spinzle became acting governor.7,19

In letters exchanged that July and later kept in the archive, Pieban told Spinzle that, tragic as it was, the fault was Holiday's own; he preferred, he wrote, to believe that her choices had meant something. Spinzle replied that blaming the death on any one person was analytically unsound and that no one was to blame, least of all her, and she refused to argue the matter further.20,21,22,23

Grief

The Coloradan members who had been close to Holiday were devastated. Snowby, overwhelmed by guilt, fell into a depression, with only Abby and Pieban to support her; Kennie struggled to cope and began taking more risks; and Spinzle felt a depth of grief she was not used to feeling. Abby tried to persuade her sister and their friends to leave Ninzard, and told their mother.18

Across Ninzardia, Capitalia and Hybriden grieved and questioned whether the community should exist at all, Sacraday sank into chaos and recrimination, and Solorado, devastated, all but died out.7 Gabriel returned briefly and, with Spinzle, used the Resistance Party's channels only to console the grieving.19 On 7 July 2018 the Legislature voted to close Sacraday and Solorado the next day. Gabriel left again, this time for good, and Spinzle deleted all of her accounts and left Ninzard, not returning until the summer of 2019.8,4

After the RSN War, Apollos and other leaders discouraged discussion of the two deaths, and information about them was deleted. Many members took this as respect for the privacy of the two, and some who had called Apollos a murderer apologized.24

Legacy

Members who lived through it dated the end of the Golden Era to the deaths of Tiny2 and Holiday; one later wrote that no one could go on playing games and pretending all was well with two friends in the ground.6 The crisis of faith that followed led a sizable cohort to refuse to integrate into the official hierarchy, among the beginnings of the Underground,25 and when it later emerged how much of the community's early history had been engineered by its founders, some older members, counting the two deaths among its costs, turned their anger into support for Cat's campaign against them.26 Liver, thirteen at the time, traced the start of her radicalization to that summer, when she watched the fun of the Golden Era turn into real death.27

Her death remained a charge in political quarrels. At the vice-presidential debate of 10 August 2020, Kennie claimed that the Capital had “killed Tiny2 and Holiday,” drawing an extreme reaction from the audience,28 and on 9 September 2025 Aceiron said that three people had killed themselves on Apollos's watch, a remark many older members called low.29 Apollos carried unresolved survivor's guilt over the two deaths for years.30

In “The Things We Refuse to Look At,” written for Legislature XI on 29 April 2026, Lesty set Holiday's death in a long record of the community's failures toward its girls: a teenager who had resigned as governor, held morally responsible for her ex-boyfriend's death by a community that blamed her until she stopped responding.31
