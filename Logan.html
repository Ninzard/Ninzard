Third Faction and District 3 (2018–2020)

Logan Macomber (born 8 December 2004), known as Logan, is a former member of the Ninzard Project from Oregon who served as the 5th Governor of Ninzardia from 15 August 2018 to 13 February 2020. An administrator of District 3, the former Third Faction, he was elected as an independent in August 2018 with O'Ducky as his running mate, defeating Rudolf, and became the first elected governor to come from outside both the Ninzardian and Nightian factions.1 He was re-elected easily against Reddaze in February and again in August 2019.2,3

A self-described “bro gamer” known for edgy insults and mood swings, Logan presided over a looser, less political Ninzardia in which gaming and fun returned while Nightwork grew darker and more serious.1,4 In October 2018 he twice griefed the server of the new Legislature before joining the leaders who founded the Ninzard Council.5 Worn down by 2019, he did not campaign for a fourth term, lost to Navarch on Capitalia's votes in February 2020 and quietly left the community. The nostalgia that followed came to be called “Logan syndrome”, and tales of his deeds circulated as the “Loganian legend”.6

Early life and Third Faction (2017–2018)

Logan grew up in Oregon.1 He is thought to have found his way into Ninzard around 2017 through a connection to another community, TFN (The Funk Ninja). In March 2018 he began to contribute actively to the Third Faction, which then stood apart from both Ninzardia and Nightwork, and he helped it grow. He later said that he had first joined to sabotage the Third Faction but had changed his mind after becoming “too attached.” Apollos remembered him in those months as “calm” and “chill,” in sharp contrast to the reputation he would later have as governor.1,7

Until the summer of 2018 the Third Faction was run by Vice President Slamo, Advy and, surprisingly, Rudolf, who enforced order. On 8 August 2018 Apollos announced that it would merge into Ninzardia as one of its districts. Within Ninzardia, Capitalia became known as “District 1” or “D1”, Hybriden as “D2” and the newcomer as “District 3” or “D3”; it was renamed BobaBase on 31 December 2019. The merger also gave rise to the “D3SF” meme, short for “District 3 Secret Forces”, after a conspiracy theory that Apollos commanded a covert network of hackers who acted only on his orders.7,8

2018 election

Governor Faber did not stand in the gubernatorial election of 12–14 August 2018 and promised a clean transfer of power to his successor. Everyone elected in that summer cycle except the president would serve a six-month term from 15 August 2018 to 13 February 2019.9,1

Logan, by then a D3 administrator, ran as an independent with O'Ducky as his running mate: William Williamson, born 9 September 2004, an Irish moderator from D3 known as “Ostentatious Ducky” and notorious in his own right. His main opponent was Rudolf, the Orie candidate, who chose the former Capitalian General Danbi as his running mate in an attempt to win over moderate voters.1

Logan attacked Rudolf for abusing staff and for his emotional volatility. His critics answered that Logan had the same “toxic gamer” personality and mood swings, and Rudolf accused him of immaturity.1

Logan won 83 of the 155 votes cast. He was the first elected governor in the Ninzard Project to come from outside both the Ninzardian and the Nightian factions.1 In the same cycle Kay was re-elected General of Capitalia, Advy returned as General of Hybriden and Zythos, an Orie, became the first General of D3,9 while Apollos was re-elected president, carrying Ninzardia by 90 votes to Abby's 65.10

Governor of Ninzardia (2018–2020)

First term

Logan took office on 15 August 2018, the day Apollos began his second term.1,11 The faction he inherited was diminished: Sacraday and Solorado had closed that summer, Hybriden's activity had fallen off sharply, D3's was uneven, and Capitalia, under General Kay, was the only district that was stable and fully functioning.12,13

On 22 September 2018 Logan and Governor DegreeZ of Nightwork allowed members to move freely between the districts. Many Ninzardians, along with former Third Faction members and refugees from the RSN, moved into the Nightian districts. The move strengthened the lawless “Gray Zone” of peripheral servers left by the RSN War and made the border between the two factions all but meaningless.12,14 Within weeks many Nightians resented the newcomers and compared Apollos to Antareze and his “forced assimilation”; Apollos answered that he had mandated nothing, and DegreeZ closed Nightwork to new Ninzardians.15

Ninzardia itself changed in character. After the RSN War and Logan's arrival, the faction became noticeably more lax about its rules, put fewer limits on expansion and took itself less seriously. Young members of the Golden Era took the places of many of the OGs, and creativity, gaming and a sense of fun returned; many Ninzards took up Fortnite. Nightwork moved the other way, growing gloomier, more secretive and more political, its members so used to raids and hacking that many stayed invisible online.4 Reddaze, a Third Faction administrator, set out the new mood in a message signed by the faction's leadership:

「It's time we get back to be a fun drama free server. Invite your friends, ask about crazy things, share your yt vids, see what games others play. … No more drama is how we need to be.」

— Reddaze, Third Faction administrator, for the Ninzardia Leadership

Ninzard Council (October 2018)

On 13 October 2018 Apollos set up a server to re-establish the Legislature and invited the members of the Capital and the governors and deputy governors, followed by moderators from Hybriden, D3 and Parry. In the early hours of 14 October, while most leaders slept, Deputy Governor O'Ducky deleted all of its chats; asked why by Vice President Slamo, he said the Legislature was killing activity in D3 and Hybriden and that his concern was Ninzardia first. The Capital started again on 15 October without him, and at midnight on 16 October Logan griefed the server himself, telling Slamo he had done it so that Apollos would learn from his mistakes.5

On 17 October the Capital gathered a third time and removed Logan's and O'Ducky's permissions. Logan renamed himself “Xorbit”, Apollos unknowingly gave the permissions back, and on 18 October Logan griefed the server again. The episode embarrassed the Capital; Whyskitter, the General of Main, offered to set the new Legislature up properly and was ignored. Later on 18 October, Apollos, Slamo, Raffaele, Logan and O'Ducky opened a group chat to negotiate the Legislature's terms, and DegreeZ and Xorbit joined them in agreeing an informal structure.5

On 20 October 2018 Logan was among the fifteen leaders of the Capital, the two factions and their districts who together established Legislature X, officially the “Ninzard Council.” O'Ducky, who had pushed hard for it, was made its Chairperson.5

PiggieMLG and Spikester (November 2018)

When Capital Adjudicator Raffaele published evidence on 19 November 2018 that the targets of Governor PiggieMLG's “war” had nothing to do with the Pirate Raiders he claimed to be fighting, Logan condemned PiggieMLG's “degenerate behavior.”16 The Ninzard Council voted to impeach PiggieMLG on 27 November, and after Spikester forced him out, Logan joined Apollos, Slamo and Raffaele in endorsing Spikester for Governor of Nightwork.17 Spikester worked with Logan through the transition and stayed in close touch with the Council.18

Second and third terms (2019)

Ninzardia held its winter elections on schedule on 11–13 February 2019, separately from Nightwork. Logan was re-elected without effort against Admin Reddaze and began his second term on 14 February. Kay, Advy and Zythos kept their districts.2 He greeted Admin Guaper's bid to become General of D3 with a crude, slur-laden jab.2

In August 2019 Logan was again easily re-elected over Reddaze, and his third term began on 15 August. In D3 Meikaqu defeated Zythos and Guaper in a three-way race to become its second General, after which Guaper declared “war” on the district and its staff.3 On 31 December 2019 Apollos and Meikaqu renamed D3 the District of BobaBase.8

The year 2019 was the height of expansionism and of the Underground.19 Ninzardians closed ranks against the Underground's movements and the OuterBand, and Logan's vocabulary spread with them: he popularized “peepee boy” for anyone who seemed oversensitive, weak or insecure, and a homophobic nickname for the OuterBand's members that Ninzardians turned on the Underground's Celestial supporters.3

Defeat (2020)

Logan was up for re-election in the cycle of 11–13 February 2020 but did not campaign. The events of 2019 had left him resigned and powerless; he said publicly that he saw his only job as keeping Ninzardia safe from the chaos of Nightwork and the Underground, and he blamed Governor Spikester for that chaos.6

Hybriden and BobaBase both voted narrowly for Logan, but Capitalia gave his opponent, Admin Navarch, so wide a margin that it decided the race. Many members were saddened by the result. Shortly afterward Logan quietly left the community, to the dismay of his most ardent supporters.6 Navarch, an Orie from Illinois with Clexas as his deputy, took office as the 6th Governor of Ninzardia on 14 February 2020, and many in Hybriden harassed him and held Logan up against him.6

Reputation

Logan's edgy insults made him a frequent target of charges of racism and white supremacy. Apollos defended him, saying that most of his comments were jokes meant to do no real harm; Logan himself never denied the charges and answered them with sarcastic mock agreement.1 In an open letter against Apollos on 30 August 2019, a Hybridian moderator counted Logan among the racists to whom Apollos had given a strong voice, and claimed that Apollos made him watch over the Discord server all night at the cost of his sleep.20

Legacy

After Logan's departure, “Logan syndrome” became the name for the nostalgia members felt for his governorship, and stories of his “remarkable deeds” began to circulate as the “Loganian legend.”6 His successor Navarch kept Ninzardia's individualistic culture without resorting to the drastic restrictions Governor Syn imposed in Nightwork.21

Loganian legend

The best known of the stories concerns his birth. According to the Loganian legend, he was born during a “Pineapple Express” atmospheric river that crippled the Pacific Northwest. Warm rain melted the Coast Range snowpack and sent the Nehalem River through Tillamook County, carrying logs, and at about six in the evening a Douglas fir fell across the power lines and blacked out the valley. The Macombers, at a farmhouse three miles out of town, set off for the hospital in their four-wheel-drive truck, only to be cut off by a mudslide; on a knoll above the flood his mother gave birth in the truck's covered bed by the light of its high beams, in winds of fifty miles an hour.1

The newborn, the legend says, did not cry in the freezing air but stared down the storm, holding a pine needle in his fist like a tactical knife. When volunteer rescuers finally reached the farm by inflatable boat at midnight, they found him calmly chewing a piece of dried salmon, apparently annoyed that the storm had disturbed his sleep.1
