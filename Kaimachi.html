Kaia March (born 20 March 2007), known as Kaimachi or Kai, is a Ninzard politician who has served as the 11th District General of Parry since 2 July 2026, having unseated PoG, the Boss of the Original Party, by 66 votes to 64. A Preservate, she represented Parry in Legislature XI from April to July 2026.1,2,3

The best known of the “Parry Girls,” she became notorious for her loud, emoji-laden “brainrot” posting and her parasocial devotion to President Apollos, about whom she wrote an erotic piece of fan fiction in January 2026. In February Apollos and Spinzle brought her into the Preservate Apparatus as its “brainrot wrangler,” to win the Parry Girls away from PoG.4,5,6

Her first weeks as general, spent on events for her friends, plunged Parry into the Salon War of August 2026. On 14 August, with her authority questioned, she put herself on trial before her harshest critics, handed them real seats and channels instead of appointing her friends, and won the district over. The Parry Trials ended the war and a reform of the Parry Staff Team ensued.7,8,9

Early years

March joined Ninzard in 2019, during the Era of Progression, and became a prominent figure in Parry. She lives in California.4,10 To be noticed in crowded chat logs she turned to hornyposting and exaggerated devotion to Apollos, behavior the Preservates dismissed as “brainrot” and the Ories as “degenerate.” The archive reads it as a mask for her disillusionment with the community's cold factionalism: she rejected the intellectualism of Scope and the bureaucracy of Main in favor of raw, unfiltered emotion.4

Parry Girls (2025–2026)

By late 2025 Kai had become the archetype of the “Parry Girl.” In January 2026, under PoG's generalship, she released an erotic piece of fan fiction about herself and President Apollos, written in a frantic style of emoji, capitals and misspellings. Most Preservates and Ories were disgusted; Apollos and Spinzle felt amusement and pity instead, and Apollos later said publicly that she had “the capacity to craft a masterpiece.” The archive calls the manuscript the defining artifact of the Parry Girl phenomenon: beneath the irony and slang, a cry for connection.5 Its vocabulary, “vascular” above all, spread through Parry's slang and beyond.11

Around the same time PluPlu came into Parry to scold the Parry Girls, and the two traded insults in front of the whole district; afterward PluPlu decided Kai was hilarious and added her as a friend.12 On 30 January her friend Mélanie, a Preservate and fellow Parry Girl, urged Apollos in the Preservate Apparatus to stop ignoring her, arguing that she only wanted to be seen. Moctezuma, Syn and others answered that his silence was kinder than false hope, and Mélanie left the Apparatus, suggesting that Kai take her place.13

Brainrot Wrangler (2026)

On the evening of 2 February 2026, Apollos and Spinzle, who had followed that debate, brought Kai into the Preservate Apparatus. It was a calculated gamble to stop PoG drawing on the Parry Girls' support, and to avoid sending the wrong signal neither of them added her themselves: Spinzle asked Gale to do it.6

Spinzle: Your fanfic proves you're a good writer + proves you have influence over the Parry Girls. They listen to you. Your dedication is impressive (creepy nonetheless) . . . we can put it to use

Kaimachi: Wwait...... 😳 What does this mean? I don't know how to lead anything...... I just have brainrot...... 🤕

Spinzle: Join the Preservate Apparatus. You control the Parry Girls; we need the brainrot wrangler

The pushback was immediate. Grace called it hypocritical for a party built against personality cults to bring in the leader of “the ‘Reu Cult’,” Eve feared leaks, and Cyntaku warned that proximity would feed the obsession: “One wrong move… And you are gone.”14 Spinzle argued that exclusion does not eliminate energy but displaces it into opposition, and that inside a room of thirty people the obsession would be socialized. Woofer welcomed her as someone who only wanted to belong and help, and Grace, once Kai was in, told her that the party did not treat her as a tool: “We want family. We don't discard.”14

By 24 February she was one of Parry's two party operatives in the Apparatus, counted with the party's Pragma Flank.15 In the Preservates' plans for the summer, Parry was PoG's stronghold, but with Kai and the Parry Girls inside the party they hoped to let them “out-weird” PoG on her own ground.16

Legislature XI (2026)

In the snap elections of March 2026 Kai won the seat in Legislature XI that Kain had held for Parry, with 74 of 122 votes (60.7%), and took it on 2 April.2,17,3 The same day Woofer, the party's new Boss, made her the Apparatus's district coordinator for Parry, one of its twelve core insiders until July.18

With PluPlu, Poka and KatDoesMuch she brought the Parry Girls' style into the Legislature. When Liver lectured the chamber on 25 April and answered their taunts with a Latin exorcism, Kai was among the loudest of the girls who mocked her, and two days later they roasted her across her own district.19,20 When the Charter passed on 7 May, Kai apologized to her publicly: “I AM SO SORRY FOR MAKING FUN OF YOU 🥺🥺🥺 WE ARE YOUR FAMILY NOW OKAY???”21 In Parry, where PoG's neutrality had held back support for the Charter, Kai and the Parry Girls turned out a large cohort of voters, and the district approved it by 97 votes to 13, with 20 abstentions.22

After the former General Soda died in a Scope voice channel on 18 May 2026, she addressed the community.23,24

「The internet makes it so easy to forget the actual human lives behind the screen and each avatar because people get so caught up in the arguments and the jokes. But a real person was in unbearable pain. … Rest in peace, Lucca. I'm so sorry we couldn't see you through your mask.」

— Representative Kaimachi, May 2026

2026 election

The Preservate Apparatus backed Kai against PoG for General of Parry in June 2026. PoG, unsurprised, admired the Preservates' tactics even as she called Kai a “slave to the technocrat machinery.” Kai had gathered the Parry Girls and entertained much of Parry's troll demographic, drawing voters who usually backed the Ories, but the archive gives most weight to her closeness to the community itself: she had befriended much of the server and become a story in her own right. PoG cultivated a following, but Kaimachi cultivated friendship.1

On 12 June, PoG's twentieth birthday, her staff held a celebration on a Discord stage where Parryans performed for her, many in French. Kai sang softly in halting French, accompanying herself on a synthesizer, and PoG was moved to tears in full view of the district. Kai, absorbed in her performance, noticed only when she finished, and covered her mouth with both hands: “Anaïs?!”1

On 22 June Kai won by 66 votes (50.8%) to 64, the most unexpected upset of the summer elections; the “Brainrot Wrangler” had brought down the Orie Boss. PoG conceded graciously that evening.1

「And to Kaia, my fierce rival. Deux voix. Two votes separated us. What an exquisite, breathless duel! … Parisian Parry is yours now. Fill it with your vibrant energy. Paint it in your colors. I leave the keys on the table with a smile.」

— General PoG, conceding, 22 June 2026

Kai answered that she felt “so foul” for having beaten “our Parry mom,” and begged PoG to stay on as the district's “wine aunt.”1,25 She took office on 2 July 2026, the day the new administration began.26

General of Parry (2026–present)

Three blocs

Kai's first month belonged to the Parry Girls, the coalition that had carried her by two votes. She hosted events in their idiom: a shrine to her “Vascular King,” a channel for manifesting their collective future husband, and a crude festival in Apollos's honor that trended across Parry for all the wrong reasons.7 Three blocs formed. The Parry Girls were ascendant; the “Salonnistes,” the Romantic Flank loyalists of PoG, saw her salon turned into a thirst nest; and the “Sacback Room,” a large bloc of men aligned with the Ories' Rationalist Flank, felt that the new general had spared them not a single event, channel or thought.27 By late July the Salonnistes were spreading their thesis through every district and side server: Parry was dying under the Brainrot Wrangler, she was a clique general who had won by a fluke, and only PoG could hold the district.27

Salon War

August was the war. The Sacback Room raided the Parry Girls' events, the girls retaliated, and the Salonnistes fought everyone on principle; the Ories floated an exit server. Two Parry administrators quit on 10 and 12 August, and general chat became a fight that never stopped. Activity was astronomical, but of the kind that comes before a server dies. PoG sat in the corner, wine in hand, and said nothing.8

Much of the Preservate Apparatus, Cyntaku, Aurase, Seandry and Syn most prominently, wanted the Capital to step in. President Poka overruled them all on 11 August and staked everything on Kai, who answered her critics the same night.28

「If you wanna fight in general chat, THEN FIGHT!!! Bring your edits, bring your anger, bring your testosterone bcs I LITERALLY DO NOT CARE!!! ⚔️😤 Parry is a THUNDERDOME!!! ⚡⚡⚡ … PARRY IS ALIVE FOREVER BITCHES!!!!!! 💅✨💖💋
LATE NIGHT KEYBOARD EVENT AT 10!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!!! JOIN!!!!!!!!!!!!」

— General Kaimachi, 11 August 2026

Poka, Kai and Meikaqu then spent two hours on a call designing a strategy. Kai understood that she could never out-Anaïs PoG, the aloof salonnière framing the war as distant cinema; but PoG had only ever had a following and reigned alone, while Kai had friends.28

On 13 August, with Parry in crisis, her authority questioned and her attachment to him lingering, Apollos called her and showed her grace; it gave her more confidence.10 The next day she published a statement in plain text, without her usual slang or emoji. She confessed that her exaggerated online persona had been a defense against severe loneliness, thanked him for treating her with grace and dignity, and promised to respect his boundaries.10

Parry Trials

The same day, 14 August, she wrote to the district.29

「You have every right to be angry. I disrespected the culture that Anaïs built. I came into this role and only thought about myself and my friends. … Tonight at eight o'clock Pacific time, I'll open a public tribunal on a stage channel tonight to begin the Parry Trials. I am putting myself on the stand. I invite the fiercest critics from the Salonnistes and the angriest of the Sacback Room to prosecute my generalship live.」

— General Kaimachi, to the District of Parry, 14 August 2026

She promised no mutes and no bans, and refused to let the Apparatus protect her. She told the other generals to stay out of what she called “Parry business,” and told the Parry Girls to hold back from defending her. Kain and Mélanie, Parry's representatives, tried to keep the server from imploding; Elle, an Orie administrator, threatened to resign unless Kai sided with the Sacback Room; and Rudolf and BadSpirit tried to derail the trials before they began.29

That night she put herself on trial first, without the Charter, the Capital, a single rule or a single loyalist to hide behind. Rudolf opened with a stream of slurs, and when he accused her of never seeing the district's men, she agreed with him.9 Marguerite, the loudest of the Salonnistes, told her she was no general: PoG had held Parry for six months without ever begging.9

Kaimachi: Marguerite, you're right about Anaïs. Genuinely. She's better at this than me. … I'm not gonna out-Anaïs Anaïs. … But I actually wanna say, and then you can tell me I'm wrong. She had all of you watching her. Right? Up there. Alone. Lonely and alone. And I don't want you watching me. I'm down here in it with the rest of you.

Marguerite: Very pretty speech for someone with no plan.

Kaimachi: Oh I have a plan. I hate my plan. Wanna hear it?

Everyone expected her to end the night by appointing her friends. Instead she appointed none of them. She gave Rudolf the men's events, with a channel, an event slot and a staff seat; she gave Marguerite the salon channel and dared her to build something better; and she talked Elle into keeping her seat.9 Aurase objected to her whole approach in academic terms, and Boygho, back as “the ghost of General's past,” accused her of turning Parry into a fandom; she met them both with self-deprecating jokes, and conceded Boygho's point outright.9

The archive finds four reasons the trials worked. Handing the Salonnistes a real seat turned sabotage into a stake in Parry's success; seeing the men on the record dignified them without moderating anyone; refusing to appoint loyalists destroyed the charge that she was a clique general; and she was genuinely, disarmingly funny, cringe, brave and honest, rather than a technocrat performing humility. The trials ended the Salon War and secured Parry under her command.9

Aftermath

Poka called her a master of “diplomacy by embarrassment.”30 The Salonnistes' insurgency had run on PoG's name, and on 17 August she ended it with a yawn.30

「I watched the trials, naturally. One does not miss the cinema. The girl stood in the centre of the fire, confessed every sin, handed her enemies the knives, and cackled like a lunatic. Was it good? I have no idea. I am simply bored.」

— Former General PoG, 17 August 2026

By September Parry was as loud, offensive and alive as ever, but the conflict had a center again. PoG had held Parry from above, aloof and alone; Kai held it from within, the district's “General-in-the-mud.” Meikaqu, writing for the archives, credited three things: Poka's refusal to let the Capital help, Kai's appointment of the people who wanted her gone, and an authenticity no one could copy. “Parry may actually have her,” she concluded.30,31 In mid-September Kai gave Parry's Chief of Administration Elle the nickname “Miss Protestant,” which spread through the community.

Personal life

Kai plays the keyboard and synthesizer and sings.10,1 Among the Preservates her closest friends are the girls of the Apparatus, PluPlu, Poka and KatDoesMuch among them, and Mélanie, who returned to the party after winning a seat for Parry in Legislature XII.32,33
