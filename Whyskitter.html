Maxime Mélançon (born 25 October 1998), known as Whyskitter, is a former Ninzard politician who served as the 3rd District General of Main from 27 June 2018 to 15 January 2019. A loyalist of Governor Spikester, he opened Main to members of rival communities under the governor's policy of “Progression,” and lost the district to Syn in the snap elections of January 2019.1,2,3

General of Main (2018–2019)

Whyskitter took office on 27 June 2018, the day General Xorbit left Main to become Deputy Governor of Nightwork.4 A veteran of Spikester's RAT wars, he supported war as a “defensive measure” and was counted among the governor's loyalists.5,3

Progression

By January 2019 Spikester and Whyskitter were carrying out “Progression,” inviting members of rival communities, the Underground and the Rocket Squad Nation, into Nightwork to “rehabilitate” them.1 Whyskitter invited new members into Main en masse, as AJ did in Volley, drawing objections and threats to start a rival server, and Spikester, his legitimacy under threat, called snap elections for 13–14 January 2019.2

Defeat

Whyskitter campaigned on optimism, promising that things would get better; Syn, running as an independent, campaigned on making Nightwork secure.6 Main elected Syn, who succeeded Whyskitter on 15 January 2019, and every general chosen that day was an independent.2,3 Cyntaku, elected in Volley the same day, later remembered Whyskitter as one of Spikester's “little acolytes.”7
