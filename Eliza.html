Elizabeth Avalon (born 2 July 1997), known as Eliza and monikered the Shepherd, is a British financial analyst and co-founder of the Ninzard Project who served as 1st General of Hybriden in 2013.1,2,3

As one of the “First Triumvirate” of founders alongside Antareze and Hokan, she co-created Zarden, hosted Hybriden, and proposed recruiting young neighborhood children so emotional attachment would sustain long-term retention.1,4 She left Ninzard in June 2016 after Antareze rejected her romantic confession, though her legacy fueled her younger sister Cat's destructive presidency (2022–2025).5,6 In 2025, Avalon intervened after Cat's arrest to verify Kay's investigation and fund restitution for Cat's victims.3,7

Founding of Ninzard (2010–2012)

Avalon met Christian Akita (Antareze) on gaming forums while initially presenting as a boy, bonding over Gundam Gunpla.1 After revealing her gender, she and Akita co-created the Zarden TeamSpeak server, forming the Broader Project of Zarden (BPZ) with Hokan.1,8

A psychological thinker, Eliza proposed recruiting younger neighborhood children because emotional attachment would ensure long-term retention and democratic legitimacy.1 She hosted the second 32-slot TeamSpeak server, Hybriden, recruiting online members including Raffaele and Abby, as well as her ten-year-old sister Cat.1,9,10 She also helped Antareze cultivate his secret “Orion1” persona and co-drafted an early constitution with Hokan that Antareze rejected.9,11

General of Hybriden and departure (2013–2016)

Elected 1st General of Hybriden in December 2012, Eliza was unseated a year later by Abby after Hybridians tired of her ties to the political establishment.2,12 On 21 June 2016, Antareze's 18th birthday, Eliza asked to meet him in person and confessed her romantic feelings. Antareze replied: “I harbour no emotions. I feel nothing for you.”5 Devastated, Eliza left Ninzard and briefly confided her grief in her sister Cat before resuming her emotional distance by fall.5

Post-Ninzard and the Catx3 crisis (2022–2025)

Eliza's academic and social success inspired a severe inferiority complex in Cat, who sought to destroy Ninzard as Eliza's “intellectual property.”6 At Christmas 2022, Eliza met Raffaele at the Avalon estate and deduced he was the boy she had invited to Hybriden a decade earlier.13 In July 2025, the Sactown Pound named their stray black cat “Eliza Kitten” to spite President Cat.14

When Cat was arrested in August 2025, Elizabeth—now a senior British financial analyst—took leave to manage the crisis, retaining King's Counsel to secure an Article 8 media injunction while verifying Kay's evidence.3 On 1 September 2025, she flew to California to visit Meikaqu in the hospital, holding her hand for four hours, apologizing on her sister's behalf, and guaranteeing family restitution payments so Cat's coerced accomplices could receive non-custodial plea deals.7,15
