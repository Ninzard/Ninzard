Grace Coolahan (born 27 August 2003) is a former Ninzard politician who served as the 4th Capital Adjudicator of the Ninzard Project in 2020, under President Apollos, and as the 8th General of Waffle from 2 July 2025 to 14 March 2026. One of the three founders of the Preservation Party, she led it as Boss in 2022 and was Deputy Governor of Nightwork under Adelynn from 2023 to 2024.1,2,3,4

An administrator in Main when Apollos unexpectedly chose her as Capital Adjudicator, Grace resigned after he dismissed the plan she and Syn had drawn up to limit expansion, and on 1 October 2020 founded the Preservation Party with Syn and Cyntaku. She won Main that month but lost it to Aeromage in May 2021, and was twice defeated for vice president, by Helen in 2020 and by Snowby in 2021.5,6,7,8,9

Made Boss after Cyntaku's defeat in July 2022, she backed Adelynn for governor and served as her deputy through the Catx3 Regime, which threatened them both. Elected General of Waffle in 2025, she resigned in March 2026 to stay with Adelynn after she was struck by a vehicle, and left the community alongside her on 1 July 2026.3,10,11,12,13

Early life

Grace was born on 27 August 2003 and lives in the Los Angeles area, near her close friends Adelynn and Woofer. A Catholic, she is described by friends as studious, hardworking and responsible with money, the most focused member of their circle and its “glue,” with a calm, older-sister presence; in later years she took up hiking.14 She has a younger sister.15

Before 2020 she was one of the administrators of Nightwork-Main and a member of the Original Party, and was not considered a prominent figure in the community.1,16

Capital Adjudicator (2020)

Appointment

On 30 April 2020, as President Apollos replaced the expansionist Vice President Hokan with Helen, he appointed Grace the 4th Capital Adjudicator, succeeding Raffaele, who had resigned in February. The choice surprised the community. Grace was known for her conscientiousness and her skill at gathering and passing on information, but was also seen as rigid and overly strict. Apollos chose her partly for her commitment and discipline, and because she disagreed with him professionally and on logical grounds, which he believed would guard against groupthink; he said he felt at ease in her presence. Governor Syn strongly supported her.1

The Underground cleanup

Within days Helen, without consulting Grace or the president, deleted many of the Underground's platforms, provoking a backlash across the community; Grace and Apollos publicly backed her so that the Capital would appear united.1 When Helen again acted alone on 30 May, banning several members of the Underground and nearly starting a war, Grace and Apollos negotiated a peace on 4 June and set 2 July 2020 as the start of the lockdown that sealed off the community's platforms.17 That summer she was among the leaders who fiercely backed Apollos's re-election.18

Resignation

Closer to Apollos personally than Syn was, Grace worked with the governor on a policy to settle their dispute over expansion. Under their proposal Nightwork's generals would screen one new member for each district every month, admitting at most 48 a year. Apollos laughed and rejected it. Grace, who had worked hard to keep Nightwork and the Capital connected, felt he had thrown that work away, and resigned on 23 September 2020.5 During the same month's standoff between Syn and the districts of Parry and Volley, she had urged him to find a compromise rather than “kill the entire community,” but he refused.5

The Preservation Party (2020–2021)

On 1 October 2020 Grace founded the Preservation Party with Syn and Cyntaku, on a platform of defending and protecting Nightwork's members.2,19 She had left the Original Party in the belief that Syn had the most logistically sound plan for protecting the community and its institutions.16

General of Main

In the October 2020 elections Main, whose members had turned against General Arcus for defying Syn, ousted him and elected Grace as its 6th general.6 In November she surprised many by endorsing the Ories' Clexas in Ninzardia's snap election for governor.20 In May 2021 she lost Main to the Orie nominee Aeromage, an upset given her standing as one of Syn's most important allies.7

Vice-presidential races

When the Capital agreed to hold a vice-presidential election in December 2020, Syn put Grace forward against Helen. The race was seen as a safe contest between two former members of the Capital, and Helen won 55% of the vote to Grace's 45%.8

The Preservates nominated her again in 2021. Grace accused Helen of running a cult, of authoritarian tendencies, of a lack of restraint and of habitual lying; Helen called her “disloyal,” without a personality and calculating.16 The rematch stirred little enthusiasm until Snowby of the Poo Party, quietly backed by Apollos, surged in December. The vote was delayed by a week after Snowby was banned from Discord and Grace fell ill, and on 24 December 2021 Snowby won amid fatigue with the establishment; Grace barely passed 20%.9 Unknown to the candidates, Cat had been paying for slander and memes against Helen and Grace.21

Boss of the Preservation Party (2022)

The screenshot incident

By 2022 Grace was again an administrator in Main and one of the most trusted figures in the Preservate Apparatus; many had expected Cyntaku to choose her as running mate that January.22,23 When Generals Geo and Molly spread a rumour that a member close to Governor Rony was underage, Grace checked with the member herself on 4 May 2022, confirmed that she was an adult and told the party to drop the rumour. She then edited her own question, cropped a screenshot so that the member appeared to give a younger age, and sent it back to her.23

The archive describes the act as an impulsive, nihilistic surrender after months of absorbing the Poopulists' hostility and her own party's paranoia. Many Nightians saw an uncharacteristic lapse; many Ninzardians read it as a deliberate threat. Adelynn reprimanded her privately, a lesson credited with making her a more disciplined leader, and Grace apologized publicly to Rony and the Poopulists, who dismissed the apology.23 Cat, who had planted the original rumour, found the episode hilarious.24

Party leader

After Cyntaku's defeat in July 2022, the party chose Grace as its Boss on 30 July, a compromise between its radicals and its moderates.3,25 She turned the Preservates' attention to Governor Spinzle's handling of Nightwork and away from the Cat Capital and Ninzardia, and held the party together for three months.3 When Geo proposed paying the Poopulists to leave, she reluctantly agreed and presented the offer herself to keep it credible, but the plan was sabotaged and the party mocked. Grace admitted feeling “stupid,” and began to ask whether the Preservates themselves had been in the wrong.26

Defeat in Main

After Geo's breakdown, Grace stood for her old seat in Main and lost the snap election of November 2022 to the Orie Sundae by 110 votes to 57 (34.1%), a result considered a critical failure for the party. Sundae, unknown to voters, was working for Cat.27,28 Grace later said that the defeat was the point at which she “began to touch more grass.”27 With the party splitting between isolationists and moderates and Molly urging her to resign, she judged herself unqualified to lead and said she would step down by the end of the year.3

Deputy Governor of Nightwork (2023–2024)

2023 election

Grace had considered Adelynn too moderate, but backed her bid to unseat Spinzle and ran as her deputy. The decision split the party: the moderates followed Grace into what became the Logos Flank, while traditional isolationists formed the Axiom Flank.3,25 At the debate on 30 December 2022 she called a question about the candidates' gender absurd, and accused the administration of refusing to take responsibility for unregulated growth.29 Adelynn and Grace won by three votes, 279 to 276, helped in Main by their reputation there, and at Grace's insistence Adelynn became Boss of the party.4,25 Both turned down large sums from Cat; her ledger records $4,490 offered to Grace and nothing accepted.4,30

Adelynn's deputy

After Apollos unmasked himself as General Assailant in April 2023, Grace and Adelynn designed the Sponsorship Standard, under which candidates and staff had to pass voice verifications with their district general and with Adelynn or Grace.31,32 On the advice of Raffaele and General Kay, they removed Meikaqu from the Preservate Apparatus as a “high risk.”33 When the party debated nominating Lyman in Scope, Grace rejected Adelynn's suggestion of courting his opponent instead, saying the time for that had “long since passed.”34 From July 2023 Adelynn handed about half of her administrative work to Grace, whom she trusted to put the institution ahead of personal and political ties.35

Cat's threats

In 2024 Cat used Adelynn's phone to force Grace to reveal her identity, then threatened both women and their families. Grace refused to resign, saying it would not make her any safer, and installed cameras around her home.36,10 The charges later laid out against Cat include the extortion of Adelynn and Grace.37

Grace supported Adelynn's decision to put her leadership to a plebiscite in June 2024, arguing that seeking the voters' mandate would secure future ones.38 As the strain told on Adelynn's health and studies, Grace visited her in person several times that summer and became her chief source of support.39

2024 election

By late November 2024 Adelynn had gone two nights without sleep keeping watch over Nightwork, and Grace begged her on a call to stop.40

To hell with the election, Addie. If we lose, we lose. But I'm not losing you.

— Grace, to Adelynn, November 2024

When Adelynn's account was banned and a family emergency called her away, Grace took over the watch, and she ran much of Nightwork's business that winter.40,41 They lost to Aceiron in December 2024 with 45.6% of the vote.42

General of Waffle (2025–2026)

2025 election

After the defeat Grace moved to Waffle, where General Matcha made her Head of Staff. When Matcha ran for governor in 2025, the party nominated Grace to succeed her. Her opponent, Shuffle, a veteran of the Israel Defense Forces, had been nominated by Aceiron to unsettle Waffle's left-wing base, and Grace defended Matcha's refusal to ban her. Grace won 72 of 126 votes (57.1%).43,11

Apollos's return

After Cat's fall in August 2025, Grace was among the Preservates who brought Apollos back. She shared Cyntaku's memory of his instability from her own time in the Capital, but judged that no one else had as good a chance against Aceiron; when Cyntaku called his record “awful,” Grace told her that she had one too. Apollos urged Grace to run herself, which she adamantly refused.44 With her Head of Staff, Rae Fox, she worked to raise turnout against Aceiron in Waffle.45

Over the following months she came to trust Apollos's judgment and stopped worrying about his instability, and he came to respect her.14 With Generals Woofer and Ixan she closed her district for Thanksgiving 2025, and when General Mel derided the closures, Grace encouraged Wafflers to switch off their invisible status so they could mock her.46 In the Dreadnought Debate of November 2025 she argued for keeping the party's structure as it was.47

2025 re-election

In November 2025 ViaTea launched the Liberation Party and challenged Grace in Waffle, calling her “the face of the police state run by technocrats.”48 Grace expected an uphill race and told the party she would not pour all her effort into it, but Apollos kept her as the nominee and campaigned in Waffle himself.48,49 After the Liberation raids of December, Lashae left ViaTea's party and endorsed Grace, who won 93 of 128 votes (72.7%) in a race expected to be close.50,51

The Charter and the Chud War

In the party's debates that winter, Grace warned that forcing the Charter through while half the community wanted “liberation” would delegitimize it on its first day.52 When the party drafted Hyperio to stop Aceiron in Scope, she questioned whether coordination behind closed doors would leave voters feeling that their influence was an illusion, before conceding, “if this is survival mode, then let's survive.”53 When the Cat Files were released, she reflected on the safeguards she and Adelynn had built.15

There is grace inherent in the process of healing, yet wisdom resides in establishing boundaries. In light of these considerations, I consider boredom a luxury.

— General Grace, 14 January 2026

She opposed bringing General Kaimachi into the Apparatus, calling it hypocritical for a party built against personality cults, but once Kaimachi was admitted Grace told her: “We want family. We don't discard.”54 During the Chud War of 14 February 2026 she asked the Capital to call the police and appealed to the belligerents to treat one another with dignity.55,56 Two days later, when General PoG accused the Capital of surveillance, Grace pointed out that the men had broadcast their own locations.57

If you shout your coordinates through a megaphone in the middle of a city, you don't get to act shocked when people hear you. … I consider my responsibility to be to ensure that my members can log off at night without fear of a SWAT team or a drive-by because a Discord argument escalated.

— General Grace, 16 February 2026

After NGin's followers staged the Samson Coup in Volley on 25 February, Grace urged Apollos to ban them, telling him that this was “the instant at which you establish a boundary.” He refused, and Shuffle dismissed her as a “sheltered moralist.”58 In March she challenged Vice President Spinzle's use of a fabricated image to extract a confession from Apollos's attacker, arguing that the assault should simply have been reported to his university.59

Resignation

In the early hours of 13 March 2026 Adelynn was struck by a vehicle in Los Angeles. Grace went to the hospital to help her family and act as her proxy, and told the Capital that she would resign as General of Waffle; she went offline on 14 March.12,60 Members sent their farewells to both women, and on 2 April Woofer named them Honorary Veterans of the party.12,61 The Preservates nominated Shuffle to succeed Grace, and with her endorsement Shuffle won the snap election with 57.1% of the vote.62

Later life (2026)

Grace returned briefly on 25 April 2026 to tell Cyntaku to stop answering General PoG, who she said had baited her into looking hysterical.60 On 2 May she drove Adelynn, still in a leg brace, and a Sacramento member who had long attacked the Preservate leadership to the Holloway Summit at General Kay's home, collecting Cyntaku from the airport on the way.63,64 When the Charter won its referendum that month, she told the party that getting the community to agree on anything at all was a miracle.65

Grace left the community on 1 July 2026, shortly after Apollos, and alongside Adelynn. Declining to write a formal farewell, she left a short final message urging members to drink water, do their homework, spend time with their families and not squander the peace on petty drama, and telling Woofer how proud she was of her.13

Personal life

With Adelynn, Cyntaku and Woofer, Grace is one of the Blue Quartet, a circle of friends formed in the early Preservate Apparatus whose members met in person several times.66,14 Cyntaku credited Grace and Adelynn with drawing her out of the community's grip after 2022, recalling that Grace's advice was simply to “go for a walk.”67

Political style

Grace belonged to the Logos tradition of the Preservation Party, favouring rules, procedure and democratic mandates.68,38 Cyntaku remembered her as “a bureaucrat in the best possible way” who liked order and things that made sense,69 and ViaTea counted her among the most perceptive and dependable people in the community even while criticizing the screenshot incident.23 Her critics cast her as the face of a technocratic establishment and as a moralist.48,58 She described her own aim as a community whose members could log off at night without fear.57,13
