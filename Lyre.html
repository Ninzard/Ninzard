Lyre is a conservative member of the District of Main and a figure of the Original Party's Rationalist Flank. Between 2024 and 2026 she stood four times against General Woofer for control of Main, losing each race, and in June 2026 she became the last opponent of Woofer's record-breaking eighth term. A Sacramento Orie, she belonged to the local circle around Liver and Arielle and was among the Ories present at the siege of Caleb Jensen's house during the Chud War.1,2,3

Campaigns for Main

Lyre became General Woofer's perennial challenger in the District of Main, running against her under the Original Party in four consecutive district elections. She lost each by a wide margin: 55 votes to 119 in June 2024 (31.6%), 67 to 121 in December 2024 (35.6%), and 71 to 147 in June 2025 (32.6%).1,4,5

Her fourth and final run came in the June 2026 Summer Cycle, when Woofer won her eighth consecutive term and the widest margin in Main's history, 235 votes to Lyre's 25 (90.4% to 9.6%). What support Lyre drew came from her own cultural prominence in Main and from friends who backed her candidacy; the Ories were not widely thought to have any realistic chance in the district. In her victory message Woofer thanked Lyre for “a respectful and kind campaign.”6

Chud War

Lyre was at the center of the mundane incident that set off the Chud War. On Valentine's Day 2026 she needed to retrieve a MacBook charger she had left at Caleb Jensen's house; having no car of her own, she was driven there by Liver, who despised Caleb. A scout of the Holy Iron Flank, watching the house, photographed Caleb handing Lyre a gift bag and stepping toward a distressed Liver, and the blurry telephoto image — read by NGin as a betrayal — triggered the voice memo that mobilized the Flank.3

That night Lyre was inside Caleb's house during the siege, with AJ, Arielle, Rhydeu and ScottyAte, while NGin and his followers surrounded it and shouted for Caleb to come out. Woofer stayed in contact with her throughout, and the two worked to ease the tension and to reassure the anxious Blue Quartet; Caleb asked that the police not be called, fearing for his bedridden mother. Lyre joined Caleb, Arielle and ScottyAte in a group chat with some of the Iron Flankers, which helped signal to the Capital that no police were needed, and later stepped outside to a cheering crowd. A selfie Caleb took outside with Lyre, Arielle, AJ, Rhydeu and ScottyAte became one of the war's most memed images.2,7

Rationalist Orie

After the fall of Catx3 and the community's turn away from politics, Lyre was among the Ories who apologized for their part in the toxicity that had divided Ninzard, saying she had not understood its consequences.8 In the aftermath of the Chud War she worked with AJ, Danbi and ScottyAte to make the Original Party more reasonable.9

By 2026 Lyre had become a leading voice of the Original Party's Rationalist Flank. The Rationalists, no longer tied to Caleb Jensen's older redpill politics, pressed for a “grounded,” realistic view of Ninzard as, in the end, a conglomeration of Discord servers, and refused to romanticize or escalate the community's past. Lyre and HazyDaze best represented this flank, which rejected the high stakes and “LARP” of the Preservation Party.10

Liver and the Sacramento circle

Lyre belonged to Liver's small Sacramento circle with Arielle. Liver, who disagreed sharply with their politics but saw the two right-wing girls as “lost lambs,” was fiercely protective of them: she lectured them on Catholic dogma while they wanted to talk about ordinary conservative politics, and when Lyre fell into a depressive episode Liver sat with her in a voice channel for six hours, reading her Bible verses and Irish poetry. When Liver heard NGin's screaming voice memo over coffee with Lyre and Arielle, she closed her eyes and muttered, “Protestant behavior.”3

When Liver resolved to drive to Los Angeles at the end of April 2026, Lyre was the only person she told. Lyre then revealed to the entire Orie Apparatus that Liver was visiting Adelynn, and the news reached the wider community when ScottyAte posted it in Legislature XI.11 Liver later wrote that a distance had grown between them; what hurt, she said, was looking at the sidebar and seeing that Lyre and Arielle were online and said nothing.12

Beliefs

Lyre is a conservative and, in faith, a Protestant. Though a committed Orie, her campaigns against Woofer were marked by civility rather than the vitriol of the Chud War years, and Woofer publicly thanked her for running kindly.6
