Abigail Abernethy (born 20 June 2000), known as Abby, is a former Ninzard politician who served three non-consecutive tenures as District General of Hybriden between 2014 and 2019 and was the opposition nominee in the 2014 presidential election.1,2,3

Recruited in 2012 by Eliza, she brought the Colorado cohort—including her younger sister Snowby, Spinzle, and Holidae—into the Project.1 She unseated Eliza in Hybriden in December 2013 and became known as a warm, maternal figure, challenging President Antareze in 2014 on a platform of constitutional democracy and anti-cyberwarfare.2,4 She later backed Apollos's rise in 2016, but broke with him after the 2018 Gold Road suicides and retired in 2019.5,6,7

Early involvement (2012–2014)

Protective of her younger sister Mary and Mary's bullied friends in Colorado, Abernethy was pitched Ninzard by Eliza as a moderated safe haven and brought the Colorado cohort into the Solorado and Hybriden legions.1 In Hybriden, she befriended Eliza's younger sister, Cat, who vented to her on Skype.8,9

In December 2013, Abby ran against General Eliza in Hybriden. During the campaign, she sang Disney's “Let It Go” in a voice channel; for four minutes, server toxicity evaporated as Meikaqu and Mary typed lyrics in chat. She defeated Eliza, appointed Meikaqu and Tiny2 as representatives, and was re-elected in June 2014 under the Resistance Party.2,10

2014 presidential campaign

When Antareze declared war on Nightwork in October 2014, Abby condemned the move as “pointless” and asked, “What is the intended purpose of a war?”11,4 The Resistance, Tomato, and Alienova parties united behind her as a compromise candidate against Antareze in December 2014. Campaigning as a “mother figure” for legion autonomy and a written constitution against Antareze's “cyberstate,” she won Hybriden (59.3%) and Solorado (83.3%), losing narrowly overall with 132 of 285 votes (46.3%).4,12

Later generalships and departure (2015–2021)

Abby backed Apollos in 2015 and returned as 6th General of Hybriden under the Original Party in July 2016.13,3 She opposed Sacraday General Judah's conduct, was present when Hokan and MMC mocked Apollos in 2017, and lost her seat to Advy in December 2017.14,15,16

After Holidae's suicide in June 2018, Abby accused Apollos of having “blood on his hands,” prompting him to call her a “fake person.”6 She informed her mother, Alexandria Abernethy, about Ninzard and persuaded Advy to step aside so she could win a snap election as 8th General of Hybriden on 22 June 2018.6,17 In the Legislature, her bid for the presidency was blocked by Kay over her “emotional outbursts.”17 Believing her sister had left Ninzard, Abby retired in March 2019.7 When Snowby was elected Vice President in December 2021, Abby alerted their mother and confronted Snowby at her college dorm, leading Snowby to cut contact with her family.18
