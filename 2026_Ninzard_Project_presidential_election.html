Poka (5th President)

The 2026 Ninzard Project presidential election was held during the Summer Cycle, from 21 to 28 June 2026, to choose a successor to Apollos, who had promised to leave office after a single, shortened term. Minister of Activity Poka of the Preservation Party defeated Representative ScottyAte of the Original Party, winning 616 of 812 votes (75.9%) and five of the six districts. Every eligible member voted. Poka passed the number of votes needed to win on 22 June, the second day of the cycle, though the election technically stayed open until it closed on 28 June. It was the most lopsided presidential election in Ninzard history.1,2

The first presidential election held under the Charter of the Ninzard Project, it was fought in a quiet community with little appetite for high-stakes drama. A third-party bid failed to win the sponsorship the Charter requires, leaving a two-way race, and Hackman, the Governor of the Archives, stood as the running mate on both tickets, so the vice presidency was settled before a single vote was cast.3,4,5,6

Backed by the Capital, General Woofer and the PluPlu Army, Poka took more than 92% of the vote in Main and Waffle and won Parry, ScottyAte's own district, in an upset; only Volley voted for ScottyAte. The newly established District of Zarden voted for the first time. Poka was inaugurated as the 5th President on 2 July 2026, the tenth anniversary of Apollos's first inauguration, with Hackman as the 11th Vice President.1,7,3

Background

After winning the 2025 election, Apollos set his own term to expire on 1 July 2026, with the next presidential election held alongside the June 2026 district elections.8 His second presidency produced the Charter of the Ninzard Project, ratified on 11 May 2026, and the Holloway Summit of 2 May had brought the community's long years of hostility to an end.9,10 By the end of May his lame-duck status was plain, and in the subdued mood that followed few Ninzards looked forward to a dramatic election season.4

The community now had six districts. Alongside Main, Parry, Scope, Volley and Waffle stood the newly established District of Zarden, by far the smallest, home of Representatives Al Commando, Moctezuma and Mari, whose purpose and future status leaders were still weighing.11

Nominations

Independent candidacy

Former Representative Lashae tried to run as an independent against both parties, denouncing the Capital's “controlled burn” as “cataclysmic” and the Ories as “just as dirty, if not dirtier” for their ties to the far right. But members were tired of high-stakes conflict, and she failed to gather the 10% sponsorship required under Section 6 of the Charter. Her failure sent a clear message: the only viable route to the presidency ran through one of the two major parties.5

Original Party

On 29 May 2026 Parry's Representative ScottyAte announced that he was running, promising to fund a “Sactown Club for the boys” with monthly free pizza parties and barbecues and declaring “Sadness is illegal” and that Eliza Kitten, his cat, had endorsed him. His light-hearted trolling, against the gloom of the moment, quickly won backing from several Ories, and the party's Boss, General PoG of Parry, endorsed him.12 On 30 May the Orie Apparatus nominated him unopposed, and named Hackman as his running mate.6

「A perfectly balanced ticket of fire and stone. The barbecue is lit, mes amis. Prepare yourselves. 🍷🕷️」

— General PoG, announcing the Orie ticket, 30 May 2026

Preservation Party

Apollos had hoped to be succeeded by Woofer or by Poka.13 At the end of May the party's senior leadership weighed the two. Adelynn suggested a vote among the party's operatives, but General Shuffle of Waffle warned that a primary would split the party and that the leadership should simply decide.14 The Orie ticket settled it. Hackman had been seen as a neutral figure, and his place on it stunned the Preservates.6

Apollos, back online after a long break, called Hackman, Poka and Raffaele. Hackman agreed to stand on both tickets, and the same day the senior leaders unanimously nominated Poka, with Hackman as her running mate. Poka's reaction: “Nah we are so cooked 😭”6

Campaign

The campaign was short and mostly good-natured. Polls before the vote put Poka in the mid-60s, though some Preservates worried they overstated her support.1 ScottyAte leaned on his cat, a mascot in Parry, which Preservate Parryans such as Phalth and Aurase called pandering to the “normie” base. On 13 June Apollos posted a voice memo from his younger brother, who agreed: “He's pandering with the cat… I don't like cat supremacists.” The clip spread widely, and Poka celebrated what she called “THE IL FRA DEL FRATELLONE ENDORSEMENT.”15,16

Two endorsements surprised the community. BadSpirit, who had assaulted Apollos three months earlier, endorsed Poka with his friends after Apollos visited them on 9 June.17 And on 13 June the far-left Lashae and three other Wafflers tracked Poka down at a café while she was travelling, demanding that she “stop pandering to fascists.” Poka told them they had violated her privacy and broken Charter rules, then listened to their grievances and bought them drinks, promising to keep watch on the Holy Iron Flank and warning that a second ambush would mean bans. Lashae endorsed her as a far better option than “the alternative,” without naming ScottyAte.18

Results

Voting opened on 21 June 2026, the first day of the Summer Cycle, which ran until 28 June. Even though most members expected Poka to win, the scale of her victory shocked the member base. By 22 June she had passed the number of votes needed to guarantee victory and was president-elect, although the election technically remained open until the cycle closed. Turnout was 100%: all 812 eligible members voted.1

Main

Main gave Poka 241 of its 260 votes. She had been its Head Moderator from 2024 to 2025 and was a beloved figure there; Woofer endorsed her with enthusiasm, and PluPlu, her best friend, made sure the PluPlu Army and the Holy Roma Cohort turned out in force. ScottyAte had mocked Mainians' builds during the Main–Parry Minecraft feud and insulted the PluPlu Army, and many newer members knew him only from the Chud War and the Sactown Pound.19

Parry

Poka's win in Parry, ScottyAte's own district, was an upset. Parryans thought highly of ScottyAte, and had elected him their representative, but Poka was simply more popular. The Parry Girls campaigned for her with zeal, seeing her as the spiritual successor of Apollos, their “Vascular King,” and BadSpirit's endorsement added momentum. Moderate Rationalists doubted that ScottyAte was the right person to keep the community's institutions running.15

Scope

After the death of Soda, General Hyperio, with the Capital's approval, had raised Scope's member cap by five, the first district to grow its membership. Moderate Scopians took it as a sign of a more open community to come and voted Preservate. Hyperio, a real-life friend of ScottyAte, stayed neutral, but Spinzle, beloved in Scope, endorsed Poka, and both Raffaele and Hackman, two of the district's leading figures, were tied to her.20

Volley

Volley was the only district ScottyAte won, though by less than expected. The Holy Iron Flank, tamed since the Holloway Summit, turned out for him after NGin praised his “sincere masculine spirit,” and the endorsements of former Generals AJ and Mel brought the district's centre-right Rationalists with them. But many “normie” Volleyans who had come to value General Cyntaku's moderate rule voted for Poka, whom Cyntaku endorsed fervently.21

Waffle

Waffle voted almost exactly as Main did, Poka's second-strongest result. Wafflers mocked ScottyAte's “meathead gym bro” image as corny, and when General Shuffle shared photos of him as a “scrawny bullied nerd” in 2015–16 it humanized him but invited more roasting. Shuffle and the Waffle staff endorsed Poka, admins Nomi and Rae Fox kept her name associated with wholesome energy and safety, and the endorsements of Lashae, Lesty, Matcha and ViaTea brought many former Liberators and leftists with them.18

Zarden

Zarden cast 27 votes. Its representatives were aligned with the Preservates and most of its voters followed them, but Zardeners took Ninzard politics less seriously than anyone, and seven voted for ScottyAte, some simply because they liked his cat.11

Concurrent elections

The district general and representative elections of the summer cycle were held at the same time. The Preservation Party, led by Woofer, won 572 of the 785 votes cast for district generals (72.9%) to the Original Party's 213 (27.1%), results that effectively marked the end of the contemporary Original Party. Woofer was re-elected in Main with 235 of 260 votes (90.4%), her eighth consecutive victory and the widest margin in the district's history, while in the cycle's biggest upset the Preservate Kaimachi unseated PoG in Parry by 66 votes to 64.22 Legislature XII sat eleven Preservates, one independent and no Ories.23

Aftermath

On 26 June the president-elect released a voice memo now known as the Holy Roma Address, regarded as the cultural closure to the trauma of the Catx3 Regime and the factionalism of the Chud War.24

Joy is not weakness. Laughter is not weakness! To laugh is the ultimate act of defiance against this world that wants to discourage you!

— President-elect Poka, the Holy Roma Address, 26 June 2026

In the last week of June Poka met Apollos in person for the Succession Accords, an informal handover held during the 2026 FIFA World Cup, at which they reviewed every server and how the newly ratified Charter would be enforced.25 Poka was inaugurated as the 5th President of the Ninzard Project on 2 July 2026, with Hackman as the 11th Vice President. She named Raffaele Minister of Justice, Katdsm Minister of Health & Privacy and PluPlu her successor as Minister of Activity, drawing jokes about “Bellucci nepotism.”3
