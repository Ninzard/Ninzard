Chiara Bellucci (born 29 February 2008), known as PluPlu, is an Italian Ninzard politician who has served as the 2nd Minister of Activity under President Poka since 2 July 2026. A Preservate, she represented the District of Main in Legislature XI, the body that drafted the Charter, and in June 2026 was re-elected with more votes than any other candidate in Main.1,2,3

Raised in Rome, she joined the Ninzard Project at the age of eight in 2016, when her cousin Raffaele introduced her to Poka; their friendship founded the PluPlu Army, the loud and fiercely loyal following that became a force in Main.4,5 In June 2023 she revealed her own name to the whole community so that President Cat could no longer hold it over Raffaele, and she became one of Main's administrators under General Woofer.6,7

Early life

Bellucci was born on 29 February 2008 and grew up in Lazio, in Rome. She struggled at school and felt lonely, and found solace in Ninzard, growing up within its culture.4 Her older cousin Raffaele grew up in New York.5

Hybriden (2016–2023)

In November 2016 Raffaele, then fourteen, stole the Club Penguin account of a lonely ten-year-old Italian girl in New York, Poka. Ashamed, he confessed and invited her to Ninzard's TeamSpeak, and to match her energy he introduced her to his eight-year-old cousin. The two girls bonded at once, talking in rapid-fire Italian and English and sharing memes, and the foundation of the PluPlu Army was laid that afternoon.5 PluPlu belonged to the “Golden Era Cohort,” the second generation of members who joined during Apollos's first presidency, with ScottyAte, ViaTea and Woofer.8

In Hybriden Raffaele became the “Padrino,” the godfather, of the young Italian cohort, PluPlu and Poka above all. While others dismissed the pair's chaotic humor as immature, he recognized their social intelligence, spoke Italian with them in private channels, taught them the community's politics and kept strict admins from banning them. The PluPlu Army never turned on him in return.9 With Poka, Ploutos and other Hybridians she also learned his “Dark Arts” of dividing hostile communities against each other.10

In late 2019 Raffaele used Poka, then thirteen, as bait in an operation against a predator in the Underground. When the eleven-year-old PluPlu found out what her cousin had made her best friend do, she screamed at him over the phone, crying, that it was evil. Her anger decided his resignation as Capital Adjudicator on 29 February 2020; he spent the following months making amends, and the two girls forgave him.11,12

During Cat's 2023 presidential campaign, which Raffaele was aiding, the early PluPlu Army, then called the “S+1 Army,” made images portraying the founder Hokan as an old and dated narcissist.13 On 14 February 2023 Cat, Snowby, Pieban and Meikaqu visited the Bellucci family in Lazio with Raffaele; PluPlu, unaware that her guests were caught up in crimes, taught them Italian phrases over dinner.14

Bellucci War Cry (2023)

By June 2023 Raffaele had broken with Cat. Knowing that she had his family's addresses, he warned her that any move against them in Lazio would bring down her entire operation, and he kept all of it from his cousin.6 When PluPlu found out, she declared publicly, “I AM CHIARA BELLUCCI, COME FIGHT MY FAMILY IN ROME,” revealing her own name to the whole community. Raffaele phoned her at once, shaking with rage and fear, called her a “bambina stupida” for painting a target on the family and asked whether she had a death wish. She cut him off: as long as she stayed a secret, she told him, she was his weakness.6

By making herself a public figure she turned a hidden vulnerability into a person the whole community would see attacked, and Cat could never again use her as a bargaining chip. Raffaele, who had thought only of hiding her, realized that the cousin he had tried to shield was his strongest ally.6 Cat came to believe that the Belluccis had laid a trap for her and never approached the family.15 After people paid by Cat burned Raffaele's home on Christmas Day 2023, he told PluPlu the whole truth. She took it calmly, seeing Cat's turn to crude violence as proof that the devil had been stripped of her subtler powers, and stood by her decision to name herself.16

Main (2023–2025)

Cat wiped out Hybriden in June 2023,17 and PluPlu found a home in the District of Main, whose members, after her defiance, took her in as a heroine.6 General Woofer soon made her an administrator. Some Mainians, Ories especially, called the appointment reckless because she had revealed her own name; she shot back, “RAGAZZI, SINCE WHEN DID ORIES CARE ABOUT SECURITY???” By the end of the year Woofer was calling her the “light in a dark place.”7 Under Woofer, Seandry led Main's administrators, among them Ploutos and PluPlu, while Poka led its moderators.4 When Cat bought off established Mainians to seed her talking points in the district, PluPlu and Poka buried them under an avalanche of memes and all-caps shrieking about penguins.18

Her following, “PluPlu's Army,” led by Poka, grew out of genuine affection for her charm. Conspiracy theories cast the goofy administrator as secretly a “dangerous high-level mafia hacker,” which the archive reads as a measure of her ability to build networks and inspire loyalty in a way that members who thought only of power could not otherwise explain; her social and emotional intelligence was her superpower.4

Apollos's second presidency (2025–2026)

PluPlu had voted for Aceiron in the gubernatorial elections of December 2024 and June 2025, but in the presidential election of September 2025 she backed Apollos, whom she had always admired, with Woofer and the rest of Main's staff. The support of the PluPlu Army was vital to his win in Main.19 Apollos was openly protective of her, both as the cousin of Raffaele, with whom he shared a quiet, brotherly bond, and as proof that the spirit of “Old Ninzard” had survived Cat: she remained, in the archive's words, “sanguine, creative, and goofy.”20

「chiara is my niece to me, capito? if any of you treat her wrongly, i absolutely promise you, i will do unspeakable things to you.」

— President Apollos, jokingly, to Mainians, 3 November 2025

She and Poka used their influence to troll the Original Party. On General Boygho's first day in Parry they had Preservate friends tell him all at once that the PluPlu Army was starting a “civil war” against him. Boygho called her “one of the most problematic administrators of Ninzard” and asked the president to investigate; Apollos concluded that he was “being trolled by some italian girls.”21 When Apollos took Poka from Main on 3 November 2025 to make her the first Minister of Activity, PluPlu theatrically protested the loss of her best friend.22

On the eve of Thanksgiving she posted a declaration of love for Ninzard.23

「BUT YOU ASK ME "PLUPLU, SE È UN INCUBO, PERCHÉ SEI ANCORA QUI??" WHY DO YOU STAY IN THE SEWER?? … BECAUSE RAGAZZO, WE HAVE THE PLUPLU ARMY. … WE SURVIVED THE MONSTERS RAGAZZI. WE SURVIVED EVIL CAT. WE BLED TOGETHER. … IT IS A DISASTER, SÌ. MA È IL MIO DISASTRO. IT IS MY MESS.」

— PluPlu, 26 November 2025

The next day, while Main was closed for the holiday, Mainians who had been let onto Parry's Minecraft server had their Italian-themed builds destroyed, and Parry staff, ScottyAte among them, laughed along and called Italian a “garbage language.” PluPlu, back the following day, prepared a raid with Poka after a sympathetic Parryan leaked the server's address: the PluPlu Army destroyed the builds of those who had harassed the Italians and then crashed the server. Boygho protested; Apollos, while not endorsing the raid, said that the griefing had cut both ways. Watching from the sidelines, Power of Greed saw in it an “act of passion” worthy of the Golden Era and announced her candidacy a few days later.24

In December 2025 the PluPlu Army helped carry Woofer to re-election in Main with 83.6% of the vote.25 In late January 2026 PluPlu went into Parry to scold the Parry Girls for their posts about Apollos and traded insults with Kaimachi in front of the whole district; afterward she decided that Kai was hilarious and added her as a friend.26

Legislature XI (2026)

By February 2026 PluPlu was one of Main's four representatives in Legislature XI, the fifteen members who carried the Charter through.1 In the Preservate Apparatus she was one of Main's party operatives from 24 February, and from 2 April, under the new Boss Woofer, one of Main's two district coordinators with Poka, among the twelve core insiders who led the party until July.27,28

She brought her style into the chamber. On the night of 25 April 2026 she, Poka, Kaimachi and KatDoesMuch clashed with Liver, the traditionalist Catholic Representative for Scope. PluPlu mocked her as a “plastic Catholic” from Sacramento, set against her own Rome “next to the Vatican,” and cast a joke curse on her: “MALEDIZIONE DELLA PIZZA ALL'ANANAS!!!”29 Two days later the four were still roasting Liver across her own district.30

Ahead of the Charter referendum the PluPlu Army drove Main's support, while Poka talked with every Mainian she could and fed meme after meme to the army; when PluPlu found out that Poka had stopped eating properly, she made her go offline.31 The Charter passed on the night of 7 May 2026, and Liver posted her account of her life, Confessions Beneath My Armor, in the same hour.32

「sasha. amore mio. mi dispiace tanto. i am so incredibly sorry. i was so blind and so arrogant and i pushed you. you are not wicked. you are so strong. … even in the dark, GOD is with you. and i will pray for you every day. ti voglio bene, sasha.」

— PluPlu, to Liver, 7 May 2026

When Apollos ate pineapple pizza drenched in ranch on his Charter ratification broadcast, a jab at her curse, she was one of the eight representatives who voted for a joke motion to impeach him “for crimes against humanity.”33 She was the last of the fifteen to react to Soda's death on 18 May 2026, and when Lless mocked such tributes as a performance, KatDoesMuch defended her.34

「tonight, i feel so cold. because a real boy is dead. i did not know Lucca well. he had darkness inside himself and this made him say cruel things. but deep inside, he was only a ragazzo freezing to death in the winter because of his own thoughts. … your hands can reach to another hand. use them to reach out and call all your famiglia, call your amici. and you call us too. always.」

— Representative PluPlu, 18 May 2026

Minister of Activity (2026–present)

In the 2026 presidential election PluPlu made sure that the PluPlu Army and Main's “Holy Roma Cohort” turned out for Poka with maximum energy, and Poka won 241 of Main's 260 votes.35 When Hackman was named running mate on both tickets, PluPlu called him a traitor and Poka complained of being stuck with him, but the anger was a performance: both trusted him.36,37 In the summer elections Main re-elected PluPlu first among its four representatives, with 231 votes (88.8%).2

On 23 June she flew to Vancouver with President-elect Poka and Raffaele, where Apollos met them. The next day at BC Place they watched Switzerland beat Canada 2–1 at the World Cup, and the two Italians mocked Apollos relentlessly. PluPlu returned to Rome on 27 June.38

Poka nominated her Minister of Activity, the office Poka herself had held, and she was confirmed with the rest of the cabinet and took office on 2 July 2026; Advy won the snap election for her seat in Main. Rivals accused the new president of “Bellucci nepotism” over the appointments of PluPlu and Raffaele, but most of Ninzard supported them.3 When Kaimachi came back from her call with Apollos on 13 August 2026, PluPlu greeted her with the news that she had been praying the Rosary for her for two hours.39

Personal life

PluPlu is Catholic; the archive credits the Roman Catholicism of Main's Italian cohort with the district's calm, domestic spirit.40 Her best friend is Poka, whom she calls Aletta,23 and she calls Apollos “Fratellone Rubi” and Spinzle “Sorellona Edita.”26

She is known for an intentionally exuberant Italian manner: rolled R's, invocations of “Roma,” all-caps rants that end in “CAPITO???,” sudden bursts of “VAFFANCULO” and keyboard-smash laughter. Yet she is emotionally perceptive enough to know that she is performing, and is rarely hateful. With Poka she used graphic bodily humor to deflate the “alpha male” Calebros and neo-Nazis, and her calm speaking voice, unlike Poka's energetic one, balances the chaos.4,41
