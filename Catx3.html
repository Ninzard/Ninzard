Catherine Avalon (born 14 July 2002), known as Catx3 or simply Cat, is a former Ninzard politician who served as the 3rd President of the Ninzard Project from 20 July 2022 to 19 August 2025. The younger sister of Eliza, one of the community's founders, she led the Disco Party and its Meowsalute movement. Her administration is remembered as the Catx3 Regime.1

Cat came to power when Apollos resigned moments before the polls closed in July 2022, leaving her, the runner-up, as president.2 Re-elected with 66.6% of the vote in January 2023, she built a personality cult around a network of paid loyalists, and on 1 January 2024 abolished elections in Ninzardia altogether.3

A 26-month counterintelligence operation led by General Kay exposed the full extent of her administration's abuses. Her presidency ended on 19 August 2025, when Ninzardia's servers were wiped, and she was arrested in the United Kingdom three days later.4 The Charter of the Ninzard Project, ratified in May 2026, was written in large part to make sure nothing like her presidency could happen again.5

Early life

Avalon was born on 14 July 2002, three years after her sister, Elizabeth “Eliza” Avalon, one of the First Triumvirate who founded Ninzard. Accounts of her childhood describe a girl who felt constantly measured against her sister's academic success, a rivalry later seen as the root of her contempt for the community Eliza had helped build.6

Early Ninzard career (2014–2022)

Hybriden and the Underground

Cat spent her early years in Hybriden, where she grew close to the 2014 presidential nominee Alexa. When Apollos won the 2016 election, she privately dismissed the whole community:7

「All pointless. Only saddos take this shit seriously. It's fun when nobody cares.」

— Cat, in a private message on the 2016 presidential election

After the upheavals of 2018 she moved to Scope, watched the Third Faction from its edges, and drifted into the unofficial servers of the Underground, where she built a reputation in the OuterBand districts.7

Caffaele

In October 2020, while rebuilding Hybriden, Raffaele sought her out, impressed by her intelligence and her command of several languages, and was surprised to learn she was Eliza's sister.8 The two met in person over Christmas 2021 and became a couple known as “Caffaele.” Unknown to him, she was already using bribes to tilt elections, including the 2021 vice-presidential race.8 Over the following years her payments reached figures across the community, from district generals to party operatives.9

The Disco Party and Catsland

In June 2022 Cat quietly turned the Disconic Party against its leader, Vesper. He called a leadership vote expecting a fair contest and watched every member vote for her; after her election as president she renamed it the Disco Party. On 17 June 2022 Governor Rony approved the creation of Catsland, and Cat was unanimously elected its first District General. The district, with its Japanese-language culture and a loyalty pledge ending in “Meowsalute! :3,” became the base of her movement.10

2022 presidential election

Believing Apollos unbeatable, Raffaele designed a “silver medal strategy” that cast Cat as the president's natural complement under approval voting. Cat had learned of Apollos's plan to resign from Vice President Snowby, and kept it from Raffaele.11 On 19 July 2022 Apollos posted his resignation moments before the polls closed. With 287 of 490 approvals (58.6%), Cat was the runner-up and was seated as the 3rd President the next day.2

Presidency (2022–2025)

Early presidency

Cat opened with a crackdown on alternate accounts in the name of “safer and fairer elections,” presenting herself as a democrat.12 Raffaele served as her First Gentleman and closest advisor.13 When General Geo of Main called her a “wannabe dictator” in August 2022, she answered with a Breaking Bad parody that her Meowist supporters adopted as a slogan:12

I am not in danger, Nightwork. I AM THE DANGER. A girl opens her door and gets hacked, and you think that of me? No, I AM THE ONE WHO HACKS!

— President Cat, August 2022

Within months Geo, the most prominent Preservate in Nightwork, had been brought down by a scandal Cat engineered.14

2023 re-election

In January 2023 Cat defeated former Vice President Hokan of the Original Party with 537 of 806 votes (66.6%), the largest raw tally the Project had yet seen, carrying every district except Capitalia.15 Snowby, re-elected as vice president, outperformed her by eight points, which irritated her.16

Resistance in Nightwork

Nightwork, led from January 2023 by Governor Adelynn, became the centre of resistance. When Apollos unmasked himself on 1 April 2023 as General Assailant of Waffle, a persona Cat had helped him build, she claimed she had known all along and publicly endorsed Adelynn's new Sponsorship Standard.17 On 17 June 2023 she opened a new district, Lair, under Blithem.18

The vice-presidential election of 4 July 2023, the last ever held, pitted her candidate Meikaqu against Spinzle of the Bethany Alliance, a rare coalition of Ories and Preservates. Spinzle swept Nightwork, but Meikaqu won 578 to 557 on totals swollen by Catsland and Lair, whose votes came from accounts Cat and Meikaqu controlled. Cat mocked the result: “One does rather wonder who on earth that lone dissenting voice in Catsland could possibly have been!”19

Abolition of elections

On 1 January 2024 Cat announced that there would be no further elections anywhere in Ninzardia, calling the community's democracy “a nation roleplay, children pretending to be a country.”3

We fractured because of democracy, faithfully, on schedule, twice a year, for over a decade. So I am switching the machine off.

— President Cat, New Year's address, 1 January 2024

Governor Adelynn replied that Nightwork would keep voting: “A game governed by universally accepted rules holds greater value than a serious institution that is accountable to a single person.”20 On 1 January 2025 Nightwork, under Governor Aceiron, formally cut its ties with her regime in what became known as Nexit, stripping her of her claim to rule over a thousand members.21

Kay's Eyes

General Kay of Capitalia, known as “Mother Ninzard,” returned to office in May 2023. Cat could find no weakness to exploit in her, and privately came to fear her as a version of Eliza she could not outmatch. Over 26 months Kay quietly gathered 4.2 terabytes of evidence against the president.22

Fall

By mid-2025 Cat's paranoia was deepening and her payments to loyalists were faltering; allies such as Sundae, Kendall and Alaric abandoned her.23 Her presidency ended on 19 August 2025, when Ninzardia's servers were wiped out and her remaining leadership collapsed.4 For the first time in its history, Ninzard had no president.24

Arrest and the Cat Files

Cat fled, and on 22 August 2025 she was arrested in the United Kingdom at the request of U.S. authorities. Community members handed their evidence to law enforcement, and she remains imprisoned.4

During Apollos's second presidency the Capital held back the full record, known as the Cat Files, to protect victims and settle legal disputes, while Ories demanded its release. Poka, then Minister of Activity, cried when she was shown the evidence and helped distribute it to community leaders. The full files were released in January 2026, to widespread shock.25

Legacy

Former Vice Presidents Snowby and Meikaqu, once treated as accomplices, were later reclassified as hostages of a cycle of financial dependence and blackmail.1 The Charter of the Ninzard Project, ratified on 11 May 2026, was designed to permanently outlaw the Dark Arts and safeguard the community from the legacy of the Catx3 Regime.5

Her name became an insult in its own right: in April 2026 Poka branded General PoG “Cat 2.0 but with a baguette.”26 On 20 August 2026, the first anniversary of her fall, President Poka refused to commemorate the date, declaring in her Bathos Address that Cat did not belong on anyone's calendar.27
