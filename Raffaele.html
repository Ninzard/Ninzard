Raffaele Guglielmo Bellucci (born 4 October 2002), known as Raffaele or the Padrino, is an Italian-American Ninzard politician and hacker who has served as the 2nd Minister of Justice of the Ninzard Project since 2 July 2026, under President Poka. He was earlier the 3rd Capital Adjudicator under Apollos from 2017 to 2020, General of Hybriden from 2020 until its destruction in 2023, and the 4th Boss of the Original Party from 2023 to 2024.1,2

A member of Hybriden since 2013 and a student of the “Dark Arts” of hacking and psychological warfare, he became the protector of the community's young Italian cohort, his cousin PluPlu and Poka among them.3,1 As Capital Adjudicator he uncovered and later published the Watchmaker Files, which showed that the first president, Antareze, and the anarchist Orion1 were the same man.4,5 From 2020 he mentored and then dated Cat, whom he helped bring to the presidency in 2022; after discovering her crimes in 2023 he sacrificed his own district to expose her, and his testimony later helped keep her in custody.6,7,8

After years as a political outsider, he took part in drafting the Charter of the Ninzard Project in 2026, and his appointment to Poka's cabinet drew jokes about “Bellucci nepotism.”9,2

Early life

Bellucci was born on 4 October 2002 and grew up in New York City in a Catholic Italian-American family; his grandfather was born in Lazio, where his uncle and his younger cousin Chiara, later known as PluPlu, live.1,10,11 As a young man he went to church almost every week and to confession about once a month.12

Hybriden and the Dark Arts (2013–2017)

Student of the Dark Arts

Raffaele joined the District of Hybriden in 2013 and became a quiet intellectual fixture there, known for a calm manner and for preferring influence to the spotlight. He was promoted to staff under General Orion1, and when Apollos succeeded Orion1 at the start of 2016 he made Raffaele an administrator, a post he kept under General Alexa.1,13

On 13 November 2015 Orion1 hijacked the teenage Raffaele's computer, turned back the remote access trojan Raffaele had sent him and lectured him on discipline, becoming his mentor in what the community called the Dark Arts.4 He was also a friend of Hyperio, the leader of the war against Ninzard in 2015–2016, and taught the Hyperionites how to use such trojans properly.1

The penguin and the Padrino

By the age of fourteen Raffaele was running a small grey-market operation from his bedroom, mining cryptocurrency on strangers' computers and stealing gaming accounts with a fake “Club Penguin coin generator.” In November 2016 one of his victims was a ten-year-old girl in his own city, whose screenshots showed folders in Italian and an unsent plea to Disney Support. Unable to buy the account back, he opened a note on her screen, confessed, apologized and invited her to a TeamSpeak server, where she shouted at him under the name she had just chosen: Poka. He introduced her to his cousin Chiara, and the two girls' friendship founded the PluPlu Army that same afternoon.3

Shaken by what his code had done, Raffaele deleted his phishing tools and vowed never again to exploit the innocent, the vulnerable or the young, turning his skills only on those he thought “deserved it.” He became a “Padrino” (godfather) to PluPlu and Poka in Hybriden, speaking Italian with them in private channels, teaching them the community's politics and keeping strict admins from banning them. The PluPlu Army never turned on him, which later gave him a kind of diplomatic immunity in Main.3,1

The Alienova Party

After the December 2016 elections nearly wiped out the Alienova Party, its new leader Tiny2 kept it alive with Raffaele supplying much of its intellectual infrastructure.14 He was not an enthusiastic supporter of Apollos, although he had voted for him in 2016.1

Capital Adjudicator (2017–2020)

Appointment

After the Legislature removed MMC on 1 July 2017 and rejected four of Apollos's nominees in turn, Syn, Snuck, Spinzle and Kushay, leaving the post empty for almost two months, Apollos turned to Raffaele, whose intellect reassured the Resistance Party's “cultural gatekeepers” and who had the backing of Nightwork, the moderates and most of the Ories. On the fifth attempt the Legislature approved him by ten votes to three, with only Judah, Lless and Snuck against.15,1

Before accepting, Raffaele asked Apollos whether he saw appointing a rival as a threat to his own future. Apollos answered simply that he only “wished the community would get along.” The answer won Raffaele's lasting respect: he came to see Apollos as the “realest” person in Ninzard and promised to work to the same end. He took office on 26 August 2017. Tiny2 took his appointment as a defection and began insulting both men publicly, and Snuck taunted him with homophobic slurs and called him a “con artist” for his hacking.1

The Watchmaker Files and the Archives

In November 2017, while migrating old TeamSpeak permission logs to Discord for the president, Raffaele found a 2014 command that President Antareze had run during the Nightwork War. Its idiosyncratic syntax, with triple underscores between variables, and its compilation signature matched the script Orion1 had used against him in 2015: the “Watchmaker” and the anarchist “Enforcer” had been one man. He showed Apollos the proof in a voice call, and the two agreed to bury it in a password-locked folder they called the Watchmaker Files.4

Raffaele also compiled files on the other founders, and with Apollos established the community's official archive, intended in part to set the record straight about the founders.4,16

2018 gubernatorial campaign

At Apollos's suggestion, and with the endorsement of Tiny2, who had resigned from the party he led, Raffaele took over the Alienova Party and ran for Governor of Ninzardia in March 2018. Campaigning for “Ninzardian restoration,” he argued that the community had degraded into “third-rate trolling” and attacked the Resistance Party's Daniel as a “second-rate Gabriel.” His attacks briefly made the Alienova Party a credible contender, and Apollos endorsed him over his own party's nominee, MMC, but voters tired of the negativity and Daniel won with a plurality; Raffaele came second.17

After the deaths of 2018 left the community in crisis, Raffaele offered himself to the Legislature as Apollos's successor. Most legislators objected because of his closeness to Tiny2, and Spinzle warned that it would carry “the optics of dancing on his grave.”18 With Generals Kay and Advy and the administrator Rudolf he kept the Ninzardian Faction in order until it elected a governor that September, persuaded Apollos to keep Hybriden open, and introduced the young Seandry to the community's quieter leaders, among them the future members of the Channeling-4.19,20,21,22

The Era of Progression

When Apollos considered a “dream team” with Raffaele as vice president and Syn as Capital Adjudicator in 2019, Raffaele told him it would not last without a change in the community's culture, and Apollos reappointed Hokan instead.23 Raffaele went on to help Hokan run psychological operations against the OuterBand, persuading members to download trojans while the Capital stirred rival Underground factions against each other.24,25 In September 2019 he released a redacted dossier of the Watchmaker Files to the whole community, deliberately destroying the myth of the founder that the Underground had turned against the Capital.5

Raffaele came to despise Governor Spikester of Nightwork, a fellow Italian whose drug trade he documented, and quietly stirred Nightians against him.26 He told the governor on 27 February 2020, “You disgrace the name you carry,” and the movement he and Apollos encouraged soon left Spikester powerless.27

The Quorey operation

In late 2019 the Channeling-4 investigated Quorey, the leader of the Celestial Party, over a campaign of ransomware, extortion and abuse of young members. To breach his defences Raffaele used the thirteen-year-old Poka as bait in a honeypot, coaching her through months of messages and, in December 2019, a disguised program that gave him hidden access to Quorey's computer from New York. The operation brought Quorey down, but it left Poka exposed to disturbing material, and it became the act Raffaele most regretted.28,29

Resignation

Ashamed, burned out and disillusioned with the founders and with Hokan, Raffaele decided to step down after PluPlu, then eleven, learned what he had made her best friend do and screamed at him over the phone that it was evil. He resigned on 29 February 2020 and spent the following months making amends to the two girls, who forgave him.30 Seandry resented him for leaving at a critical moment.30

2020 election and the debate with Apollos

Raffaele remained leader of the Alienova Party, which Tiny2 had left to found the Celestial Party shortly before his death. He was seen as a viable presidential candidate in 2020 but refused to run, and instead hosted the contested nomination that made Czulia leader of the party's successor, the Revamp Party.31,32

In mid-June 2020 his remark that the community had destroyed its capacity for intellectual discourse, a failure he admitted sharing, led to a famous debate with Apollos, who was seeking a third term. Raffaele told the president he was in pain and should hand over the office; Apollos called him “Coddled Raf,” a centrist who held safe positions that were easy to defend.31

Raffaele: Reuben, there exists an audacity to suddenly show up and say, “whoops, I should not have let unchecked growth occur during my tenure.” These are principles you wear like costumes out of political convenience, and they're separate from admitting to your mistakes.

Apollos: no, you just happened to stay coddled in the middle lane with your safe political stances.

Raffaele: To ignore the transient emotions of the mob is what makes a disciplined leader. To guide the community through its pain while keeping it intact is what makes the greatest leader.

— The Apollos–Raffaele debate, June 2020

Members watching harassed Raffaele afterwards, and Seandry dismissed him as someone “who's had it easy.” Raffaele concluded that public debate was useless against populism: power was won in the backroom, not on the stage.31

General of Hybriden (2020–2023)

The “Italian Mafia district”

When Apollos re-established Hybriden after the 2020 election, Raffaele won its snap election in September 2020 as an independent and rebuilt it into a tight-knit district apart from the rest of the community. He trained a defence force in the Dark Arts to break up any hegemony forming in the post-Underground communities, and kept those factions divided and distracted for years. Hybriden became known as the community's “Italian Mafia district,” and his catchphrase, “This is how we do it in Roma,” entered Ninzard's vocabulary.33,12,34

Vice President Helen wanted to delete Hybriden and ban him, which provoked Apollos to declare that Raffaele was “NOT to be touched… ever.” His refusal to confirm or deny rumours of a secret D3SF force gave the small district unprecedented soft power, and the fear it inspired was one of the things that kept Governor Syn's Preservates from declaring war on Ninzardia.33,35 He was re-elected in August 2021.36

Syn: Your entire personality is stolen straight from Akita's Orion1. … Everything about you is stolen and LARP.

Raffaele: Your entire personality is counterphobia, to which you have named the Preservation Party.

Syn: Wrong. I make. You steal. I owned fear into conviction and power. You copy conviction from men because you have none.

Raffaele: Conviction that is born from fear will always serve fear in the end. Do not flatter yourself.

— Raffaele and Governor Syn, early 2021

Politically homeless

From 2020 to 2021 Raffaele belonged to no camp. He opposed the Preservates' indefinite lockdown of Nightwork but thought the Ories had stopped being “smart,” voting for Aceiron in October 2020 and then for Syn twice in 2021, the second time declaring that “the Ories have lost their minds.” He despised the Poo Party as “the purest form of anti-intellectual degradation” and came to believe that the community's gridlock needed a powerful “leviathan” to break it.37

Mentor to Cat

In October 2020 Raffaele sought out Cat, a polyglot with a bad reputation from the Underground, and was surprised to learn that she was the younger sister of the founder Eliza. He taught her and other Hybridians, among them PluPlu, Poka, Ploutos, Meikaqu and Snowby, how to divide hostile communities and turn them against each other, and he gave Cat psychological profiles of the community's leaders, an explanation of the weaknesses of its voting systems and the advice to present herself as a “coddled” centrist. His word made her trusted by leaders of every party and gave her access to the community's news outlets and its archive.34,38

They met in person over Christmas 2021, and on Christmas evening she kissed him. Their relationship, made public in Hybriden in the last days of the year, became known as “Caffaele”; Apollos acknowledged it with a single “🤨”. The couple also revealed that Eliza had once sought a relationship with Antareze.39,40

2022

Raffaele and Cat worked together to deepen the community's polarization in 2022. After the Ellie screenshot incident he reported Apollos to Discord with out-of-context messages, and the president was banned on 9 May. On 11 May he told General Geo he could “confirm” that the D3SF, led by Al Commando, was about to dox Geo's staff, while warning Apollos that Geo's NMAT planned a raid; the news outlets ran the same stories at once, and Geo declared war on BobaBase. Cat supplied the provocations and Raffaele the verification.41

Apollos grew suspicious, and when confronted Raffaele claimed to be “stress testing” the community's defences. Sensing that Apollos and Spinzle were closing in, he and Cat sacrificed Runix, Cat's agent on Spinzle's ticket, to deflect attention.42 In July Raffaele publicly called Apollos mentally unfit to run again and alluded to a secret Apollos had trusted him with; Apollos blocked him, and unblocked him only after Raffaele apologized.43 The remarks hit Apollos harder than he admitted, and he resigned during that month's election.44

Believing Apollos unbeatable, Raffaele managed Cat's campaign with a “silver medal strategy” that presented her as the president's natural complement under approval voting. Cat had learned of Apollos's coming resignation and kept it from him. Apollos won 340 approvals, Cat 287 and Al Commando 265, and when Apollos stepped down Cat became president on 20 July 2022.45,46

First Gentleman and Boss of the Ories (2022–2024)

The Bonnie and Clyde Capital

As Cat's partner Raffaele became the “First Gentleman” of Ninzard, and the two were partners in crime into early 2023. He spent most of his money flying between New York and England to see her, refusing to let her pay, and over Christmas 2022 met her family, who approved of him, and her sister Eliza.47 He was present, silent, on the call in which Cat humiliated Hokan over his debts, which drove Hokan into his doomed presidential campaign.48 When Hokan lost in January 2023, Cat decided that the First Gentleman should lead the Ories, and the Orie Apparatus accepted Raffaele as its Boss rather than let the party die.49

Breakup

In March 2023, suspecting that Cat was hiding crimes from him, Raffaele copied the hardware identifiers and file lists from her computer while she slept. They showed stalking orders against several people, among them Apollos. He confronted her in tears, listed every earlier betrayal of his trust, and ended the relationship. Cat told Apollos that Raffaele had left her out of jealousy.50

The Last Stand of Hybriden

From April to June 2023 Raffaele openly opposed his former partner, though many dismissed his warnings as heartbreak. When General Kay returned in May he gave her the data from Cat's computer, and they planned how to tie Cat to it.51 Raffaele built a dashboard for Hybriden's custom bot and, on 16 June 2023, told Cat it would publish fake recordings of Eliza at midnight unless she stopped it. Unable to reach it through her VPN, Cat dropped her protection for a few seconds, logged in from her home connection in Leeds and wiped Hybriden. The district vanished, but its server had already sent her real IP address to Raffaele, who passed it to Kay; it became the foundation of the investigation that brought Cat down.7,52

Cat abolished Hybriden and doxxed Raffaele. When he warned her to leave his family in Lazio alone, PluPlu revealed her own name to the whole community so that Cat could no longer hold it over him: “WE ARE BELLUCCIS!” Most of Hybriden's members, the young Italians among them, were taken into Main.53 Aceiron, then General of Scope, gave Raffaele refuge in his district.54

Christmas arson

On Christmas Day 2023 Raffaele's house was set on fire and left uninhabitable. The cause was ruled undetermined in early 2024, but he was certain it was Cat, and in 2025 he confirmed that she had paid others to do it. Living on a friend's couch in Queens, he came to see the fire as a divine chastisement for helping Cat to power, wired his savings to his uncle in Lazio to secure the family home, and told PluPlu the truth.11

Boss of the Original Party

As Boss, Raffaele kept the Ories from collapse but refused to run for governor himself for fear of exposing himself further to Cat.54 He tried to steer Aceiron, the party's rising star, toward a “rationalist” politics, but detested his aggressive “Caleb Treatment”: “This is not how we do it in Roma.” Pressured by the Orie Apparatus, he prepared to resign before the June 2024 elections, and Aceiron won the leadership.55,56

「You have the fire of a revolutionary, but you think like a vandal. You'd burn the house down to keep yourself warm.」

— Raffaele, to Aceiron, July 2024

Aceiron answered that Raffaele had brought Cat to power and that no confession would cleanse him; Raffaele answered with scripture, and Aceiron removed him from the Orie Apparatus. Too “Orie” for the Preservates and too “elitist” for the Ories, he remained politically homeless, though he still called himself an Orie and voted for Aceiron in December 2024.56

The fall of Cat (2025)

After Cat's arrest Raffaele gave a sworn affidavit on the arson and her cyberstalking, which helped establish the pattern of violence used to deny her bail in the United Kingdom.8 In his Reflections he refused to claim he had been deceived: he had seen what she was, he wrote, and had taught her anyway because “her cruelty gave me a thrill that decency never could,” calling the fire “il contrappasso.”10

In the September 2025 presidential election he publicly backed Aceiron while quietly persuading voters in Scope and Main, where his Italian connections ran deep, to support Apollos's comeback.57,58 He and Apollos kept what the archive calls an “omertà” bond: loyal to each other even on opposite sides, if rarely close.59

Legislature XI and the Charter (2026)

Representative for Scope

Raffaele sat for Scope as an Orie in Legislature XI, which drafted the Charter of the Ninzard Project.60,61 When Al Commando lamented what had become of the Ories, Raffaele answered, “I did not leave the Orie Party; the Orie Party left me.”62 After the Chud War of 14 February 2026 he told NGin, Aceiron and the Liberators that their silence was not honour but shared criminal liability: “Take the Charter, or take the handcuffs.”63

On 24 February 2026 he uncovered NGin's plan to seize the Orie Apparatus that night with a “Samson Switch” threatening to have Discord delete the whole community. He warned the Orie leadership, kept NGin negotiating until morning while the party rebuilt its server elsewhere, and with the Capital rerouted the script into a dead end. At 5:17 AM he told the whole community in a voice memo that the “hostage situation” was over.64

The limitation clause

In the debate on Part I of the Charter Raffaele argued against beginning with a list of “inviolable rights”: “the absolute freedom of the wolf is the absolute death of the sheep.” He proposed instead a limitation clause putting the community's survival first, which Adelynn and others came to support, and with AJ and ScottyAte he persuaded Aceiron to vote for the revised text, which passed 15 to 0.9

The Ninzard Project guarantees the rights and freedoms set out in this Charter, subject only to such reasonable limits as can be demonstrably justified to ensure the physical, legal, and psychological survival of the community.

— Raffaele, proposing the Charter's limitation clause, 2026

On 30 March 2026, after Aceiron was hospitalized with a fractured skull and Snuck was arrested, Raffaele resigned his seat to stand by Snuck's family, and he later helped the Capital pay for Snuck's defence lawyer.65,66

The Holloway Summit and the Five Kings

On 1 May 2026 he offered every American member $50 for writing a résumé, a bounty Apollos announced for the Ninzard Career Initiative, and offered NGin alone $500 for one, an offer that “will never be $501.”67,68 He arrived at the Holloway Summit of 2 May with a box of pastries and offered NGin an espresso, which NGin knocked from his hand; afterwards Apollos invited him and Aceiron, two long-time Ories, into the Preservate Apparatus. That night he sat on the patio with Elusion and talked Meikaqu through her questions about God and Cat.69,70,71

On 8 May Apollos and Syn drove to Seattle's airport to collect Raffaele, who had secretly arranged for Snuck and Aceiron to meet them there: “This is our Roman custom boys.” The reunion became known as the Five Kings meetup, and that evening he posted their group selfie against the Space Needle.72,73 He gave the closing prayer at the memorial for Liam Higgins on 23 May.74 On 1 June he chaired a meeting in San Jose with Meikaqu, Hackman, Poka and ScottyAte on threats against members, insisting that the evidence be preserved and handed to investigators and that “nobody improvises.”75 At the end of June he joined Poka in Vancouver for the Succession Accords, the informal handover from Apollos.76

Minister of Justice (2026–present)

Poka named Raffaele the 2nd Minister of Justice, succeeding Kay, alongside his cousin PluPlu as Minister of Activity. Rivals spoke of “Bellucci nepotism,” but most of the community backed the choice: Poka called him the ideal transitional figure for moving on from the old guard, and the one best equipped to handle the lingering threat from Scalen.2

Personal life and views

Raffaele is 6 ft 1 in tall and has described himself as gaunt and “ghoulish.” He earns a good income working from home and as a cryptocurrency investor, though he has admitted to spending money freely. By 2021 he had left his family's home for what he called his “fortress” in New York, the house that burned in 2023.39,12,47 A practising Catholic, he once believed his faith set him morally above the “mob” he scammed, a belief he later called “the pride that ruined me”; after the fire he came to see protecting his family as a penance.12,11 In 2022 he set himself up as the community's “neutral arbiter” of intelligence, and members have counted his ability to shift narratives as part of a Ninzard “deep state.”41,77
