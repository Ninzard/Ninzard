Kendall Laramie (born 14 December 2002), known as Kennie, is a former Ninzard politician who served as the 5th District General of Waffle in 2023 and as the last Deputy Governor of Ninzardia, under Governor Sundaze, from 12 April 2024 until the faction was dissolved on 19 August 2025. A childhood friend of Spinzle from Colorado, she was one of the eight known members of the Edith Gang.1,2,3,4,5

She ran for vice president in 2020 as the running mate of Clenzy of the Celestial Party, winning 7.3% of the vote.6 After she fell out with Spinzle over Soda, Waffle elected her for the Original Party in the snap election that followed Apollos's unmasking as General Assailant in April 2023. That July she lost the race for Governor of Nightwork to Adelynn by 480 votes to 160, at the time the largest landslide in any governor's race in the community's history.1,2,7

After the defeat she went over to President Cat, who had been secretly paying her and whose ledger lists her as the sixth-largest recipient of her money. With the rest of the Edith Gang except Aurase, she cut Spinzle off in November 2024. After Cat's fall she was indicted for conspiracy to commit wire fraud, and in February 2026 she was permanently banned from the Ninzard Project.8,9,10,11,12

Early life

Laramie was born on 14 December 2002.1 As children in Colorado she, Spinzle, Snowby and Holiday formed a small circle of their own, in which Kennie was the protector: hot-tempered, she stood up to anyone who tried to harm or mock Spinzle or the others.13 Snowby's older sister Abby noticed that the girls were being bullied, and when Abby brought the Colorado cohort into Ninzard in 2012 as the Solorado legion, they came with her.14

The deaths of Tiny2 and Holiday in 2018 devastated the Coloradan members who had been close to Holiday. Kennie struggled to cope with the loss and began taking more risks.15 When Spinzle left the community that summer, she cut herself off from her friends, Kennie among them.16

Vice-presidential campaign (2020)

In the 2020 presidential election Kennie was the running mate of Clenzy, the Celestial Party's nominee. At the vice-presidential debate on 10 August 2020, against Vice President Helen and Idodo of the Revamp Party, she accused Apollos and Helen of using the D3SF, a secret force of hackers that existed only in a conspiracy theory, to wage RAT wars on the OuterBand and clean up the Underground.17,18 Helen laughed, and Idodo questioned the absurdity of the claim. Kennie replied that the Celestial Party was simply open about its aim of using remote access trojans to win hegemony, while the Ories pursued the same goal behind a veneer of morality, and that RAT hegemony was the only way to take control of the Underground. She then exclaimed that the Capital had “killed Tiny2 and Holidae,” drawing an extreme reaction from the audience.17

Apollos was re-elected on 12 August with 60.3% of the vote. The Clenzy–Kennie ticket won 51 of 697 votes (7.3%), 46 of them in the three OuterBand districts.19,6,20,21

Rift with Spinzle (2022)

When Spinzle became Governor of Nightwork in July 2022, Soda, another member of the Edith Gang, succeeded her as General of Scope. Pressed into expansionist policies that Scope's members hated, he became deeply unpopular, and Roland, the Poopulist Governor of Ninzardia, criticized him, souring relations with Soda's friends, Kennie among them.22 That summer she was also among the Ories who sought the party's presidential nomination.23

Cat used her influence over Roland and the Ninzardian leadership to attack Soda in public, and Apollos joined in, calling him “unfit for command.” Spinzle neither fired nor defended him. Kennie, Soda's fiercest defender, saw her silence as cowardice. Cat offered Kennie the emotional validation and “unconditional loyalty” that Spinzle would not, and Kennie, feeling abandoned by her friend, began a slow drift toward the Cat Capital.1

General of Waffle (2023)

At midnight on 1 April 2023 Waffle's well-liked General Assailant revealed himself as former President Apollos, and Governor Adelynn banned him for impersonation. Kennie, who had joined Waffle and already held a grudge against Apollos over the attacks on Soda, rallied the angry district in the snap election that followed and won as the Original Party's nominee.2

Her three months in office went badly. Waffle's members grew frustrated with her drama-dominated leadership and increasingly suspicious of her, and she damaged the friendly, wholesome atmosphere Assailant had built and, with it, the Original Party's standing in the district.24,25 Her run for governor made her ineligible to stand for general again. In July 2023 the seat went to ViaTea, an Orie who presented herself as a principled moderate and a critic of Cat, by 67 votes to 55.24

Race for governor (2023)

The Original Party nominated Kennie to challenge Adelynn in the gubernatorial election of 4 July 2023.26 The Orie Apparatus asked her not to campaign aggressively, so as not to damage the Bethany Alliance's efforts against Cat; she ignored the request. She portrayed Adelynn as naive for failing to see through Assailant, an argument that backfired, because Waffle had been created under the Orie governor Spinzle, who had been fooled as well.7

Her feud with Roland, which began when he insulted Soda's leadership, also hurt her. In February 2023 it drew in Snowby, who was thrown out of BobaBase after an argument with Roland, and by late March the exchanges had turned vicious; Kennie and Snowby answered with false accusations against him. The community was left exhausted by the drama.7 Although Kennie insulted Cat in public, she still took part in Cat's events and stayed close to her circle, including her best friend Sundaze and Snowby, Cat's vice president. The later discovery that Cat had been paying her confirmed what many suspected.7

Adelynn reached out repeatedly to ask for Kennie's views and to arrange a debate. Kennie turned down every request and accused the governor of having a “saviour complex.”7 Even Molly, the Preservate General of Volley, who often clashed with Adelynn, endorsed the governor, believing Kennie significantly worse.24

Adelynn won 480 of the 640 votes (75.0%) to Kennie's 160 (25.0%), carrying all five districts, at the time the largest landslide in any governor's race in the community's history. Kennie did best in Parry, with 39.0%, and worst in Main, with 8.3%.7,27

Ninzardia (2023–2025)

Kennie took the defeat bitterly. Cat offered her more money and better prospects if she abandoned the Nightian Faction altogether, declared herself out and committed fully to the Ninzardian Faction, and she accepted.8 By September 2023 she was one of the “goons” on Cat's payroll, whom Cat used for tasks such as sending friend requests to test whether someone was really a Ninzard.28,29

Deputy Governor

On 12 April 2024 Cat appointed her Deputy Governor of Ninzardia under Sundaze, in place of Toby. She was the last person to hold the office.3,30 By then Cat and Sundaze had made Ninzardia a one-party faction whose referendums were rigged.30

That year Spinzle, investigating Cat, spent fourteen minutes inside Cat's Discord account and found evidence of money laundering tied to Snowby and Kennie.31 Her two childhood friends' involvement with Cat was one reason Spinzle stayed in the community, and she was troubled to see them still spiraling.32,31

On 20 November 2024, swayed by audio Cat had leaked and by Snowby's account of events, the Edith Gang turned on Spinzle. Kennie, Soda and Pieban joined an ultimatum accusing her of reckless endangerment and toxic obsession over the Dead Man's Switch she had set to expose Cat, and one by one they blocked her on every platform. Only Aurase refused to take part.10

That winter Kennie and Sundaze used mutual acquaintances and alternate accounts to drag Aceiron and the Ories into private group chats to stir up drama. Aceiron, suspecting a plot by Cat, mostly refused to engage and blocked whoever added him. The “Calebros” harassed the two women, and Sundaze brought in members of Cat's Meowsalute Cult in return; Cat encouraged the conflict.33

Break with Cat

In 2025 Meikaqu privately warned Kennie and Sundaze that only downfall awaited and that they should cover their tracks, and the two began deleting traces of their work for Cat. When they grew absent, Cat cut their wages, and by July she had stopped paying them altogether. By then they were finished with her.34,35

Cat's ledger for March 2022 to April 2024, recovered after her fall, lists Kennie sixth among the recipients of her money, with $24,618 accepted and $1,492 refused. By Meikaqu's account, Cat had come to regard her loyalists, Kennie among them, as “trash.”36,9,29

Indictment and ban (2025–2026)

The deletion of Ninzardia's platforms on 19 August 2025 ended Cat's presidency and Kennie's term as deputy governor.37,4 Nightians ostracized her, along with Snowby, Pieban, Sundaze and Alaric, and demanded that they all be removed from the community for good.4

Federal agents came to her off-campus apartment at 7 a.m. and handed her an indictment for conspiracy to commit wire fraud. She had convinced herself that she had scrubbed her digital footprint, and she insisted to the agents that she was “just a Discord moderator.” They seized her electronics and drove her away in an unmarked car.11

Her plea agreement took six months to finalize. The conditions of her federal probation, which bar her from unmonitored social platforms, took effect in early February 2026, and she was then permanently banned from the Ninzard Project. On 19 February Minister of Justice Kay announced the bans.12

「Mary, Pierre, Alaric, Sundae, and Kendall were permanently banned from the Ninzard Project. … The justice system does not operate on Discord time. … Bans were carried out the moment their legal status was finalized. They are gone.」

— Minister of Justice Kay, 19 February 2026

Personal life

Kennie's best friend in Ninzard was Sundaze, and she was close to Snowby.7 By 2025 she lived in an off-campus apartment.11 By the time Spinzle, by then very frail, reconnected with Apollos in 2025, Kennie and Snowby had stopped concerning themselves with her.38
