Madelyn Schroeder (born 27 December 2003), known as Cyntaku, is a Ninzard politician who has served as General of Volley since 22 November 2025. One of the three founders of the Preservation Party, she was the 6th Governor of Nightwork from 7 January to 10 July 2022, and before that the 4th Deputy Governor under Syn.1,2,3,4,5

Drawn into Ninzard's world at the age of ten, Cyntaku was among Volley's first members and in January 2019 was elected its 4th general as an independent.6,7,8 As Syn's deputy she helped build Nightwork into a fortress against the Underground, and on 1 October 2020 founded the Preservation Party with him and Grace.8,2 Her governorship, which earned her the name “the Iron Lady,” was marked by the Nightian-Ninzardian Assembly, the pardon of Helen and the May Panic, and ended in a landslide defeat by Spinzle.9,10,11,12

Out of office she rebuilt her life with the help of Grace and Adelynn, and opposed Apollos's return in 2025 before reconciling with him.13,14,15 Recruited by Apollos after Mel's removal, she won Volley from the far-right NGin in the “Battle of the Gingers” of November 2025 and has been re-elected twice since.16,1,17,18 She is one of the contributors to the community's archival record, where she wrote her own account of her life.19,20

Early life

Schroeder was born on 27 December 2003 and grew up in Wisconsin. Often ill as a child, she was teased at school after a classmate misspelled her name as “Madie” on a birthday card and others noticed it could be read as “may die”; her red hair also set her apart. Her family later moved to California, where she started fifth grade, and on her tenth birthday her father gave her a laptop of her own, which became her refuge.6 She lives in Sacramento.15

Early years in Ninzard (2014–2018)

Hyperion

An older neighbour, Hyperio, invited her into his Hyperion TeamSpeak server, then at war with Nightwork. The youngest member and probably the only girl, she was used for her voice, reading lines he wrote to Nightwork's members. When Hyperion became part of the Ninzard Project in June 2015 she followed, and through the Civil War of 2015–2016 she was kept out of the violence, which she later credited with keeping her safe.6,21

Volley

When Antareze and Governor Elusion created Volley for the former Hyperion members on 19 March 2016, Cyntaku followed an older friend from her neighbourhood, Jordan, known as OffBeat, who was elected its general. She joined the Resistance Party because Jordan was in it, and in 2016 backed Gabriel for president; after Apollos won, he messaged her: “Told you your guy sucked.”21,22 When Jordan let her real name slip in a call, others spelled it the old way, and Cyntaku adopted “Madie” as her own.22 Jordan lost Volley in December 2016 and later left the community, but Cyntaku stayed through the deaths of 2018, reasoning that it was “better to be a hostage in a war zone than a ghost in my own life.”22,23

General of Volley (2019–2020)

Alarmed by Governor Spikester's “Progression,” which opened the community to the Underground, Cyntaku ran for General of Volley as an independent in January 2019 on what she called a platform of “leave me alone.” She defeated AJ of the Original Party and took office on 15 January 2019, at fifteen.7,24,8 She became the wall against the Underground in Volley, and found an ally in Syn, newly elected General of Main, who shared her hatred of it.8 Once quiet, she grew more sociable and earned a reputation as friendly and down-to-earth, and was re-elected in June and December 2019.24,25,26

Deputy Governor of Nightwork (2020–2022)

Syn Administration

After Spikester was impeached in February 2020, Syn asked Cyntaku to run with him. Both independents and staunch opponents of expansion, they won, and she took office as the 4th Deputy Governor of Nightwork on 1 March 2020; Lesty succeeded her in Volley.5,8 She described the partnership as “the Bouncer and the Architect”: Syn confronted their enemies while she wrote the policies that kept them out. They imposed strict borders and identity checks, and she and Syn personally reviewed new members.8,27

The Preservation Party

On 1 October 2020 she founded the Preservation Party with Syn and Grace.2 In that month's elections she championed Starlight for Scope and Char for Volley over Syn's objections; Starlight won on Syn's tiebreak.28 She was amused by the following Syn had attracted among female members, teased him about it, and encouraged them.29 With Syn she negotiated with Governor Clexas of Ninzardia, who disliked what she saw as the Preservate leaders' obsessive roleplay, to ease the barriers between the two factions.30

Syn and Cyntaku were re-elected in October 2020, January 2021 and May 2021. She recalled that their margin in May was a single vote, and that it radicalized her: she concluded that the community had to be governed “with force.”31,27,32,8

Governor of Nightwork (2022)

Election

When Syn announced on 4 January 2022 that he would resign, the Preservation Party nominated his deputy, “the Iron Lady,” and he endorsed her. She surprised many by choosing Adelynn, an administrator in Main, as her running mate. On 7 January she won 51% of the vote to 25% for Aceiron and 24% for the Poopulist Runix.3 Apollos, who had endorsed Runix, nonetheless praised her efforts to moderate her tone.33

Administration

Cyntaku softened Syn's hardline stance, opening some districts under oversight, but refused Apollos's request to lift the ban on Ninzardians entering Nightwork, citing their harassment of its members.34 Minutes after the district generals voted to expel Helen in February, she announced the Nightian-Ninzardian Assembly as a new governing body, with the governor breaking ties; Adelynn warned that without a mandate it would be seen as arbitrary. With her casting vote the Assembly passed an Anti-Bullying Amendment letting the governor override district rules, which Spinzle obeyed and Parry refused; a community-wide version failed.9 At Spinzle's suggestion she also introduced voter registration with personal voting PINs.35

When Volley's general resigned in March, Cyntaku endorsed Molly, an administrator from Main, and put the snap election to all of Nightwork. On 16 March it ended in a 155–155 tie and she declared Molly the winner, but Volley's members, the other generals and the Capital objected, and Mel took the post instead. Convinced the community was plotting against her, she sharpened her rhetoric, and with General Geo created NightlyNews, a sharply partisan news outlet.36

Cat

Cat, who found Cyntaku easy to provoke with crafted narratives, cultivated a close relationship with her and fed her fears.37,38 In March 2022 Cat offered her $479. Though she was broke, she refused (“Keep it,” by her own account), and Cat's manner turned cold.37,39 Cat's ledger lists the sum as refused.40

The pardon and the May Panic

On 24 April 2022 Cyntaku and General Geo pardoned Helen and readmitted her to Main without consulting the other generals or the Capital. The Assembly, convened at the opposition's demand, voted against her; Cyntaku declared it “contaminated beyond a chance of restitution” and had Adelynn dissolve it.35,10 She later wrote that she had pardoned Helen because she terrified her enemies, and that it destroyed her credibility overnight.39

Her paranoia deepened. She accepted a false rumour against Governor Rony without checking it, later apologizing to him directly,41 and dismissed mounting evidence against the anti-troll squad in Main as fake.42 When Apollos and several Poopulists were banned from Discord on 9 May 2022, she banned Poopulist-aligned Nightians, saying she was protecting Nightwork from the same fate.11 Yet when Runix leaked Spinzle's campaign plans to her, she told Spinzle, saying she wanted to “play fair.”43

Defeat

Even Adelynn considered leaving the re-election ticket.44 The only debate against Spinzle was regarded as a public execution of the incumbent.45

If you are expecting a community that is filled with peace, sunshine, and rainbows, Edith is unable to deliver that promise. Do you know why? Because a spotless community is a mere idealism.

— Governor Cyntaku, debating Spinzle, July 2022

She lost with 114 of 344 votes (33.1%), carrying only her home district of Volley. Claiming suspicious voting elsewhere, she at first refused to step down, and handed over power a day late, on 10 July 2022.12,4 On 30 July Grace succeeded her as leader of the party.46

Out of office (2022–2025)

Cyntaku withdrew to the back of the community and watched Cat's rise, Geo's breakdown and Spinzle's governorship with what she called vindication mixed with horror.47 In the quiet of Adelynn's governorship she became close friends with Grace and Adelynn, who urged her to log off. She began walking daily, and in spring 2024 took up volleyball, the sport of her old friend Jordan.13

After Cat's fall in August 2025 Cyntaku was one of the strongest opponents of bringing back Apollos.14

Let him back? Not just back, but as our presidential nominee? Are we collectively concussed?

— Former Governor Cyntaku, to the Preservate Apparatus, 22 August 2025

She agreed reluctantly once the rest of the Blue Quartet came round, but objected to Spinzle as his running mate, believing she “lacked a soul.”14,48 In late October, at the height of his controlled burn, she apologized to him: “I had the wrong read on you, and I let that shape how I treated you.”15

General of Volley (2025–present)

Battle of the Gingers

When General Mel banned thirty members of Volley in November 2025, Cyntaku gathered declarations for her impeachment, and on 13 November Volley removed Mel by 100 votes to 23.49 With NGin of the far-right Holy Iron Flank the frontrunner, Apollos asked her to run as Volley's “native daughter,” and she agreed.16 The snap election of 21 November was dubbed the “Battle of the Gingers,” since both candidates had red hair. Cyntaku won 71 votes (56.8%) to NGin's 46 (36.8%) and 8 for Molly; she was asleep when the result came in.1

She took office on 22 November without logging in, leaving Kay and 7orB to carry out the handover so that her victory would be a non-event.50 The next day she issued her first statement.50

The “cleansing” is not happening. I'm not banning the opposition. Making martyrs out of you is boring, and I'm not giving you the satisfaction of a “shadow war.” You may stay. … Disagreement is allowed. Cowardice isn't.

— General Cyntaku, to Volley, 23 November 2025

Re-election

Cyntaku set no Thanksgiving curfew, but took a break from the server herself to test her staff.51 In December Aceiron's friend AJ challenged her. NGin endorsed her with a racist attack on AJ; she disavowed the endorsement and muted NGin for a week. Her honour-based approach to rules calmed the district, though extremism remained, and ViaTea's charge that she was courting racists was widely dismissed as performative.52 Liberation Party raiders wiped out Volley's channels on the eve of the vote.53 She won 86 of 129 votes (66.7%).17

2026

When the Cat Files were released in January 2026, she wrote of how Cat had stoked her fears in 2022.54

I spent so many nights convinced I was the only person seeing the “truth,” thinking I was fighting some grand conspiracy against the Capital. I let that paranoia turn me into someone I hated.

— General Cyntaku, 14 January 2026

She opposed admitting General Kaimachi to the Preservate Apparatus, telling Spinzle she had “earned the right to be paranoid” before apologizing for snapping.55 As the Chud War broke out in Sacramento on 14 February 2026, she warned Volley's members that anyone who hurt someone would face lasting consequences, and asked the Capital to call the police.56,57 Two days later she rebuked General PoG for treating it as distant “Discord lore.”58 She wanted NGin banned after his followers staged the Samson Coup in Volley while she was offline, but Apollos refused.59

In an exchange with AJ over the Charter, she defended its Section 3 as “the literal deadbolt” keeping the Capital out of the districts.60 After Adelynn was injured in March, Cyntaku stood for Boss as the “conservative” choice, promising to stay only until the end of Apollos's term, but Woofer won with 29 of 36 votes; Cyntaku joined the party's twelve core insiders.61,62 On 1 April she settled a feud between Scope and Volley with General Hyperio at a public “April Fools' Peace Summit.”63 When PoG claimed on 25 April that she had given Geo his power, Cyntaku answered furiously, protesting to her party that she had been eighteen and dealing with a general on Cat's payroll, until Grace told her to stop typing.64

On 2 May she flew down from Northern California for the Holloway Summit, and in Grace's car sat down beside a Sacramento member who had been her bitter enemy for two years, offering a soft, slightly awkward smile.65 At the end of May she warned that pairing Woofer with another technocrat would let the Ories paint the party as “the boring, sterile bureaucracy,” and the leadership nominated Poka, whom Cyntaku endorsed fervently in Volley.66,67 On 22 June 2026 she was re-elected with 75 of 130 votes (57.7%) over Chud Alessandro, and gave him and NGin a platform in Volley for their male-centred events.18 When Parry descended into the Salon War that August, she was among the Preservates who urged the Capital to intervene, and President Poka overruled them.68

Personal life

With Adelynn, Grace and Woofer, Cyntaku is one of the Blue Quartet. The other three live near one another around Los Angeles, and she saved money and time to visit them from Sacramento; she regards them as among the few people she can be genuine with. Within the group she is the one who most often makes decisions and takes the initiative, and she is aware of her tendency to anxiety.15 Volleyball keeps her grounded.15,13

She has contributed to the community's archival record, to Sections IX, XI–XIII, XVI–XVII and XIX–XX, and wrote a first-person account of her life for Phase IV.19,20

Political style

As deputy and governor Cyntaku was the architect of Nightwork's isolationist Preservate rule, and her governorship came to be seen as authoritarian; she has acknowledged that the Anti-Bullying Amendment was a tool to silence the opposition.39,69 Her opponents mocked her as paranoid, and the archive attributes many of her actions in 2022 to that paranoia, which Cat exploited.37,35 As General of Volley she has governed through an honour-based system rather than bans, and her writing returns again and again to the need to log off and live offline.52,18 Reflecting on her past, she observed in late 2025 that “it's one thing to have an ego and quite another to rely on it.”70
