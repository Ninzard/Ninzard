Anaïs Lefebvre (born 12 June 2006), known as Power of Greed or PoG, is a French Ninzard politician who has served as Boss of the Original Party since 22 February 2026. She was the 10th General of Parry from 1 January to 2 July 2026, the only opposition candidate to win a district in the Preservates' landslide of December 2025, and led the Liberation Party until it dissolved after the Chud War.1,2,3

A member since 2017, when she joined Apollos's Third Faction from Paris at the age of eleven, she spent her early years as a quiet observer, founding the Grass Party and twice turning down Cat's money, most famously over dinner in Paris in June 2025.4,5,6 Known as “the Spider,” she became the face of the opposition to the Charter of the Ninzard Project, preaching a “Romantic Resistance” that defended passion and conflict against the Capital's pursuit of safety, and turned Parry into a salon nicknamed “Parisian Parry.”4,7,8 She lost Parry to Kaimachi by two votes in June 2026, but remains Boss of the Ories.3,9

Early life

Lefebvre was born in Paris on 12 June 2006, sharing a birthday with Spinzle, who is three years older. Her parents, a diplomat and a corporate executive, raised her in a highly structured household of piano lessons, international schools and high expectations. An attention difficulty that went undiagnosed until adulthood was treated at school as a lack of discipline, and the archive describes her life in Paris as one of “zero friction, zero danger, and zero dopamine.”4 She went on to study literature and philosophy, interests that shaped her politics.4

Early Ninzard career (2017–2025)

The Third Faction and the Underground

Lefebvre discovered Ninzard while playing Minecraft in 2017, at the age of eleven, and joined the Third Faction, where she met a thirteen-year-old Apollos. Its unpolished, chaotic energy gave her the stimulation her life in Paris lacked, though her parents repeatedly cut off her internet access, forcing her into several hiatuses.4 When the community fractured into the Underground Era she ventured deeper, watching figures such as Spikester and Cat at work, but she stayed at its edges and never committed to its toxicity, finding its “edgelords” boring and unoriginal.4

The Grass Party

She founded the Grass Party as a vantage point from which to watch the community as a cynical, untouchable observer, and transferred to Nightwork-Parry before Syn's lockdown of Nightwork in 2020.4 From the background she fuelled populist narratives and grassroots campaigns that gave a voice to ordinary members. The party's name stood both for those grassroots and for the need to “touch grass”: she held that the community could only last if it found activity beyond Discord, bringing positive, romantic passions into Ninzard in a sustainable way.5

Turning down Cat

On 14 February 2022 Cat, who was then quietly buying influence across Nightwork, offered the leader of the Grass Party €1,000 for her backing. PoG turned the money down, disliking that Cat sought to buy her support rather than earn it.5

By 2025 she had dissolved the Grass Party, abandoned her grassroots campaigns and retreated to a mid-level administrative post in Parry under General Rudolf. A non-partisan who belonged neither to Cat's opposition nor to her loyalists, she stood outside the surveillance that General Kay had built around the president.6 On 28 June 2025, desperate to escape it, Cat met her at an exclusive restaurant in Paris, with Meikaqu at her side. She offered PoG an encrypted drive said to hold the private journals of her sister Eliza, the founder whose story PoG longed to know; access to her bot networks and blackmail files; a 60% cut of her fortune; and the presidency itself, if PoG would use European banking connections to launder the rest and let her vanish. The archive judges that had PoG accepted, Cat would probably have evaded prison.6 PoG told her she “preferred the mystery” of Eliza, paid for her own wine and left without looking back.6

「Ma chérie... there is no romance in what you do. You are a sweaty little girl running from consequences... I will not save you.」

— PoG, to Cat, Paris, 28 June 2025

The Liberator (2025)

Spider of Paris

On the evening of 3 November 2025, days after Boygho won Parry's snap election and Apollos broke with Snuck, PoG asked the president for a private call, parts of which she recorded. In a slow, breathless French accent she described Snuck as a man “throwing stones at his family” whose rampage had made the president's party weak, then offered to run in Parry that December as a Preservate. Apollos, flustered and amused, declined, but marked her as a highly intelligent operator who was quietly building ties across the political spectrum.10 The archive calls her Nightwork's spider: a player who weaponized intimacy and flattery, signalled by her spider avatar, to draw members into her web and take what she needed from them.10

Candidacy

In late November 2025, when the PluPlu Army stormed a Minecraft server and wrecked the builds of members who had harassed its Italians, PoG watched with fascination, seeing the raid as an “act of passion” that recalled the Golden Era. A few days later she announced her candidacy for General of Parry for the Liberators, the Liberation Party that ViaTea had launched that month against both major parties.11,12 When Aceiron purged the Orie Apparatus on 4 December, General Boygho withdrew and endorsed her; Aceiron invited her into the Orie Apparatus and endorsed her too, making her a candidate of the Liberator–Orie Coalition, and her campaign gained massive momentum.13

Election

After midnight on 21 December 2025, the community's thirteenth anniversary, ViaTea led the Liberators in raids on Main, Scope and Volley; PoG refused to take part.14,2 The vote was held the next day. PoG's fervent following packed voice calls to hear her speak and circulated “aura” edits of her, and she beat the Preservate Burnette by 79 votes to 51 (60.8%), the only coalition candidate elected anywhere. Asked by Spinzle what had happened, Burnette answered simply: “anais lefebvre.”2 In her victory address, Preservate Boss Adelynn conceded that perhaps that was how it should be, since a healthy community needed dissenting voices.2

That night ViaTea, rejected by her own district, turned on her party and screamed at PoG as a traitor for staying out of the raids. PoG recorded the outburst, muted everyone but ViaTea, and shared the clip as “art,” a “beautiful display of passion.” It went viral; ViaTea resigned, called PoG a “snake” and left on hiatus. The Liberators rejected ViaTea's choice of Matcha as her successor and turned instead to PoG, their only winner.2

General of Parry (2026)

Parisian Parry

PoG took office on 1 January 2026, opening her generalship with a letter in French to Apollos, her “cher Président,” urging him not to surrender his soul to icy reason.8 Under her, Parry ceased to be the dumping ground for the Original Party's cast-offs and became a salon. She held together a coalition of Ories, Liberators and independent trolls by charisma alone, tolerating the Ories as “colourful passions” and debating, laughing at and absorbing the elements the Capital ignored; they stayed loyal because she was the only leader who refused to silence them. Unstable, argumentative and often offensive, Parry became the cultural capital of the opposition, with a vitality the sanitized districts of Main and Waffle lacked, and it was nicknamed “Paris,” or “Parisian Parry.”8 The district's insular dialect, which had grown up under her predecessors, continued to flourish under her.15

The Cat Files

When the Capital released the full Cat Files in January 2026, PoG was the first of the community's leaders to respond, posting “The Consecration of Our Scars” to the NPC! server shortly after midnight on 13 January. She praised those who had stood up to Cat, among them Adelynn and Kay, and argued that the ordeal had not broken the community but consecrated it.16

「Yesterday, we were innocent, and the innocence is fragile. Today, we are tragic, and the tragedy is unbreakable.」

— General PoG, “The Consecration of Our Scars,” 13 January 2026

She was also among the loudest critics of the so-called Ninzard Deep State, arguing that however commendable Kay's efforts had been, her very presence threatened the spirit of the Ninzard Project.17 On 17 January she introduced herself to the community by her real name and declared that the Charter would “kill our community in slow motion.” The sole survivor of the opposition had become the figurehead of the movement against the Charter.4

The Salonnière

When Evhom, a Jewish member of Parry, complained of harassment by trolls in January 2026, PoG told her that dignity was something she had to hold herself, and offered her a new role, the “Salonnière,” to help curate the district's culture and drown the trolls out “with better art.”18 Kaimachi, a leading figure among the “Parry Girls,” was by then the district's most notorious member. On 2 February Apollos and Spinzle brought her into the Preservate Apparatus, a gamble meant in part to stop PoG from drawing the support of the Parry Girls.18

The Chud War

During the Chud War, after shots were fired at Aceiron's house in Sacramento, PoG accused the Capital on 16 February 2026 of running a surveillance state, refusing ID checks and the GPS logs that Spinzle had held over the belligerents, and declaring that “to be unknown is the only freedom we have left.” Many Ories and Liberators applauded. Woofer replied within the hour that the belligerents had livestreamed their own locations and accused PoG of defending the privacy of neo-Nazis and rioters, and Cyntaku reminded her that, however distant it seemed from France, these were real people.19

On 21 February Minister of Justice Kay identified the shooter as Tristen Mercer, known as Triste, and designated the Liberation Party a criminal entity. Seven minutes later PoG declared the party finished.20,1

「The dream of Liberation did not die in a debate hall; it bled out on a sidewalk in Sacramento because a boy confused violence with conviction.」

— General PoG, 21 February 2026

Boss of the Original Party (2026–present)

Joining the Ories

In the same statement PoG refused to retreat into what she called the sterile white rooms of the Preservation Party, and announced that she was joining the Original Party, a ruined and leaderless house but one “that feels,” not to follow its masters but “to teach it how to speak again.”1 She met little resistance. On 22 February 2026 she was appointed Boss of the Original Party, endorsed by HazyDaze, the caretaker Boss since the Apparatus had forced Aceiron out after the December election, and backed by most of the Orie Apparatus. Even Aceiron, by then a senior member, did not resist, though some of the “Calebros” remained suspicious of her.1,2 Many former Liberators followed her into the party and helped rebrand it, forming the Romantic Flank, which overlapped heavily with the Rationalist Flank; ViaTea and Matcha refused to join and declared themselves independents.1

Against the Capital

When three Ories assaulted Apollos on his university campus on 5 March 2026 and fled as soon as he squared up to them, and Spinzle extracted a confession from his attacker with an AI-generated photograph, PoG called the affair “peak cinéma.” She mocked the three as frightened mice, and hailed Spinzle's ruthless deception, carried out behind her own allies' backs, as a love confession, “l'amour fou,” and “absolutely magnifique art.”21 Her breathy, poetic voice memos to the community provoked a furious reply in Italian from Minister of Activity Poka, to the community's delight.22

In the referendum on the Charter that spring she stayed neutral, which held back support in Parry. Kaimachi and the Parry Girls countered the apathy, and the district voted 97 to 13 in favour, with 20 abstentions. The Charter passed on 7 May 2026 with 82% of the community's vote.23

The Spider's Broadcast

On 23 April 2026 PoG posted a fifty-two-second video, filmed in a dim room in her “Dark Academia” style of black blazer, loose red tie and wire-rimmed glasses, in which she told the members urging her to challenge the Preservates that she would not run for president.24

「I am a curator of art. I am not a kamikaze. My heart is, and will stay always... in Paris.」

— General PoG, the Spider's Broadcast, 23 April 2026

Within ten minutes a Scopian had reversed and sped up the clip, revealing hidden French phrases and a final, teasing “Maybe I will run for president.” Apollos mocked her as “too scared to run a real campaign,” while ScottyAte defended the video as high art.24

Dispute with Woofer

On 25 April PoG turned on Woofer, then the Preservates' likeliest successor to Apollos. Recalling that Woofer had served as General Geo's administrator at fifteen, she suggested that no one survived “the inner circle of a paranoid predator” by being innocent, and called her the “sterilized, bureaucratic product of trauma.”25 Cyntaku accused her of rewriting history to score points against a teenager, until Grace returned to warn that PoG had baited her into looking hysterical. Poka answered with a voice memo branding PoG “Cat 2.0 but with a baguette,” a phrase that spread across the community, and Woofer replied without anger.25,26,27

2026 presidential election

PoG endorsed Representative ScottyAte when he launched his presidential campaign on 29 May 2026, and with her backing the Orie Apparatus nominated him unopposed the next day, with Hackman as his running mate. She announced the ticket herself as “a perfectly balanced ticket of fire and stone.”28,29 The surprise choice of Hackman pushed the Preservates to nominate Poka, who defeated ScottyAte with 75.9% of the vote, including 56.2% in Parry.29,30

Defeat in Parry

PoG governed Parry with a deliberately unpredictable hand, elevating unexpected members and keeping appointments fluid so that her staff competed for their places and her approval. The quarrels this caused seemed to amuse her, and she treated the resignations as a filter that left a small core of fiercely loyal staff willing to serve with “max passion.” Some Parryans came to see her as an ivory-tower figure watching the disorder from afar, wine in hand, and her habit of calling every conflict “cinema” wore on those who wanted stability.3 Yet she remained popular. Parry's activity rose dramatically, at times nearly rivalling Main with half its members, and the Orie Apparatus held her up as its model of a leader, one who put culture first where earlier Rationalists had chased numbers.3

The Preservates ran Kaimachi against her, whom PoG dismissed as a “slave to the technocrat machinery.” On PoG's twentieth birthday, 12 June 2026, her staff held a celebration on a Discord stage where Parryans sang to her, many in French. When Kaimachi sang in halting French at a synthesizer, PoG was moved to tears in full view of the district, though she thanked Kaimachi as she had every other performer and carried on as though unaffected.3 On 22 June Kaimachi beat her by 66 votes to 64, the most unexpected upset of the summer elections. PoG conceded gracefully that evening.3

「And to Kaia, my fierce rival. Deux voix. Two votes separated us. What an exquisite, breathless duel! … Parisian Parry is yours now.」

— General PoG, conceding Parry, 22 June 2026

Her term ended on 2 July 2026, when Kaimachi took office.31

The Salon War

With no Ories in Legislature XII, the Orie Apparatus kept PoG as Boss, since no one else seemed willing to salvage the party.9,32 By August 2026 the party had divided into Rationalists, who wanted to “ground” Ninzard in a realistic, “normie” culture, and Romantics, best represented by PoG and ScottyAte, who held that the community could not be separated from its trauma and sought a culture of art, creativity and face-to-face friendship, while rejecting the Preservates' moralizing and their treatment of Ninzard as a nation-state.9

In Parry, Kaimachi's first weeks of events for her friends among the Parry Girls angered the district's other blocs, among them the Salonnistes, the Romantic Flank loyalists of their former general, who insisted that only Anaïs could hold Parry. As the Salon War raged through August, PoG sat in the corner, wine in hand, and said nothing.33 After Kaimachi put herself on trial before her critics and won the district over, PoG had the last word, and the Salonnistes' campaign died the moment she yawned.34

「I keep hearing my name in your little uprisings: “pour Anaïs, restore Anaïs.” I am flattered, but a cause is such tedious work. … I am simply bored.」

— Former General PoG, 17 August 2026

Writing the same day, Meikaqu judged that PoG had held Parry from above while Kaimachi held it from within, and that Kaimachi's way would last: crowds drop a performer the moment a better act appears, but are slower to walk out on a friend.35

Political philosophy and style

PoG's opposition to the Capital differed from that of its earlier adversaries. Where the Ories had resisted it for the freedom to be cruel, she resisted it because she believed its “safety” was a form of death. In late-night voice calls and long, poetic posts she argued that Ninzard's value came from its friction, and that a community without the risk of heartbreak, betrayal or chaos was merely a “LinkedIn group chat.”7 She condemned Cat as tasteless and “cruel without purpose,” but argued that the reaction to her crimes, the Charter, the surveillance and the sanitization, was an overcorrection that would “lobotomize” the community.7

「Do not confuse safety and virtue. Safety is, in truth, a lack of passion. You are building a museum for a history you are too afraid to live again.」

— General PoG, 3 January 2026

The archive treats her and Spinzle as mirrors: both highly intelligent, introverted yet socially dominant, and able to dismantle opponents with words rather than bans. Younger and unscarred by the leadership traumas of the Golden Era, PoG took up the laissez-faire ideology that Spinzle had championed in 2018 and later abandoned, and turned it against her, treating her with condescending pity as a “fallen angel” who had traded her wings for a suit and a clipboard.7 She thanked Antareze, in French, for bringing the community together and giving it life, love and passion.7

She ruled through narrative rather than through administrative competence, like Adelynn, or force, like Aceiron, and cultivated a “Dark Academia” image of blazers, loose ties and wire-rimmed glasses that set her apart from both the corporate polish of the Preservate leadership and the vulgarity of the “Calebros.”4 She signs her statements with a spider.21,1
