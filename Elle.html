Arielle Hope Fairbairn (born 18 July 2005), known online as Elle, is an American Ninzard administrator and Original Party politician. She has served as an administrator of the District of Parry since July 2024 and as its head of administration since August 2026.1,7 She has also been a senior operative in the Original Party Apparatus since December 2025.2 Under General Kaimachi, she and her friend Phalth, a Preservate, are the most senior members of the Parry Staff, and both enjoy high trust across the district.

Elle grew up in the Sacramento area and was an early member of the Nightwork diaspora. Her local Sacramento cohort includes Caleb Jensen and Lyre. Scott Dellinger (ScottyAte) and Sasha Gallagher (Liver) are also members. She is associated with the pragmatic center of the Original Party and has a reputation for procedural steadiness. She stayed out of the radical factions that formed in the district between 2023 and 2026.3,4

In 2022 Elle carried out the Ellie screenshot incident, a hoax in which she convinced General Geo Martinez that she had moved to Canada and sold him fake intelligence on Al Commando.5 She later mediated during the 2026 Chud War and spoke at the Parry Trials in August 2026.6,7 General Kaimachi gave her the nickname Miss Protestant in 2026. She is known for defending Reformed Presbyterian theology in public debates with Catholic and secular opponents.8

Early life

Elle was born on 18 July 2005 in Sacramento, California. She was raised in a Presbyterian family of Scottish-American descent in the city's northeastern suburbs. She attended public schools and was active in Reformed church youth groups. Friends in the community describe her as dry-witted and punctual, with a preference for domestic routine.3,4 She has named toasted sourdough with salted butter as her favorite food.4

As a teenager she became friends in person with several Sacramento-area peers who later became prominent in Ninzard. These included Caleb Jensen and Lyre, along with Lora and Sasha Gallagher. Gallagher later turned to radical Catholic traditionalism, and Jensen moved into populist organizing. Elle remained a mainstream conservative Presbyterian, which made her a lasting ideological counterweight within the group.4,9

Early Ninzard career (2016–2021)

Elle joined the Ninzard Project in mid-2016, during the expansion that followed the Ninzard Civil War. She came to the community through neighborhood gaming friends and settled in the District of Nightwork-Parry early in the Apollos I presidency.1 She stayed out of the factional disputes of the period. She supported the Original Party as a "normie" conservative and took part in community gaming events and voice channels.3

2017 Blender Bot incident

In November 2017, 12-year-old Elle took part in a disruption in the District of Parry with Lyre and Sasha Gallagher, who were the same age. It began as a six-hour text argument over whether Mott's or Welch's fruit snacks were "doctrinally superior" for Sunday school.10

The argument moved to voice chat, where the three tried to host a Minecraft horse race. They left a music bot unmuted, and it played a 10-hour loop of an industrial blender crushing ice at high volume in the district's main voice lounge.10 General Kain (Stephen Baek) logged on shortly before 3 a.m. local time to stop it. He did not know the three were middle-school girls from California. He muted voice across the district and posted in public chat, “if i hear one more blender noise or one more fruit snack argument in this vc i am banning the entire middle school class.” The episode gave the three a reputation as a comedic nuisance in early Parry history.10

Ellie screenshot incident (2022)

In spring 2022, tensions rose between General Geo Martinez of Nightwork-Main and the populists of BobaBase. During this period Elle carried out a hoax that later accounts rank among the most effective deceptions of the years before Cat's presidency.5

Martinez was deeply suspicious of President Apollos's alleged "District 3 Secret Forces" (D3SF). In April 2022, Elle contacted him privately under the username "Ellie." She told him her family had moved to Burnaby, British Columbia, and that she was taking community college classes near Apollos's university.5 As proof she sent him stock photographs of Vancouver bus stops and suburban intersections taken from public image boards.5

Over the next three weeks Elle convinced Martinez that she had gotten into a coffee shop used by Apollos and BobaBase administrator Al Commando. She said she had obtained a USB drive holding "confidential D3SF raid rosters" along with Commando's real identity.5 Martinez repeatedly offered administrative guarantees and server currency for the files. Elle sent him heavily redacted PDFs made from her family's grocery lists and her high school chemistry study guides. One file was a handwritten recipe for lemon bars. She had removed key names with Microsoft Paint.5

The hoax collapsed on 28 April 2022, when Martinez cited the "Burnaby Field Reports" on a coordination call with the Nightwork generals. Governor Cyntaku asked to see the source documents, and the file was identified as a Safeway receipt for paper towels and skim milk.5 The episode embarrassed Martinez and raised Elle's standing in Orie backchannels.5

Cat presidency and Parry administration (2022–2025)

Elle kept a low profile during the presidency of Catx3 from 2022 to 2025. Several of her Sacramento peers were compromised through payments or blackmail. She refused outside money and avoided Catsland and Lair, the platforms Cat controlled.11 She remained in Nightwork-Parry and worked on community infrastructure under General Rudolf.12

In July 2024, after Xorbit's faction collapsed and Rudolf reorganized the district, Elle was appointed to the Parry Staff Team as a district administrator.1 The appointment was seen as a compromise. Rudolf handled external politics and anti-Preservate messaging. Elle handled moderation tickets and server permissions and also oversaw onboarding. Her work shielded ordinary members from arbitrary enforcement.12

She kept the post under General Boygho and under General Power of Greed (PoG), who took over in late 2025. She often disagreed with PoG's "romantic" salon style of governance. She remained central to the district's daily operations, clearing ticket backlogs and blocking raids by the far-right Holy Iron Flank.1,13

Chud War and Sacramento standoff (2026)

On 14 February 2026, during the Chud War, Elle was at Caleb Jensen's home in Roseville with Lyre and ScottyAte. Andre Jenkins (AJ) and Rhydeu were also there. The group was trapped inside when 14 armed members of the Holy Iron Flank (HIF), led by Ethan O'Neal (NGin), surrounded the house.6

Jensen experienced severe post-traumatic stress, and the men in the house postured at the windows. Elle contacted Preservate Representative Shuffle (Yael Azoulay) and Minister of Justice Mikayla Holloway over Discord.6 With Lyre, she worked with the Capital to keep police from forcing entry, since Jensen's bedridden mother was in the house.6

Shortly before midnight, Elle and Lyre went out onto the front porch with ScottyAte and AJ. They stood between the two sides and initiated a handshake that ended the immediate standoff. Minutes later an unrelated drive-by shooting broke a front window.6 Photographs of Elle standing between armed Calebros and Iron Flankers on the lawn circulated widely as a symbol of solidarity in the Sacramento diaspora.6

Salon War and Parry Trials (2026)

After the Ninzard Charter was ratified in May 2026, Parry split into factions during the handover from General PoG to the newly elected Preservate General Kaia March (Kaimachi).7 Three blocs formed. The "Parry Girls" backed March and the "Salonnistes" backed PoG. The "Sacback Room" was made up of disaffected male gamers led by Rudolf and BadSpirit.7

By August 2026 Elle was frustrated with March's leadership, which centered on parasocial events devoted to former President Apollos. These included the "Goon to ReuReu" streams.7 Elle saw the administration as a female-dominated clique that neglected the server's gaming functions. She threatened to resign five times in three weeks.7

On 14 August 2026, March held the "Parry Trials," an open-stage tribunal in which her critics questioned her in public.7 Elle took the microphone and described the breakdown of the district's administration. She confronted March over her neglect of male members and the exhaustion of the staff team.7

I have been an admin in this district for two years. I have threatened to quit like... five times this month alone, because you treat a six-hundred-person server like your personal diary. We have real tickets, real moderation issues, and real members who want to play games without seeing ten thousand words of erotic fanfiction pinned in announcements.

Elle, during the Parry Trials, 14 August 2026

March responded by admitting her failures. She declined to appoint close friends to staff and recognized the "Sacback Room" and the "Salon" as autonomous community channels.7 Elle withdrew her resignation after March accepted the criticism without resorting to bans or Capital intervention. She agreed to serve as head of administration in the restructured Kaimachi cabinet.7

Under Kaimachi, Elle and her friend Phalth, a Preservate administrator known as “the Parry Admin,” became the most senior members of the Parry Staff, and both enjoy high trust across the district. The partnership of an Orie and a Preservate in the district's day-to-day running helped to build a bipartisan environment in Parry.

Miss Protestant and theological debates

After the Holloway Summit in May 2026, Elle became one of the community's more prominent religious voices.8 She argued from a mainstream Reformed Presbyterian position grounded in the Westminster Standards. Sasha Gallagher (Liver) promoted traditionalist Catholicism, and parts of the far right favored esoteric paganism.8,9

In mid-September 2026, during a long voice-channel debate in Parry on divine sovereignty and human agency, General Kaimachi referred to Elle's strict adherence to Reformed doctrine by saying, “Everyone please quiet down, our resident Miss Protestant is currently checking the catechism to see if we're allowed to laugh!”8 The nickname spread across the community as a meme.

Between September and October 2026, Elle and Gallagher held a series of theological debates. The debates covered sola scriptura and the Marian dogmas, among other topics.8 Observers noted that Gallagher often used Latin exorcisms and apocalyptic language. Elle answered with close grammatical exegesis and historical citations. Community archivists called the exchanges the "Sacramento Counter-Reformation."8 Among her regular audience is Phalth, who enjoys watching her debate.
