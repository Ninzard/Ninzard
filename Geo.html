Jorge Martinez, known as Geo, is a former Ninzard politician who served as the 8th District General of Main from 7 January to 2 November 2022. A radical Preservate, he ran Main as a fortress against the Poo Party, banning its catchphrases and cutting the district's membership, and his feud with the Poopulists made him the most mocked leader of 2022.1,2,3

Unknown to the community, President Cat paid him from April 2022 while working to destroy him. His generalship ended when he was hospitalized at the end of October 2022, and Sundaze, one of Cat's agents, succeeded him.4,5

Election

A former administrator on the Main Staff Team, Geo beat General Danbi in the January 2022 elections and took office on 7 January.1,2 His first act was to ban Apollos from Main for “disruptive behavior.” His last message to the president, “Go back to your pubescent group,” drew the reply “I'm already here.”2

General of Main (2022)

Policies

Geo reversed many of Danbi's policies and cut Main's membership from 135 to 120 by 1 April 2022. Activity rose, and Syn approved, but moderates objected to his policing of the district. He rebuilt the staff around loyalty to himself, decreeing that “disloyalty to the district equates to renouncing your membership here,” and gave official standing to the Nightwork-Main Anti-Trolls, a band of his supporters notorious for harassing Poopulists and female members.2,6 He banned “ME GUSTA” on 15 January and “Geodude,” the Poopulists' name for him, on 23 March, muting even members who meant the Pokémon; Vice President Snowby called him a “manchild,” and Syn called the rule “moronic.” Meanwhile a fifteen-year-old administrator, Woofer, quietly kept the district running.2

Cat

Cat began paying Geo on 4 April 2022. On 17 April she published sexual messages he had exchanged with her while she posed as a sixteen-year-old, along with his school's address, and Al Commando's taunts over the affair began the feud that defined the summer.4,2,3 Geo nonetheless took her money again, to fund his Anti-Trolls, not knowing that she was leaking their plans to Al Commando.7,3 He stood for president that July and finished tied for sixth with 126 approvals of 490, and was narrowly re-elected in Main, by 83 votes to Danbi's 73.8,9

Trade Incident

On 11 September 2022 Geo tried to pay the Poopulists to dissolve their district, BobaBase, and leave. His staffer Alonso offered Al Commando and Governor Rony $600; Rony held out for $1,000. Geo demoted Alonso and released a statement accusing them of greed, and the whole community mocked him for it.10

「Take things too seriously? You rejected $600 for greed, ego, and to keep a shitty inactive dead server. … Keep entertaining us. I look forward to it.」

— General Geo, after the Trade Incident, September 2022

Fall

In October 2022 Geo came apart. On 29 October he shut Main down over a supposed threat from Al Commando, and his messages grew erratic after Cat cut off his money the next day and demanded repayment.11,12 On 31 October he was taken from his home to a psychiatric hospital; Cat later claimed responsibility. Main elected Sundaze to replace him in a snap election on 2 November.5
