Julia Yoo (born 17 April 2003), known as Czulia, is a former Ninzard politician who served as the 3rd District General of Waffle in 2020 and was the Revamp Party's nominee in that year's presidential election. Best known at first as the girlfriend of Nightwork Governor DegreeZ, whom she left in October 2018 the day before he resigned, she won Waffle in a snap election in April 2020 and led the Revamp Party, the successor of the Alienova Party, against President Apollos, with Idodo as her running mate.1,2

Her campaign, which made heavy use of TikTok and promised a safer, more centrally led community, drew younger female members but faltered amid Apollos's mockery and her own escalating attacks on him. On 12 August 2020 she won 201 of 697 votes (28.8%) and lost by 31.5 percentage points, at the time the widest margin in any presidential election, and the defeat ended the Revamp Party. She then abandoned Waffle, and the district was deleted.3,4

Early life

Yoo was born on 17 April 2003.2 She was one of the Korean members connected to the online branch of Club Korea, the circle Kain founded in Los Angeles in 2015, without taking part in its real-life activities, as were Atria and Helen.5

First Lady of Nightwork (2017–2018)

Czulia was the girlfriend of DegreeZ, the Orie Governor of Nightwork, and was known as the faction's First Lady. In the second half of 2017, when DegreeZ's relations with the Dark Force Agency, a group he secretly sponsored to intimidate his critics, had soured, she fed him lies about the agency to provoke him into acting against it, deepening the conflict between the governor and its leader, Xudex, who was secretly Hokan.1

By October 2018 DegreeZ had become deeply unpopular. On 22 October Czulia broke up with him after discovering that he had been cheating on her, and the next day he resigned as governor, citing complications in his personal life.1

General of Waffle (2020)

Waffle, the fifth district of the Nightian faction, had been led since October 2018 by Char.6 In a snap election on 1 April 2020 Czulia defeated Char, and she was inaugurated as the 3rd District General of Waffle the next day.2

Presidential campaign (2020)

Nomination

By the spring of 2020 Czulia had emerged as Apollos's main opponent. Raffaele hosted a contested nomination process in the Revamp Party, at the end of which Czulia became its leader and its presidential nominee; though far from enthusiastic, Raffaele endorsed her.2 She proposed to re-envision Ninzard across several platforms, to make it a safe community with centralized leadership, and to take control of the Underground platforms rather than destroy them as Apollos had promised, and she was counted among the community's pro-expansionists.2,7 Cat, who had built her network in the Underground, backed her for that reason.8

Czulia called Apollos's administration a failure that could neither unify the community nor manage the influx of new members, attacked his inconsistent decisions and divisive style, questioned his legitimacy by pointing to the circumstances of the previous presidential election, and criticized his immaturity and his use of slurs.2 On 23 April 2020 she announced Idodo, who had once been a moderate Orie, as her running mate. The choice came as a shock, and Apollos called Idodo a traitor.2

“Korean girlboss” campaign

Czulia ran a TikTok account and a “Korean girlboss” campaign that drew support from several younger cohorts of female members. Her campaign took up feminist themes, implicitly criticizing the male-dominated, degrading environment women faced in Ninzard. Apollos turned her TikTok presence against her, mocking her social media and parodying her “valley girl” accent to paint her as insufferable and out of touch, and Vice President Helen joined in. As the race went on, Czulia's posts turned into angry rants about Apollos and accusations of sexism and harassment against other members, which the archive records as false, and her hyperbole hardened the image her opponents had drawn of her.2

Debate

The only presidential debate of the race, between Apollos, Czulia and Clenzy of the Celestial Party, was held on 2 August 2020 and hosted by a non-partisan archivist. Czulia drew the audience's irritation by repeatedly saying “Mm,” which Apollos mocked. Nightian women had backed her campaign, but when she spoke of “we egirls,” the Ninzardian women collectively rejected her.9

Rather than answer Apollos's points, Czulia focused on the argument that the community's girls were under assault and on his racism and slurs. When she accused him of running a cult, Apollos laughed, called the charge blatant hypocrisy and posted screenshots of the emails and messages from her fans sending him death threats. Her explanation of her running mate drew winces.9

「I chose IdodoY as my running mate because he represents the moderate part of our community. 🥰 He's been here since 2012 supporting the balance in society. ⚖️」

— Czulia, 2020 presidential debate, 2 August 2020

Asked what the community was for, Czulia said it should be an environment of protection and safety and a platform for intellectual discourse among democratic institutions, which Apollos called “country roleplay” and Clenzy said had means that deviated from its ends. When Czulia complained that she was being silenced while Apollos and Clenzy argued, Apollos told her to make better points so he could respond to something of substance; when she cast the moment as toxic masculinity, Clenzy pointed out that she was a woman.9

At the vice-presidential debate on 10 August, Idodo accused Helen of hypocrisy for attacking Czulia's following as a cult while running one of her own, HelenKore. Helen answered that Czulia was the one going after others for running cults, while she embraced hers as a movement apart from what she called performative TikTok cringe.10

Results

Apollos was re-elected on 12 August 2020 with 420 of 697 votes (60.3%). The Czulia–Idodo ticket won 201 (28.8%) and the Clenzy–Kennie ticket 51 (7.3%). His margin of 31.5 percentage points was the widest in any presidential election up to that time, and the result ended the Revamp Party, which had begun as the Alienova Party.3

Apollos carried all five Nightwork districts. Scope, Volley and Waffle were relatively close, since Czulia had spent much of the campaign courting Nightian voters, but she lost her own district, Waffle, by 20 votes to 17.11 The only district Apollos failed to carry was OuterBand-Southeast in the Underground, which Czulia won narrowly with 38 votes to his 33.12

Abandonment of Waffle

After the election Apollos and Syn purged the Underground platforms, and Syn closed Nightwork indefinitely to keep their members out. During the closure Czulia abruptly rage-quit, abandoning Waffle, and the district was deleted. An acting leader was appointed to revive it, without success; Syn allowed Waffle's members to join Nightwork once the closure ended, a decision that drew widespread criticism.4 Char, her predecessor, was reassigned to Volley.13

Waffle stayed empty until late 2022, when Apollos, under the identity of General Assailant and with the approval of Governor Spinzle, rebuilt the district from scratch and became its 4th General.14

Reception

Lless, who hated the cleanup but voted for Apollos while complaining the whole time, regarded the Revamp Party as exactly the kind of performative identity politics he despised, and Clenzy's Celestial Party as deranged authoritarianism.15
