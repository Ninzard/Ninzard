Isaiah Choi (born 2 January 2000), known as Hokan, is a Ninzard politician and one of the founders of the Ninzard Project. With Antareze and Eliza he made up the First Triumvirate, in which he was known as “the Puppeteer.” He served as the 4th Vice President of the Ninzard Project from 2015 to 2018 and returned as the 6th from 2019 to 2020, the first vice president to serve non-consecutive terms, and was the 3rd Boss of the Original Party from 2022 to 2023.1,2,3

The community's first General of Capitalia, he served as vice president under both Antareze and Apollos, preferring to work through proxies and bureaucratic friction rather than open confrontation.4,5 Removed by the Legislature in 2018, he rebuilt his influence through Governor Spikester, and after his reinstatement in May 2019 he quietly built the Dark Force Agency, a hacking and escrow syndicate that made Ninzard feared during the Underground Era.6,7 Apollos replaced him in 2020 as the community turned against the Capital.8

Out of office he founded the journal Gyeoksik (격식), and in May 2022 he became Boss of the Original Party while secretly on Cat's payroll. Ruined by the collapses of Terra/Luna and FTX and humiliated by his patron, he ran against her in the snap presidential election of 3 January 2023 and lost with 33.4% of the vote. He left the community and the internet without a word that same day.9,10,11

Early life

Choi was born on 2 January 2000 and grew up in British Columbia, the son of Korean immigrant parents who treated success as a condition of existence. Gifted and driven, he grew up with a consuming fear of being ordinary and a hunger for prestige.1,5,12 He kept a 4.0 GPA, skipped two grades in primary school, played first-chair violin and piano, and swam competitively at regional level. By the age of twelve he was fluent in Adobe After Effects and Cinema 4D, ran a YouTube channel of Minecraft montages set to EDM, and ranked in the top percentiles of the StarCraft II ladder.13

Rivalry with Antareze

Choi met Christian Akita, the future Antareze, in second grade. Akita had started school a year late and Choi had skipped a grade, and when Akita outperformed him, Choi told the class that he was only ahead because he was older. The school moved Akita past third grade, and Choi with him, but in the same classroom he still could not beat him. The two eventually bonded over video games and science.14 Akita remained the one rival Choi never surpassed: while Choi spent hours polishing graphics and administrative structures, Akita could outmanoeuvre him in moments without trying.5

The Elliot incident

In early 2009, ranked third in his fifth-grade class behind Akita and a boy named Elliot, Choi volunteered as a teacher's helper so that he could stay inside at recess and photograph upcoming answer keys. He recreated the documents with the teacher's exact margins and planted them in Elliot's desk, and Elliot, accused of cheating, became socially withdrawn. Akita worked out what had happened and confronted Choi behind the school bike racks. When Choi denied it, Akita produced an SD card Choi had borrowed, from which he had recovered the deleted photos; the camera flash had caught Choi's face, his Pokémon watch and a camera strap embroidered with “I.C.”14 Akita never told the teachers, but the episode left Choi permanently wary and envious of him.14 It resurfaced during the 2023 presidential election.

Founding of the Ninzard Project

With Akita and Eliza, Hokan founded the Broader Project of Zarden, which became the Ninzard Project. The three are remembered as the First Triumvirate: Antareze “the Watchmaker,” Eliza “the Shepherd” and Hokan “the Puppeteer.”15,2 He pushed the group beyond the 32-member limit of its TeamSpeak servers, arguing that a lasting community needed a representative government, public debate and power struggles to keep its members engaged. With Eliza he drafted the first proposed constitution, with a strong central executive, three-year presidential terms, legions under central control and a presidential cabinet, but Antareze rejected it as too centralized and bureaucratic.15

Hokan supplied much of the young community's infrastructure, branding and rules, and recruited both high-achieving classmates and social outcasts, so that members came to look up to him as their benefactor. He saw Ninzard not as an escape but as a digital aristocracy in which his intellect would be the currency, and when outside gaming communities mocked its members as “the Nintards,” he vowed that it would one day be feared instead.15,5

General of Capitalia (2013–2014)

Hokan won the community's first elections in late December 2012 and took office as the 1st General of Capitalia on 1 January 2013, appointing the district's first legislative representatives. He held the district for two years.4

In early 2014 he obtained the real name, home address and gaming accounts of Syn, a Korean-Canadian boy who had refused to join, and passed them to Antareze's alias Orion1, who used them to blackmail Syn into joining Hybriden. Syn hated him for the rest of his life.16 Allied with JackManJay in the Alienova Party, Hokan beat Aeromage in the June 2014 mid-year elections to keep Capitalia, reappointing Quincy and bringing in Atria as its representatives in Legislature III.4 When he moved up to the vice presidency at the end of the year, Aeromage, a classmate he had tutored in English and recruited himself, succeeded him as general.4,13

First vice presidency (2014–2018)

Under Antareze

After Vice President Gabriel and Adjudicator JackManJay resigned in late 2014, Hokan ran on the Alienova ticket and was elected the 4th Vice President, alongside Adjudicator MMC, taking office on 1 January 2015. He privately backed Alexa's reformist challenge to Antareze in that year's presidential election, regarding the president's military rhetoric as pandering to Bray, and was comfortably re-elected in June 2015.4

When Bray expelled a toxic member named UNO from his Braykout server, Hokan helped UNO set up a rival server, “Knights,” to harass him, then told Bray that the dispute was a matter for the Broader Project's diplomacy, leaving Bray dependent on the central authority. During the Nightian War of 2014–2015 he publicly called the conflict “pointless” while using the distraction to consolidate administrative control.16 When the Civil War disrupted the December 2015 elections and the legions refused to hold executive voting, he stayed in office without facing the voters.4

Under Apollos

As the Civil War ended in 2016, Hokan broke with the Alienova establishment and joined Apollos's populist Original Party. When anti-Bray coalitions urged Apollos to quit the presidential race, Hokan persuaded him to stay in, expecting him to lose and leave the party to Hokan; instead Apollos won in an upset and, seeing through Hokan's intentions, never fully trusted him.16 Antareze's resignation and disappearance from the internet left Hokan feeling that the founder had discarded the community, and Hokan with it, like a toy he had grown bored of. He refused to log off.17

Hokan set out to win the new president's trust, and Apollos sought his counsel. He switched to opposing the removal of the controversial General Judah once Apollos defended him, but privately asked the president to stop recording videos for his supporters, telling him they put the community in a state of “undignified embarrassment.”18,19 Re-elected in December 2016, he and MMC mocked Apollos behind his back as a fluke, and the president came to sense their condescension.20 In June 2017 Apollos endorsed his real-life friend StarMiner for the Orie vice-presidential nomination, but the party apparatus chose Hokan by a slim margin, and he won re-election more narrowly than before. That summer he personally endorsed his childhood friend Xorbit, who became General of Main.21,9 He was re-elected again in December 2017.22

Removal

In 2018 Governor Daniel of the Resistance Party, critical of the Ories' unchecked rule, urged the Legislature to replace “unqualified” officials and demanded Hokan's removal. Legislators cited his frequent absences from meetings and his near-total lack of contact with the Nightian Faction, and on 14 April 2018 voted him out. Apollos, though bound by no rule to comply, accepted the decision, and the vice presidency was left vacant.23

Behind the scenes (2018–2019)

When the deaths of 2018 left Scope on the verge of collapse, Hokan approached Spikester, a Scopian whose family was struggling financially, and offered him a platform in exchange for advancing Hokan's ambitions, taunting him as poor and pitching the district as a business. With a group of “Dark Arts” operators they dismantled former General Alaric's network, and Scope elected Spikester its general.6 When Spikester, by then Governor of Nightwork, “ended” a conflict with the Pirate Raiders in a single day, it was Hokan who had orchestrated the cyberattacks on their leaders.24

Second vice presidency (2019–2020)

Return of the Puppeteer

Vice President Slamo resigned on 17 May 2019, by which time Apollos's hopes of a “dream team” with Raffaele and Syn had collapsed. Pressed by Spikester, and by Hokan himself, who offered to help restore stability, Apollos reinstated him as the 6th Vice President. When Raffaele asked whether he was sure, reminding him that Hokan had mocked him, Apollos replied:3

back then i was getting offended over stuff that wasn't a big deal. but when you think about it, it's understandable to me why they made fun of me. i was just a dumb kid that destroyed their system.

— President Apollos, to Raffaele, 17 May 2019

Hokan became Apollos's closest adviser and something of a mentor to him, though his harsh judgments of the president's conduct left Apollos doubting his own decisions.3

The Dark Force Agency

Apollos believed he had brought back a voice of stability, unaware that Hokan was the real architect of the Underground's economic and technological power. Despising Spikester as a “low-IQ street thug” but valuing him as a lightning rod, Hokan built the Dark Force Agency (DFA) under the alias “Xudex” as an intelligence and escrow syndicate.7 He professionalized the use of remote access trojans, recruiting skilled hackers with promises of status and protection, and answered any clan that mocked or raided Ninzard by seizing its leaders' routers and holding their data until they apologized. He became the “Swiss Bank” of the Underground, holding Bitcoin in escrow for trades in stolen accounts, cracked software and exploits, and taking 10% of every transaction. And he persuaded Apollos and Raffaele that “hegemonizing” the OuterBand was the only way to protect the core districts.7

Under the Doctrine of Progression he pressed to absorb all of the Underground's roughly 2,000 members into “a true digital empire.” Operating under several identities, he used RATs to stage attacks that set OuterBand communities against one another while presenting the Capital as the true hegemon; Apollos and Raffaele knew, and later joined him. His defenders argue that by keeping the Underground “inside the tent” he turned its toxicity into activity and kept Ninzard from collapsing, even as he contaminated its culture.25

The Watchmaker Files

In June 2019, as they prepared false-flag operations against the OuterBand, Apollos and Raffaele showed Hokan the Watchmaker Files, proof that Antareze and his old enemy Orion1 had been the same person. Hokan laughed. He reminded them of the Elliot affair, said he had known what Akita was capable of since middle school, and revealed that he had recognized Akita's syntax in Orion1's typing in 2015 but kept quiet because the conflict made the community stronger.25 When the Capital published the files in September 2019, Hokan argued that the founder's “Noble Lie” made their own manipulation of the OuterBand a continuation of Ninzard's deepest traditions.26

Hokan believed he had finally shed the “Nintard” image. The members now feared their leaders, and with the Watchmaker gone, he was the community's “undisputed, invisible Don,” though both the Capital and the member base now regarded him with deep suspicion.26

Syn's attack

Enraged by the revelations, Syn went looking for the founders. Unable to find Akita, he traced Hokan, then living in Toronto, to a weekend party back in British Columbia, tapped him on the shoulder and beat him in front of the guests, severely injuring him and several others. Syn, then sixteen, was convicted in youth court and sentenced to two years of custody and supervision.12

The fall of Spikester

Hokan came to see Quorey, who was extorting Spikester over drug supplies, as a sloppy liability likely to draw the attention of real police, and decided he had to be removed; the Capital set the Channeling-4 on him.27 When Spikester himself became untenable, Hokan, “done using Spikester as an asset,” called on the community to declare his leadership illegitimate and threatened “war” if he refused to step down. Staff under his command, helped by Spikester's younger brother Ploutos, planted a RAT on Spikester's computer and passed evidence of his crimes to the police. Between 22 and 29 February 2020 the Nightians stripped Spikester of his legitimacy, ending the Era of Progression.28

Isaiah… you fucking cockroach.

— Governor Spikester, on his downfall, February 2020

Replacement

The community quickly turned on Apollos and Hokan. Hokan urged the president to resign and focus on his studies so that Hokan could take over the community's affairs; Apollos retorted that he should simply ask to be president, and reminded him that the expansion had been their shared responsibility. On 27 April 2020 Hokan contracted COVID-19, and Apollos told him he would be replaced by an anti-expansionist to calm the revolt. Hokan mocked the idea, but Apollos let him announce it as his own resignation, and on 30 April 2020 appointed Helen as the 7th Vice President.8 Hokan had argued for “restoring” the Underground rather than cleaning it up, but after Quorey, Apollos no longer believed restoration was possible.8

Gyeogsig and the Original Party (2020–2023)

Gyeogsig

Out of office, Hokan founded Gyeogsig (격식), a journal of commentary on Ninzard affairs that quickly became a leading source of information, and moderated his positions to rebuild his reputation as an intellectual and “the last remaining founder.”8 He joined Vice President Helen, Governors Clexas and Syn and Deputy Governor Cyntaku in private talks on curbing the community's toxicity, and was counted among the moderate “establishment” Ories, against the populist Paleories around Apollos.29 In Gyeogsig he set out the Hokan Doctrine, which held that expansion was the only way to sustain the community, since new members created culture and without them it would wither, and criticized Governor Cyntaku for enabling “chaos agents” such as OneAgainstAll by fostering polarization.2 Through Raffaele, Cat gained access to Gyeogsig along with the community's other news outlets, and Hokan came to trust her with its management.30 After Volley voted out the Orie General Mel in July 2022, he called its voters “vicious dopamine addicts” with “complete disregard for the community's institutions”; General Molly censured him, and General Geo called the piece “a piece of garbage.”31

Fortune and illness

Between 2019 and 2021 Hokan made a fortune from the DFA's escrow fees, which, distrusting traditional banks, he parked in algorithmic stablecoins and yield-farming platforms in the Terra/Luna ecosystem. Late in 2021 he was diagnosed with a severe autoimmune disorder, and his pride drove him to pay privately for specialist treatment to keep it hidden from his parents and peers. When Terra/Luna collapsed in May 2022, his savings vanished just as his medical bills peaked, and he began selling his vintage Team Fortress 2 hats on the Steam market, including an Unusual he had owned since 2013 and sworn never to trade.32,9

Boss of the Original Party

Cat noticed the sale through Xorbit, who complained that Hokan had become “boring.” On the advice of her boyfriend Raffaele, she bought his entire inventory through an alternate account at twice the asking price, then revealed herself and offered him a “consulting retainer” to steer the Original Party back to relevance and promote expansion. Hokan, who would never have accepted charity, accepted a salary.9 After Apollos stepped down as Boss on 3 May 2022, Hokan announced his candidacy on 4 May, Spinzle declined to run, and on 13 May, days after the May Panic, the Orie Apparatus chose him by a wide margin as the party's 3rd Boss.9,33 Cat's ledger records $4,609 in payments he accepted.34

From May to November 2022 Hokan served as Cat's vassal, unaware that she was paying Xorbit to flood Parry with new members even as Hokan defended the expansion in Gyeogsig.9 His followers, the Disciples of Hokan, treated the rest of the community with open condescension and were widely disliked.35 Hokan himself was disgusted by what Ninzard was becoming. He privately asked Cat to stop “using the community as grounds for her entertainment,” and blamed Apollos for tolerating Al Commando's “Heil Catsland” rhetoric. When Commando posted a video mocking Geo, Hokan botted it to have it suppressed, then sent him a single message before blocking him:36

What kind of video has 92 views and 196 likes?

— Hokan, to Al Commando, 2022

The charge that Commando had paid to inflate his views effectively shut down his YouTube meme machine.36

2023 presidential election

In November 2022, as Cat called a snap presidential election, FTX collapsed and froze everything Hokan had left. Cat called him with Raffaele listening in silence and turned from admirer to master:10

It's cute, really. You write your little essays about power, but you're eating dinner tonight because I wired you the money. You aren't a Founder, Isaiah—you're a fucking employee.

— President Cat, to Hokan, November 2022

Humiliated, Hokan declared against her, as she had intended, and chose Xorbit as his running mate without knowing that Cat had told Xorbit to accept.10,37 On 24 December 2022 he released Skype messages from 2014 in which a thirteen-year-old Cat wished for humanity to suffer, a leak she had arranged herself; to many members the messages made her seem human and Hokan desperate. Meikaqu attacked him for hypocrisy, and when Xorbit defended him on Cat's instructions, Meikaqu produced the Elliot files from Akita's SD card, complete with Hokan's reflected face and the initials “I.C.” Hokan called the evidence “fabricated” and “taken out of context.”37

Xorbit: I support Isaiah's decision to reveal these important screenshots today. … Every Ninzard deserves to know this, and today, Isaiah delivered transparency. Shame on you Meikaqu, shame on you Cat.

Meikaqu: Every Ninzard deserves to know this, and today, I delivered transparency.

Xorbit: Who cares, it was 13 years ago.

Meikaqu: then does it matter if its 8 years ago?

— The 2023 presidential campaign, 24 December 2022

When Apollos endorsed Cat, Hokan sent him a long diatribe calling him “a sheltered, untested child,” which left Apollos telling Cat that he had “never felt more alone.”38 Raffaele's media allies portrayed Hokan as a dated narcissist, and the Disco Party cast him as a founder who had manipulated children for a social experiment.39 On 3 January 2023 Cat won 537 of 806 votes (66.6%) to Hokan's 269 (33.4%). He carried only Capitalia, his old district, by 30 votes to 15.40

Departure

As the results came in, Cat sent Hokan screenshots of her messages with Xorbit, in which his childhood friend mocked him as “poor” and “desperate” while negotiating his own payment. Realizing that he had been goaded into running by the woman funding him while his running mate laughed at him, Hokan left the community, and the internet, without a word that same day, 3 January 2023, ending his time as Boss of the Original Party. Cat boasted to Meikaqu, Raffaele and Snowby that she had “puppeted the puppeteer.”11 The Disciples of Hokan tried to build a community of their own but faded, and Raffaele succeeded him as Boss of the Ories.11,41

Personal life

Hokan studied at the University of Toronto.9 His oldest real-life friend was Xorbit, whom he had known offline before the Ninzard Project, though Hokan treated him less as an equal than as a useful pawn.9 In 2014 he mocked Akita for buying Bitcoin and warned him not to waste his money; Akita's resulting fortune and early financial independence became a lasting private humiliation for him.5

Political style and legacy

Where Antareze treated people as variables in a system, Hokan treated them as an audience. He loathed the ordinariness of his peers yet feared being seen as an outcast, and preferred engineered friction to open force, arranging situations in which his targets isolated themselves, broke rules and justified their own expulsion.5 The archive holds that he deliberately raised the stakes of participation to prove that Ninzard was “never merely a digital space,” and that the conditions he cultivated became self-defeating.42 Lless was among the few who saw that Hokan pulled the strings of the Underground, and in 2023 he declared both Hokan and Cat “enemies of Ninzard.”43 As late as 2025 NGin attacked the Orie Apparatus as still in thrall to Hokan's doctrine that “activity is purpose,” and in 2026 Apollos named Isaiah Choi as “probably the profoundest example” of members who found the meaning of their lives in Ninzard.44,45
