Malachi Holloway (born 1995 or 1996), known in the Ninzard Project as M, is an American restaurateur and the older brother of Kay, the former Minister of Justice. A peripheral figure who never held office, he nonetheless remained bound up in the community's web of real-life friendships.1,2 In Sacramento he ran a restaurant that served as a sanctuary for the city's Ninzard cohort until it closed during the COVID-19 pandemic, after which he moved to Los Angeles with Kay.

M looked after Kay through the last months of her investigation of President Cat, sheltered Sev in their home and in August 2025 went with his sister to the FBI as her legal guardian and proxy.3,4,5 On 2 May 2026 the Holloways hosted a gathering of thirty members from every faction at their Los Angeles home, which became known as the Holloway Summit. When NGin, the leader of the Holy Iron Flank, arrived in a fury, M embraced him and prayed over the room until he wept, a moment that ended the Flank's war with the community.6,7,8,9,10

Early life and family

M is the older brother of Mikayla Holloway, known as Kay, who was born on 14 May 2000 and grew up in Sacramento.11 He was thirty years old in May 2026.2 Kay entered the Ninzard Project as a teenager and became one of its most consequential figures; M never held office or stood for election, and the archive counts him among the peripheral figures who lived at the margins of the community yet remained woven into its offline relationships.1

Restaurant in Sacramento

For years M ran a restaurant in Sacramento. It became a sanctuary for the Sacramento cohort, the large circle of Ninzards who lived in and around the city, and a place where its members could gather in person. The restaurant shut down during the COVID-19 pandemic, and afterward M moved away from Sacramento with Kay. The two settled in a house in the suburbs of Los Angeles, which they share.12

Kay's investigation (2024–2025)

M attended church every week. Kay became a Christian in 2023, after recovering from a severe illness, though during her investigation she sometimes missed services because she had fallen asleep at her desk.4 In July 2024 Kay tracked down Sev, a former driver for President Cat who had been hiding from her, and offered him a place to live. He stayed with the Holloways, helped Kay gather physical evidence against Cat and became a Christian himself, and M often took him along to church.3,4

Kay told her brother what she was doing. When Cat sent one of her agents to photograph the Holloways' home in late May 2025, hoping to frighten Kay, the agent found the house already set up as bait, with signs planted outside that read “Hi Catherine.”13

The investigation took a heavy toll on Kay. In its last months she stayed awake for two or three days at a time, flinched at loud noises and stopped looking after herself; when M tried to get her to eat, sleep or clean, she snapped at him or did not respond at all. While she slept, sometimes for twelve to eighteen hours, M took over her surveillance operations, though watching his sister's physical decline made him feel sick. By August 2025 her hand tremors had grown so severe that he took her to the hospital.4

After Cat's fall, Kay surrendered voluntarily to the FBI Field Office in Sacramento with her evidence, among it Spinzle's decrypted Dead Man's Switch and her own surveillance archive. M accompanied her as her legal guardian and proxy, together with a lawyer specializing in cyber law, and Kay secured a non-prosecution agreement in exchange for her cooperation as a witness against Cat.5

Holloway Summit (May 2026)

On 2 May 2026 the Holloway residence, which the community had taken to calling the “Deep State Headquarters,” hosted a gathering meant to end the hostility between the Capital's circle and the Holy Iron Flank, the far-right faction led by NGin. Kay greeted the guests at the door with M beside her in a “Kiss the Cook” apron. Before the rest arrived, he asked everyone to bow their heads and led them in prayer.2

Liver, who arrived with Adelynn, Grace and Cyntaku, later described the man in the canvas apron who offered her melon and a glass of water and showed what she called a fatherly care. When M prayed before the meal, he asked God to keep the young men of the Flank safe on the highway and named them one by one: NGin, Chud Alessandro, Athaniel, Highlander and Cedario. Hearing him pray for the friends she had once led, Liver broke down on the floor of the living room.12

Twenty-three people were waiting when M went back to the kitchen to prepare the brisket.14 When the Flank finally arrived, Athaniel drifted over to the kitchen island and asked shy questions about the seasoning, and M handed him a loaded plate. NGin, by contrast, paced the room in a rage, calling Kay a federal asset, and recoiled when M offered him potato salad.6,15

When NGin raised a wooden bat toward him, M stepped out from the kitchen with his hands open and told him that nobody there hated him. He then wrapped his arms around him. NGin shrieked and fought the embrace, but M held on and wept into his hair until the young man's knees gave way and he collapsed sobbing against M's apron.6,15 Rocking him like a father holding a child after a nightmare, M began to pray over the room.7

「Hold this boy, Lord. Wash away the poison. Wash away the lies that told him he had to be cruel to be strong. He's just a boy who wanted a home. They all are. … Let them leave their weapons at the door. Let them know that the war is over. It is finished. They don't have to fight anymore.」

— M, praying over NGin, 2 May 2026

As he prayed, Kain slid down the wall in tears with ViaTea kneeling beside him, and AJ wept with Laser's hand on his back. M closed by asking God to bring them all “back into the light.”7 Aceiron, who arrived last as the thirtieth guest, crossed the room to embrace NGin, and the two pulled apart reconciled.8,9 The thirty became known as the “Holloway Summit 30.”8

That night the whole assembly, former Iron Flankers, Ories and Preservates alike, agreed without any prompting to attend Sunday service the next morning with M and Kay. Idodo briefed Apollos on the day, from the arrivals to M's prayer.9 M was in the kitchen by six the next morning, turning pancakes on the griddle, and by half past eight the guests were on their way to the church the Holloways attend, where NGin sat beside him through a service of electric guitars and drums.16

Legacy

The Summit became a turning point in the community's recovery from the years of Cat and the Holy Iron Flank. When Liver posted her account of her life, Confessions Beneath My Armor, five days later, she included a dedication to him.17

「To Malachi Holloway. You stood in the breach. You offered a prayer that saved the lives of wandering boys. Your compassion shielded us all.」

— Liver, Confessions Beneath My Armor, 7 May 2026

Days later Athaniel carried the story to the Flank's holdouts in Volley, telling them that he had expected a brawl in Los Angeles and received a feast.18

「In that house in Los Angeles I saw a father holding a crying boy. Malachi held Ethan and he prayed for his soul. I wanted a father to hold me like that.」

— Athaniel, to the Holy Iron Flank in Volley, May 2026

Aceiron, who had prayed in a hospital bed in April 2026 after a head injury and described the result as “the quiet,” later said that M gave that feeling its name: grace. He was baptized at a Presbyterian church on 20 September 2026.19 On a later trip to Seattle, when Syn dismissed faith as a psychological crutch, Raffaele answered that a secular worldview could not explain the grace they had witnessed in the Holloway living room, and in her farewell to the community Adelynn wrote that the walls of the Holloway home had turned years of calcified animosity into “undeserved, pure grace.”20,21

Personal life

M is a non-denominational Christian and lives in Los Angeles with Kay; the two attend the same church.16 Guests at the Summit remembered his deep, resonant voice and his warmth, and the archive describes him crossing the room toward NGin with the calm of a father approaching a frightened child.2,15
