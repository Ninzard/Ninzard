Albert Luke Mancini (born 27 August 2004), known as Al Commando, is a Ninzard troll and politician who represented Zarden in Legislature XI, the body that drafted the Charter of the Ninzard Project, for the Preservation Party. A high school friend of Apollos and an administrator of BobaBase, he came third in the 2022 presidential election as the nominee of the Poo Party.1,2,3

His feud with General Geo of Main through 2022 became the defining cultural conflict of the era, and his meme video of Geo's most famous rant became a Ninzard classic; the archive later showed that Raffaele had built up his persona and that Cat had used the feud to wear down the Preservates.4,5 He left the community after falling out with Apollos in November 2022, returned in 2025 as the president's “Propagandaminister,” and in 2026 was one of the twelve core insiders of the Preservate Apparatus.6,7,8,9

Early life

Mancini was born on 27 August 2004. He was a real-life friend of Apollos from high school, and with Rony, Zylosted and Zavil belonged to the president's circle of school friends, later known as the Gang of Five.10,1 The archive describes him as treating the Ninzard Project as “an interactive theater of the absurd,” with a nihilistic sense of humour, a talent for finding the psychological weaknesses of his targets and no grand political agenda. The name “Al Commando” was not entirely his own: Raffaele curated the persona's mythos, including the alias itself, which Mancini then adopted.4

BobaBase (2022)

The Gang of Five

Al Commando served as an administrator of BobaBase, a district of Ninzardia, under Governor Rony. With Apollos, Rony, Deputy Governor Zylosted and his fellow administrator Zavil he made up the Gang of Five, a prominent group within the Poopulists that was seen as the opposite of Nightwork-Main's radical “Axiom” Preservates; the Preservates' NightlyNews accused it of trolling to divide the community.1 In late January 2022 Helen threatened him and Rony with sensitive information such as their home addresses. He denounced her fiercely, and some Ories called him a “crybaby” for the strength of his reaction.10,11 That spring he was among the early supporters of Nexit, the proposal that Ninzardia cut its ties with Nightwork, before changing his mind at the final meeting of the Nightian-Ninzardian Assembly.12

Through 2022 BobaBase's Poopulist leadership took part in the far-right rhetoric spreading through Nightwork, and the archive names Vice President Snowby, Al Commando and a BobaBase member named ComradeTony as its most prominent voices.13

Feud with Geo

Al Commando first drew Geo's fury by attacking him in Nightwork-Main over his messages to a girl Geo had believed was underage. His mocking refrain, “Which state?”, was taken up by Geo's other enemies, and NightlyNews began to frame Geo's visits to BobaBase as a fight against the “D3SF,” a secret force that did not exist.14,15 The feud was fed from above. Cat passed screenshots of Geo's private rants to Rony and Al Commando to provoke threats from BobaBase, then handed the threats to NightlyNews as acts of war,16 and on 11 May 2022 Raffaele told Geo that he could “confirm” Al Commando was the leader of the D3SF.17

The rivalry became the “Summer of Schizoposting,” the defining cultural conflict of mid-2022. Geo answered with rage and moral condemnation, Al Commando with irony and nonsense; when Geo wished him suffering on 23 July over a rumour, probably started by Commando himself, that he had been in a car accident, Commando replied with a surreal non sequitur. Raffaele's news outlets headlined his low-effort posts as “D3SF Tactical Strikes,” while Cat fed Geo fabricated reports that Commando led an organized ring targeting his family and leaked the Nightwork-Main Anti-Trolls' chat logs to him. Mancini was largely unaware of the machinery amplifying him and simply leaned into the role.4

On 26 July 2022, after Geo had claimed victory over BobaBase, Cat sent Al Commando the location of Geo's family home, which at her instruction he was the first to post publicly, and he used Google Maps to reclassify the house as a “Dog Business,” an episode that entered Ninzard slang.18,19,20 Later that summer Geo's administrator Alonso offered him $600 to delete BobaBase and give up the name “Al Commando”; Commando asked to be paid in Ethereum, then supplied a fake email address, and Geo demoted Alonso for failing to close the deal.21

2022 presidential election

Al Commando was the Poo Party's nominee in the presidential election of July 2022. Cat saw him as one of her two most dangerous rivals for second place, and funded agitators to amplify the most annoying elements of his party so that he looked like the leader of a riot rather than a government.22 Vice President Snowby dreaded governing alongside someone as chaotic as him.23 Under approval voting he won 265 of 490 votes (54.1%), behind Apollos and Cat, who was seated as president when Apollos resigned.2,24 Lless later said he had voted for Commando purely as a protest.25

The “Edgy Era”

As autumn came, Al Commando escalated his rhetoric to hold the community's attention. He began claiming that 23% of Ninzards were Jewish and controlled the community's news platforms, a claim the far right of Nightwork took up; in reality the platforms were controlled by Cat and her circle, who had secretly spread the claim themselves.5 When Geo remarked on 16 October 2022 that Zavil “didn't act Black,” Commando accused him of racism, and two days later Geo posted his most famous rant against him. Commando read it out over a Neon Genesis Evangelion clip and posted the result as “It's funny how they call him ‘The Al Commando.’” Snowby spread it across the news platforms, it drew several hundred views and spawned imitations, and Cat left a comment under it. When Geo banned the video in Nightwork-Main, Mainians began posting disguised links to it.5

Unchecked by Rony or Apollos, he then took on shock imagery: posts featuring knives and implied threats, photographs with the Iron Cross and the Reichskriegsflagge, and the slogan “Heil Catsland,” which Poopulists read as mockery of the “authoritarian” charges against Cat and outsiders read as neo-Nazi aesthetics. The archive dates the “Edgy Era” of BobaBase from this shift, and notes that its ambiguity was the point. Lyman called him an “ally,” NGin said he should have won the presidency, and Cat privately praised his “boldness.”5 Hokan saw him as a symptom of Apollos's moral rot.26

After the N16 Incident Al Commando posted a second video, “Geo says THE N-WORD!?!?!?!?,” parodying Death Note. Hokan botted it to have it suppressed, accused Commando of buying views, and blocked him, which effectively ended his YouTube meme machine.27 On 29 October a message from Commando to a new Main moderator was enough for Geo to lock down his server,28 and the next day, as Geo fell apart, Commando messaged him out of concern; he then shared their private exchange, whose contents Geo denied, and it spread across the community.29 Cyntaku, who watched it from the sidelines, later wrote of the feud:

I watched Geo go to war with Al Commando. It wasn't a battle. Geo was unraveling in real time.

— Cyntaku, recalling 202230

Departure

In the Main snap election of November 2022 Commando backed the Orie Sundae, who, unknown to voters, was working for Cat.31 When Apollos was being stalked at his university that month, Commando and Zavil stayed in his dorm room until 4 a.m. hoping to catch whoever kept pulling the fire alarm.32 Days later Apollos posted a video showing his friends' faces and lashed out when Zavil asked him to take it down. Zylosted and Zavil left Ninzard on 18 November, Al Commando left too, and although Apollos apologized within the hour, the three refused to forgive him.6

Meanwhile General Xorbit, facing allegations of his own, had paid a female member to try to “honeypot” Al Commando and then accused him of being a groomer. Once the allegations against Xorbit were confirmed, Commando answered on 21 November 2022 that Xorbit was making things up because he had “nothing against me,” calling it “Geo-tier behaviour.” It was one of his last public statements before he disappeared from the community.7 Cat had his address, but honoured Apollos's request to leave his former friends alone.33

Return (2025)

By July 2025 the Gang of Five had reconciled, all except Zylosted, and Al Commando was again among Apollos's friends on a group call that summer.34 During Apollos's comeback campaign that September, Commando provided the voiceover for the short reply, made in five minutes, with which the president answered an attack ad from Aceiron's campaign.35 In November his mocking Discord status about General Mel's refusal to close Volley for Thanksgiving drew a rebuke from her and set off a wave of members mocking her the same way.36

NGin's campaign ad for Volley that month targeted Commando with antisemitic imagery alongside Apollos and Aceiron.37 In the party's Iron Flank Debate of 17 November he argued that NGin's followers were so extreme that they would alienate the rest of the community even if they won; when Matcha accused him of caring more about being mocked than about their hostility, he replied, “Of course I'm opposed to this.”38 After Cyntaku won Volley that week, she thanked him for his meme videos: “Considering where we were at in 2022, it's kind of surreal having you in my corner, but I'm really glad you are.”39

As Apollos's “Propagandaminister” he made the Capital's videos. His teaser of 13 December 2025, a beacon of light and a man falling from the sky to “Hooked on a Feeling,” heralded the return of Hyperio to the race in Scope.40 His Christmas message, a parody of NGin's ad that replaced its music with “Merry Christmas” and urged everyone to stay on the nice list, was widely applauded as a wholesome answer to the neo-Nazis; NGin counted him among the “race traitors.”8

Legislature XI (2026)

By January 2026 Al Commando sat in Legislature XI for Zarden, a small community named in homage to the Broader Project of Zarden that became Ninzard's sixth district in 2026.41,42,43 In a private group chat with Gale, Moctezuma, Rickard and Shuffle he helped stage “nightmare blunt rotations,” adding rival members to the same chat to provoke them; on 23 January Evhom, a Jewish member of Parry, accused him of antisemitism.44 In the debate of 2 February on admitting Kaimachi to the Preservate Apparatus, Seandry reminded Grace that she had once called Commando a “security risk,” and Vice President Spinzle argued that bringing provocative figures inside had worked with Apollos, with Commando and “kind of” with Gale.45 After the Chud War he posted an AI image of NGin and his followers as a K-pop group, which spread widely after Woofer and PluPlu reacted to it.46

When the Preservation Party reorganized on 24 February 2026, he became a party operative for Zarden in Apollos's Pragma Flank.42 That night he alerted Apollos to NGin's planned coup against the opposition, the Samson Switch.47 He was one of the fifteen representatives in Legislature XI when its debates on the Charter began on 28 February,3 and when Adelynn and Spinzle unexpectedly agreed on Part I, he wrote, “Oh my god it's happening.”48 On 2 April Woofer made him District Coordinator for Zarden, one of the twelve core insiders who led the Apparatus until 2 July 2026.9

You know the economy is cooked when the Ninzard president is giving more jobs for the people than the actual government

— Al Commando, on the Ninzard Career Initiative, March 202649

As NGin's movement lost its power to intimidate that spring, Commando dismissed one of his voice memos as “washed,” and answered the declaration in Legislature XI that the Holy Iron Flank was over with the line quoted at the head of this article, to wide agreement.50 The Flank's members nicknamed him “Kippah Commando.”51 At the broadcast of 11 May 2026 in which Apollos signed the Charter, he was one of the seven representatives who voted against the joke motion to impeach the president.52 In the presidential election that June his home district voted 74.1% for Poka.53

Later life

Zarden had no representatives in Legislature XII, which was seated under President Poka in July 2026.54 In August Al Commando passed on to Poka's circle the story of Apollos's unsuccessful courtship of a university student, including the banana Apollos had brought to the beach, which Poka recorded in the archive.55

Legacy

The archive remembers Al Commando as the face of the Summer of Schizoposting and the “Edgy Era,” and leaves open whether his shock imagery was extremism or satire of the community's fear of it.5 It also records how little of his persona was his own doing: Raffaele built the myth, and Cat used it to keep the Preservates fixed on BobaBase while she consolidated her power.4 His “Dog Business” prank gave Ninzard a lasting piece of slang, and his old adversary Cyntaku ended up thanking him for his videos.20,39
