Guilherme Machado (born 5 October 2001), known as Gui, is a Portuguese-American Ninzard politician and administrator from Sacramento, California.1 He served as the 2nd Chief of Administration of Nightwork-Parry from 2 January 2017 to 12 August 2022.2,3 He ranks among the longest-tenured staff members in the history of the district.2

Machado received his initial appointment from District General Kain.2 He maintained administrative continuity across multiple leadership changes in Parry.2 He served under Kain and Atria.2,4 Later he worked under Helen and Aurase.2,5 He concluded his administrative tenure under General Xorbit.3

In August 2022, Machado clashed with Xorbit over server governance.3 Machado voiced vocal opposition to President Cat's Katastoles crackdowns.3 He also challenged Xorbit's rapid expansion policies and alleged financial misconduct.3 Xorbit removed Machado from office on 12 August 2022, provoking widespread community backlash.3 Machado later ran for District General of Parry three times as a candidate for the Preservation Party.6,7

Early life and appointment (2001-2017)

Machado was born on 5 October 2001.1 He grew up in Sacramento, California, within a Portuguese-American household.1 He joined the Ninzard Project during its early TeamSpeak era, associating with the Sacramento cohort that eventually formed the Nightian Faction.8

On 2 January 2017, newly elected Parry District General Kain appointed Machado as the 2nd Chief of Administration.2 Machado took responsibility for user registration and day-to-day server permissions.2

Chief of Administration (2017-2022)

Machado provided administrative stability in Parry during frequent political turnover.2 He retained his post when independent candidate Atria defeated Kain in January 2019.4 He continued his duties when Helen Kang assumed the generalship in July 2019.9

During the Underground Era and the subsequent lockdown policies of Governor Syn, Machado opposed arbitrary bans.10 He retained his office through the transition to BobaBitcoin under Kain and later under Aurase.5 Colleagues viewed Machado as an accessible official who prioritized routine moderation over factional warfare.2

Removal and opposition to Catx3 (2022)

In July 2022, Machado ran for District General of Parry as a Preservation Party candidate against Aurase and Xorbit, receiving seven votes.6 Following Xorbit's victory, Machado resumed his duties as Chief of Administration.3

Tensions emerged almost immediately.3 President Cat initiated the Katastoles, carrying out severe purges and doxxing campaigns against political targets.11 Machado publicly denounced the Katastoles as an abuse of authority.3

Machado also raised objections to Xorbit's local governance.3 Xorbit initiated aggressive recruitment drives in Sacramento synagogues to pad membership counts.12 Machado warned that rapid expansion invited sectarian friction and compromised server security.3 He accused Xorbit of corruption and mismanagement of community resources.3

On 12 August 2022, Xorbit dismissed Machado from his administrative post.3 The decision triggered immediate outrage across Parry.3 Machado held broad personal popularity among rank-and-file members.3 Xorbit ignored protests and left the office of Chief of Administration vacant rather than name a successor.3

Later political campaigns (2023-2026)

Following his dismissal, Machado remained active in the Preservation Party.7 In July 2023, he challenged incumbent General Rudolf for leadership of Parry.7 Machado campaigned on democratic norms and stable moderation.7 He secured 69 votes (44.8%), falling short of unseating Rudolf.7

Machado ran against Rudolf again in December 2023.13 Rudolf retained the seat, while Machado garnered 33 votes (27.3%).13 During the 2024 political cycles, Machado supported Governor Adelynn Leclaire's efforts to isolate Cat's influence.14 He supported the ratification of the Ninzard Charter in May 2026.15
