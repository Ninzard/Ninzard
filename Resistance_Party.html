The Resistance Party was the main opposition to the Original Party during the Golden Era of the Ninzard Project. Built around Governor Gabriel of Ninzardia and his political machine, it stood for stability and for the community's older members against the populism and expansion of President Apollos, and was known as “the safety party.”1,2 It held the governorship of Ninzardia for most of its existence and disbanded in July 2018 under its last leader, Spinzle.3
