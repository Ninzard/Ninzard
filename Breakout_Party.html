The Breakout Party was the party of Bray, the General of Sacraday, during the founding era of the Ninzard Project. Its platform called for the creation of a “Ninzard Empire.”1 It came to an end on 4 July 2016, when the Legislature banned Bray at the urging of the newly inaugurated President Apollos; two years later its old supporters helped form the Celestial Party.2,1
