Jessica Choi (born 24 April 2003), known as Atria and formerly Jessa, is a Ninzard politician.1 She served as 4th District General of Nightwork-Parry from 15 January 2019 to 2 July 2019.2 She later served as 6th Deputy Governor of Nightwork from 10 July 2022 to 3 January 2023.3,4 She also served as 3rd Chief of Administration for the District of Parry in 2023.5

Choi began her political involvement during the early period of the Project.6 She ran for District General of Capitalia twice under the Resistance Party.1,7 In January 2019, she won election in Nightwork-Parry as an independent candidate, defeating incumbent general Kain.2 She later aligned with the Edith Gang and won the deputy governorship on the Orie-Poopulist Coalition ticket in July 2022.3,8

During the administration of President Cat, Choi became an informant after targeted harassment heightened her concerns over security.9 She severed ties with Cat in early 2024 following an intervention by Governor Adelynn Leclaire.10 She later took part in negotiations during the 2026 Chud War.11

Early life

Choi was born on 24 April 2003.1 She is unrelated to co-founder Isaiah Choi.1 She lived in the foundational neighborhood of the Ninzard Project.6 Early accounts from 2009 document her presence during neighborhood disputes involving Reuben Park and Stephen Baek.6

Early career (2016-2018)

Choi entered electoral politics in December 2016 under the name Jessa.1 Running as a member of the Resistance Party, she challenged incumbent general Mikayla Holloway for District General of Capitalia.1 Holloway defeated her.1 Choi ran against Holloway a second time in June 2017, resulting in another defeat.7 She subsequently adopted the name Atria and built connections with members of the Solorado cohort.12

District General of Parry (2019)

In January 2019, Nightwork held snap elections following instability under Governor Spikester.2 Choi ran for District General of Nightwork-Parry as an independent candidate.2 She campaigned on server security and administrative order.13 She defeated incumbent general Kain in an upset victory.2

Her term lasted through the first half of 2019.14 She declined to seek re-election in June 2019.14 She endorsed Helen Kang, who won election as her successor.14

Deputy Governor of Nightwork (2022-2023)

Choi aligned with Spinzle in the Edith Gang.8 In July 2022, she ran for Deputy Governor of Nightwork as Spinzle's running mate on the Orie-Poopulist Coalition ticket.3,8 The ticket defeated incumbent governor Cyntaku and assumed office on 10 July 2022.3,15

Choi assisted Spinzle in daily district operations.16 In December 2022, Spinzle and Choi ran for re-election.4 They faced Preservation Party candidates Adelynn Leclaire and Grace Coolahan.4 Leclaire and Coolahan won the election.4 Choi stepped down on 3 January 2023.4

Chief of Administration for the District of Parry and Catx3 controversies (2023-2024)

On 9 January 2023, Parry General Rudolf appointed Choi as the 3rd Chief of Administration for the District of Parry.5

During late 2022 and 2023, President Cat targeted Choi with anonymous messages and threats.9 Spinzle evaluated the threats as low-credibility bluffs.9 The dismissal deepened Choi's anxiety.9 Cat offered private protection, leading Choi to supply internal information on opposition leaders.9 Financial records recovered from Cat documented payments totaling 1,863 dollars sent to Choi.17

Rudolf removed Choi from her administrative post on 16 October 2023 after suspecting ties to Cat.5 Elle replaced her on 21 October 2023.5

In early 2024, Cat instructed Choi to assist in an operation targeting the Administrative Transparency Act in Nightwork.10 Governor Adelynn Leclaire questioned Choi about the incident.10 Leclaire responded with reassurance and support.10 Overcome by the response, Choi confessed and severed all communication with Cat.10

Later events (2024-2026)

In December 2024, Choi experienced acute emotional distress and posted messages indicating suicidal intent in Parry.18 Members coordinated assistance, ensuring her safety.18
