Reuben Apollos Park (born 4 April 2004), known as Apollos, is a Ninzard politician who served as the 2nd President of the Ninzard Project from 2016 to 2022 and as the 4th President from 2025 to 2026. He is the only president in the community's history to serve non-consecutive terms, and its longest-serving leader.1

Elected at the age of twelve as the nominee of the populist Original Party, Apollos won three terms and presided over the Golden Era, the RSN War, the Underground Era and the Great Polarization, leaving a mixed legacy of sustained longevity and negligence.2 Out of office, he secretly rebuilt Waffle under the alias “Assailant,” serving as its 4th District General before unmasking himself on 1 April 2023, a reveal that exposed how deeply his successor, Catx3, had infiltrated Nightwork.3

Apollos worked alongside Spinzle, Kain, Kay, ViaTea, and 7orB on the counterintelligence dossier file gathering effort against President Cat, who was eventually exposed on 19 August 2025.

After the fall of Catx3, Apollos returned in August 2025 as the nominee of the Preservation Party and defeated Caleb Jensen with 62.2% of the vote.4 His second presidency produced the Charter of the Ninzard Project, which outlawed the Dark Arts and was ratified on 11 May 2026 in a broadcast in which he signed the document and then ate pineapple pizza off it.5 He left office on 1 July 2026 with an 80% approval rating.6 The next day, the tenth anniversary of his first inauguration, his protégé Poka was inaugurated as the 5th President.28

Early life

Park was born on 4 April 2004 and joined the Ninzard Project as a child. An alternate Discord account he created in 2015, “chonax,” resurfaced years later as the face of his Assailant persona.3 Among the community's original members he was known by the Korean nickname 도토리 (Dotori, “acorn”), a name he later said only the OGs still remember.7

Park joined the Original Party, which had begun as the Tomato Party, in 2014, and on 11 July 2015 he succeeded Kain as its Boss. During the Ninzard Civil War of 2015–2016 he appeared on Hyperio's side under the name chonax. In December 2015, in elections held over President Antareze's objection, he defeated Orion1 to become General of Hybriden, taking office on 1 January 2016.8 There he promoted Raffaele to administrator,9 and Abby succeeded him when he became president.10 Only years later did he learn that Orion1 had been Antareze all along.11

First presidency (2016–2022)

2016 election

Apollos's election on 2 July 2016 shocked Ninzard's entire establishment. Running with the populist Original Party, he won his largest margin in Nightwork, and most of the newly elected district generals came from his party, giving the Ories one of the strongest mandates in the community's history.10 He later listed his own one-to-one campaigning of 2016 among the community's “game-changing metas.”12 Vice President Hokan and Capital Adjudicator MMC moved quickly to ally with the twelve-year-old president, who governed in a “minarchial” style.2

Golden Era

The first two years of his presidency became known as the Golden Era. In 2017 Apollos and his friends formed the Third Faction, the first break from the Ninzard–Nightwork binary: an apolitical, lightly regulated community built around gaming events and Minecraft, open to anyone in Ninzard. Alongside Capital Adjudicator Raffaele, his administration also opened the community's official record, the same record from which this page was recovered.13

Gold Road and the RSN War

The Golden Era ended in 2018. Gold Road, a rebel branch that had broken away in protest of his administration, collapsed in tragedy, and the Legislature removed Vice President Hokan for his absences. Rather than hold the scheduled election, the Legislature voted on 2 July 2018 to keep Apollos in office and appointed his pick, Slamo, as the 5th Vice President.14

Through the RSN War, waged by Seandry's Rocket Squad Nation, Apollos dismissed the self-declared “Sun King” as crazy and ignored his daily screeds. When the war ended, the community demanded a real vote. On 12 August 2018, without campaigning, Apollos won 197 of 353 votes (55.8%), far ahead of Governor DegreeZ.15

Underground Era

His second term coincided with Governor Spikester's “Progression” and the rise of the Underground, a sprawling network of hacker and scam platforms. After Slamo resigned on 17 May 2019, Apollos reinstated Hokan as the 6th Vice President, the first vice president to serve non-consecutively.16 In 2020, as revolt against the Capital grew, he replaced Hokan with the anti-expansionist Helen as the 7th Vice President and appointed Grace as Capital Adjudicator.17

Apollos sought re-election to finish cleaning up the Underground, expressing disbelief that he was “a 16-year-old kid who governed over a dangerous community of hackers and scammers.” At the only debate of the 2020 race, held on 2 August against Czulia and Clenzy, he warned that Clenzy's proposed RAT force would provoke rival communities and invite future leaders to abuse it.18

「the dark arts have consumed the ninzard project. the only way to fix it now is to eliminate its source—the underground.」

— President Apollos, 2020 presidential debate, 2 August 2020

Ten days later he won 60.3% of the vote, the widest margin of any presidential election, and within three days symbolically dismantled every remaining tie to the Underground platforms.19

Great Polarization and departure

By his third term, the Preservation Party had risen against the Ories. In the 2021 vice-presidential election Apollos quietly backed the dark-horse candidate Snowby against his own vice president, Helen; Snowby won and became the 8th Vice President on 25 December 2021.20 He resigned as Boss of the Original Party on 3 May 2022.21

Apollos stood again in July 2022 and led the count with 340 votes, but posted his resignation moments before the polls closed on 19 July. Under approval voting, the runner-up, Cat, was seated as the 3rd President the next day.22

Between presidencies

The Catx3 Regime

Cat's presidency, known as the Catx3 Regime, made Apollos one of its principal targets. In October 2022 her phantom identity “Mauricio” claimed responsibility for an $18,000 bounty on his head, opening a harassment campaign that ran into 2024. Isolated after a falling-out with his “Gang of Five” and a scathing lecture from Hokan in December 2022, he confided in Cat herself, unaware of her role.23

General Assailant

With Cat's help and Governor Spinzle's sponsorship, Apollos took on a new identity and rebuilt the long-lost district of Waffle from scratch. On 1 December 2022 he took office as General Assailant, the 4th District General of Waffle, succeeding Czulia. Wafflers believed him to be a British man. Within two months he had restored a vibrant community, and he was re-elected as an independent. What began as an attempt to prove his critics wrong turned into an effort to expose Cat.3

After promising a “big April Fools' announcement,” Assailant revealed himself as the former president at midnight on 1 April 2023. Governor Adelynn banned him for impersonation, and Kendall, nominated by the Original Party, won the snap election that followed. Wafflers later came to view the stunt with gratitude, as a catalyst for Cat's eventual downfall.3,4

Second presidency (2025–2026)

Comeback

Cat's de facto removal in August 2025 left Ninzard without a Capital for the first time in its history, and Aceiron declared himself de facto president. The Preservates searched for “literally anyone else” before turning to Apollos. Former Governor Cyntaku led the resistance, while Seandry, once leader of the RSN, joined Grace, Woofer and Adelynn in arguing for him.24

On 29 August 2025 Apollos announced his candidacy with a video intercutting clips of his younger self and ending in a diss track. He chose Spinzle as his running mate, won the nomination of the Preservation Party the next day, and founded the Pragma Flank on 1 September.25

2025 election

Of 757 votes cast, the largest election to that point, with 97.1% turnout, Apollos won 471 (62.2%) to Aceiron's 286 (37.8%). He carried every district except Volley, including an upset in the Orie stronghold of Parry and 92% in Waffle, where voters remembered Assailant. For the Golden Era cohort it was a “Welcome Home” victory.4

Capital and cabinet

Apollos was inaugurated on 22 September 2025 at the age of 21, pledging to serve a single term ending on 1 July 2026. His administration rebuilt the servers of Main, Parry, Scope, Volley and Waffle in under 24 hours, after which he issued a Trump-style statement promising to “MAKE NINZARD GREAT AGAIN… AND THIS TIME, WE'RE DOING IT THE RIGHT WAY.” On 3 November 2025 he split the office of Capital Adjudicator into three ministries: Justice (Kay), Health & Privacy (7orB) and Activity (Poka).26

The Charter

Apollos entered office with a single goal, kept quiet during the campaign to avoid politicizing it: a charter that would give the community lasting rules and institutions.4 The Charter of the Ninzard Project, a constitutional framework designed to permanently outlaw the Dark Arts, won an 82% supermajority on 7 May 2026.

The community expected a solemn signing. Instead, on 11 May, Apollos posted a video lasting two minutes and twenty-two seconds. He crumpled a parody “Goonstitution,” pulled the real Charter out of a pizza box, signed it in green pen beside a crude doodle, then ate ranch-drenched pineapple pizza off the page. Ninzard historians regard it as “the absolute pinnacle of statecraft through shitposting.” Apollos called the method Theatrics of Theatrics: by stripping the Charter of its intimidating aura, he signalled that the rules were absolute but the government enforcing them was “just a bunch of idiot gamers.”5

Farewell

With his work done, Apollos declared the start of a “lame duck era 🦆” on 30 May 2026. On 1 July he delivered a ten-minute farewell video that opened, “After July 1st, I am kaput. Capiche?” He told the community it had helped him more than he had helped it, and closed with a plea to live with grace and passion.27 He left Ninzard that same day. On 2 July, the tenth anniversary of his first inauguration, Poka, his former Minister of Activity, was inaugurated as the 5th President.28

Post-presidency and personal life

Park processed the community's trauma through a return to Christian theology, embracing agape, the self-sacrificial love of the other. In August 2026, during the Salon War in Parry, he answered a member's ongoing harassment not with a ban but with a two-hour call spent playing worship songs on acoustic guitar, an episode remembered as “Apollos's Acoustic Worship Intervention.”29

Political style

Apollos distrusted groupthink and tended to oppose popular opinion, popular people and, at times, the Original Party he had built. His shifts, most notably from expansionism to anti-expansionism, led critics to call him unprincipled and left loyal supporters feeling betrayed.1 He campaigned through diss tracks and videos, and in his second term perfected a deliberately unserious register, from the Uncle Samite persona to the pizza broadcast, meant to lower the temperature of a traumatized community.5 Asked in 2020 what the community was for, he answered: to have fun, play games, meet new friends and build new bonds.18

Detractors pointed to his volatility. Objecting to his 2025 nomination, Cyntaku said: “He's unstable, he can't keep a schedule to save his life, and he is exhausting to be around.”24

Legacy

Apollos left office with an 80% approval rating, permanently overwriting the legacy of his first presidency, and is credited with dismantling the e-cult culture of the Ninzard Project.6 The Dark Arts he vowed to root out in 2020 were finally outlawed by his own Charter six years later. His first presidency remains the longest tenure of any Ninzard leader, achieved at a heavy cost to his mental health.1

If you have fire, if you have passion, if you have love… do not hide it!

— President Apollos, farewell address, 1 July 202627
