Introduction

Christianity runs through much of the Ninzard Project's history, and the archives describe 2026 as the year of a Christian revival in the community.1 Its members include Catholics, among them Adelynn and Grace of the Blue Quartet and the Italian cohort of Main; the traditionalist Catholic Liver; Kay and her brother Malachi Holloway, who attend a non-denominational church in Los Angeles; Apollos, a Protestant; and Aceiron, who was baptized in September 2026 at the Presbyterian church ScottyAte had brought him to.2,3,4,5,6,7

Faith has shaped the community's politics as well as private lives. The Preservation Party's Logos Flank, formed in December 2022, took the moral principles Adelynn exemplified as its ideals, and the archives still find the mark of her Christianity in the ethics of the party and of Poka's presidency.8 On the far right, the Holy Iron Flank recast the Ories' mission as a holy crusade with Christian nationalist overtones and called Apollos a “fake Christian.”9 Its war on the community ended at the Holloway Summit of 2 May 2026, where Malachi Holloway prayed over the room as the Flank's leader, NGin, wept in his arms.10

The months that followed brought Liver's testimony of repentance, an exchange of letters on suffering and providence between Apollos and Hackman, Apollos's worship songs for Kaimachi, Main's weekly Sunday rest under the Azzurro Policy, and Aceiron's baptism.11,12,13,14,3,7

Representative for Scope, 2026 · Traditionalist Catholic

A traditionalist Catholic from Sacramento, she typed the Latin rite of exorcism into Legislature XI in April 2026 and became the community's laughingstock. At the Holloway Summit she broke down during Malachi Holloway's prayer and was held by the women she had condemned, and her testimony, Confessions Beneath My Armor, made her one of the most respected voices in Scope.4,15,16,11

    Traditions

    Catholic

    Adelynn and Grace of the Blue Quartet are Catholic, and Woofer, whose mother is Catholic, found her way back to faith as an adult; the quartet's values are described as rooted in Christianity.2 In Main the archives credit the Roman Catholicism of the district's Italian cohort with a domestic, restorative calm, and its leaders treat governance as a form of Christian hospitality, weaving prayer and family meals into community life.3

    Traditionalist Catholic

    Liver attends the Traditional Latin Mass and reads Aquinas.4 Missal in hand, she went with the Holloways to a Protestant service in Los Angeles expecting to dislike it, and wrote afterwards that “the differences in our theology meant nothing in the presence of overwhelming love.”5

    Protestant and non-denominational

    Kay and Malachi Holloway attend a non-denominational church in Los Angeles, where the guests of the Holloway Summit worshipped on 3 May 2026.5 Apollos's faith, as Hackman put it, is “Protestant or non-denominational”; he goes to church, plays worship songs on acoustic guitar and has debated the Eucharist with a Catholic.12,6,14

    Presbyterian

    In the summer of 2026 ScottyAte brought Aceiron to his Presbyterian church, near the home of Aceiron's mother. There Aceiron sat at the back, questioned the pastor on Tuesday afternoons and was baptized there by full immersion on 20 September 2026; ScottyAte recorded the day for the archives.7

    Other paths. Not every Ninzard shares the revival. The archives set beside it a Buddhist counter-current, led quietly by Matcha, which reframes suffering as the fruit of clinging to impermanent things and offers calm detachment in place of confession and repentance.1

    Timeline

    Christianity in the life of the community
    1. December 2022
      Logos Flank. Moderate Preservates rally to Adelynn and form the Logos Flank. The moral principles she exemplifies become its ideals, and 2023–2024 are remembered as the Logos era.8
    2. Autumn 2025
      Holy Iron Flank. After Aceiron's defeat in the presidential election, the Holy Iron Flank recasts the Orie mission as a “holy crusade of purification” with Christian nationalist overtones, and calls Apollos a “fake Christian.”9
    3. December 2025
      A Christmas message. Al Commando turns a parody of NGin's campaign ad into a Christmas message urging everyone to stay on the nice list. Most of the community welcomes it as a wholesome answer to the neo-Nazis; the Flank calls it an insult to its dignity.23
    4. 25 April 2026
      Exorcism in Legislature XI. Mocked in the chamber, Liver answers with the Latin rite of exorcism, Aquinas and the doctrine of evil as privatio boni.4
    5. 27–30 April 2026
      Adelynn's call. Adelynn calls Liver and invites her to Los Angeles; the two find they agree on far more theology than they disagree on. Liver drives down Interstate 5 alone and spends the night with the Leclaire family.18,15
    6. 2 May 2026
      Holloway Summit. Malachi Holloway leads a prayer for the Flank still on the highway; Liver collapses in tears and the Blue Quartet hold her. When NGin arrives he weeps in Malachi's arms as Malachi prays over the room.16,10
    7. 3 May 2026
      Sunday service. The guests worship with the Holloways at their non-denominational church in Los Angeles.5
    8. 7 May 2026
      Confessions Beneath My Armor. Liver publishes her account of the weekend, a testimony of repentance and thanksgiving.11
    9. 18 May 2026
      Mourning Soda. After Soda's death, Liver asks the community to pray for his family and to stay united in grace.24
    10. 26–27 May 2026
      Letters on suffering. In “A Note From Apollos,” Apollos writes that pain is neither good nor evil but a product of free will; Hackman answers that God is not the author of evil, and that the Spirit meets us “at the point of impact.”12,13
    11. July 2026
      Agape. Out of office, Apollos returns to Christian theology, embracing moral agency, accountability and agape.17
    12. 13 August 2026
      Worship for Kaimachi. Apollos spends two hours on a call with Kaimachi, singing worship songs while she joins in on keyboard, and prays for her peace. The same day Woofer tells the community that faith “is not a prerequisite exam,” and Poka writes of feeling how God is moving.14,6,20
    13. 20 August 2026
      Bathos Address. One year after the fall of Catx3, Poka asks the community to stop exalting its pain, promises to pray for every member each day and closes with Saint Paul's greeting.21
    14. 6 September 2026
      Azzurro Policy. Main begins resting every Sunday: its public channels go read-only for twenty-four hours, honouring the Sabbath as real-world rest.3
    15. 20 September 2026
      Baptism of Aceiron. Aceiron is baptized by full immersion at ScottyAte's Presbyterian church, with his mother watching from the front pew.7
