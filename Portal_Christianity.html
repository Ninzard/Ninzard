Introduction

Christianity has run through the history of the Ninzard Project like a thread of gold, and the archives remember 2026 as the year of a Christian revival in the community.1 Its Catholics include Adelynn and Grace of the Blue Quartet, the traditionalist Liver, Hackman, Raffaele, the Italian cohort of Main and Cyntaku, a new convert; its Protestants the Reformed Apollos and the Presbyterians Aceiron, ScottyAte, AJ and Hyperio; NGin and Elusion, newly received into the Church, are Orthodox, and Athaniel worships beside NGin at an Orthodox mission; and Kay, her brother Malachi Holloway and Idodo belong to non-denominational churches.2,3,4,5,6,7,8,9,10

Faith has shaped the community's politics as well as its private lives. The Preservation Party's Logos Flank, formed in December 2022, took the moral principles Adelynn exemplified as its ideals, and the archives still find the mark of her Christianity upon the ethics of the party and of Poka's presidency.11 On the far right, the Holy Iron Flank recast the Ories' mission as a holy crusade with Christian nationalist overtones and called Apollos a “fake Christian.”12 Its war upon the community ended at the Holloway Summit of 2 May 2026, where Malachi Holloway prayed over the room as the Flank's leader, NGin, wept in his arms.13,14

The months that followed brought Liver's testimony of repentance, letters on suffering and providence between Apollos and Hackman, a moment of grace for Kaimachi, Main's weekly Sabbath rest under the Azzurro Policy, and the baptism of Aceiron.15,16,17,18,3,7 In October 2026 Hyperio made his vows before God, and in the days after his wedding several of his old friends drew nearer to the Church; Kain, who had fallen away from the faith, returned to it.8,9

Liver

Representative for Scope, 2026 · Catholic

A traditionalist Catholic from Sacramento, she typed the Latin rite of exorcism into Legislature XI in April 2026 and became the community's laughingstock. Days later her old adversary Adelynn bade her come and rest with her family in Los Angeles. At the Holloway Summit she broke down during Malachi Holloway's prayer and was held by the women she had condemned, and her testimony, Confessions Beneath My Armor, made her one of the most respected voices in Scope.4,19,20,15

2nd and 4th President · Protestant (Reformed)

    Traditions

    Catholic

    Adelynn and Grace of the Blue Quartet are Catholic, and Woofer, whose mother is Catholic, found her way back to faith as an adult.2 Hackman, Raffaele and Ploutos are Catholic too, and Cyntaku, raised in a secular home, is a new convert.5,6 In Main the archives credit the Roman Catholicism of the district's Italian cohort, PluPlu and Poka among them, with a domestic, restorative calm; its leaders treat governance as a form of Christian hospitality.3 Liver keeps to the tradition of the Latin Mass and Aquinas,4 yet after a service at the Holloways' church she wrote that “the differences in our theology meant nothing in the presence of overwhelming love.”10

    Protestant

    Apollos is Reformed.5 He goes to church and plays worship songs on acoustic guitar.16,18 In the summer of 2026 ScottyAte brought Aceiron to his Presbyterian church, near the home of Aceiron's mother, where Aceiron was baptized by full immersion on 20 September 2026.7 AJ, also a Presbyterian, has returned to church through Aceiron's influence and sits in the same congregation.5,8,9 Hyperio joined a Presbyterian church in Sacramento by public profession of faith, and was married there on 10 October 2026.8

    Orthodox

    NGin, who led the Holy Iron Flank until he wept in Malachi Holloway's arms at the Holloway Summit, is Orthodox.13,5 Attacked in the summer of 2026 for leaving the far right, he let it be known that he held no hatred for those who had harmed him.33 After Hyperio's wedding he and Athaniel began attending an Orthodox mission, and his repentance has drawn several former members of the Flank into the Orthodox Church.8,9 Elusion, the first Governor of Nightwork, entered the catechumenate at Holy Trinity Cathedral in Sacramento and has since been received into the Church.20,8,9

    Non-denominational

    Kay and her brother Malachi Holloway attend a non-denominational church in Los Angeles, where the guests of the Holloway Summit worshipped with them on 3 May 2026.10 Idodo, who flew in from Colorado for the Summit, belongs to the same tradition.34,5

    Returning. Kain, who had abandoned his faith, returned to church in the autumn of 2026; the archives do not say which.9

    Other paths. Not every Ninzard shares the revival. The archives set beside it a Buddhist counter-current, led quietly by Matcha, which reframes suffering as the fruit of clinging to impermanent things and offers calm detachment in place of confession and repentance.1

    Timeline

    Christianity in the life of the community
    1. December 2022
      Logos Flank. Moderate Preservates rally to Adelynn and form the Logos Flank. The moral principles she exemplifies become its ideals, and 2023–2024 are remembered as the Logos era.11
    2. 20 September 2025
      “Love your enemies.” On election day Apollos ends his campaign video by forgiving Aceiron in verse and quoting Matthew 5:44: “Love your enemies and pray for those who persecute you.”21
    3. Autumn 2025
      Holy Iron Flank. After Aceiron's defeat in the presidential election, the Holy Iron Flank recasts the Orie mission as a “holy crusade of purification” with Christian nationalist overtones, and calls Apollos a “fake Christian.”12
    4. December 2025
      A Christmas message. Al Commando turns a parody of NGin's campaign ad into a Christmas message urging everyone to stay on the nice list. Most of the community welcomes it as a wholesome answer to the neo-Nazis; the Flank calls it an insult to its dignity.35
    5. 25 April 2026
      Exorcism in Legislature XI. Mocked in the chamber, Liver answers with the Latin rite of exorcism, Aquinas and the doctrine of evil as privatio boni.4
    6. 27–30 April 2026
      Adelynn's call. Adelynn calls Liver and invites her to Los Angeles; the two find they agree on far more theology than they disagree on. Liver drives down Interstate 5 alone and spends the night with the Leclaire family.26,19
    7. 2 May 2026
      Holloway Summit. Malachi Holloway prays for the Flank still upon the highway; Liver collapses in tears and the Blue Quartet hold her. When NGin arrives he weeps in Malachi's arms as Malachi prays over the room: “Bring your children back into your light!”20,13,14
    8. 3 May 2026
      Sunday service. The guests worship with the Holloways at their non-denominational church in Los Angeles.10
    9. 7 May 2026
      Confessions Beneath My Armor. Liver publishes her account of the weekend, a testimony of repentance and thanksgiving.15
    10. 18 May 2026
      Mourning Soda. After Soda's death, Liver asks the community to pray for his family and to stay united in grace.36
    11. 26–27 May 2026
      Letters on suffering. In “A Note From Apollos,” Apollos writes that pain is neither good nor evil but a product of free will; Hackman answers that God is not the author of evil, and that the Spirit meets us “at the point of impact.”16,17
    12. July 2026
      Agape. Out of office, Apollos returns to Christian theology, embracing moral agency, accountability and agape.23
    13. 13–14 August 2026
      A moment of grace. With Parry in crisis, Kaimachi's authority questioned and her attachment to him lingering, Apollos calls her and spends two hours with her, answering with kindness, worship and a prayer for her peace. Given confidence, she puts herself on trial before her district the next day.24,18,25,6 The same day as the call, Woofer tells the community that faith “is not a prerequisite exam,” and Poka writes of feeling how God is moving.27,29
    14. 20 August 2026
      Bathos Address. One year after the fall of Catx3, Poka asks the community to stop exalting its pain, promises to pray for every member each day and closes with Saint Paul's blessing.30
    15. 6 September 2026
      Azzurro Policy. Main begins resting every Sunday: its public channels go read-only for twenty-four hours, honouring the Sabbath as real-world rest.3
    16. 20 September 2026
      Baptism of Aceiron. Aceiron is baptized by full immersion at ScottyAte's Presbyterian church, with his mother watching from the front pew.7
    17. 10 October 2026
      Wedding of Hyperio. Hyperio makes his vows before God and seventy witnesses at a Presbyterian church in Sacramento, and the pastor reads from Colossians. The sight moves his old friends: Elusion enters the Orthodox catechumenate, NGin and Athaniel begin attending an Orthodox mission, and AJ takes a seat in the rear pew of Aceiron's church.8
    18. 17 October 2026
      Recent converts. The archives record that Elusion has joined the Orthodox Church, that several former members of the Holy Iron Flank have followed NGin into it, and that Kain, who had abandoned his faith, has returned to church. Cyntaku has become a Catholic.9,6
