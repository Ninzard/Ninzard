Edith Bethany Arden (born 12 June 2003), known as Spinzle, is a Ninzard politician who served as the 10th Vice President of the Ninzard Project from 22 September 2025 to 1 July 2026, under President Apollos. She was previously the 7th Governor of Nightwork from 2022 to 2023, the only governor from outside the Preservation Party during its four years of dominance from 2020 to 2024.1,2

A member of the Resistance Party, she became General of Solorado and Deputy Governor of Ninzardia in 2017, and briefly held the community together as acting governor after the deaths of 2018 before leaving it altogether.3 She returned in 2019, rebuilt Scope as its general and in July 2022 won the Nightwork governorship for the Original Party with 66.9% of the vote. Her Integrity Protocols were designed to blunt the influence of Cat, who made her one of her chief targets, and she lost re-election to Adelynn by three votes in January 2023.4,5,6

Out of office she secretly gathered evidence against Cat, and her Dead Man's Switch of November 2024, though defeated by Cat in person, delivered that evidence to General Kay.7,8 She returned as Apollos's running mate in 2025, drafted the agreed text of Part I of the Charter of the Ninzard Project and left office alongside him. She is one of the contributors to the community's archival record.9,10,11

Early life

Arden was born on 12 June 2003 and grew up in Colorado.12,7 Her circle of real-life friends, known as the Edith Gang, grew largely out of Solorado's original cohort of 2012 and included Snowby, Aurase, Pieban, Soda, Kendall and Atria; Snowby and Kendall were her childhood friends.4,13 She was also a close real-life friend of Holidae.14

Ninzardia (2017–2018)

General of Solorado

Running for the Resistance Party on more moderate positions than its leadership, Spinzle defeated the Original Party's 7orB in the June 2017 election and took office as the 4th General of Solorado on 2 July 2017.15 That summer President Apollos nominated her as Capital Adjudicator. The Legislature rejected her along strict gender lines, every woman voting for her and every man against, amid jokes that the president was “white knighting” for a girl he liked. Club Korea, whose chats Apollos had opened to her, nicknamed her 얼음녀 (“Ice Girl”) for her deadpan manner.16

Deputy Governor

In September 2017 Holidae chose her as running mate for Governor of Ninzardia. Apollos broke with his own party to endorse them, and they won in a landslide; Spinzle became Deputy Governor on 1 October 2017.17 When Holidae withdrew from the community in early 2018, Spinzle quietly ran Ninzardia in her place, and in February 2018 she called the snap referendum that removed Sacraday's General Elsy.18

The Last Legislature

The deaths of Tiny2 and Holidae in June 2018 devastated Spinzle, who had been visiting Holidae often. When Governor Daniel resigned on 30 June, she became acting governor and convened the Legislature to settle the community's affairs.14,3 When Raffaele offered himself for the presidency, she warned that it would carry “the optics of dancing on his grave.” Asked why she would not take it herself, she replied:

Because my best friend killed herself.

— Deputy Governor Spinzle, to the Legislature, 2018

With a single sentence she reminded the community that real teenagers had died, and the Legislature kept Apollos in office without an election.3 At her suggestion it closed Sacraday and Solorado on 8 July 2018. She disbanded the Resistance Party, which she had led since Daniel's resignation, and left the community, telling Apollos he was “not obligated” to stay in his post.19

General of Scope (2021–2022)

Spinzle returned quietly in late 2019 as an independent and joined Scope with General Hackman's permission.20 Elected as its 8th general for the Original Party, defeating the incumbent Starlight, and taking office on 22 May 2021, she turned a chaotic, overlooked district into an intellectual and creative hub, and Scopians came to trust her above any other leader.21,4 A moderate of the party's establishment wing, she repeatedly turned down calls to lead the Ories and to run for governor, even as a “Draft Spinzle Initiative” gathered pace; Apollos called her “smart, reliable, and very competent.”22,4

Governor of Nightwork (2022–2023)

2022 election

On 30 April 2022 Apollos, Spinzle and the Poo Party formed the Orie–Poopulist Coalition and nominated her for governor against the Preservate incumbent, Cyntaku.23 Cat tried to win her over with offers of money towards her tuition. Spinzle turned them down, joking that the first was “too small for me to accept” and then asking Cat to stop “flexing” her wealth; Cat's ledger records $51,200 in offers that Spinzle refused. Cat bribed her original running mate, Runix, instead, and he was replaced by Atria once he was found to be leaking her campaign's plans.24,4

For clarification, I never sought to pursue a community of “peace, sunshine, and rainbows.” I only desire to pursue a less Balkanized community.

— General Spinzle, debating Governor Cyntaku, July 2022

The campaign's only gubernatorial debate was regarded as a public execution of the incumbent. Spinzle won 230 of 344 votes (66.9%), carrying every district except Volley and all 45 votes cast in Scope.25 Sworn in on 10 July 2022, a day late after Cyntaku briefly refused to concede, she gave a one-word inaugural address: “Hello.”26

The Integrity Protocols

Spinzle soon concluded that Nightwork's unrest was being manufactured by the Cat Capital, and abandoned her plan to govern passively.5 After an investigation into grooming allegations, she banned a Nightwork-Main moderator found to have preyed on female members.26 Her draft Nightian Constitution, presented on 22 August 2022, stalled for lack of consensus, but she enacted a set of reforms known as the Integrity Protocols. The Candidate Viability Standard required candidates to secure the public endorsement of 10% of their district's electorate, ending the flooding of ballots with meme candidates; the Administrative Transparency Act required generals to keep public logs of every ban and mute, cutting arbitrary bans by nearly 40%; and the General's Veto let three of the five district generals override the governor.5 She also approved the rebuilding of Waffle by the mysterious “Assailant,” later revealed to be Apollos.27

Cat's campaign against her

Cat came to hate Spinzle, whose financial independence and emotional detachment left her nothing to exploit.5 With Raffaele, she set out to wear down the administration through bot raids, leaks and staged crises, and persuaded Apollos that Spinzle's procedural approach was failing. Apollos came to distrust her judgment, her deputy Atria began passing information to Cat, and Snowby and Pieban, struggling financially, drifted toward Cat after finding Spinzle more absorbed in governance than in their troubles.28

Defeat

Against Adelynn in January 2023, Spinzle defended her hands-off approach while Adelynn accused her of passivity in the face of rapid expansion.6 Apollos endorsed Adelynn, criticizing Spinzle's handling of the influx of new members. Spinzle lost by three votes, 276 (49.7%) to 279 (50.3%), while outperforming the Ories' general nominees in every district.6

Out of office (2023–2025)

2023 vice-presidential election

In July 2023, at Raffaele's urging, the Ories nominated Spinzle for vice president against Cat's candidate, Meikaqu, and the Preservates joined them in the Bethany Alliance. She won Nightwork with 507 of 640 votes (79.2%), but lost the overall count 557 to 578 on totals swollen by Catsland and Lair, whose votes came from accounts Cat and Meikaqu controlled.29

Elder stateswoman

Spinzle stayed on as an elder stateswoman, trusted by both Cat and Governor Adelynn, who used her and General Kay as informal go-betweens.13 After the arson at Raffaele's home destroyed records of the community's founding, she began copying its archives with the help of the Channeling-4, and later served as Governor of the Archives before Hackman succeeded her.30 When old messages of hers containing ironic slurs leaked, Adelynn removed her moderator permissions; Spinzle accepted the decision without complaint.13

Behind a façade of indifference, she was investigating Cat. In early 2024 a test confirmed that Cat used a custom Discord client, and Spinzle later gained access to Cat's account for fourteen minutes, gathering evidence of money laundering involving Snowby and Kendall and of the grooming of minors. General Kay asked her to keep it sealed until the case was complete.13

The Dead Man's Switch

Fearing for Snowby, whose name was on Cat's shell companies, Spinzle chose a faster route. On 5 November 2024 she uploaded the evidence to a server in Frankfurt programmed to submit it to the FBI and the IRS unless her home computer kept checking in, then revealed the arrangement to Cat in a video call.7 Cat flew to Colorado instead. On the night of 6 November she forced her way into Spinzle's home, wiped her computer, assaulted her and threatened to ruin Snowby, and Spinzle stopped the timer.7 Stopping it alerted General Kay, who secured a copy of the dossier, so the evidence that would end Cat's presidency was now beyond Cat's reach.8 Cat then turned Snowby against her, and on 21 November 2024 Spinzle went on an indefinite hiatus.31

Endorsements and the fall of Cat

Returning in late December 2024, she named that winter the “Nightian Great Depression”32 and endorsed Aceiron for governor in a deliberately mock-prophetic post that described Nightwork as a “lifeless spreadsheet of numbers.” It moved him two to three points in the polls.2 Early in 2025 she left the community to join Apollos outside it,33 but endorsed Aceiron again that June, calling his opponent Matcha a “sanctimonious hypocrite.”34 On 19 August 2025 she was one of the six, with Kay, Apollos, Snuck, 7orB and ViaTea, who exposed the full extent of Cat's crimes. Cat's last known contact with the community was an anonymous call to Spinzle.35

Vice presidency (2025–2026)

2025 election

When the Preservates turned to Apollos to stop Aceiron, he named Spinzle as his running mate, describing her as calm under pressure and a complement to his more emotional personality. The party nominated them on 30 August 2025, over the objections of Cyntaku, who believed Spinzle “lacked a soul.”36 They won 471 of 757 votes (62.2%), and she became the 10th Vice President on 22 September 2025.1

In office

As vice president she co-signed the Cat Files, released to community leaders on 1 January 2026 and to the public on 12 January.37 During the Chud War of 14 February 2026 she opened a public negotiation channel, and her warning there helped disperse the crowd in the Sacramento parking lot.38 When three Orie students assaulted the concussed Apollos on his university campus on 5 March 2026, she used an AI-generated image to bluff his attacker into a written confession, keeping even her own party in the dark. She defended the deception as a matter of operational security, though many in the Preservate Apparatus remained uneasy.39

So, yes. The image is synthetic. But the written confession BadSpirit voluntarily provided to me to avoid that non-existent photo is entirely real.

— Vice President Spinzle, March 2026

She drafted the revised Part I of the Charter, which Legislature XI adopted unanimously after she and Adelynn persuaded Syn to vote for it,9 and helped coordinate the Capital's response when Aceiron was gravely injured in Los Angeles in March 2026.40 After the Holloway Summit of 2 May 2026 she wrote that “logic has its limits,” and when the Charter won 82% in its referendum that month, she publicly sparred with Representative Lless over its critics.41,42

A system can contain the damage, but only grace can heal the wound.

— Vice President Spinzle, 2 May 2026

Departure

Spinzle left office with Apollos on 1 July 2026 without issuing a final statement.10 Hackman succeeded her the next day as the 11th Vice President, under President Poka.43

Rivalry with Adelynn

Spinzle's defining intellectual rivalry was with Adelynn, who unseated her as governor in 2023 and later led the Preservation Party while Spinzle served as vice president. Their recorded exchanges trace the relationship from opposition to partnership.6,44

The 2022 debate

At their gubernatorial debate on 30 December 2022, Adelynn warned that the rapid growth of Parry and Main threatened the fairness of future elections, while Spinzle defended leaving growth to the districts themselves.6

Adelynn: I believe that the expansion of BobaBitcoin has had a detrimental impact on Nightwork politics. … At this rate, a candidate may only need to garner support in one district to win elections for the entire community.

Spinzle: I would suggest that these are simply normal electoral dynamics. If Nightwork-Volley's constituents desire the same rate of growth as Parry and Main, they'd vote for an expansionist candidate. It's a matter of democratic decision rather than dysfunction necessitating the governor's micromanagement.

Adelynn: Edith claims, “It's a matter of democratic decision, rather than dysfunction necessitating the governor's micromanagement,” and her suggested remedy comprises the same laissez-faire attitude. In my opinion, this is not acceptable.

— Nightwork gubernatorial debate, 30 December 2022

Adelynn won by three votes. On the day of her victory, Spinzle advised her to clean up Nightwork through gradual attrition rather than a jarring shift. Adelynn disagreed, though she soon found a forced cleanup more precarious than she had expected.45

The Dreadnought Debate

In November 2025, with both women now in the Preservate leadership, they clashed over Apollos's proposal to expand the party's Apparatus. Adelynn warned that a larger, more centralized party would trap the community in “cyclical factionalism”; Spinzle answered with an analogy to the battleship Dreadnought, whose integrated design made every rival warship obsolete in 1906.46

Spinzle: To say we must perfect our internal structure before we can expand or engage in the political environment is akin to saying we must design the greatest gunsight before we can carry the battleship into combat. It's a waste of time.

Adelynn: Edith. I agree with your principles. My only disagreement is with your conclusion. … In accordance with your military analogy, we assume the role of the “battleship.” We embody the archetypal symbol of institutional power that validates their entire existence.

Spinzle: Let us concede to your analogy. We represent the battleship as an emblem of the establishment. The Ories are torpedo boats, disordered agents. … Our proposed plan is precisely the destroyer screen.

— The Dreadnought Debate, 12 November 2025

Adelynn ultimately conceded: “Honestly, I have confidence in their strategy. I merely felt a compulsive urge to articulate a response.” Two days later Apollos reappointed her as Boss of the party.46,44

Agreement on the Charter

The rivalry's most celebrated moment came during Legislature XI's debate on the Charter in early 2026. When Snuck argued that the Charter should not try to legislate the culture of every district, Adelynn agreed with him, and so, for once, did Spinzle.9

Adelynn: Snuck is entirely correct. We should not attempt to mandate universal morality and sought to define “dignity” across the entirety of Ninzard. … The Charter should solely dictate what is lethal.

Spinzle: AGREEMENT ??????

Al Commando: Oh my god it's happening

— Legislature XI, 2026

Spinzle drafted the revised Part I within half an hour, and the two then argued side by side to win over Syn. Spinzle insisted that an “explicitly stated reason” for every disciplinary action meant a single automated log line, not “a five-paragraph essay,” while Adelynn argued that it would eliminate the “Gray Zone” Cat had exploited. Part I passed with all fifteen votes.9

When Spinzle turned 23 on 12 June 2026, Adelynn's message summed up the relationship: “Bon anniversaire, ma vieille. You are, against your considerable efforts, very difficult not to love.”12

Personal life

Spinzle and Apollos rekindled their friendship in 2024 and grew close over long-distance conversations between Colorado and British Columbia. By mid-2025, as Cat fell, they had become a couple, known in the community as “Reudith.”47 The relationship ended in July 2026 amid differences over faith.48 She has written extensively for the community's archival record, contributing to Sections XVI to XX of Phase IV.11

Political style

Spinzle is known for her composure, deadpan humour and preference for systems over personalities. Admirers credit her with intellectual rigour and an imperviousness to bribery and intimidation; critics, including Apollos at the end of her governorship, found her passive, aloof and more interested in theory than in people.4,28,6 She championed district autonomy and free expression against the Preservates' isolationist governors, while building institutions meant to restrain whoever came after her.25,5
