Sean Tinoco (born 4 May 2004), known as Seandry, is a Sacramentan administrator of the District of Main and one of the most respected technical figures in the Ninzard Project. Once an enemy of the community, he founded the Rocket Squad Nation (RSN) as a teenager and waged the RSN War against Ninzard; after his defeat in 2018 he integrated into the community and, with Ploutos, Hackman and Advy, co-founded the cybersecurity group Channeling-4 (C-4).1,2

Across the 2020s Seandry became the Head of Administrators of the District of Main under General Woofer and a central figure in General Kay's long investigation into President Catx3. He served in the Senior Leadership of the Preservate Apparatus during the drafting of the Charter, and in June 2026 he was elected a Representative for Main in Legislature XII. Once an erratic, arrogant child, he grew into a calm, respectful, if slightly mischievous leader.2

Rocket Squad Nation

Seandry's first contact with Ninzard came through its hostility. A child prodigy who had won several math competitions, he never hid his intelligence, and classmates resented him for it; after one contest win, the District of Sacraday, led by General Bray, incited its members to harass and beat him. Nightians and Sacradians alike targeted him, and his grudge against the community grew profound.3,4

In 2017 he founded the Rocket Squad Nation, a rival community built, in his words, to destroy Ninzard, and declared himself its “Sun King.” The RSN drew in disaffected Ninzards and others Seandry banned or barred from the project had cast out; among its early members were the future Scopian Sable and the Catsland defector Laser, both of whom he named “Captains,” and Boygho, who had broken with the community after a falling-out with DJNinja.5,4 Seandry taught himself a great deal about Remote Access Trojans (RATs) and quietly deployed them against Ninzard members. Near the end of middle school he was caught and reported, suspended, and stripped of the academic awards he was about to receive.4

The RSN declared war on the community on 30 June 2018 with a raid on Nightwork.6 The conflict ran until 7 August 2018, when Nightwork, acting on intelligence that Seandry and the RSN leadership were assembling a dossier of real-life addresses for a mass-swatting campaign, vandalized and dismantled the RSN's platforms and exposed the plan. Seandry surrendered and agreed to fall under the Capital's authority.7

Integration and Channeling-4

President Apollos did not celebrate the RSN's defeat. Instead he befriended its former Sun King, telling Seandry that he too had been bullied and resented for his inability to stay quiet. Seandry was struck by how much he could relate to Apollos, and he quietly withdrew into the community to support its leaders from the background. Capital Adjudicator Raffaele, recognizing his potential, connected him to the quieter figures of the establishment — General Kay, former General Advy, and the trusted administrators Hackman and Ploutos.8

In August 2018 those four — Seandry, Ploutos, Hackman and Advy — founded the informal California group “Channeling-4,” abbreviated C-4. Loyal to Apollos, Raffaele and Kay, they formed a silent but important technical network, and from 2018 to 2025 they were the quiet moderate establishment behind the project's leadership; in 2025 they reorganized into the Pragma Flank of the Preservation Party.1 Apollos had encouraged Seandry to build C-4 so that he might make true friends, and Seandry always held the former president in high personal regard — Apollos had been one of the few to validate his sense of being looked down upon no matter what he achieved.9

Administrator and the Underground

As the Underground Era took hold, Seandry put his technical skill to work for the community's leaders. Under Vice President Hokan he posed as a friend of Governor Spikester — Ploutos's older brother — and induced him to install a RAT, gathering the evidence that led to Spikester's downfall. When Spikester later confronted him, Seandry answered with the line that became his signature: he had never needed to win, he said, he had only needed friends.10 The following autumn, C-4 opened a covert investigation into the ransomware attacks and the doxxing of Kay traced to the dangerous actor Quorey.11

In 2021 General Danbi, setting a precedent of bipartisan staffing in Main, appointed Seandry among the district's senior administrators.12 He also became an unexpectedly close friend of the reserved Governor Syn, who before his 2022 resignation called Seandry's friendship one of the most important he had.13 On 24 March 2022, General Geo — then at the height of a meltdown — banned Seandry from Main for using the banned word “Geodude” three times, and Raffaele took him into Hybriden; General Sundaze later declined to restore him.14 When Woofer won Main at the end of 2022, she reinstated Seandry as an administrator.15

In July 2023, the Bethany Alliance — a rare Orie–Preservate coalition built to expose President Catx3's rigged elections — ran Seandry as the running mate of the Capitalian administrator Scanty for Governor of Ninzardia.16 In the only district with legitimate results, Capitalia, the ticket won overwhelmingly, but Cat's bot-filled districts of Catsland and Lair manufactured a landslide against it.17

Investigation of Catx3

Seandry became a key technician in Kay's 26-month investigation into Catx3. Cat herself judged him one of the “exceptionals” — on Raffaele's advice, she never even attempted to bribe him, concluding there was no leverage to be found.18 Seandry quietly assisted Advy in stewarding Capitalia, the one district insulated from Cat, and was among the Nightwork leaders to whom Kay first relayed her suspicions.19

In the forensic work Raffaele named “Operation A1P,” Seandry monitored the Ethereum mempool, writing a script to log the exact millisecond a transaction appeared during the three-second windows when Advy posted bait; repeated forty times over a week, the method tied Cat's wallet to her activity with near-total certainty.20 Later, working with Syn and Sable, he parsed blockchain ledgers to trace laundered funds to a UK company registered to Catherine Avalon in Leeds.21

For the last stretch of Cat's presidency, Seandry helped Kay exploit a persistent “ghost access” to Cat's accounts, altering single cells in her ledgers, corrupting file frames, and leaving quiet dead-drop messages — among them, “All you ever needed were some friends.” Cat grew to hold him in particular contempt.22,23 In the final phase he sat on Kay's containment team, which scrubbed leaks of the case from public platforms.24

Head of Administrators of Main

When the Catx3 Regime collapsed in August 2025, Seandry was among those who pressed the Preservate Apparatus to bring Apollos back to lead the party against Caleb Jensen. He told the Blue Quartet that he understood Apollos “on a deeper level,” praised the social persuasion and confidence the rest of the party lacked, and argued that Cyntaku's doubts about the former president came from confirmation bias; Adelynn said she trusted his and Grace's judgment.25 In the 2025 presidential election his backing, alongside Woofer's, helped carry Main for Apollos.26

Under General Woofer, Seandry served as Main's Head of Administrators, with Ploutos and PluPlu as administrators beneath him and Poka leading the moderators. The archive records his transformation: the erratic, arrogant founder of the RSN had become one of the most respected administrators in Ninzard, a calm and mischievous leader who had helped bring down Cat.2 Apollos, for his part, was surprised by how loyal Seandry had remained, and counted all of C-4 — Sean, Niccolò, Gareth and Advik — among his closest connections outside the party.27

In the debate of 2 February 2026 over admitting Kaimachi to the Preservate Apparatus, Seandry pushed the party to embrace the community's chaotic energy rather than fear it, reminding the room how often its caution had been wrong.28

「Kai is not Cat. Kai is not Olivia. Cat was a predator who hid in the dark. Olivia was a quiet snake. Kai is a cringe, loud obsessive teenager. Kai shouts her intentions from the rooftops. We won because we embraced the chaos and pointed it in the right direction, not by being the Party of No Fun. Stop being scared of your own community.」

— Seandry, Preservate Apparatus, 2 February 2026

Charter and Legislature

After the March 2026 assault on Apollos at his university, Seandry mounted a furious defense of him in the Apparatus; Spinzle muted both him and the radical Lunaix, who had called the attack “karma,” for a day.29 In the Apparatus structure of 24 February he was a Pragma party operative for Main,30 and when Woofer became Boss on 2 April he joined the Senior Leadership alongside Ploutos, Spinzle and Apollos.31

In the March 2026 snap election to fill Adelynn's seat in Legislature XI, Seandry took 150 of 237 votes (63.3%), finishing third behind the winner, Ploutos.32 In the June 2026 Summer Cycle he was newly elected to Legislature XII with 162 votes (62.3%), joining the Main delegation for the new legislature.33 Under President Poka, Main's representatives were Advy, Ploutos, Seandry and Syn.34
