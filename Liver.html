Sasha Gallagher (born 30 May 2005), known as Liver, is a Ninzard politician from Sacramento who represented the District of Scope for the Original Party in Legislature XI from April to July 2026. A traditionalist Catholic who first heard the community as a child through her older brother's headset, she grew into the most sincere voice of its far right and the muse of NGin's Holy Iron Flank, whose members she nonetheless held in contempt.1,2,3,4

Turned into a laughingstock after she typed a Latin exorcism into the Legislature in April 2026, she accepted an invitation from her old adversary Adelynn to rest with her family in Los Angeles, and was at the centre of the Holloway Summit of 2 May 2026, where NGin and his followers laid down their war on the community. Her testimony of repentance, Confessions Beneath My Armor, made her one of the most respected members of Scope; she went on to win Volley's holdouts over to the Charter and, in June 2026, to run for General of Scope.5,6,7,8,9,10,11

Early life

Gallagher was born on 30 May 2005 and grew up in a working-class Irish-Catholic household in Sacramento. Her father, an immigrant from Belfast, was a violent alcoholic, and her mother rarely intervened.1 In 2014, when she was nine, her older brother Declan found refuge in Nightwork, a TeamSpeak community run by Elusion. When the shouting began downstairs, Declan would take her into his closet and put his headset over her ears so that she heard Elusion's voice instead; the calm voices of the Nightwork boys were the first sound of safety she had known. When Declan was sent to juvenile detention in 2016, she inherited his computer and took over his place in the community.1,12

「To the rest of Ninzard he was a historical footnote. Yet to me he was the ghost who saved my childhood.」

— Liver, on meeting Elusion at the Holloway Summit, May 202612

Radicalization (2018–2025)

The deaths of Tiny2 and Holiday in 2018, when she was thirteen, convinced her that Ninzard was not merely toxic but sinful. She likened the open borders of the Era of Progression in 2019 to the fall of Rome, and during the lockdowns of 2020 she retreated into the works of Julius Evola, Thomistic theology and authoritarian political theory. In Scope she found a mentor in Lyman, with whom she spent hours in locked voice channels; by late 2020 she regarded democracy as a failed experiment and believed that only a strict, theocratic hierarchy could save souls from their own desires.1

She loathed President Cat as the embodiment of the community's spiritual rot. She held that women were made for domestic life rather than leadership, and despised women in politics such as Mel; yet because she judged the men of Ninzard too weak to lead, she felt bound to speak, likening herself to Joan of Arc, a woman made by God to take up the sword only because the men of her generation would not.1 Though she found Aceiron degenerate, she voted for him against Apollos in the 2025 presidential election, seeing Apollos's return under the Preservate banner as a moral backlash.1 The archive counts her, with NGin, among the “esoteric far-right fascists” of Aceiron's coalition.4

The Holy Iron Flank (2025–2026)

Muse of the Flank

Even on the far right she was an anomaly. Where most of the Holy Iron Flank treated neo-Nazism as an aesthetic or a joke to provoke the Capital, she held her beliefs with complete sincerity. To NGin and his followers she was their ideal traditional wife and their ideological muse; she, for her part, thought them socially repulsive.1 She was fiercely protective of two right-wing Orie girls, Lyre and Arielle, whose politics she disliked but whom she saw as “lost lambs.” When Lyre fell into a depressive episode, Liver sat with her in a voice call for six hours, reading her Bible verses and Irish poetry.1

The archive calls her and the Marxist Preservate Lesty “dark mirrors of one another”: both intelligent, principled women who thought Ninzard sick, both scornful of the Capital's pragmatism, and both utterly sincere in a community of ironic trolls. Lesty pitied Liver as a victim who had become dangerous, and wanted her and the Flank deplatformed.13

The Chud War

The spark of the Chud War, the day of real-world confrontations in Sacramento on 14 February 2026, was a photograph of Liver.14,1 That day she drove Lyre to Aceiron's house to collect a forgotten charger. Seeing Liver in the grip of a panic attack in his driveway, Aceiron came out to calm her, and a Flank member watching the house took a blurry picture that seemed to show an intimate moment. NGin took it as betrayal and called the Flank to war. Liver heard his screaming voice memo over coffee with Lyre and Arielle, closed her eyes and muttered two words.1

「Protestant behavior.」

— Liver, on NGin's call to war, 14 February 2026

That evening she drove to the parking-lot standoff between the Flank and Aceiron's supporters, rolled down her window at the two dozen young men holding baseball bats, and left before the Capital had even intervened.1

「You are all acting like absolute, undisciplined children. You have no dignity, no spiritual center, and no honor. I'm going to evening Mass.」

— Liver, at the parking-lot standoff, 14 February 2026

When she learned hours later that Triste had shot out a window of Aceiron's house, she laughed: to her it proved that secular young men without divine order would destroy one another.1

“This is spiritual rot”

On 28–29 March 2026, as AJ and NGin traded racist and homophobic abuse in voice memos and the community wondered whether a second Chud War was coming, Liver lost patience with them both.15

「Both of you are rolling in the mud like feral, godless animals and abandoned your so-called principles the moment the mob turned on you, proving exactly what I have always said about the secular right's spiritual bankruptcy! You have no faith! Therefore, you have no spine! … The Capital is watching this laughing, sitting in their modernist towers, watching you all fucking CANNIBALIZE yourselves because you both LACK the divine order required to govern! Forget Discord—forget governing on Discord! You can't even govern yourselves! WAKE THE FUCK UP, IDIOTS! THIS IS SPIRITUAL ROT! BOTH OF YOU ARE INFECTED! JUST REPENT!」

— Liver, in a sixty-second voice memo, 29 March 2026

AJ told her to let the men talk. NGin, enraged, rushed to defend her honour, and AJ mocked him as her lapdog.15

Legislature XI (2026)

Liver was elected for Scope in the snap elections of March 2026 and took her seat in April as one of the “Four RadicaLs,” the far-right and far-left Representatives, with Lesty, Lless and Lyman, who were thought saner than their less intellectual allies and who would help to pass the Charter.3 She and the Parryan ScottyAte were the only Ories among its fifteen members, and that spring she was named among the Original Party's dark-horse candidates for president.2

Unusually for the far right, she wanted a Charter, though not the Capital's. Where the Preservates saw it as a legal framework against doxxing and server deletion, she wanted a moral constitution, a “digital Decalogue” that would also ban brainrot and degeneracy, and she thought Apollos's “controlled burn” the height of cowardice.1 In the Legislature she answered the long essays of her fellow RadicaLs, among them Lless's Marxist critique of the Capital.16

「Reading that manifesto feels exactly like watching a clinician sanitize a nursery with bleach, only to toss a live grenade into the crib just to observe the blast radius.」

「There is absolutely no motherly nature in a Marxist. There is no grace. There is only a cold, mechanical compulsion to deconstruct things until there is nothing left but dust. Tossing academic buzzwords at a crisis of the soul is like trying to heal a gunshot wound with a gender studies syllabus.」

— Liver, response to Lless's essay, April 2026

The essayists wore on the chamber. On 16 April a parody rap, the “Goonstitution,” mocked them all and came with AI-made pictures of Liver marrying NGin.17

The exorcism and the pilgrimage (April 2026)

The exorcism

On the night of Saturday 25 April 2026, an argument in Legislature XI between Liver and a group of younger members led by PluPlu, Poka, Kaimachi and KatDoesMuch got out of hand. Liver told KatDoesMuch that she was “the dead end of Western civilization,” and when Woofer greeted the chamber from Ploutos's account while the two watched a film, she denounced Woofer as “a lukewarm, compromised hypocrite” for being alone with a man late at night. The mockery that followed drove her to the Latin rite of exorcism, to Aquinas, and to an antisemitic slur against another Representative.5

Liver: I do not seek the validation of men whose frontal lobes have been liquefied by modern pornography and neoliberal apathy. I seek a man who understands the weight of the Hyperborean mandate. Not a spiritually bankrupt boy who shares his Discord account with a girl out of wedlock. It is pathetic.

She left the chamber that night in Latin, with a last word for her tormentors.5

「Thomas Aquinas would have called for a crusade to cleanse this chat. He would have demanded the Inquisition to burn your routers at the stake. I am physically shaking with horror at the blasphemy I have witnessed tonight. Libera me domine de morte aeterna! … I cannot remain in this digital cesspool of fornication and mockery for ONE more second. I am going to chant the Pater Noster until my lungs bleed.」

— Liver, leaving Legislature XI, 25 April 2026

Two days later she was, in the archive's word, a lolcow, roasted across her own district by PluPlu, Poka, Kaimachi and KatDoesMuch.6 What hurt, she later wrote, was looking at the sidebar and seeing that Lyre and Arielle were online and said nothing.9

The reply to Lesty

Between 28 and 30 April, in a reply to an essay by Lesty, Liver made what the archive called a rare display of self-awareness and humility before the whole community.6 She accused the Capital of sacrificing “the flock to preserve the fence,” and then turned on herself.18

「For my own part, I apologize to this community. I have not been innocent of the very pride I condemn. I have let my zealotry blind me to the suffering of the boys I yelled at. I have been the Pharisee praying loudly in the temple and thanking God I am not like the publicans while forgetting my own desperate need for mercy. I am sorry.」

「They must stay to build these broken and screaming boys into men who can be good fathers. They must stay to guide these lost and neurotic girls into women who can be good mothers. They must stay to bind the wounds and not to laugh at the bleeding. Stay to care for people, not to study them.」

— Liver, response to Lesty, April 2026

She granted that Lesty was right to call Ninzard an idol, but kept her doubts about the Charter: “Where grace is absent, no Charter can hope to mend what is broken.”18 Lesty thanked her for taking off “the armour of your convictions,” but wished she had the courage to extend the same grace to the people her “lost boys” were trying to destroy; Lless told her to “keep praying to pixels.”19,20

The call

Adelynn, back on Discord since 25 April and still recovering from her injuries, wrote to Liver on 27 April to ask how she was. Liver told her not to patronize her. Adelynn asked to call instead, because speaking was easier for her than typing, and after a long hesitation Liver answered.6,7

Liver: I will not tolerate a lecture. If you intend to deliver a secular reprimand disguised as mediation, save your breath.

Adelynn: Bonjour, Sasha. I assure you, delivering lectures requires lung capacity that I don't possess right now.

Liver: Ninzard is a digital Sodom.

Adelynn: Who protects you?

Liver: I rely upon the Lord.

Adelynn: The Lord provides community. But you keep retreating behind theology like a fortress wall.

Liver: I uphold standards.

Adelynn: My house is full of loud siblings. We have an empty chair at the dinner table. Come to Los Angeles.

Liver: To visit you?

Adelynn: To rest. You spend your nights fighting for the souls of the people you care about. But who protects you, Sasha? Who makes dinner for you?

— Liver and Adelynn, 27 April 2026

The question, Liver wrote, “shattered the last of my armor.” The two found that they agreed on far more theology than they disagreed on, and she accepted Adelynn's invitation.7,6

Los Angeles

On 30 April she made the drive alone down Interstate 5 from Sacramento and spent the night with the Leclaire family.6 Only Lyre knew where she had gone, and on 1 May she told the Orie Apparatus. With Minister Kay's permission, ScottyAte broke the news to the whole community.21

ScottyAte: Guess where Sasha is. @NGin

ScottyAte: Lol. She's at Leclaire's. Drove from NorCal to SoCal.

Poka: WAIT FUCKING WHAT?!?!?!?!?!?!?

— Legislature XI, 1 May 2026

NGin answered with a five-second voice memo, “It's time,” and before dawn set out for Los Angeles with four of the Flank: Athaniel, Chud Alessandro, Highlander and Cedario.21

The Holloway Summit (May 2026)

On the morning of 2 May Grace came to collect Adelynn and Liver, who apologized to her for her past words; Grace told her to “leave the past in the past.” At the airport they picked up Cyntaku, Liver's bitter enemy for two years, who slid into the back seat beside her, took her hand and told her it was a joy to see her.22,23 They drove to Kay's home, where Kay's brother Malachi Holloway was cooking for the guests and where Liver found Elusion, the voice from her brother's headset, now a bearded man of twenty-seven.23,24

When NGin's car pulled into the driveway that afternoon, the young men inside would not get out, and NGin shouted at Kay through the speaker, demanding to know what had been done to Sasha. Liver took the microphone and told him to stop acting like a coward and come into the house.25

「Thomas Aquinas wrote that the irascible appetite moves man either toward difficult goods or away from difficult evils. Ethan's rage had appeared monstrous to me in prior days. At this moment I perceived the sorrow lurking beneath the surface. Anger is born of a wound.」

— Liver, on NGin's arrival

Inside, NGin broke down weeping in Malachi's arms. Liver and Cyntaku knelt beside him with their hands on his back while Malachi prayed over the room, and when he had finished the two women held each other and sobbed.8 That night, unable to sleep among the thirty guests on the floors and sofas, she found Elusion and Raffaele on the patio beside a citronella candle and told Elusion what his voice had meant to a frightened nine-year-old.26,12

On Sunday she went with the Holloways to their non-denominational church, dreading the loud bands and smoke she expected of a Protestant megachurch. Instead she was moved by the plain kindness of the congregation.27

「The differences in our theology meant nothing in the presence of overwhelming love. Finally I understood. Our God is not a dictator. Our God is a Father welcoming His children home.」

— Liver, on the Sunday service, 3 May 2026

As the guests left that evening she exchanged numbers with Meikaqu, while Cyntaku and NGin laughed together by the stairs.28 To her the weekend marked the end of “the long and dreadful age of digital violence.”29

「There amidst the ruins of our former selves, the covenant betwixt us was sealed not in fear, nor ideology, nor violence... but in love.」

— Liver, on the end of the Holloway Summit

Confessions Beneath My Armor

On 7 May 2026, writing as Scope's Representative, she published Confessions Beneath My Armor, her account of the weekend, which opened with thanks to those who had received her.9

「I offer highest praise to Almighty God. He looked upon a proud and wicked girl and answered her wrath with a flood of unearned mercy. His grace endures.」

「I offer profound gratitude to the Leclaire family. They opened their doors to an enemy. They broke my pride with a warm meal and a steadfast love. To Adelynn. You saw the frightened child hiding beneath the theology. You extended a hand of peace. I remain in your debt.」

「To Grace and Madelyn and Natalia. I spoke vile words against your names. I judged your souls with a cold and hardened heart. Yet you knelt beside me on the floorboards. You held me in my shame. You taught me the meaning of sisterhood.」

「And to Ethan and the boys of the Holy Iron Flank. We wandered a barren wilderness. We sought glory in hatred. We found our salvation in surrender. I pray for your peace.」

「Let this record stand as a testament to forgiveness. The Lord binds our wounds.」

— Sasha Gallagher, 7 May 2026

The same week, with the Charter referendum held up in Volley, she went to meet Volleyans in person and persuaded them to vote for it.10

Later activity (2026)

When the former General Soda died in a Scope voice channel on 18 May 2026, Liver was among the Representatives who addressed the community, urging anyone sent the recording to delete it unwatched and asking them to pray for his family.30

「Do not fall into the abyss of nihilism. The agents of the wicked will attempt to use Liam's death to sow despair and to plunge us back into a dark age of factional hatred. We must stand as an immovable wall against them and hold fast to one another. Reject darkness. Stay united in grace, and pray for the soul of Liam Higgins.」

— Liver, 18 May 2026

By then, renowned for her account of the Holloway Summit, she was deeply beloved in Scope, but she had little interest in another term or in any Ninzard election. The Orie Apparatus persuaded her to stand for General of Scope in the Summer Cycle of June 2026. Expecting to lose, she treated the campaign as a chance to share her testimony and to urge the district toward a more Christian direction, and in a district still shaken by Soda's death she became an unexpected source of reassurance. She lost to the incumbent Hyperio by 94 votes to 41, a stronger result than expected, as many Scopians voted for her out of respect.11 In the presidential election held at the same time she endorsed ScottyAte. Her term ended on 2 July, and Scope's seats in Legislature XII went to Sable and Laser.11

Personal life

Gallagher keeps the books for a small hardware store on the edge of Sacramento. Her parents have separated but live two houses apart, and she drives between them to look after them; her father is gravely ill.9 She attends the Traditional Latin Mass.5

「I stay because Lyre and Arielle are there. I stay because the boys from my real life are there. I stay because without them my life is just a quiet cycle of ledgers and hospitals and dying parents. … Ninzard is, in truth, as much a tether for me as I would have myself be for them.」

— Liver, Confessions Beneath My Armor, May 2026
