Mark Ionescu (born 31 October 1998), known as Elusion, is a former Ninzard politician and the founder of Nightwork who served as both 1st Governor of Nightwork and 1st District General of Main from 11 July 2015 to 1 January 2017.1,2,3

An older intellectual among the Sacramento diaspora known as “The Old Wizard,” Elusion led the opposition to UNO's violence inside the Sacranight legion in 2013 and deadlocked twice with General Hyperio in the 2013–2014 Sacranight elections before leading a mass walkout to establish Nightwork as an independent community on 25 April 2014.1,4,5 After leading Nightwork through the Nightian War of 2014–2015, he was forced to integrate the community into the Ninzard Project after President Antareze (operating as Orion1) compromised his root server credentials.6,7

Defeating Hyperio by a single vote in June 2015, Elusion oversaw the 11 July 2015 merger and defended Nightwork against Hyperio's insurgency during the Ninzard Civil War of 2015–2016.2,8 He served the first months of Apollos's presidency before retiring on 1 January 2017, returning nearly a decade later in May 2026 to join the Ninzard Career Initiative review committee and attend the Holloway Summit.3,9,10

Early life and Sacranight (2013–2014)

Ionescu was born on 31 October 1998 in California to a Romanian-Jewish father, an archivist for the California State Archives, and a second-generation Ethiopian mother, a systems architect for a major California utility provider.1 An avid World of Warcraft and StarCraft player, he commanded grudging reverence among the younger teenagers of the Sacramento diaspora, who dubbed him “The Old Wizard” and “The Awakener” (and 도사 among Club Korea) for his ability to de-escalate physical street fights simply by showing up and speaking calmly.1

Within the Sacranight legion (“The Jungle”), Elusion led the intellectually inclined faction that sought to oust UNO over his physical violence and hazing.4 In the December 2013 Sacranight general election, Elusion and incumbent General Hyperio tied at 32 votes each out of 64 cast; a January 2014 runoff ordered by President Antareze produced a second deadlock at 28–28.5 With the legion paralyzed, Elusion walked into local Sacramento parks in April 2014, persuaded the quieter members to leave Hyperio, and established Nightwork as a separate community on 25 April 2014, one day before Sacranight formally disbanded.1,5

The Nightian War (2014–2015)

While building Nightwork's server clusters—supplied with imported Japanese components by Giada Tagliaferro (OffBeat) and defended in debate by a fourteen-year-old Lless—Elusion faced false-flag threats from Hyperio, who posed as Ninzards to provoke hostilities.1,11,12 Although Antareze initially assured Elusion that the Capital had issued no threats, street altercations between Nightians and General Bray's Sacraday clique led Nightwork to declare war on Sacraday on 4 October 2014, prompting Antareze to declare war on Nightwork two days later.11,13

At the height of the war in 2015, Antareze, operating under the alias Orion1, launched a dictionary attack against Nightwork's ServerQuery interface. Elusion routed the incoming connections into an iptables TCP tarpit that stalled Antareze's Russian staging VPS.6 Using the tarpit as a distraction, however, Antareze exploited a misconfigured VIP channel permission to generate a Server Admin token and—because Elusion had run the TeamSpeak 3 binary as the Linux root user—used a directory-traversal vulnerability to download /etc/shadow and the server's SQLite database, seizing Elusion's AWS backups.6

If you humiliate me in front of them, they won't join Ninzard. They'll just quit. You'll be ruling over ashes. Give me back my Admin token. I will sign a peace treaty.

— Elusion, to Orion1 in the “Checkmate” channel, 2015

Blackmailed with the release of private messages in which he had mocked his own members to appease UNO, Elusion agreed in June 2015 to integrate Nightwork into the Ninzard Project.6,7

Governor of Nightwork and General of Main (2015–2017)

Merger and the Ninzard Civil War

In the June 2015 elections Elusion defeated Hyperio—who had dissolved his own server to contest Nightwork from within—by a single vote, and joined Antareze's Home Party.2,14 Following a ratification vote on 11 July 2015, Nightwork merged with Ninzardia as three legions; Elusion became the 1st District General of Nightwork-Main and de facto 1st Governor of Nightwork alongside generals DegreeZ and Hyperio.2

Resenting Elusion's overarching authority, Hyperio hacked Elusion's accounts and griefed the other Nightwork legions on 6 October 2015, igniting the Ninzard Civil War.8 Throughout the winter of 2015–2016 Elusion's loyalists clashed with the Hyperionites in Sacramento, while Elusion was formally elected Governor of Nightwork in December 2015.15,16 Following Hyperio's arrest and the collapse of his insurgency in March 2016, Elusion and Antareze established Nightwork-Volley on 19 March 2016 under OffBeat to reintegrate former Hyperion members.17,18

The Golden Era and retirement

Following Antareze's resignation and Apollos's upset victory in July 2016, Elusion briefly assumed leadership of the remnants of the Home Party while sitting in Legislature VII as an independent, and his office as Governor of Nightwork was formally codified in Apollos's electoral reforms.3,19,20 During the early Apollos presidency, Elusion and DegreeZ were the only leaders to back Apollos against removing Sacraday General Judah.21 In December 2016 Elusion announced that he would not seek re-election and left the community on 1 January 2017, succeeded by DegreeZ as Governor of Nightwork and Xorbit as General of Main.3

Later years and 2026 return

Elusion's precedence as first Governor remained a touchstone in Nightian politics for years, invoked by Governor Cyntaku during her July 2022 debate with Spinzle.22 On 2 May 2026, at age twenty-seven, Elusion returned to the community to join Raffaele and Spinzle on the Ninzard Career Initiative review committee and attended the Holloway Summit in Los Angeles as one of the “Holloway Summit 30.”9,10

That night on the Holloway patio, Scope Representative Liver told Elusion that when she was nine years old, her older brother had placed his headset over her ears in a closet so that Elusion's calm voice on TeamSpeak would drown out their father's drunken rages. Speaking with Liver and Meikaqu, Elusion reflected that he had spent years studying the “architecture of Christianity” like a structural blueprint before realizing that intellectual order built solely out of fear estranges the soul from love.23
