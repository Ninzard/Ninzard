Political parties of Ninzard

The Ninzard Project has had organized political parties since at least 2014, when the community's founding president, Antareze, led the Home Party and Hokan won the vice presidency for the Alienova Party.1,2 From 2016 the populist Original Party of Apollos dominated the community, first against the Resistance Party and, since its founding on 1 October 2020, against the Preservation Party; the Original and Preservation parties have dominated Ninzard politics ever since.3,4,5 The leaders of those two parties are called Bosses.

Smaller parties have risen and fallen around them, from the Disco Party that carried Cat to the presidency in 2022 to the Poo Party of the Poopulists and the short-lived Liberation Party of 2025–2026. Every party has a colour of its own, in which its name glows throughout this encyclopedia.

Minor and troll parties

Elections have often drawn joke parties. In the presidential election of July 2022, which drew 46 candidates, new troll parties were created for the sole purpose of the campaign. The Gusta Party's ME GUSTA won 259 of 490 approvals, ahead of the Original Party's nominee, and Alaric won 198 for the No More Anime Party, under whose banner two other candidates also ran.6,7

Coalitions

  • Orie–Poopulist Coalition (2022): formed on 30 April 2022 by Apollos, Spinzle, the Poo Party and the Ories, which carried Spinzle to the governorship of Nightwork that July.8
  • Bethany Alliance (2023): the Original and Preservation parties' joint opposition to Cat's Disco Party in the elections of July 2023, which disbanded once it had exposed her rigging of them.9
  • Liberator–Orie Coalition (2025): Aceiron's alliance with the Liberation Party for the December 2025 elections, in which it won only Parry.10,11

Factions and movements

Within the two major parties, factions have often mattered as much as the parties themselves. Among the Ories were the populist Paleories of 2021, Aceiron's “Calebros” and NGin's far-right Holy Iron Flank, and since 2026 the Rationalist and Romantic flanks.12,13,14 The Preservates have been divided into the isolationist Axiom Flank, Adelynn's Logos Flank and Apollos's Pragma Flank.15 Outside the parties stood movements such as Cat's Meowsalute and ViaTea's Liberation Movement, whose militant wing, Triste's Liberation Front, called itself a militia rather than a party.16,17
