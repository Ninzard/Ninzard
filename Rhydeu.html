Ryan Rashad Jenkins (born 18 July 2004), known as Rhydeu (pronounced RYE-doo, /ˈraɪ.duː/), is an American Ninzard politician from Sacramento and a long-time member of the Original Party and of the “Calebros,” the loyalists of Aceiron. A cousin of AJ, he was one of the forty-six contenders for the presidency in 2022, and in July 2023 he was the Ories' nominee for General of Main, losing to Woofer by 160 votes to 32.1,2

On 14 February 2026, during the Chud War, he was one of the Ories besieged with Aceiron in his house by NGin's Holy Iron Flank, and one of the three who stepped outside to face them. A selfie that Aceiron took outside the house with him, AJ, ScottyAte, Lyre and Elle became one of the war's most memed photos.3,4 Since July 2026 he has been close friends with Athaniel, a former member of the Flank, and the two play Nintendo Switch games together.

Early life

Jenkins was born on 18 July 2004 in Sacramento, California, and is African-American. He is a cousin of AJ (Andre Jenkins), a fellow Sacramentan some ten months older and a former General of Volley for the Original Party.5 A long-time Orie, he belongs to the “Calebros,” the circle of Aceiron's friends and loyalists within the party.6

Name

His username began with his first name, Ryan, worked into an edgy middle-school gamer pun on “ride you” (“Rhy-deu”). When Kain saw the spelling in the early TeamSpeak and Discord servers, his MapleStory instincts brought to mind the legendary Korean warrior Ryude (류드), and he began calling him “Ryudeu,” RYE-doo, as an in-joke that fixed both the spelling and the pronunciation across the server. In voice channels the “Calebros” and the Ories call him “Rhy” (/raɪ/) or “Rye-doo” (/ˈraɪ.duː/).

Candidacies (2022–2023)

Presidency (2022)

In the presidential election of July 2022, held by approval voting, Rhydeu was one of forty-six members who either sought a party's nomination or gathered thirty independent backers. He was one of sixteen Ories, among them Aceiron, Hokan, Kennie and Mel, who sought the Original Party's nomination; it went to Idodo, and Rhydeu did not reach the final ballot.1 Apollos, who led the count, resigned moments before the polls closed on 19 July, and Cat, who came second, became president.7

General of Main (2023)

In the Nightwork elections of 4 July 2023 the Ories nominated Rhydeu for District General of Main against the Preservate incumbent, Woofer, who had unseated Sundaze that January.8 Woofer was re-elected with 160 of the 192 votes cast (83.3%) to Rhydeu's 32 (16.7%), the Ories' weakest result in any district of Nightwork that day.9,2 In the governor's race on the same ballot, Main gave the Orie nominee, Kennie, only 16 votes against Governor Adelynn's 176, so Rhydeu drew twice as many Mainian votes as his party's candidate for governor.10

Chud War (2026)

On 14 February 2026 the Chud War carried the quarrels of the Calebros, the Holy Iron Flank and the Liberators onto the streets of Sacramento.6 That night Rhydeu was at Aceiron's house with AJ, Elle, Lyre and ScottyAte when NGin and his followers, armed with improvised weapons, surrounded it and shouted for Aceiron to come out. Aceiron locked the doors, covered the windows and told everyone to wait for the Flank to go home. AJ, Lyre and ScottyAte found the situation amusing; Elle and Rhydeu were anxious.3

Shortly after 23:33, when NGin had sent the most notorious of his voice memos, Aceiron asked AJ, Rhydeu and ScottyAte to step outside, so that the Capital would not call the police. The Iron Flankers, taken by surprise, shouted racial slurs at AJ and Rhydeu but did not attack them. ScottyAte walked up to NGin and offered his hand, which NGin shook, and a dozen unarmed Calebros came up to join the banter; Elle and Lyre then stepped out to a cheering crowd.3 Minutes later, at 23:40, two shots were fired at the house from a passing car. No one was hurt, and the shooter was later identified as the Liberator Triste.11,12

A selfie that Aceiron took outside the house with Rhydeu, ScottyAte, AJ, Lyre and Elle was memed relentlessly, as was the selfie of NGin and his Iron Flank, fourteen of them in the street holding up crowbars.4

Calebaited incident (2026)

At the end of February 2026 Rhydeu was in a voice call with Aceiron, AJ and Lyre during Aceiron's nightly stream when a Calebro, secretly bribed by Kain, sent Aceiron a video said to be a “hot mic” recording of Apollos admitting that he was afraid of him.13

Aceiron: Yo, they just sent me—

Rhydeu: What they sendin' you?

Aceiron: —a hot mic recording of Reu!

Rhydeu: Play it then! Play it!

— Aceiron's stream, February 2026

The audio turned out to be an AI clone of Aceiron's own voice reading lurid fan fiction, which Kain had made in revenge for Aceiron's insults. Rhydeu could only laugh in disbelief. The recording of the moment, which Kain then spread across the community, became known as the “Calebaited” incident.13

Personal life

Rhydeu and Athaniel became good friends in July 2026, and the two often play video games together on the Nintendo Switch. They had stood on opposite sides of the Chud War, in which Athaniel marched with the Holy Iron Flank; at the Holloway Summit of May 2026, Athaniel was among the Flankers whom AJ, Rhydeu's cousin, invited to join a race of Mario Kart 8 on the Switch.14
