Scott Dellinger (born 17 May 2003), known as ScottyAte or Scotty D., is an American Ninzard politician of the Original Party from Sacramento and a moderator on the Parry Staff Team under General Kaimachi. A childhood classmate of Aceiron, he fought as a boy in Hyperio's ranks in the Ninzard Civil War, and he twice challenged Kain for General of Parry as the Grass Party's candidate, in 2020 and 2021.1,2,3 In July 2025 he founded the Sactown Pound, a vigilante cell that hunted President Cat in Sacramento for a month, and adopted the stray black kitten, Eliza Kitten, that became Parry's mascot.4,5,6

During the Chud War of 14 February 2026 he defused the siege of Aceiron's house by stepping up to NGin and offering his hand.7 He represented Parry in Legislature XI from 28 February to 1 July 2026, helped persuade Aceiron to vote for Part I of the Charter, and was the Original Party's nominee for president in June 2026, losing to Poka by 616 votes to 196.8,9,10,11 A Presbyterian, he brought Aceiron to his church and wrote the archive's account of Aceiron's baptism in September 2026.12

Early years

Dellinger was born on 17 May 2003 and grew up in Sacramento, where he was a classmate of Aceiron. As a nerdy, scrawny boy who was used to being bullied, he was among Hyperio's “soldiers” in the Ninzard Civil War of 2015–2016, alongside Aceiron, and took part in a fight with Elusion's supporters in a park.1 Some time later, Mitchell, one of Elusion's supporters, caught him walking home from school and beat him with his friends. Aceiron ran to defend him, and when ScottyAte came back with a stick to fight them off, Mitchell's group held Aceiron down and broke his arm in front of him.1 The attack shook the Hyperionites, and in February 2016 Hyperio fought Mitchell to avenge the two boys and the others Mitchell had hurt.1,13

The archive also counts him among the Golden Era Cohort, the second generation of members of Apollos's first presidency, with PluPlu, ViaTea and Woofer.14 Shuffle later shared photos of him from 2015–16, when, in her words, he had been a “scrawny bullied nerd”; by 2026 he stood six foot three.15,7

In the October 2020 elections he was the only notable challenger to General Kain in Parry, then called BobaBitcoin, running for the Grass Party; Kain was easily re-elected.2 Nominated again by the Grass Party, then led by Power of Greed, in May 2021, he came close to beating Kain, who held on by a narrow margin.3 In 2022 he was among the presidential contenders as a member of the Gusta Party, one of that year's troll parties.16

He was an old friend of Aceiron and AJ, and when Aceiron grew lonely in 2023, he began spending more of his time with the two of them in Ninzard.17 He is also a real-life friend of Hyperio.18

Sactown Pound (2025)

After President Cat assaulted Governor Aceiron on 11 July 2025, the Sacramento-based Ories of Parry and Volley concluded that the fight against her had to move into the physical world. On 20 July ScottyAte founded a cell first called “Operation Euthanasia,” soon known as the Sactown Pound or simply the Pound, and styled himself its “Captain”; despite its ties to the Ories, he called it apolitical.4 Its aim was to find Cat when she next appeared in the Sacramento area and kill her, and his messages to its members called for violence against her and anyone who supported her. About two dozen joined, most of them disaffected men from Parry and Volley, among them AJ, Rudolf, Boygho, Gale and Kain; Shuffle, the only woman, came along to watch.4

The Pound patrolled the neighborhoods where Meikaqu was thought to be hiding, armed with crowbars, tire irons and baseball bats. ScottyAte also imposed a mandatory “touch grass” rule, blaming Ninzard's past failures on “vitamin D deficiency” and “Discord brain,” so the hunt was broken up by group hikes in the Sierra Nevada foothills.4 The group's culture was abrasive: he and the Volleyans made General Rudolf, who is of Chinese descent, the target of racial mockery, which Rudolf returned in kind.19

Eliza Kitten

On one of the patrols, near the American River, ScottyAte pulled a malnourished black stray kitten out of a culvert. The group named her Eliza Kitten, after Eliza, Cat's estranged older sister and one of Ninzard's founders, purely to spite Cat. The kitten rode on the dashboard of his car during the hunt and became the Pound's mascot and logo. PoG, then one of Parry's senior staff, approved of her, and Rudolf's part in the Pound helped make her the unofficial mascot of the District of Parry.5

Hyperio and Kay

On 25 July thirteen members of the Pound drove to the auto shop where Hyperio worked, and ScottyAte, holding the kitten, offered him command of the unit. Hyperio heard him out, laughed, and walked the whole group out of the shop, telling Rudolf at the door to “go home” and “get some bitches,” a line Ninzard soon shortened to “GSB.”20

The Pound then turned to Kay, who was known to be tracking Cat, and demanded the addresses of Cat and her associates. ScottyAte and Boygho bombarded her with messages, and in one voice message he told her:21

「Give us the coordinates. We will handle the dirty work. Keep your hands clean. Just point us in the right direction.」

— ScottyAte, to Kay, July 2025

Kay refused, knowing that a killing would wreck the federal case she had spent two years building. Her evidence led to Cat's arrest, and ScottyAte declared the Pound's end on 20 August 2025, a month after he had founded it.21,6 He kept the kitten, and went on posting updates about her in Parry's chat.6

Parry staff and the Minecraft feud (2025)

By late 2025 ScottyAte was on Parry's staff. On Thanksgiving, 27 November 2025, while Main was closed, Parry let some Mainians onto its Minecraft server, and Parryans wrecked their Italian-themed builds; staff members including ScottyAte laughed and called Italian a “garbage language.” PluPlu and Poka answered with a raid by the PluPlu Army that crashed the server.22 ScottyAte, who hardly played Minecraft, had mocked the Mainians' builds from screenshots, and the feud cost him dearly in Main during his presidential campaign.23

Chud War (2026)

On 14 February 2026 the Chud War brought Ninzard's quarrels onto the streets of Sacramento.24 ScottyAte refused to join the Sactown Rivalists, a group of Calebros led by Rudolf, believing that it had strayed from the Pound's mission.25 When a Liberator threatened on Discord to kill his cat and posted his address, he took her to his grandmother's house and drove around with a crowbar looking for them, then went to the parking lot where the Holy Iron Flank and the Rivalists had squared off. His height and build intimidated every side.25,26

Through the evening he drove back and forth between the lot and Aceiron's house, with Gale among his passengers. When he overheard NGin's plan to march on the house, he got there first and warned Aceiron.27 He spent the siege inside with Aceiron, AJ, Elle, Lyre and Rhydeu, and joined Aceiron, Elle and Lyre in a group chat with some of the Iron Flankers that Apollos set up.7 To keep the Capital from calling the police, Aceiron then sent him, AJ and Rhydeu outside. ScottyAte walked up to NGin, four inches shorter than him, and stared him down while NGin's followers got ready to attack. Then he held out his hand, and NGin shook it.7

When two shots were fired at the house shortly before midnight and the Calebros accused the Iron Flank of trying to assassinate Aceiron, ScottyAte kept the two sides from fighting and told them that the Liberators were the likely culprits; Kay had him tell everyone to keep quiet about Ninzard when the police arrived.28 A selfie that Aceiron took outside the house with him, AJ, Elle, Lyre and Rhydeu became one of the war's most memed photos.29 Afterward he worked with AJ, Danbi and Lyre to make the Ories more reasonable.30

Legislature XI (2026)

After Rudolf was removed as a representative for his part in the Samson Switch, ScottyAte won Parry's seat in the snap election and took office on 28 February 2026, one of the Legislature's four Ories.31,8 He was also one of its trolls, and much of what he posted there was slurs and Donald Trump GIFs.8 In the Charter talks of March 2026 he agreed that doxxing, swatting and abuse material had to be banned, but warned against vaguer rules:9

「Bro, we all agree on no doxxing, no swatting, no CSAM. Nobody wants that shit here. But if you make the very first rule no toxicity or whatever vague safety shit, Blue will use to neuter Parry and Volley.」

— ScottyAte, March 2026

When Aceiron and Syn were the only votes against the revised Part I, he, Raffaele and AJ talked Aceiron round (“Take the deal, Caleb”), and Part I passed with all fifteen representatives. ScottyAte celebrated with a photo of Eliza Kitten and the words “Jesus is King.”9

On the night of 29 March 2026 Aceiron was badly hurt in Los Angeles and hospitalized. Fearing that his family would take it for a political hit, Apollos had Advy wake ScottyAte shortly after midnight, judging him the only one who could look Aceiron's younger brother in the eye and keep him calm.32

「Yeah. Yeah, okay. I'm putting my shoes on. Send me the hospital info when you get it. I got Benji.」

— ScottyAte, to Apollos, 30 March 2026

He sat the brother down and told him the truth, and because the news came from an Orie veteran rather than from the Capital, the brother accepted it. ScottyAte stayed in touch with him afterward.32,33

On 25 April, when PoG, his own party's Boss, attacked Woofer over her time as General Geo's fifteen-year-old administrator, ScottyAte answered: “Going after a 15yo is a massive L. You don't punch down like that.”34 That spring he also defended Ploutos when Lless attacked him over his brother, then threatened Lless with violence.35 On the eve of the Holloway Summit, annoyed by NGin's aimless tirades and with Kay's permission, he told the Legislature that Liver had driven to Adelynn's home in Southern California.36 When the Summit ended the Chud War, he wrote, “Proud of my boys. The war is over,” and asked Kay to invite him next time.37

After Soda's death in May 2026, he posted:38

「Soda and I had our beefs, we argued, we trolled. But at the end of the day, he was one of the boys who built this place. He was here since the beginning. … Rest in peace, Lucca. I'm sorry it got this dark for you, bro. Prayers up for his family.」

— ScottyAte, 18 May 2026

Presidential campaign (2026)

On 29 May 2026 ScottyAte announced that he was running for president:39

「I am running for President. No cap. I have decided. … If victorious, I will fund the Sactown Club for the boys. Monthly free pizza party and BBQ. No more sadness. Sadness is illegal. Eliza Kitten endorses me.」

— ScottyAte, 29 May 2026

His trolling lightened a gloomy moment and won him quick backing from the Ories. PoG, the party's Boss, endorsed him the same day:39

「Scott Dellinger is not a philosopher. He offers the fire of the barbecue. He offers the pizza. He holds a creature small and fragile in his arms and he gives it the shelter. When the weapons were drawn in a California parking lot, he did not run. He stood in the breach and chose brotherhood over bloodshed.」

— PoG, 29 May 2026

The Orie Apparatus nominated him unopposed on 30 May, with Hackman as his running mate.10 Hackman was also Poka's running mate, so that the Capital's security and transition arrangements would carry on whoever won.40

On 1 June ScottyAte met Raffaele, Hackman, Meikaqu and Poka in San Jose, where Raffaele told them that a man claiming to be Scalen had sent members of Scope a video threatening the election and showing Woofer's home address. ScottyAte wanted to go after him himself; Raffaele and Hackman talked him into restraint, and the two candidates agreed to go on campaigning against each other as before.40

ScottyAte: But this stays out of the campaign. Nobody on my side uses Nat for points. If they do, I deal with them.

Raffaele: Politically.

ScottyAte: Sure.

Raffaele: Scott.

ScottyAte: Yes. Politically.

Apollos called both candidates highly qualified and argued that ScottyAte had grown a great deal since the Sactown Pound, though some Preservates still saw him as dangerous.41 On 13 June Apollos's younger brother accused him of “pandering with the cat,” and many members agreed; in Parry, Phalth and Aurase said the same.42,43 He was endorsed by PoG, Aceiron, AJ, Mel, NGin, Lyre and Liver. NGin, his opponent in the Chud War, praised his “sincere masculine spirit.”44

Poka won with 616 of 812 votes (75.9%) to his 196 (24.1%), the most lopsided presidential election in Ninzard's history.11 He carried only Volley, where the Holy Iron Flank and the Rationalist Ories around AJ and Mel turned out for him.44 He lost his own district, Parry, 57 to 73: the Parry Girls campaigned hard for Poka, and moderate Rationalists doubted that he was the right person to keep the community's institutions running.43 Main, where the Minecraft feud had left him distrusted and many newer members knew him only from the Chud War and the Sactown Pound, gave him 7.3%, and Wafflers mocked his “meathead gym bro” image as corny.23,15

Faith and the Romantic Ories

After the election the Ories divided into Rationalists and Romantics, and ScottyAte and PoG became the faces of the Romantic Flank. Its members rejected the Capital's moralizing about touching grass as infantilizing, and favored face-to-face friendship between Ninzards instead, as at his barbecues.45

ScottyAte is a Presbyterian. In the summer of 2026 he introduced Aceiron to his church near the home of Aceiron's mother, and on 20 September 2026 Aceiron was baptized there. ScottyAte wrote the archive's account of the baptism, signed “S.D.,” ending:12

「We didn't know what to make of our leadership, who now sat in the back of a church. Caleb Jensen let us wonder.」

— ScottyAte, on Aceiron's baptism

AJ later joined the same church.46 ScottyAte is one of the archive's contributors, credited in its eighteenth and twenty-first sections.47,48 At Hyperio's wedding on 10 October 2026 he sat on the aisle beside Aceiron, and in the parking lot afterward he was the first to congratulate the groom, who pulled him into a quick embrace.49

Parry Staff

ScottyAte is a moderator on the Parry Staff Team under General Kaimachi and Elle, its Chief of Administration. He serves alongside Boygho, Kain and Rudolf, his old comrades from the Sactown Pound, and Phalth and Aurase, who accused him of pandering with his cat in 2026.
