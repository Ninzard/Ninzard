Marguerite, nicknamed Mouse by Kain, is a member of the Parry Staff under General Kaimachi and the loudest of the Salonnistes, the faction of Parryans loyal to Kaimachi's predecessor, PoG. At the Parry Trials of 14 August 2026 she told Kaimachi that she was no general; Kaimachi answered by giving her the district's salon channel and daring her to build something better than Kaimachi's own.1,2

Salonnistes

Under PoG, who governed Parry for the first half of 2026, the district had become a salon that lived on argument, offense and spectacle, presided over by a general who watched its quarrels from above. After Kaimachi beat PoG by two votes and turned her first month in office over to her friends, the Parry Girls, three blocs formed in the district. Marguerite belonged to the Salonnistes, PoG's loyalists from the Romantic Flank of the Original Party, who watched Kaimachi's events in honor of Apollos and saw the salon PoG had built turned into what they called a thirst nest.3,1,4

By late July 2026 the Salonnistes had a thesis: Parry was dying under Kaimachi, a clique general who had won by a fluke and governed for her friends, and only PoG could ever hold the district. They spread it across other districts, side servers and private messages, and through the war of August they fought the Parry Girls and the Sacback Room alike as a matter of aesthetic principle.1,4

Parry Trials (2026)

On 14 August 2026 Kaimachi opened a public tribunal on a stage channel and invited her fiercest critics to prosecute her, with no mutes and no bans. After Rudolf, Marguerite asked for the floor.5,2

Marguerite: Anaïs held this district for six months without ever once begging and she never did this humiliation ritual. She sat above it. Everything worked. You won by two votes and now that it's on fire, you want us all to hold hands and feel our feelings. This is embarrassing. You're not a General.

Mélanie: OH you did NOT just—

Kaimachi: Mél—Mélanie, no. No, I got it, I got it. Marguerite, you're right about Anaïs. Genuinely. She's better at this than me. … I don't want you watching me. I'm down here in it with the rest of you.

Marguerite: Very pretty speech for someone with no plan.

Kaimachi: Oh I have a plan. I hate my plan. Wanna hear it?

Marguerite: Go on.

Kaimachi's plan was to appoint none of her friends and to give her enemies real seats instead. She handed Rudolf the men's events, and then turned to Marguerite.2

Kaimachi: And Marguerite—the salon channel. It's yours. Yours. If Anaïs is the only one who could ever build something real here—okay, cool, then go ahead and build it. Build it! I dare you to make it better than mine.

Marguerite: What?

Kaimachi: I'll take the girl who hates me and shows up! I'll take it over a fan who forgets my name by this fall.

The archive reads the trials as turning sabotage into stakeholding: the Salonnistes drew their power from being an aggrieved court in exile, and once Kaimachi gave them a real seat and a channel, their martyrdom evaporated. Taking the seat made them invested in Parry's success; refusing it would have shown them as wanting only to burn the district down.2 Three days later PoG herself declared that a cause was “such tedious work” and that she was simply bored, and with that the Salonnistes' campaign for her return died.6 Meikaqu named the same move as one of the three things that won Parry for Kaimachi: she gave positions to those who wanted her gone and dared them to do better.7

Parry Staff

Marguerite is a member of the Parry Staff Team under General Kaimachi, in charge of the salon channel Kaimachi gave her at the trials.2 Kain calls her “Mouse.”
