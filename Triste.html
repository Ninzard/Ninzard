Tristen Mercer (born 2 May 2002), known online as Triste, is an American political extremist and former militant leader within the Ninzard Project.1 He served as the founder and paramilitary commander of the far-left Liberation Front from December 2023 until his arrest in February 2026, and later integrated the organization as the militant wing of ViaTea's Liberation Party.1,2

Initially an operative under General Geo's Nightwork-Main Anti-Trolls (NMAT) and subsequently a paid contractor for President Catx3, Mercer defected from the Capital in late 2023 following a severe personal crisis and ideological disillusionment.2,3 He established the Liberation Front to wage asymmetric digital warfare against the community's emergent far-right factions, including the Holy Iron Flank (HIF) and the followers of former Governor Caleb Jensen (the "Calebros").2

Mercer's activities culminated on 14 February 2026 during the "Chud War," when he conducted a drive-by shooting targeting Jensen's family residence in suburban Sacramento.4,5 Apprehended by a Sacramento Police Department SWAT team on 20 February 2026, Mercer was indicted on multiple state felony counts, resulting in the formal designation of the Liberation Party as a criminal organization and its immediate dissolution.6

Early life and ideological formation

Tristen Mercer was born on 2 May 2002 and raised in Sacramento, California.1,2 Accounts of his early life describe a volatile household environment marked by domestic conflict between his parents and chronic financial hardship, requiring Mercer to assist in providing for three younger siblings through low-wage retail and service industry employment.2 These formative experiences contributed to a pronounced anti-authoritarian disposition and an adversarial relationship with institutional authority.2

In 2021, Mercer re-emerged within online circles with his partner, Maya. During this period, he became an outspoken proponent of anarcho-communism, polyamory, and radical relationship models, arguing that traditional monogamy was rooted in capitalist concepts of proprietary ownership and male insecurity.2 He frequently engaged in ideological debates, framing his anti-traditionalist worldview as a conscious rejection of patriarchal norms.2

NMAT and Catx3 network (2022–2023)

In early 2022, Mercer briefly joined the Nightwork-Main Anti-Trolls (NMAT) under General Geo, viewing the group as a potential bulwark against online reactionary rhetoric.2 He departed within weeks, citing disgust with the organization's internal culture, which he characterized as misogynistic and predatory toward female members.2

Following his departure from NMAT, Mercer was recruited by the administration of President Catx3. Misinterpreting Cat's authoritarian governance as an anti-patriarchal subversion of the male-dominated Ninzard establishment, Mercer operated as a paid contractor on her ledger between 2022 and 2023, receiving $1,460 while refusing $363 in disbursements.2,3

In late 2023, Mercer suffered a dual ideological and personal collapse. Maya dissolved their relationship, citing a desire for personal stability and conventional masculinity, which Mercer interpreted as an emasculating invalidation of his political philosophy.2 Simultaneously, he recognized that Cat's administration was not a revolutionary project, but an exploitative, commercialized criminal enterprise.2 Mercer formally severed ties with the Catx3 Capital in December 2023.2

The Liberation Front (2023–2025)

Following his defection, Mercer aligned himself with ViaTea's emergent Liberation Movement, establishing the "Liberation Front" as an autonomous, militant anti-fascist faction.2 While ostensibly dedicated to defending marginalized and LGBTQ+ members, the group operated primarily as an aggressive paramilitary organization, conducting doxxing operations and coordinated cyber-harassment against the Holy Iron Flank and the Calebros.2

During Caleb Jensen's 2025 gubernatorial administration, the conflict intensified. Jensen publicly ridiculed Mercer, labeling him "Cryst" and mocking his past writings on relationship dynamics, which inflamed Mercer's resentment.2 In November 2025, Mercer formally integrated the Liberation Front into ViaTea's newly founded Liberation Party as its militant enforcement arm.1

On 21 December 2025, Mercer and ViaTea coordinated retaliatory raids across several Nightwork districts.7 While the assault on Main was immediately repelled by General Woofer's staff, operatives loyal to Mercer severely vandalized Scope and completely deleted Volley's server channels.7 The action resulted in widespread community backlash, alienating key allies and prompting internal resignations within the party.7

The Chud War and attempted assassination

On 14 February 2026, long-standing factional hostility culminated in physical confrontations in Sacramento, known as the "Chud War."4 After right-wing factions converged on Jensen's residence, Mercer armed himself with an unregistered 9mm semi-automatic handgun and patrolled the perimeter in an unmarked blue sedan, monitoring the Capital's crisis channel.2

Mercer interpreted the ongoing stand-off and the far-right rhetoric as an existential failure of Ninzard's democratic and legal structures.2 At 23:40 PST, moments after transmitting an explicit death threat against Jensen into an encrypted group chat, Mercer drove past the residence and fired two rounds at the home.2,5 One bullet missed the structure, while the second shattered a front-facing window near where Jensen and his ailing mother were sheltering.2,5 Mercer fled the scene at high speed, abandoning the area before law enforcement arrived.2,5

Arrest and aftermath

The Sacramento Police Department launched a felony investigation that night.5,6 Investigators correlated digital threat logs, private confessions from detained participants, neighborhood surveillance footage, and automated license plate readers (ALPR) to identify Mercer's vehicle.6

At 04:30 PST on 20 February 2026, a tactical unit executed a no-knock warrant at Mercer's Sacramento home.6 He was taken into custody without incident. A search of the premises and his sedan yielded the 9mm firearm, spent shell casings matching the ballistics from the Jensen residence, and mobile hardware containing active session tokens for the "Triste" Discord account.6

Mercer was booked into the Sacramento County Main Jail and remanded without bail.6 The Sacramento County District Attorney's office charged him with:

  • Shooting at an inhabited dwelling (California Penal Code § 246)
  • Assault with a deadly weapon (California Penal Code § 245(a)(2))
  • Conspiracy to commit a crime (California Penal Code § 182)

On 21 February 2026, Minister of Justice Kay formally announced Mercer's arrest to the community and designated the Liberation Party as a criminal enterprise, prompting its immediate dissolution and the defection of remaining figures, such as Power of Greed, to other factions.6,8 As of late 2026, Mercer remains in state custody awaiting trial, facing a statutory minimum sentence of fifteen years to life imprisonment.6
