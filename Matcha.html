Emma Jeong (born 15 August 2006), known as Matcha, is a Ninzard politician who served as the 7th District General of Waffle from 1 January 2024 to 2 July 2025. Regarded as Waffle's “caretaker,” she was twice re-elected, and was the Preservation Party's nominee in the June 2025 gubernatorial election, which she lost to Governor Aceiron with 38.9% of the vote, carrying only Waffle.1,2,3,4

Unbending in her opposition to the far right, she left the Preservation Party in November 2025, writing that it had “confused pragmatism with complicity,” and joined ViaTea's Liberation Party, but refused to follow its members into the Original Party in 2026 and has since been an independent.5,6 In the spring of 2026 she and Lesty tried to reach Soda, a radicalized and deeply troubled member, learning French to talk with him, and after his death Lesty held up her compassion as a blueprint for the community. Since the summer of 2026 she has quietly led a Buddhist counter-current to Ninzard's Christian revival.7,8

Early career (2022–2023)

Matcha was among the contenders in the 2022 presidential election, as a Preservate.9 In December 2023 she ran for General of Waffle to succeed ViaTea, a close friend who was then the Original Party's nominee for governor. ViaTea endorsed neither Matcha nor the Orie candidate, Idodo, believing that either would lead Waffle well. Matcha won by 65 votes to 59 and took office on 1 January 2024.1

District General of Waffle (2024–2025)

Matcha's empathetic style of leadership resonated with marginalized members, and she came to be regarded as Waffle's “caretaker.”3 Even her staff was within Cat's reach: one of her moderators, Triste, had been secretly paid by Cat for more than a year and used his permissions on her orders.10

Plebiscite and re-election (2024)

In June 2024, when Governor Adelynn put her leadership to a plebiscite at Spinzle's suggestion, Matcha rebuked her before the Preservate Apparatus for “willingly” giving the far right an opportunity to take over the community, called her “emotionless,” and argued that letting the far right campaign against Adelynn was an unacceptable risk. Woofer accused her of “twisting” Adelynn's intentions. Once the vote went ahead, Matcha and Woofer urged their districts to vote against a snap election, and 56.0% of Nightians did.11,12 Matcha was re-elected the same day, beating Idodo again by 62 votes to 52.13

In December 2024 she won a third term, by 77 votes to 48, while Waffle gave Adelynn 72.0% in her losing race against Aceiron in the 2024 gubernatorial election.2,14 After the defeat Matcha brought Grace, the outgoing deputy governor, into Waffle as its head of staff.15

2025 gubernatorial campaign

For the June 2025 election the Preservation Party nominated Matcha against Aceiron. Adelynn judged that she had “good character” but doubted she could win; she endorsed her all the same. Matcha chose ViaTea as her running mate.3 The Ories portrayed her as a radical leftist out of touch with reality, pointing to her alignment with the LGBTQ community, her she/they pronouns and her passionate support for Palestine in the Gaza War. Spinzle called her a “sanctimonious hypocrite,” and moderates such as Raffaele saw her moral absolutism as a danger, while Seandry and Syn backed her despite their doubts.3

Her candidacy left Waffle's generalship open. When Aceiron had the Ories nominate Shuffle, an Israel Defense Forces veteran of the Gaza War, against Grace, the Waffle administrator Tegas demanded that Matcha ban Shuffle from the district. She refused, saying, “Shuffle violated no rules,” and Tegas resigned, with two moderators following him.15

Aceiron won 431 votes to 274. Voters judged that Matcha's campaign had put “conduct” before practical results, and only Waffle, by 83 votes to 43, voted for her.3,4,15 She handed Waffle over to Grace on 2 July 2025.

Break with the Preservates (2025)

When Aceiron called a presidential election in August 2025, Matcha was among the Preservates who doubted Apollos as a nominee, and she briefly considered running herself, but did not.16 During the campaign she, like Adelynn, objected to an Apollos ad that mocked Aceiron's grief.17

In November 2025 she sided with Cyntaku in favor of enlarging the Preservate Apparatus to counter the Ories' growing organization.18 Days later NGin, the Orie nominee in Volley's snap election, released an advertisement built on Nazi imagery and antisemitic slurs.19 When the Preservates discussed it, Matcha was dismayed by what she saw as her colleagues' calm, and clashed with Al Commando and Moctezuma.20

Matcha: I find it profoundly troubling that a Nazi candidate has the possibility to be elected, and the collective response here appears to be one of minimal concern? I don't think we need to overanalyze a basic moral issue.

Adelynn: Let me clarify this matter unequivocally. Indeed, it is highly alarming, and you are quite right that it fundamentally represents a moral problem. However, I do not see this as an overanalysis, as a moral dilemma often requires a thorough analysis of the most effective and principled approach to address it.

— Preservate Apparatus, 17 November 2025

On 19 November 2025, the day ViaTea announced her Liberation Party, Matcha resigned from the Preservation Party in a message to Adelynn, Grace, Spinzle and Apollos. The party expressed dismay at her departure.5

「We have become so obsessed with the strategy of survival that we have lost the very soul we sought to preserve. We have confused pragmatism with complicity. … There is a difference between keeping order and keeping quiet. I refuse to do the latter any longer.」

— Matcha, resigning from the Preservation Party, 19 November 2025

Liberation Party and independence (2025–2026)

Matcha went with ViaTea into the Liberation Party. On 22 December 2025, after ViaTea lost Waffle and broke down in a voice call, Matcha tried to calm her. ViaTea resigned as the party's leader and tried to hand it to Matcha, but most of its members would not accept her and turned instead to Power of Greed, who had just won Parry.21 When Power of Greed took over the Original Party in February 2026 and brought many former Liberators with her, Matcha and ViaTea refused to join the Ories and declared themselves independents.6

In the March 2026 snap elections Matcha endorsed the independent Lashae for General of Waffle.22 She ran herself for one of Waffle's two seats in Legislature XI and finished fourth, behind KatDoesMuch, Lesty and Rae Fox, with 60 of 126 approvals (47.6%).23 In June 2026 she, Lesty and ViaTea endorsed Poka for president, bringing many former Liberators and leftists with them.24

Efforts to reach Soda

In the spring of 2026 Matcha and Lesty spent hours trying to reach Soda, a deeply troubled young member of the far right. Lesty spoke with him in French; Matcha, who did not know the language, spent her evenings learning it to persuade him that he was worth saving.7 Scalen, who was recruiting for his new Black Sun Fraternity, convinced Soda that the two were fake friends working for the Capital, and in his last weeks Soda turned on Matcha with calculated cruelty.25,7 After his death in May 2026, Lesty's memorial essay, “A Hearth for the People of the Cold,” praised Matcha's “radical empathy” as the example the community should follow.7 Matcha was among the speakers at his memorial in the Scope stage channel on 23 May.26

「Matcha struggled through French verbs to save a boy who hated himself. That is the blueprint.」

— Lesty, “A Hearth for the People of the Cold,” 18 May 2026

Buddhist counter-current (2026)

As many members turned to Christianity in 2026, Matcha quietly led a Buddhist counter-current, described in the archive as an intellectual and psychological deconstruction of Christian guilt. Where the revival dwells on confession, fallenness and repentance, she reframes suffering as the natural result of clinging to impermanent things (anicca, 무상), and offers calm detachment in place of emotional self-reproach.8 Her account of the bodhisattva's compassion, posted on 16 August 2026, sets the martyr who throws themselves into the rain against someone who quietly holds an umbrella beside the sufferer.8
