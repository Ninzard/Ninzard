Noah Jay Byun (born 17 January 2003), known as Syn, is a Ninzard politician who has represented the District of Main in the Legislature since 1 January 2026. He was the 5th Governor of Nightwork from 1 March 2020 to 7 January 2022, and on 1 October 2020 founded the Preservation Party with Cyntaku and Grace, serving as its first Boss.1,2,3,4

Coerced into the community in 2014, Syn was one of Apollos's earliest allies and a friend in person, and their friendship became the defining ideological rivalry of his career: Apollos wanted Ninzard to stay informal, open and fun, while Syn wanted written rules, closed borders and order.5,6,7,8 Elected General of Main as an independent on 15 January 2019 in revolt against the Underground's “Progression.” He went on to become the Governor of Nightwork, becoming the first Nightian Governor who was not from California. He governed the faction as a fortress, sealing its borders, reinstating identity checks and winning five gubernatorial elections, one of them by a single vote, before resigning in January 2022. He subsequently served in the Korean military before returning in Canada in 2024.9,1,10,11,3

Out of office he remained the voice of the Preservates' hardliners, later known as the Axiom Flank, and in 2024 he cut Cat's money-laundering scheme off from Canada.12,13 Reconciled with Apollos in 2025, he opposed him again over the Charter, arguing that it should be imposed as an “axiom” rather than negotiated as a treaty.14,15,16 On 8 May 2026 he was one of the “Five Kings” who met in Seattle, a day he recorded for the Archives in his account Men Who Did Enough.17,18

Early life

Byun was born on 17 January 2003 to Korean immigrant parents and grew up in Vancouver, British Columbia. Hot-tempered from childhood, he trained in martial arts and was known for turning physical with people he knew in real life. His parents were divorcing when he discovered the community, and the online world became his escape from the shouting at home.5 His father, a gambler, ran up debts to loan sharks, which Byun tried as a teenager to manage by hiding money, negotiating with creditors at the door and calming his father's outbursts.19

Early years in Ninzard (2014–2016)

Joining

Syn joined in 2014, during Vice President Snuck's expansion policy, through Korean-Canadian friends in Ninzardia, and became a member of Hybriden. He had refused at first, until an anonymous member called Orion1 threatened to leak his name and address and break into his gaming accounts; the details had come from Hokan. Syn bore Orion1 a lifelong grudge, and Hokan an even deeper one; Orion1 was later revealed to be the founder, Antareze.5 He came to like the community anyway, and became known as one of its best gamers, respected for his logic and feared for his wrath and stubbornness. He began as a moderate who criticized Snuck but disliked the drama of the Resistance Party.5

Syn met Apollos in a park after they realized they lived nearby. Disillusioned with every other party, he backed Apollos's Original Party as the best of the field, while urging him to temper his instincts, a point on which the two argued. Apollos backed him for General of Hybriden in June 2015, but Orion1 entered the race unexpectedly and won; voters also thought Syn too young.6 Weeks later Antareze's Nightwork Draft Act transferred him from Hybriden into Nightwork, which he condemned as a ploy to push the rising Ories out of Ninzardia.20 In the Ninzard Civil War of 2015–2016 he supported Nightwork, though not blindly, insisting that its leaders be moderate, pragmatic and flexible; he hated Hyperio above all, and learned to use remote access trojans against Hyperio's forces.21

2016 campaign

With Snuck he helped run Apollos's 2016 presidential campaign, though the two traded insults and threats in voice calls to the delight of the other Ories. It was then that Club Korea gave him the name 독사 (Doksa, “viper”).22 After Apollos's upset victory, Syn urged him to govern as a moderate to disarm his critics, and Apollos largely agreed. Offered a transfer back to Hybriden, Syn chose to stay in Nightwork, where his influence had helped Apollos win. Though he had been strong-armed into joining, he had grown so loyal to Ninzard that he vowed to harm Apollos if he ever abandoned it.22

Golden Era (2016–2018)

Early in Apollos's presidency Syn urged him to draft an official constitution. Apollos dismissed it as more “bureaucracy” and said the community would never agree to one; Syn insisted he could persuade them. He also criticized Apollos's defence of General Judah, which he said had cost the Ories the community's trust. When the Ories did well in the district elections of late 2016, Apollos called Syn to tell him he had been wrong; Syn replied that the support was “transient.”7,23,24

Han River Episode

In 2017 Syn spent more than forty hours over three weeks building a perfectly symmetrical fortress on the community's Minecraft server. Snuck tunnelled beneath it, packed it with TNT and set it off while Syn was showing it to a voice call. Syn's bilingual meltdown, which Snuck answered with helpless laughter, became one of the most replayed recordings in Ninzard and changed their relationship for good.25 It also gave the community the slang words “Han'd,” “symmetry” and “shibal.”26

「I'LL FUCKING BURY YOU UNDER THE HAN RIVER… LOOK AT THE SYMMETRY! IT'S GONE! MY SYMMETRY!」

— Syn, the Han River Episode, 2017

Adjudicator nomination and Gold Road

When the Legislature removed Capital Adjudicator MMC on 1 July 2017, Apollos nominated Syn in his place, but Syn had made many enemies among the Ories, and the nomination failed by three votes to ten. Syn told Apollos to “move on.”27 He kept watch on the breakaway Gold Road community at Apollos's request, but warned him against provoking a war and eventually refused to keep spying.28 Citing personal troubles, he declined Apollos's request to run for governor in March 2018, and that spring an uncharacteristic shouting match with Governor DegreeZ hinted at the strain; Snuck answered it by spreading his photographs and trying to have him hacked, until Apollos ordered him to stop.29,30

After the deaths in Gold Road in 2018 his friendship with Apollos broke down in a public fight (see Rivalry with Apollos). That August, as Ninzardians poured into Nightwork, Syn accused Apollos and DegreeZ of forcing their admission over the wishes of the district generals, and secretly had members of Main pretend to be hacked all at once to frame the newcomers. Apollos saw through it and denounced him, but when DegreeZ tried to ban Syn, Apollos insisted he stay.31,32

General of Main (2019–2020)

December 2018

On 17 December 2018 a gathering of men to whom his father owed money turned violent in the family's home in Vancouver, and they searched the house while Syn, then fifteen, hid in his closet; members in his voice call heard glass breaking before he disconnected. In the closet he vowed to demand absolute order of himself and the world around him. He did not log in again until 2 January 2019. Governor Spikester mocked members for worrying about him.19 Sleepless, he spent his nights online, and Nightwork-Main became the one “room” left where he could install a lock.19,33

Election

Spikester's “Progression” was then filling Nightwork with members of rival communities. Declaring himself “politically homeless,” Syn ran for General of Main as an independent on a platform of making Nightwork secure, online almost twenty hours a day; he later dated his cigarette habit to that campaign. In the snap elections of 13–14 January 2019 he unseated Spikester's loyalist Whyskitter, and he took office on 15 January.9,33,34 Every general elected that day was an independent, among them Cyntaku in Volley, who became his closest ally against the Underground.9,35

Against Spikester

As general Syn was Spikester's fiercest critic, calling his expansionism “the long con,” a scheme to turn Nightwork into a business and take it over from within; Spikester called him “cyclothymic” and a “loser.”36 Syn declared the original founding vision of Ninzard “DEAD.”37 He was comfortably re-elected in June 2019 and again in December.38,39 In 2019 he also told Main that Helen was planning to hack him and its members, a claim he had inflated from hearsay; she had earlier tried to have him punished by spreading lies about him to DegreeZ, and the two, who knew each other in person, remained enemies.1

Attack on Hokan

In September 2019 the Capital's Watchmaker Files confirmed that Orion1 had been Antareze all along.40 Enraged, Syn went looking for the founders. Unable to find Antareze, whose family had moved, and told by Meikaqu that she did not know where he was, he traced Hokan, then living in Toronto, to a weekend party in British Columbia, tapped him on the shoulder and beat him in front of the guests, injuring him and several others. Syn, then sixteen, was convicted in youth court and given a two-year custody-and-supervision sentence; he finished his schooling in custody and received his Dogwood Diploma a year late.41

Governor of Nightwork (2020–2022)

Election

After Spikester's fall in February 2020, Syn asked Cyntaku to run with him: “We take the whole thing. We kill the Underground. We close the borders.” In the snap election of 29 February – 1 March 2020 the two independents were elected as the 5th Governor and 4th Deputy Governor of Nightwork, and they took office on 1 March. Some polls showed Syn more popular than Apollos, but he chose not to run against him. Arcus succeeded him in Main.1,35 Cyntaku later called the pairing “the Bouncer and the Architect”: he confronted their enemies while she wrote the policies that kept them out.35

Syn began with a crackdown on alternate accounts, requiring members to prove they were real people and banning anyone caught using an alt; among the casualties was Kyaba, Cat's second identity in Parry.42 He welcomed Grace's appointment as Capital Adjudicator but openly despised Helen, the new vice president, whom he regarded as erratic.1 He endorsed Apollos's re-election in August 2020, a campaign built on destroying the Underground, then demanded that the community be closed indefinitely. He also wanted a constitution for Nightwork; Apollos refused, saying members were “fed up” with online politics.43 Once Apollos had won, Syn purged the Underground platforms and members deemed too toxic for Nightwork, a clean-up completed on 15 August; he then closed Nightwork “indefinitely,” and refused to readmit Cat.44,2

September 2020 lockdown

On 1 September 2020 the generals of Main and Parry, the cousins Arcus and Snuck, began inviting members without warning. Syn stripped their permissions; Snuck retorted that he did not “own this house.” Apollos, calling Syn “excessively controlling,” restored them.45 With Grace, Syn proposed that each district admit one screened member a month, at most 48 a year; Apollos laughed and rejected it. Syn responded by moving all of Nightwork into a single new server closed to the president, stripping every general and staff member of their permissions and disabling every invite.46 Hackman of Scope and Lesty of Volley refused to back him, Lesty sheltering the stranded former Wafflers in a server of her own, while Parry broke away under Snuck as “BobaBitcoin.” Grace urged Syn not to “kill the entire community”; he answered that there would be “no compromise” on his members' safety. At the end of September he agreed to put his mandate to a vote.46

Founding the Preservation Party

On 1 October 2020 Syn founded the Preservation Party with Cyntaku and Grace, on a platform of defending and protecting Nightwork's members.2,10 Cyntaku recalled that Syn wanted a name that told people exactly what they were doing, so that no one could twist it.35 The Ories nominated Aceiron, who called Syn the “Governor of the Hermit Kingdom” and likened him to Kim Jong Un, and Apollos, Helen and three of the four district generals endorsed him; Syn's outburst against communism alienated many of Lesty's left-wing supporters.10 On 3–4 October he won 168 of 328 votes (51.2%) to Aceiron's 142, carrying every district but Parry, and Main by 107 to 21.10,47 On 5 October he ended the closure, restored every district except Parry to its general, and reinstated member identification, its first use anywhere in Ninzard since 2016.10

To his irritation, a following of female fans, the Synergy Fangirls, grew up around him, calling him “Daddy Noah” and selling his isolationism as the mark of an exclusive club; he kept them at a distance, wary of the party becoming a fan club.48 He complained that Apollos had dismantled the Legislature and the community's other democratic institutions and ignored term limits; the Capital agreed to a vice-presidential election in December 2020, in which Syn's nominee, Grace, lost to Helen by 55% to 45%.49 Expansion resumed only in small steps, seven new members a month across the districts, each reviewed personally by Syn and Cyntaku.50

「Either you have to privatize the platform or limit free speech. It is not possible to have unlimited free speech in a public space, especially with the looming threat of corporate censorship. The two desires are, therefore, incompatible. The policy of Ninzardia will not last for long. To suggest otherwise is merely an idealistic fallacy.」

— Governor Syn, 21 October 2020

Re-elections of 2021

Helen cut the governors' terms to four months, a move aimed at undermining him. Syn simply ran again, and on 31 January 2021 beat Xorbit, whom he called a “drunkard” and an “embarrassment to shared blood,” by a respectable margin.50,51 He followed the win with a conciliatory open letter to Ninzardia, signed “Noah.”51 Six weeks earlier he had declared: “Nothing good is left of Ninzardia. It's all Nightwork.”52 He negotiated with Governor Clexas of Ninzardia, agreeing to relax his isolationism in exchange for her easing Ninzardia's restrictions on Nightians, but his efforts to give Nightwork a constitution foundered on the opposition of Parry and of Orie moderates who saw it as a tool of his rule.53,50 He grew deeply suspicious of Raffaele, whose power in Hybriden was one of the few things restraining the radical Preservates from declaring war on Ninzardia.54

Syn: Your entire personality is stolen straight from Akita's Orion1. … Everything about you is stolen and LARP.

Raffaele: Your entire personality is counterphobia, to which you have named the Preservation Party.

Syn: Wrong. I make. You steal. I owned fear into conviction and power. You copy conviction from men because you have none.

Raffaele: Conviction that is born from fear will always serve fear in the end. Do not flatter yourself.

Syn: Spare me the sermon.

— Governor Syn and General Raffaele, early 2021

The May 2021 election was a wake-up call. Against the Ories' populist nominee, Alaric, Syn won 136 of 299 votes (45.5%) to 135, a margin of one vote. When the Ories demanded a runoff, he told them it was their own fault for opposing a constitution that would have set electoral rules, and Alaric conceded. Recognizing the limits of his power, Syn quietly abandoned the constitution and set out to win the electorate back.11 In August 2021 he beat the Orie Cubi by more than ten points; voters, the archive concluded, liked him as a person more than his policies, and he conceded some slight expansions.55

Resignation

By the end of 2021 the Poo Party held the vice presidency and the governorship of Ninzardia, and Syn had become increasingly unpopular. On 4 January 2022 he announced that he would resign once his successor was elected, and endorsed Cyntaku. He privately thanked Admin Seandry for standing by him through his hardest times, but did not answer when Apollos contacted him.3 Cyntaku succeeded him as governor on 7 January 2022, ending a Syn Administration of almost two years, and took over the leadership of the party.3,56

Out of office (2022–2025)

In May 2022 Syn attacked the radicals who had risen in his old party, endorsing Cyntaku for re-election only “reluctantly.”57 He backed Spinzle's constitutional draft for Nightwork that August, urging her to force it through on the strength of her election, and criticized her for presenting it “too late.”58 After Adelynn remade the party in 2023, the old hardliners of Syn's and Cyntaku's era became known as the Axiom Flank, in opposition to her Logos Flank.12 Ahead of the 2024 election he warned the party that Adelynn would probably lose to Aceiron, and was shunned for his pessimism.59

Canadian Firewall

In early 2024 a seventeen-year-old member in Vancouver reported that Alaric had offered him a job requiring a Canadian bank account. Recognizing an attempt to recruit money mules for Cat, Syn traced her transactions and company filings with Seandry and Sable, then flew to Montreal, where he persuaded Eve, a former Synergy Fangirl whom Cat was pressuring, to meet Cat and sign her documents while he watched from behind a pillar. He filed a suspicious transaction report, and on 12 May 2024 sent Cat a copy.13

「You routed illicit funds and signed fraudulent corporate documents on Canadian soil. … Route one packet of data north of the 49th parallel, contact one Canadian member, and the script executes. The agencies freeze your assets. Catherine, stay the fuck out of Canada.」

— Syn, to Cat, 12 May 2024

Cat cut her ties with members in British Columbia and Quebec, and the Preservate elite named the episode the “Canadian Firewall.”13 On 28 June 2025, despite his irritation with her, he endorsed the Preservate Matcha against Aceiron and denounced the moderates backing Aceiron as “neutered Ories.”60

Axiom Grandpa

When Cat fell in August 2025, Apollos urged Syn to run for president against Aceiron, but Syn declined (see Reconciliation).61 During the campaign Syn rebuffed Snuck's attempts to join the Preservates, setting off a feud the rest of the party called “childish,” and when Apollos later removed Snuck and ViaTea from the Preservate Apparatus, Syn told him, “You should have done this a long fucking time ago.”62,63 His standing had recovered since his resignation, and he took part in the Apparatus less often than before; when he did, PluPlu greeted him as “the Axiom Grandpa.”8

On 4 December 2025, after Aceiron purged his own party's leadership and allied with the Liberation Party, Syn published a critique dismissing the alliance as a “transient threat” that would be seen as “complete idiocy in the long term”; even Boygho, the general of Parry, admitted he agreed.64 That month he debated Apollos, Adelynn and Spinzle on how the Charter should treat trolls, holding to a binary rule that Apollos summed up as “obey the law or die” against Adelynn's system of small, boring penalties; outnumbered, he conceded: “You win. Idc.”65,66 On 17 December, the seventh anniversary of the break-in, he logged on to distract himself, smoking in his room to the sound of rain, and found some contentment in how many members had moved on from their old traumas.66

Representative for Main (2026–present)

Legislature XI

Syn took his seat as a Representative for Main in Legislature XI on 1 January 2026.67,68 Within the party he was regarded as the leader of the Axiom Flank, which held that the Charter should silence extremists, be imposed as an edict rather than a consensual mandate, and remove anyone who refused it.16 When Snuck rejoined the Apparatus in February 2026, their exchanges descended into Korean insult matches.69

When the Ories' draft of the Charter, posted by their Boss, Power of Greed, opened with the districts' sovereignty on 28 February 2026, Syn attacked it for barring the Capital from stepping in until a crime was already serious, calling the result “legalizing negligence,” and proposed that the Charter open instead with absolute red lines and their enforcement.68

「Anaïs writes a constitution like a poet who has never been punched in the face.」

— Representative Syn, on the Original Party's draft, 28 February 2026

He demanded that the ban on the Dark Arts and targeted harassment be written into the Charter's first section, and objected to vague phrases such as “psychological survival.” When the revised Part I came to a vote on 7 March 2026, he and Aceiron were the only representatives against it, Syn because its demand for an “explicitly stated reason” for every punishment would force the Capital “to write a fucking essay every time we mute a brainrot troll or a spammer.” Spinzle and Adelynn talked him round, and once Aceiron switched, so did Syn, making the vote unanimous at 15 to 0.70

In April 2026 he answered the Scopian representative Lless, who had argued for opening the borders as a honeypot for the far right.71 When Power of Greed suggested on 25 April that Woofer could not have been innocent as Geo's fifteen-year-old administrator, he called her a fraud who “observes violence from a balcony and critiques the posture of the bleeding.”72

「My 'authoritarian neurosis' exists because I actually had to clean the blood off the floor while you were sitting in Scope reading Žižek. … You are willing to invite the wolves inside just to prove the fence was poorly constructed.」

— Representative Syn, to Lless, April 2026

The Charter passed its referendum on 7 May 2026 with an 82% supermajority; Syn's verdict was “W.”73 When Apollos ratified it on 11 May by eating pineapple pizza off the signed document, Syn called the administration “a biological failure,” but voted against the joke impeachment that followed.74 After a veteran member, Soda, died on 18 May, Syn told the community not to romanticize his death, warned that anyone sharing footage of it would be exiled, and urged anyone with similar thoughts to seek medical help: “A Discord community cannot save your life.”75

Legislature XII

On 22 June 2026 Main re-elected him with 196 votes (75.4%), third of the district's four representatives in Legislature XII, in which every member but Advy was a Preservate.4,76 Under President Poka he was one of the few to object to her “light expansion” of the districts.77 When Parry descended into the Salon War that August, he was among the Preservates who urged the Capital to intervene; Poka overruled them, and General Kaimachi settled the war herself.78

「“Light expansion.” You are intentionally dismantling a secure perimeter because border control is no longer considered entertaining. When the baseline toxicity predictably rises to match the new capacity, do not ask the Apparatus to manage the resulting liability.」

— Representative Syn, 2 August 2026

Rivalry with Apollos

Syn's relationship with Apollos ran through his whole career. The two were friends in person and for years allies; they fell out over how Ninzard should be governed, became open enemies, and reconciled as adults. At its root was a disagreement about order. Syn believed the community needed written law, closed borders and swift punishment; Apollos believed it held together through informality, consensus and fun, and distrusted anyone who took its politics too seriously.6,7,8

Friend and adviser (2015–2017)

From the start Syn cast himself as Apollos's restraint. He urged him in 2015 to temper his instincts, and after the 2016 election to govern as a moderate, advice that shaped Apollos's early hands-off style.6,22 But he also pressed, again and again, for a written constitution, which Apollos rejected as bureaucracy the community would never accept, and in 2017 Apollos's attempt to make him Capital Adjudicator was voted down.7,24,27

Rupture (2018–2022)

The break came in 2018, after the deaths in Gold Road. When they met in a park with Snuck, Arcus and others, Apollos brushed off the Gold Road affair as merely “online.” Syn, who habitually mocked his height, threatened him. Apollos cursed at him; Syn grabbed him, and when Apollos shoved him away, threw him to the ground and beat him in front of a chanting crowd, calling him a “reckless piece of shit,” and stopped only when Apollos's nose began to bleed. When Arcus asked why he had stopped, Syn told him to “shut up or die” and seized him by the throat. He apologized the next day, and Apollos forgave him. Even then, Syn never joined those who called Apollos a “murderer”; his charge was “wantonness.”31

Apollos kept protecting Syn, sparing him a ban after his framing scheme that August, and in May 2019, after Slamo's resignation, offered him the vice presidency or the adjudicatorship in a “dream team” with Raffaele. Syn, more closed off since the break-in, asked why Apollos kept trying to bring him into high office, and told him that if he was looking for friendship or intimacy, he would not get it by appointing him.32,79

「I will ban every last one of those cockroaches. And if Raf doesn't let me, then there will be no peace.」

— General Syn, to Apollos, May 2019

Raffaele agreed that there would be no peace, and Apollos reinstated Hokan instead.79 The two worked together against the Underground in 2020 but split over what should follow it: Apollos returned the permissions Syn had taken from Arcus and Snuck, laughed off his one-member-a-month proposal, and in October 2020 endorsed Aceiron against him.43,45,46,10 By 2021 the old friends operated as enemies, and when Syn resigned in January 2022 he did not reply to Apollos's message.80,3

Reconciliation (2025)

The two made peace in the middle of 2025. When the Preservates searched for a candidate against Aceiron that August, Apollos's first choice was Syn: he endorsed “Syn/Noah” publicly and promised to stay out of the race if Syn ran, but Syn did not seek the presidency, and Apollos ran instead.61,14 After the December 2025 elections they met in person for the first time in years, and talked about their lives and about Ninzard.14

Inside the Preservate Apparatus they still clashed often enough for others to tell them to “chill out.” Syn had opposed Apollos's decision to give ViaTea power, and was proved right; he disliked Apollos's plan to reopen the community once the Charter passed, and called his insistence on a referendum an excuse for slowness. Yet after all their rivalries, the two had come to respect each other.8

Syn: Do not conflate Caleb's ineptitude with Reu's achievements. … As I already stated, my concern does not pertain to the LOC. They represent a temporary threat. My concern pertains to Reu.

Poka: @Syn Do you hate Fratellone?

Syn: @Poka No.

— The Preservate Apparatus, 4 December 2025

Axiom and Pragma (2025–2026)

The rivalry took its clearest form in the Leviathan Debate of 4 December 2025. With the opposition in disarray and four of the five district generalships in Preservate hands, Syn urged the Capital to enact the Charter at once, ban those who broke it, and end the “Controlled Burn” he believed Apollos was prolonging for the spectacle. Apollos had won 62% of the vote, he argued, and “the election WAS the discussion.” Adelynn, Grace and Poka argued for bringing the opposition into the process.15

「The Rationalists are not looking for a debate club. They are looking for a Leviathan. They want to know that the erratic behavior of the last three years is over. If we open the floor to the very agents of that chaos, we signal hesitation.」

— Syn, the Leviathan Debate, 4 December 2025

Apollos answered that a charter forced on the members as an axiom would die the moment a vandal touched it, because the community itself existed only because everyone agreed that it did.15

「a charter becomes a precarious treaty if forcefully pushed against the member base as if it were an "axiom" while you disregard the cultural consensus. … this community is an example of this model purely build upon a phenomenological imaginary structure yet EXISTS because everyone agrees it does」

— President Apollos, to Syn, 4 December 2025

Within the party the two led rival flanks, Apollos the Pragma Flank and Syn the Axiom Flank.16 The Charter that passed in May 2026 was negotiated with the opposition and approved by referendum, as Apollos had wanted, but its Part I, “Fundamental Protections,” carried the red lines Syn had fought for.70,81,73 Three days before the ratification, the two spent a day together in Seattle (see Five Kings Meetup).

Five Kings Meetup (2026)

The Five Kings Meetup was an informal gathering in Seattle on 8 May 2026 of Syn, Apollos, Snuck, Raffaele and Aceiron, five men who had spent much of the previous decade at war with one another. Syn later wrote a first-person account of the day for the Archives, Men Who Did Enough.17,18

Seattle

Apollos collected Syn in Vancouver at 11:15 that morning, an hour late, and talked the whole way through a forty-minute wait at the Peace Arch border crossing; they told the guard they were fetching a friend from the airport. At Sea-Tac they met Raffaele, who had secretly arranged for Snuck and Aceiron to fly in as well. Syn turned on Raffaele, who shrugged: “This is our Roman custom boys.”82 After Apollos nearly ran into the back of an SUV on the highway, Aceiron told him to “let Noah drive,” and Syn drove for the rest of the day.83

Over steaks at 13 Coins, for which Raffaele paid, the five talked about the recent Holloway Summit, Snuck's recovery and Aceiron's injuries. Asked by Snuck whether he had ever had a girlfriend, Syn replied that “intimacy requires a surrender of autonomy.”84 At Discovery Park, on benches above Puget Sound, Raffaele, Aceiron and Apollos spoke of their faith while Syn dismissed religion. When Snuck added that Christianity made everyone equal before God, Syn let it go: “A debate requires an opponent willing to fight. They sought peace. I refused to shatter their calm.”85

Syn: A psychological crutch. You're exhausted because you keep trying to save people who refuse to save themselves. Now you want an omnipotent administrator to carry the overhead.

Apollos: Perhaps. But the crutch supports my weight. If the outcome generates peace, then the mechanism proves its utility itself.

— Discovery Park, Seattle, 8 May 2026

As the sun set, Syn photographed the five of them against the Space Needle, with Raffaele springing up from a crouch at the last moment. Raffaele posted the picture to NPC! that evening, and it became one of the community's most famous images.86,18 Adelynn called it “the greatest victory we could have ever hoped for,” and Cyntaku, who remembered when the five could not share a voice call “without a bloodbath,” called it “a good kind of weird.”18 Syn drove the others to their late flights; at the kerb Raffaele prayed over the group while Syn stood in silence, and Snuck left him with “Stay toxic.”87,88

Men Who Did Enough

Too tired to drive home to Abbotsford, Apollos slept on Syn's couch in Vancouver. Late that night he confided that he felt isolated even among people, that the leader's mask was growing heavy, and that he feared leaving Ninzard would erase him from his friends' minds. Weighing the harm he had done against his efforts to undo it, he concluded: “One minus one equals zero.” Syn told him that guilt was an unnecessary tax, and when Apollos defended it, cut him off.89,90

「One bad act, one good act, balance equals zero? That equation is garbage. … If you have blood on your hands, own the blood. If you are cleaning the mess, own the mess. You did not have to come back. You could have left everyone else to live with the damage. Nobody forced you. That is not nothing.」

— Syn, to Apollos, 8 May 2026

“You have a brutal method of comfort,” Apollos told him. “Soft words engender complacency,” Syn replied.90 Around three in the morning Syn woke to hear Apollos crying in his sleep. He pulled the fallen blanket back over him, rested a hand on his shoulder and said quietly, “You did enough.”90

Impact and legacy

Syn founded the Preservation Party, which with the Ories has dominated the community's politics since 2020; Hackman, writing in 2026, named Syn and Cyntaku the defining figures of its first, isolationist era, which ended with Spinzle's election in 2022.2,91 The hardliners of that era survived as the Axiom Flank, a minority still identified with him in 2025 and 2026.92,16

His governorship reshaped Nightwork. He made identity checks and the ban on alternate accounts standing policy, and from 2020 the Capital could no longer override permissions in Nightwork, a limit that frustrated Apollos two years later.10,42,93 The automated moderation he and Cyntaku turned against banned words provoked Parry's trolls into misspelling everything, the origin of the Parry slang that spread across Ninzard.26 The Canadian Firewall cut Cat off from the community's members in Canada, and his insistence on absolute red lines left its mark on the Charter.13,70

His critics saw a tyrant. Aceiron likened him to Kim Jong Un, Lless saw a would-be authoritarian playing “dictator on Discord,” and Raffaele told him that conviction born of fear would always serve fear.10,94,54 Yet Lless voted for him in all three of his 2021 elections, because he hated the alternatives more, and in later years Syn's standing within the community recovered.94,8

Personal life

Byun lives in an apartment in Vancouver.89 He smokes, a habit he dates to his first campaign in January 2019.33 He holds a strictly secular view of the world, preferring “structural control” to religion, which he has said relies on “an invisible master-node that doesn't exist,” and he has said he has neither the time nor the patience for women.85,84 A Korean speaker, he has conducted many of his fiercest arguments with Snuck in Korean.69 He has contributed to the community's archival record, to Sections VII and XX, for which he wrote Men Who Did Enough.95,17

Other relationships

Cyntaku

Syn and Cyntaku met as generals in 2019, “not as friends,” she wrote, but as “co-conspirators in a mental asylum,” watching Spikester's alliances in private calls. They governed together for almost two years, and when he resigned, she wrote, he passed her the torch and told her it was her turn to hold the door.35

Snuck

Snuck, who blew up his fortress in 2017, tried to have him hacked in 2018, defied his lockdown in 2020 and turned out in 2022 to be the vandal OneAgainstAll, has been Syn's most persistent antagonist, and Syn has never spared him in return.25,30,45,96,69 Yet they campaigned together for Apollos in 2016, and in Seattle Snuck greeted him by his Club Korea name and sent him off with “Stay toxic.”22,82,88

Founders

Syn never forgave the founders for forcing him into the community: Antareze, as Orion1, coerced him into joining in 2014 with personal details supplied by Hokan, and as president moved him into Nightwork against his will in 2015. The grudge ended in the attack on Hokan in 2019.5,20,41
