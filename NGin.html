Ethan O’Neal (born 9 November 2003), known online as NGin, is an American polemicist, former political candidate, and political organizer within the Ninzard Project.1 He served as the founder and ideological leader of the Holy Iron Flank (HIF), an extremist far-right insurgent faction within the Original Party, from June 2025 until its formal dissolution in May 2026.2,3

Initially a background figure during the Underground and Post-Underground eras, O’Neal gained notoriety between 2024 and 2026 for his radical ideological positions, aggressive voice transmissions, and uncompromising hostility toward the technocratic establishment.4,5 Blending esoteric traditionalism, Christian nationalism, white supremacism, and internet subculture tropes—frequently invoking concepts such as "Hyperborean Vril energy," "Tartaria," and "Saturnian frequencies"—he positioned himself as the militant antithesis to both the Preservation Party’s managerial governance and Caleb Jensen’s populist "alt-lite" conservatism.5,6 He was also notable as one of the few figures who categorically rejected financial overtures from President Cat, famously stating that he did not take "Jewish money to hate Jews."7

O’Neal was a central instigator of the "Chud War" in Sacramento on 14 February 2026 and attempted an abortive extortion coup against the Original Party hierarchy known as the "Samson Switch" later that month.8,9 Despite his aggressive public persona, O’Neal experienced an emotional collapse at the "Holloway Summit" on 2 May 2026, where he renounced white nationalism and dissolved the Holy Iron Flank.10 He subsequently helped secure the final votes needed to ratify the Charter of the Ninzard Project and converted to Eastern Orthodox Christianity.11,12

Early involvement (2019–2024)

O’Neal joined the Ninzard Project around 2019 during the late Underground Era, having discovered the community from the Sacramento area.4,13 During the Quorey and "Deadaxz" revelations in late 2019, O'Neal was among the right-leaning witnesses influenced by President Apollos’s characterization of hidden ideological malice as cowardice, adopting a lifelong posture of absolute, unfiltered public transparency about his beliefs.14

During the presidency of Cat (2022–2025), O'Neal resided primarily in the District of Parry (then known as "BobaBitcoin"). When General Xorbit enacted the "Synagogue Strategy" to inflate Parry's membership counts with local Jewish youth, Cat deployed covert payments and alternate accounts to spark ethnic conflict.7 Cat attempted to bribe O'Neal with large cash sums to intensify antisemitic agitation; however, O’Neal flatly refused, stating: “I do not take Jewish money to hate Jews.”7 In Cat’s master financial ledger, O’Neal is recorded as having accepted $0 while refusing $1,415 USD.15 Throughout 2023 and 2024, O'Neal maintained ties with Linus Mandl (Lyman) and Torin Graves (Scalen) in the peripheral Sonnenrad Society, though he frequently expressed contempt for their reliance on anonymous, decentralized Telegram groups.16

The Holy Iron Flank (2025)

Establishment and Ideology

In June 2025, following Governor Caleb Jensen’s nomination of Israeli Defense Forces veteran Yael Azoulay ("Shuffle") as District General of Waffle, O’Neal formally broke with the mainstream Original Party establishment and established the Holy Iron Flank (HIF).2 Centered primarily in the District of Volley, the HIF operated as an ideological splinter faction dedicated to "Aryan vitalism," Christian nationalism, anti-technocracy, and the total eradication of liberal, feminist, and foreign influences within Ninzard.5,17

O’Neal coined the pejorative moniker "Uncle Samite" to describe President Apollos, asserting that the Capital was orchestrating a "ZOG-funded neoliberal simulation" designed to pacify young men through managerial therapy and corporate censorship.5,18 Following Apollos’s re-election in September 2025, O’Neal furiously confronted the Orie Apparatus, excoriating Caleb Jensen as a "castrated cuckold" and declaring the mainstream party dead.5

November 2025 Volley Snap Election

Following the impeachment of Volley District General Mel in November 2025, O’Neal secured the Original Party’s nomination for the snap election.19 On 15 November 2025, O’Neal released an infamous campaign advertisement that sent shockwaves through the community.20 Narration by O’Neal was layered over translated speeches by Adolf Hitler, antisemitic slogans, and Kanye West’s song "Heil Hitler."20 In the advertisement, O’Neal declared:

If you go outside your race because you have a need for pussy, you are only contributing to the problem if you breed a half-breed monster... Uncle Samite got the call! ACTIVITY IS NOT PURPOSE! THEY TREAT US LIKE NIGGERS! BECAUSE NINZARD HAS BEEN TAINTED BY CORRUPT CULTURES THAT HAVE BLED IT OF ITS IDENTITY! I offer no debate. I am not here to persuade the unpersuadable. This is not a discussion; IT IS A PRONOUNCEMENT! A CLEANSING IS COMING! NO MORE COWARDS! ... NGin for Volley. For the lulz.

— NGin, Campaign Advertisement, 15 November 2025

The campaign, dubbed the "Battle of the Gingers" due to the red hair shared by both candidates, pitted O’Neal against former Governor Madelyn Schroeder ("Cyntaku").21 On 21 November 2025, Cyntaku defeated O’Neal in a landslide, receiving 71 votes (56.8%) to O’Neal’s 46 (36.8%).21 Weeks later, during the regular December 2025 elections, O’Neal paradoxically endorsed Cyntaku against former General Andre Jenkins ("AJ"), declaring: “We don't need a gay nigger boy to lead us.” Cyntaku immediately disavowed the endorsement and muted O’Neal for seven days.22

The Chud War and the Samson Coup (2026)

The transmission turned O’Neal into a community lolcow. Members routinely reacted to HIF communications by spamming the Saturn emoji (🪐) and mocking his lack of real-world employment.23

The Chud War (14 February 2026)

On 14 February 2026, long-simmering tensions escalated into real-world action in Sacramento.8 After discovering that Sasha Gallagher ("Liver")—whom the HIF venerated as their "Loyal Foid" trad-wife ideal—had visited Caleb Jensen’s home to retrieve a laptop charger, O'Neal declared her actions "biological treason."8,24 Armed with crowbars and a wooden baseball bat labeled "MIDWIT CRUSHER" in Sharpie, O’Neal led 14 members of the HIF into a tense standoff against Calebros and Liberators in a Sacramento parking lot.8

The confrontation subsequently moved to the Jensen residence. With Caleb, his bedridden mother, and several associates barricaded inside, O’Neal and the HIF besieged the house.8 At 23:33 PST, O'Neal transmitted his most notorious monologue directly into the negotiation channel:8

YOU SPIRITUALLY EVISCERATED WORM! ... LARPING? YOU CALL THIS LARPING?! LOOK AT THE TIRES ON THE PAVEMENT, CALEB! FEEL THE VIBRATION OF THE... SATURNIAN FREQUENCY IN THE AIR! THIS IS THE ONLY REALITY LEFT! EVERYTHING ELSE IS A ZOG-FUNDED SIMULATION DESIGNED TO KEEP YOUR TESTOSTERONE IN THE GUTTER. YOU ARE A BIOLOGICAL DEAD END, A GENETIC CUL-DE-SAC! AND THAT FOID? SHE WAS DRAWN TO THE ROT! ... COME OUT AND LOOK AT THE MOON, YOU PATHETIC ESTABLISHMENT-HUGGING, SEROTONIN-ADDICTED FUCKING FILTH!! FACE THE NATURAL SELECTION OR DIE IN YOUR COWARDLY CLOSET WITH YOUR DISCORD NOTIFICATIONS TURNED OFF!! HEIL THE ETERNAL NINZARD! HEIL THE CLEANSE!! AND HEIL HITLER!!!

— NGin, during the Siege of Caleb’s Residence, 14 February 2026

The standoff abruptly de-escalated when Sactown Pound veteran Scott Dellinger ("ScottyAte") confronted O'Neal and initiated a handshake.8 Minutes later, at 23:40 PST, Tristen Mercer ("Triste") conducted a drive-by shooting against Caleb’s house from a passing sedan, shattering a window.8,25 O'Neal and his faction fled upon the arrival of the Sacramento Police Department.8

The "Samson Switch" Coup

On the night of 24–25 February 2026, O'Neal attempted a hostile coup of the Original Party hierarchy.9 Aided by Scopian administrator Soda, O'Neal seized administrative permissions in the Orie server and announced the "Samson Switch."9 Using a custom script linked to compromised Discord API webhooks across Main, Parry, and Scope, O'Neal threatened to flood the community’s public channels with gigabytes of dark-web execution gore and extremist manifestos to force Discord Trust & Safety to permanently delete the Ninzard Project unless his faction was granted autonomy and seats in government.9

The coup collapsed when former Adjudicator Raffaele Bellucci executed a global cryptographic token rotation, severing the webhooks and null-routing the payload into an isolated honeypot.9 Remarkably, an internal audit revealed that hours prior to the coup, O'Neal had personally interrogated, berated, and expelled an HIF member who proposed utilizing Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) for the switch, denouncing him as an "Adrenochrome Vampire" and an undercover federal agent.26 In recognition of his refusal to cross this legal threshold, President Apollos granted O'Neal and his associates amnesty from permanent bans, placing them on a mandatory two-week timeout and ordering them to post pictures of themselves outdoors.9,26

The 40-Day Fast and Rant

In March 2026, O’Neal initiated a 40-day Lenten fast of raw kefir and unpasteurized honeycomb.27 The fast was broken on 29 March when former General AJ accused the HIF of being "feds" and covertly staging the rat infestation at Caleb's residence.27 Enraged, O'Neal transmitted a blistering six-minute voice memo that became one of the community's definitive linguistic artifacts:27

I broke a holy 40-day fast of raw kefir and unpasteurized honeycomb for this?! For a LITERAL YAKUBIAN EXPERIMENT to accuse ME of subversion?! ... YOU ARE THE BRONZE AGE COLLAPSE INCARNATE! ... I PRAY TO THE SPIRIT OF YUKIO MISHIMA TO STRIKE YOU DOWN! MAY THE GHOSTS OF TARTARIA RISE FROM THE MUD FLOOD TO SWALLOW YOUR PATHETIC GLOBO HOMO SPLINTER CELL! ... HEIL THE FLANK! HEIL THE AWAKENING!! HEIL, TOTAL NIGGER DEATH!!! AND FFFUCKKK YYYOUUU!!!!

— NGin, Voice Memo to AJ, 29 March 2026

The Holloway Summit and Conversion (May 2026)

On 1 May 2026, Raffaele Bellucci offered O'Neal an exclusive, non-negotiable bounty of $500 USD through the Ninzard Career Initiative simply to write and submit a formatted curriculum vitae.28 O'Neal rejected the offer in an exhausted voice memo, declaring he would not accept "ZOG bucks to work in a retail wagie pod."28

The following morning, 2 May 2026, discovering that Sasha Gallagher had spent the night at Adelynn’s residence in Southern California, O'Neal declared "It's time."29 Accompanied by four HIF members (Athaniel, Highlander, Cedario, and Chud Alessandro), O’Neal drove down Interstate 5 armed with tire irons and the "Midwit Crusher" bat to "rescue" Gallagher from the Holloway residence in Los Angeles.10,29

Upon arrival, the HIF vanguard was disarmed by the domestic normalcy of the scene.10 Met at the door by Mikayla Holloway ("Kay") in sweatpants and socks, the boys were invited inside for smoked brisket.10 Overwhelmed by cognitive dissonance, O’Neal paced the living room screaming racial slurs and leveling his baseball bat at Holloway’s older brother, Malachi.10 Rather than retaliating, Malachi walked forward with open arms and pulled O'Neal into a tight, fatherly embrace, praying aloud for God to heal his wounds.10

The gesture shattered O’Neal’s psychological defenses. Dropping the bat, O’Neal collapsed to the floorboards and wept uncontrollably for over an hour into Malachi's apron.10 Minutes later, Caleb Jensen arrived at the residence, still healing from a fractured rib and head trauma; setting aside years of vitriol, Jensen knelt beside O'Neal and embraced him.10 The event, known as the Holloway Summit, marked the unconditional surrender and dissolution of the Holy Iron Flank.10

Following the summit, O'Neal personally traveled to an apartment complex in Modesto to reconcile with the remaining Volley holdouts, tearfully apologizing for his failures and convincing them to lay down their grievances.11 On 7 May 2026, these former extremists cast the decisive affirmative votes that ratified the Charter of the Ninzard Project with an 82% supermajority.11

In the aftermath, O'Neal renounced white nationalism, abandoned his internet personas, and entered the catechumenate of the Eastern Orthodox Church in the Sacramento suburbs alongside Athaniel.12 In July 2026, O'Neal was targeted by Torin Graves's violent splinter cell, the Black Sun Fraternity, who abducted him and tore off his fingernails in retribution for his defection.30 O'Neal survived the attack and publicly stated through intermediaries that he held no hatred for his assailants, exemplifying his commitment to Christian reconciliation.30
