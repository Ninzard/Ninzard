Mei Sasaki (born 21 December 2001), known as Meikaqu, is an American software developer, technical analyst, and Ninzard politician who has served as Governor of the Archives since 4 July 2026.1,2

A member of the Class of 2012 and the author of the 2013 "Chaos Theory," Sasaki was historically associated with the community's early development and the Golden Era, serving as the 3rd District General of Hybriden (2015), 2nd Governor of Ninzardia (2017), and 4th District General of BobaBase (2019–2020).3,4,5,6 In 2022, Sasaki joined the inner circle of President Catx3, serving as the 3rd District General of Catsland and subsequently as the 9th Vice President of the Ninzard Project from 2023 to 2025.7,8

During her vice presidency, Sasaki served as the primary technical architect of Avalon's illicit financial network, coding ticket-scalping botnets and engineering the "Poisoned Ledger" dusting attack against Nightwork's relief fund.9 Subjected to escalating domestic abuse, poisoning, and physical violence by Avalon, Sasaki survived a suicide attempt off the Dumbarton Bridge and a near-fatal stabbing in August 2025 before cooperating with federal prosecutors and counter-intelligence investigators.10,11,12 Following a federal plea agreement, she was restored to administrative office in July 2026 under President Poka.2,12

Early life and foundational era (2012–2016)

Sasaki was born on 21 December 2001. A competitive swimmer in her youth, she lived in the same townhouse complex in British Columbia as Christian Akita and Stephen Baek, observing Akita's early network scripting and multi-client gaming architectures.3,13 She joined the Hybriden server in late 2012 as an early adolescent participant.14

Chaos Theory

In mid-2013, Sasaki formulated the Ninzard Theory of Chaos (commonly called "Chaos Theory"), measuring the correlation between membership expansion and message frequency. She demonstrated that unrestricted growth diluted individual participation rates, concluding that a membership ceiling of 65 users per server maintained the optimal balance of activity and cohesion. The paper established an intellectual connection between Sasaki and Solorado administrator Spinzle.4

General of Hybriden

Sasaki was elected 3rd District General of Hybriden under Akita's Home Party on 31 December 2014, taking office on 1 January 2015.15 In April 2015, she broke with the Capital establishment, defecting to the newly established Tomato Party alongside Apollos, Kain, and Danbi.16 In the June 2015 election, she was narrowly defeated for re-election by an independent account named Orion1 (an alias operated by Akita).16

Golden Era and Ninzardia governorship (2016–2018)

In December 2016, Sasaki ran for Deputy Governor of Ninzardia alongside Danbi on the Original Party ticket, narrowly losing to Gabriel Locklear and Faber.17 Following Locklear's resignation, Sasaki ran for Governor in March 2017 with Snowby (Mary Abernethy) as her running mate; dubbed the "genki girl duo," they defeated Faber and Maria Lui.5

Taking office on 1 April 2017, Sasaki maintained a laissez-faire approach toward district governance.18 In mid-2017, after discovering that Vice President Hokan and Capital Adjudicator MMC were ridiculing Apollos in administrative calls, Sasaki recorded the conversation and delivered the audio to Apollos, causing an irreparable executive rift.19 Sasaki declined to seek re-election in September 2017 due to personal exhaustion and the dissolution of a real-world relationship.18

Underground Era and BobaBase (2018–2022)

Following her governorship, Sasaki experienced personal isolation and substance abuse, finishing high school during the 2020 lockdowns.7 On 22 September 2019, she was elected 4th District General of BobaBase (District 3), defeating GuamPlayz.6 She served until 1 March 2020, when she was defeated by Power of Greed (PoG).20 She subsequently worked as a freelance programmer while remaining an active social commentator during the late Apollos I administration.7,21

The Catx3 regime and federal conviction (2022–2025)

Catsland and the Vice Presidency

In mid-2022, Sasaki aligned with incoming President Catherine Avalon (Catx3), bonding over shared fluency in Japanese.7 In December 2022, Sasaki switched from the Disco Party to the Preservation Party, defeating incumbent General Burnette in January 2023 to become the 3rd District General of Catsland.7,22

On 4 July 2023, following snap elections, Avalon appointed Sasaki as the 9th Vice President of the Ninzard Project, succeeding Mary Carmichael.8 Sasaki served as the primary technical operator for the administration's financial schemes, writing automated ticket-scalping scripts and managing digital ledgers.9

The Poisoned Ledger

In September 2023, after discovering that counter-intelligence investigator Mikayla Holloway was monitoring Avalon's primary cryptocurrency wallet (0x7a...), Sasaki engineered the Poisoned Ledger countermeasure.9

Sasaki orchestrated the public exposure of "Giveawaygate," leaking evidence of a rigged community lottery run by Alaric Adler. When Nightwork Governor Adelynn Leclaire established a public multi-signature wallet to reimburse victims, Sasaki executed a dusting attack, transmitting trace amounts of cryptocurrency from Avalon's laundering wallet into Adelynn's relief fund and the personal wallets of the victims. This commingling ensured that any federal indictment of Avalon would simultaneously implicate Nightwork's administrative leadership and victims in felony money laundering, successfully freezing the investigation for over a year.9

Abuse, suicide attempt, and stabbing

Despite her administrative rank, Sasaki was subjected to severe domestic abuse and exploitation by Avalon. In May 2024, Sasaki was sexually assaulted by Alaric Adler while Avalon observed.23 Days later, Sasaki was hospitalized in critical condition following an intentional chemical poisoning orchestrated by Avalon.24 In late 2024, Avalon subjected Sasaki to further physical abuse and forced ingestion of bodily fluids during hospital confinement.25

On the night of 19 August 2025, overwhelmed by guilt over her complicity in the extortion of Mary and Pierre Carmichael, Sasaki jumped from an 85-foot height off the Dumbarton Bridge in San Francisco Bay. Surviving the impact due to instinctive competitive swimming reflexes, she swam twenty minutes through marshland to shore, resolving to sever her subservience to Avalon.10

On 20 August 2025, Avalon attacked Sasaki at her residence, dousing her in cooking oil and stabbing her nine times with a kitchen knife.11 After Avalon fled, Sasaki barricaded herself in a bathroom and contacted Holloway, who dispatched emergency services; Sasaki survived following emergency trauma surgery.11

Conviction and cooperation

Following Avalon's arrest in Leeds on 22 August 2025, Sasaki was indicted in the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of California for conspiracy to commit wire fraud and access device fraud.12 She initially withheld testimony to shield the Carmichael family from prosecution.26

On 1 September 2025, Ninzard founder Elizabeth Avalon visited Sasaki in the hospital, apologizing on behalf of the family and pledging private restitution to the scam victims, eliminating the restitution damages from Sasaki's legal liability.26 Sasaki subsequently provided federal prosecutors and Holloway with full access to her ledgers, botnet repositories, and decryption keys.26 Under a plea agreement, she was sentenced to three years of supervised federal probation, mandatory psychiatric care, and a permanent lifetime ban from managing financial securities or automated scraping software.12

Restoration era (2025–present)

On 2 May 2026, Sasaki attended the Holloway Summit in Los Angeles, formally reconciling with long-term political adversaries, including Sasha Gallagher.27,28 On 28 June 2026, outgoing President Apollos nominated Sasaki as Governor of the Archives (GoA); she was confirmed by Legislature XII on 4 July 2026 under President Poka.2

On 20 August 2026—the anniversary of her stabbing—Sasaki returned voluntarily to the Dumbarton Bridge, diving from the span in broad daylight as an act of psychological closure.29
