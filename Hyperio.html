Victor Torres (born 8 August 1998), known as Hyperio, is a Ninzard from Sacramento who has been District General of Scope for the Preservation Party since January 2026, a district he first led as its founding general in 2015. The first General of Sacranight, “the Jungle,” he left the Ninzard Project in 2014 to found his own community, Hyperion, and led its band of “Hyperionites” through the Nightian War and the Ninzard Civil War of 2015–2016, a campaign of raids and street fights in Sacramento that ended with his arrest in March 2016.1,2,3,4,5,6

After close to a decade away from the community, in which he became a mechanic and, in April 2024, drove President Cat out of his auto shop with a road flare, he returned in December 2025 at the request of Apollos to stop Aceiron's comeback in Scope, a district that still called itself “Old Hyperion,” and beat him with 79.1% of the vote. He was re-elected against Liver in June 2026. That year he joined a Presbyterian church in Sacramento, where he married on 10 October; the sight of him at the altar moved several of his old companions toward faith.7,8,9,10,11

Early life

Torres was born on 15 August 1999 and grew up in Sacramento, in the same neighborhood as Bray, the Sacraday leader he would spend years fighting, and AJ, whom the neighborhood wars later drew into Sacranight; Cyntaku lived a few houses away. He trained in martial arts and played contact sports from childhood, and although he stood only about five feet five, he had a reputation for great physical strength and commanded respect.3,12,13 Club Korea, Kain's circle in Los Angeles, nicknamed him 미친 (michin, “crazy”) because he was loud; he was later known as “the Demented One.”3,14

Sacranight (2012–2014)

Jungle of Ninzard

Sacranight, one of the five legions that founded the Ninzard Project in December 2012, gathered the outcasts and brawlers of Sacramento who hated Bray. Hyperio was among the older teenagers there who hazed newcomers. When the nine-year-old Aceiron answered their screaming by screaming back for hours, rejoining every time he was kicked and threatening to “rain hellfire” on high schoolers, Hyperio burst out laughing, and instead of banning the boy, he and UNO adopted him as the unhinged little brother of the Jungle.15,16

In the community's first elections, held from 29 to 31 December 2012, Hyperio was elected General of Sacranight in place of UNO, its de facto leader, and he took office on 1 January 2013.1,17 He took little part in the new Legislature, which he thought too political, and Sacranight was one of the legions most detached from the wider community.18

King of the Jungle

Sacranight was hard to hold together. UNO, Hyperio's friend and a physically violent bully, tried to rule the legion through him, and the two fought several times in real life as Hyperio asserted his authority; yet he refused calls to expel UNO and still granted some of his wishes. Elusion, who led the legion's more intellectual members, accused him of being UNO's slave. Hyperio defended UNO as a protector against the school bullies who tormented the nerds and held that violence was a fair answer in self-defense; Elusion held that it never was. By the end of 2013 UNO had lost interest and Hyperio held full control, but he had lost much of his support, and Sacranight had become known as the “Jungle of Ninzard.”19

When Bray's circle began harassing and beating Sacranians after school, Hyperio wanted to answer with violence. Elusion proposed instead to record Bray's behavior, expose him and report him to schools or the police, and Hyperio at first went along with the plan.20

Tie with Elusion

In the elections of December 2013, Hyperio and Elusion each received 32 of the 64 votes cast for General of Sacranight. A second vote in January ended in another tie, 30 to 30. Unwilling to choose between them, President Antareze took over the legion's server himself, though a separate vote kept Hyperio's three representatives in the Legislature.2 On 25 January 2014 Hyperio and Elusion both backed Vice President Kain's call for a sixth legion among the Sacra-legions.21

Secession

Around midnight on 20 April 2014, while Hyperio was asleep, a user calling himself “AnarchoArchitect” warned Sacranight that the Capital was plotting to purge it, and posted the real names and addresses of all its members as proof. Antareze's silence convinced Hyperio and Elusion that the plot was real, and with Bray's new Breakout Party openly hostile to Sacranight, both resolved to leave.22 On 25 April Elusion founded Nightwork and Hyperio founded Hyperion, and Sacranight was disbanded the next day.2,3

Hyperion (2014–2015)

Nightian War

Hyperio set out to start a war between Nightwork and the Ninzard Project. Posing as Ninzards for the fun of it, he threatened Nightwork's members until Nightwork turned on Ninzard, to the confusion of the Capital, which had made no threats.3 Around him gathered his “soldiers,” the Hyperionites. Among them was the ten-year-old Cyntaku, whom he pulled into his channels and handed lines to read, because other children trusted a young girl's voice. She later wrote that she had felt included rather than used, and that he “was experimenting with being important.”3,13

He branded the Nightwork members of southern California cowards and planned a trip to Los Angeles to frighten them, but never went.23 Club Korea, neutral in the war, saw him less as a villain than as a reaction against the corruption of Ninzard under Antareze and Bray.24

Awakening

Hyperio despised the Legislature, its debates and the “Capital Laws” as cowardice: a system built by weak men such as Antareze to protect manipulative ones such as Bray. When he learned that Bray's enforcers were hunting Trixy, the Sacraday member who had tried to report the abuse of girls at Bray's parties, he reported it to the school; when the school did nothing, he went offline and made plans.25,26

In early 2015, as three of Bray's athletes stepped out of a car to corner Trixy on her walk home, Hyperio came out of an alley dressed all in black. An argument followed, and he seized one of them by his letterman jacket and threw him with a judo sweep that broke his collarbone on the concrete. Standing over the boy, he told them that the Jungle was open. Many condemned him for striking first, and Bray's circle mocked the line as corny, but Bray's enemies cheered, and Hyperio became a local force of nature who threatened Bray's standing.26

Joining Nightwork

With Bray's backing, Hyperio and Antareze, who was then secretly operating as an anarchist hacker in Hybriden, plotted the destruction of Nightwork through hacking and real-life harassment, until Elusion's surrender to the Project in 2015 ended the plan.27 Antareze invited Hyperio to bring his community into Ninzard and command it as a legion; instead Hyperio dismantled Hyperion and merged it into Nightwork, hoping to take control from within.28 In June 2015 he told his followers that operations were pausing and that they were becoming Ninzards.13 On 30 June, with Bray's support, he lost the election for General of Nightwork to Elusion, the candidate closer to the Capital, by a single vote.4

General of Scope (2015)

When Nightwork joined the Ninzard Project on 11 July 2015 and was divided into three legions, Hyperio was elected the first general of Scope, a name chosen to suggest spyware, for the Unighted Party. DegreeZ was elected in the new legion of Parry, and Elusion became Nightwork's de facto governor.4

Ninzard Civil War (2015–2016)

Secession of Scope

On the evening of Tuesday 6 October 2015, Hyperio hacked into Elusion's accounts, wrecked the other two Nightwork legions and declared his own no longer part of the Ninzard Project. Accusing the Project of bias against Nightwork, he demanded that Antareze resign and call a new election, and began moving his followers from TeamSpeak to Discord, among the community's first steps onto the platform. His army came largely from the old Sacranight, and Apollos joined his raids on Sacraday's servers under the alias “chonax.” It was the start of the Ninzard Civil War. Few in the Project cared to fight him, though Syn, who hated him more than anyone, learned to use remote-access trojans against his side.5,29

Hyperionites

That winter Hyperio led two to three dozen boys on unplanned “pillages” to ensure there would be “no peace on the streets of Sacramento,” beating Bray's supporters and anyone aligned with Elusion. His most loyal lieutenants were two tall, powerful boys, Deebz and Lincohn. Before each raid the Hyperionites chanted to “Hooked on a Feeling,” and Hyperio, who had quickly earned his driver's permit, drove his soldiers around and taught boys without permits to drive. For many of them the raids were the most exciting moments of their lives.30,31

Christmas party attack

「SALUTATIONS MOTHERFUCKERS! SCORCHED EARTH BITCHES—NOW DIE! HAHAHAHAHAHA! Y'ALL CAN'T FUCK WITH ME! I AM FUCKING CRAZY!」

— Hyperio, 25 December 2015

On 25 December 2015 the Hyperionites picked up boys they took for Nightians off the streets and beat them, several of them with no connection to Ninzard at all, then forced their way into Bray's Christmas party, about three dozen strong against a crowd twice their size. Fighting broke out amid the panic, and there were injuries and reports of sexual assault. When a new recruit cornered a young woman and threatened her, Hyperio broke off his own fight, crossed the room and knocked the recruit out with a single punch, then ordered his men to “fight soldiers only.” Several of Bray's associates used the chaos to assault guests, and Hyperio made a point of remembering who they were.32

The raid brought a wave of new recruits. By then Hyperio was drinking heavily and smoking marijuana, and encouraged his followers to do the same; under their influence he was said to become delusional and violent.32

Mitchell

In an evening brawl in a park, Hyperio's side, Aceiron and ScottyAte among them, beat several of Elusion's supporters. In revenge an older Elusion supporter, Mitchell, and his friends caught ScottyAte walking home from school, and when Aceiron ran to defend him, they broke Aceiron's left arm while making ScottyAte watch. The attack shook the Hyperionites, who had begun to fear their increasingly erratic leader, and their morale fell.33

Early in 2016 Hyperio drove to Los Angeles at Club Korea's invitation and spent time with Kain and his friends, who told him they did not support the Capital.14 In February he challenged Mitchell to a fight to avenge Aceiron, ScottyAte and the others. Both came out bloodied, but Hyperio choked Mitchell until his friends rushed in, and won. The next day, while he was alone and still recovering, Toby, a diehard Nightwork loyalist, walked into his house with a much younger boy; Toby smashed his devices and taunted him, while the younger boy brought him water and told him firmly not to take on more than he could handle. Hyperio watched and chuckled.34 He refused to go to the hospital and had his wounds dressed by the group's medic.35 Meanwhile Antareze hacked the devices of Deebz and Lincohn and set the three against one another, until they met in person, found that all of them had been compromised, and from then on spoke only face to face.34

Final battle

Healed by March, Hyperio gathered his soldiers in person to hunt down the hacker who had turned them against each other. Only about a dozen came, many having lost faith in him; he called those who stood with him the “real Gs.” They went first to Bray's house and waited, but Bray saw them, slipped away to a friend's house and raised an army of his own. That evening Bray's boys ambushed the Hyperionites with pepper spray, dousing one another as well. Blinded but full of adrenaline, Hyperio caught two of Bray's friends, recognizing them by their shrieks, and beat them, then chased down a third boy outside his own home. When the boy's father swung a baseball bat and struck him on the body, Hyperio knocked the man down, took the bat and taunted the son for having a weak father. Police arrived within a minute. Hyperio and several others on both sides went to juvenile detention, and most were charged and put on probation; Hyperio thought it all amusing and boasted of knocking down the father.6

By mid-March 2016 the war was over. Hyperio's side had lost heart, his communications were in ruins, and he was too badly hurt, and too restricted by the law, to go on. On 19 March a new legion, Volley, was created for his former followers, and most of his boys moved there.6,36,37

「Hyperio deteriorated fast. At twelve I thought he was being dramatic. At twenty-one I know he was unraveling.」

— Cyntaku, recalling the civil war29

2016 presidential election

In the presidential election of June 2016, which followed Antareze's decision to step down, the Unighted Party, which held to UNO's “freestyle values” and wanted Nightwork to leave the Project, nominated Hyperio, who was then serving a month's ban for his part in the civil war. He campaigned for “Nexit,” Nightwork's exit from Ninzard. Apollos won in an upset, carried in large part by Nightwork's disaffected former supporters of Hyperio.38,39 Scope, defunct for nearly a year because of Hyperio's war, was rebuilt under Trixy, who was elected its general that summer with the backing of the Resistance Party.40

Years away (2016–2025)

Hyperio then drifted away from the community for close to a decade.8 He became a mechanic, working fifty hours a week at an auto repair shop in the Sacramento area, and in the fall of 2023 he met the woman he would marry when he looked at her car and declined to charge her for the diagnosis.11,7

Auto shop incident

「You push this any further, we all die in here. That's it.」

— Hyperio, to Cat and her enforcers, 13 April 2024

On the evening of Saturday 13 April 2024, President Cat came to the shop after hours with two enforcers: Toby, and a masked man with a handgun tucked visibly into his waistband. She wanted to see whether she could overpower the man Ninzard considered its strongest, and to make him run for governor that December as a spoiler against Aceiron, whose growing hold on the Original Party threatened her. She offered him $20,000 in cash and the deed to the shop, which she claimed to have acquired; Hyperio knew the claim was nonsense. If he refused, she said, Toby would break his hands, and she hinted that she would burn the place down.7

Hyperio recognized Toby as the boy who had broken into his house eight years before, judged him a hanger-on with more to lose than to gain, and took the displayed gun as a sign that his visitors were amateurs. While Cat talked, he raised the car on his lift and cracked the dump valve: the lift dropped several inches before its pawls caught with a crack that, to anyone unfamiliar with lifts, sounded like collapse. He lit a road flare, tipped over a pan of solvent and fuel, held the flare low over the floor and stepped back toward the lift. Toby froze, and Cat turned and fled.7

He did not call the police, which he thought unnecessary: it would have meant weeks of scrutiny and trouble for the business over a woman whose power he regarded as play-acting. He later gave his account to Kay, and recalled with amusement the contrast between Cat's immaculate suit and light-up shoes and the grime of the shop. It was the last recorded attempt at a transaction in Cat's ledger.7

Sactown Pound

On 25 July 2025 the Sactown Pound, a self-styled hit squad of thirteen young men led by ScottyAte, drove in convoy to the shop to make Hyperio their commander. ScottyAte walked onto the floor holding a kitten, while the rest crowded the entrance in costumes, carrying crowbars. Hyperio finished turning a bolt, looked them over and very nearly laughed. He heard out ScottyAte's offer of command and the chance to “finish what he started,” told them quietly that they were in the wrong place, and took a call from his girlfriend. Then, with a hand on the shoulder of Rudolf, who insisted that he owed it to the community, he walked all thirteen of them out of the building.41

「Listen, I think you boys are in the wrong place.」

「Go home. … And… get some bitches.」

— Hyperio, to the Sactown Pound, 25 July 2025

The parting words, aimed above all at Rudolf, spread through Parry and then the whole community, shortened to “GSB,” a stock retort to any posturing. The Pound laughed on the way out, but the rejection by the Legend of Scope stung.41

Return to Scope (2025–present)

Return of the Legend

In December 2025 Aceiron, defeated for the presidency in September, was running for General of Scope, his home district, and his Scopian supporters were rallying behind a comeback that would have handed President Apollos an institutional rival; the incumbent, Ixan, was expected to lose. Apollos spoke to Ixan and to Hyperio, still a celebrity legend in Scope. Aceiron saw it coming.42,43,8

「Go ahead. Do it. Make the announcement. Swap your prey. Drag Hyperio out of his grave if you have to. Doesn't change a damn thing. … Bring him out. I will retire him again.」

— Aceiron, 13 December 2025

Shortly after noon that day Ixan withdrew and endorsed Hyperio, running for the Preservation Party, as the man who had laid the district's foundations: “the first and forthcoming District General of Scope,” the one best fit to defend “the District of Old Hyperion.” Aceiron's support collapsed at once, even though Hyperio said openly that he would “do little to nothing” as general, which Scopians found hilarious.8 The same day Hyperio joined the Preservate Apparatus with a portrait of Charlie Kirk, Aceiron's political hero, as his icon, remarking, “Heard caleb likes this guy.” Cyntaku, who had once read his lines, greeted him at once.8,44

Cyntaku: It's been years. Wow.

Hyperio: Been a decade you held up good kid.

Cyntaku: That landed harder than I was prepared for. 😭

Apollos: he's been in a long-tern relationship, 2+ years now they plan to get married soon

Cyntaku: Wait getting married?? 🥹

Hyperio: Its all love

Cyntaku: Okay stop I'm actually tearing up. I'm so happy for you.

Hyperio: Appreciate it kid time flies

— Preservate Apparatus, 13 December 2025

Inside the party, deploying a celebrity nine days before the election troubled some. Grace warned that elections were becoming a contest of “celebrity deployment”; Woofer answered that Hyperio gave Scope's voters a way to tell quality from noise, and Grace conceded that if this was survival mode, they should survive.45

Voting began on 22 December 2025, and Hyperio's victory was clear within the first day. He won 102 of Scope's 129 votes (79.1%) to Aceiron's 27. Scope's silent majority was loyal to Apollos, Spinzle and Hyperio, and beside him Aceiron looked like a cheap imitation; a decade earlier, after bullies had broken Aceiron's arm, it was Hyperio who had avenged him.9

General of Scope

As general Hyperio was rarely on Discord. After the Chud War of 14 February 2026, in which a shot was fired through a window of Aceiron's family home, his entire public statement was a single emoji: 💀.46

He spoke up for his district when the former General Soda died in a Scope voice channel on 18 May 2026. As Scope's Representative, Lless, mocked the grief of the members who had witnessed it, Hyperio rebuked him in the community-wide NPC! server and moved to recall him.47

「@Lless You don't speak for Scope. You don't speak for anyone but your own miserable ego. Keep Lucca's name out of your fucking mouth.」

「Under Section 13, I motion to recall Julian Lancaster as the Representative of Scope. We will strip him of his title. Fuck out of our district.」

— Hyperio, May 2026

Lless called him “General Dimwit,” and Hyperio answered with a raised middle finger. On 24 May 102 of 120 Scopians voted to remove Lless, and the next day Sable was elected to serve out his term.47,48

After Soda's death, with the Capital's approval, Hyperio raised Scope's member cap by five, making it the first and only district to expand. Many of Scope's moderates took the small increase as a precedent for a more open community and voted for the Preservate Poka over ScottyAte in the presidential election of June 2026; Poka carried Scope with 80.7%. Hyperio himself endorsed neither candidate, although he and ScottyAte were friends in real life.49

Until the last minute it was unclear whether he would stand again. Then he sent a single message, “K i run,” which his supporters took up as their slogan: “Kirun!” Scope was by then a safe Preservate district, and in the Summer Cycle elections of June 2026 Hyperio was re-elected with 94 of 135 votes (69.6%) against Liver, who did better than expected as many Scopians voted for her out of respect. His supporters urged voters not to waste their ballots, and his expansion of the district won him some Orie votes.10 His new term began on 2 July 2026.

Rivalry with Elusion

Hyperio's longest rivalry was with Elusion, the older “Old Wizard” of the Sacramento boys, and at heart it was a quarrel over force. Hyperio lived by a code of physical respect: he defended UNO as a protector of the bullied, held that violence was a fair way to defend oneself, and wanted to meet Bray's cruelty with his fists. Elusion held that violence was never the answer, that UNO only perpetuated the bullying they both opposed, and that Bray should be beaten with evidence, exposure and reports to schools and the police.19,20 Hyperio despised the Legislature and the Capital Laws as cowardice designed by weak men; Elusion, the intellectual who could end a street fight just by showing up and speaking calmly, commanded a grudging reverence even from Hyperio.26,50 The same divide ran through their politics. Elusion surrendered Nightwork to the Project, stood with the Capital and joined Antareze's Home Party, while Hyperio, backed by Bray, rejected the Capital's authority and in 2016 campaigned to take Nightwork back out of Ninzard.27,4,38

The quarrel split Sacranight evenly, in two tied elections, and then split it apart: when Elusion founded Nightwork, he walked into the local parks and told the quieter children to stop spending time with Hyperio and join his server instead.2,50 Elusion beat him for the generalship of Nightwork by a single vote in 2015, and Hyperio's revolt that October, which began with the hacking of Elusion's accounts, rested on his view that Elusion held too much authority over Nightwork; it ended in March 2016 with Elusion's victory.4,5,6 A decade later Elusion sat among the Sacramento Ninzards at Hyperio's wedding, and seeing his old rival at the altar, he stopped treating theology as an abstract puzzle.11

Personal life

His name alone, the archive observes, had become a mythos: the violent fifteen-year-old in a black hoodie throwing athletes onto concrete, the warlord laughing in voice channels, the mechanic standing over a puddle of solvent with a lit flare. The community struggled to fit that legend to the ordinary truth that he had grown up and built a life.11

Faith and marriage

In late 2025 Apollos mentioned that Hyperio had been in a committed relationship for two years, and most of the community assumed he was exaggerating. His fiancée, who had grown up a Christian, had no Discord account and had never heard of the Jungle, Sacranight or the Ninzard Project; she knew his “old neighborhood friends from the internet” only as a group of damaged local boys he had watched over in a rough adolescence and sometimes felt responsible for keeping out of jail, and he kept the two worlds firmly apart. When his temper flared, she asked him to sit on the porch and drink water, and she held him to ordinary standards of kindness and patience.11

Her presence drew him toward Christianity. In the year before the wedding he sat beside her in the pews of a Presbyterian church in Sacramento, spoke with the pastor and attended weekly evening classes, and about five months before the wedding he joined the church by public profession of faith.11

They were married there on Saturday 10 October 2026, before God and seventy witnesses, in an ordinary sanctuary of white oak pews and plain glass windows; the pastor read from the letter to the Colossians. Hyperio wore a rented black tuxedo, his knuckles scarred by years of hand tools. The local Ninzards who had asked for invitations received a single order from him, and obeyed it.11

「No recording. Wear ties. Act like you were raised by humans. Or I throw you out myself.」

— Hyperio, to the Ninzards invited to his wedding, October 2026

They sat together toward the back: Aceiron on the aisle beside ScottyAte and AJ, Cyntaku and OffBeat in the row in front of them, Elusion near the center, and NGin in the last row with his eyes on the wooden cross above the communion table. No one spoke of Discord. At the reception in the church hall Hyperio spent the evening greeting grandmothers and carrying folding chairs. At sunset he found his old friends by the fence of the gravel parking lot, embraced ScottyAte, asked after Aceiron's mother and told him to look after her and stay out of trouble, and told NGin to leave his online anger behind and learn an honest trade.11

Seeing him at the altar changed them. Elusion visited Holy Trinity Cathedral in Sacramento and entered the catechumenate of the Orthodox Church, and NGin abandoned his pagan manifestos, having seen that true strength lay in quiet duty.11
