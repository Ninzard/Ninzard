Adelynn Leclaire (born 12 March 2003), known as Adelynn, is a Ninzard politician who served as the 8th Governor of Nightwork from 3 January 2023 to 31 December 2024. A member of the Preservation Party, she led it as Boss from 2023 until March 2026, and was previously Deputy Governor of Nightwork under Cyntaku in 2022.1,2,3

A moderate who had been an administrator in Main, she unseated Spinzle by three votes in January 2023 and governed Nightwork through the height of the Catx3 Regime. Her administration introduced the equalization policy and the Sponsorship Standard, and she was re-elected twice: in July 2023, with 75.0% of the vote, the largest gubernatorial landslide in the Project's history, and in December 2023. After surviving a plebiscite on her leadership in June 2024, she lost the December 2024 election to Aceiron with 45.6% of the vote.4,5,6,7

Out of office she backed Apollos's return in 2025, remained Boss of the party through his second presidency and helped secure agreement on Part I of the Charter of the Ninzard Project. She stepped away from the community after being seriously injured in Los Angeles on 13 March 2026, and was succeeded as Boss by Woofer.8,9,3,10

Early life

Leclaire was born on 12 March 2003 to French immigrants in the United States, and speaks French. A Catholic, she lives in the Los Angeles area, near her close friends Grace and Woofer.11,12 Friends describe her as easygoing, talkative and at times absent-minded, with a well-known sweet tooth for brownies; her years as governor are credited with maturing her into a more grounded leader.12

Early career (2021–2022)

Running mate

Adelynn was an administrator in Nightwork-Main when Cyntaku chose her as running mate for the January 2022 gubernatorial election. Many had expected a better-known figure such as Grace or Seandry, and the choice was considered “uncontroversial but unexciting,” but those who knew her regarded her as wise beyond her years. Cyntaku won with 51% of the vote, and Adelynn became Deputy Governor.11

Deputy Governor

During the January 2022 attacks, Adelynn condemned the attacks outright but opposed a permanent ban on Helen as disproportionate to what she considered an impeachable offence.13 When the Nightian-Ninzardian Assembly later voted to keep Helen banned, Cyntaku declared it “contaminated” and had Adelynn dissolve it.14

Adelynn grew uneasy with actions of Cyntaku's that she privately called “dishonest” and “unprincipled,” and considered withdrawing from the re-election ticket, but stayed on out of personal loyalty despite judging its prospects poor.15 Her July 2022 debate with the Original Party's deputy nominee, Atria, went largely unnoticed, though close observers rated her a far more proficient debater than either Atria or Cyntaku. The ticket lost to Spinzle and Atria with 33.1% of the vote.16

2023 gubernatorial election

After Cyntaku's defeat, many Preservates doubted Adelynn's commitment to the party because of her moderation and rigid adherence to her values, and the party appointed Grace as its Boss as a compromise. Grace's loss of a snap election in Main in November 2022 left Adelynn as the party's choice for governor. She ran with Grace as her running mate at the head of a more moderate wing, the Logos Flank, promising an equalization policy to control Nightwork's rapid growth and a cleanup of “corruption, authoritarianism, and polarization.”17,1

At their debate on 30 December 2022 she accused Spinzle of passivity in the face of unchecked expansion, warning that the growth of Parry and Main could soon let a candidate win all of Nightwork by carrying a single district.18 Apollos endorsed her, and Cat privately favoured her as well, expecting a “naive” governor she could use.4,1 Adelynn won by three votes, 279 (50.3%) to 276 (49.7%).4

Governor of Nightwork (2023–2024)

First term

The Adelynn Administration began on 3 January 2023, and on taking office she also became Boss of the Preservate Apparatus.1 Days into her term she declared that Xorbit's extortion of members he had invited to Parry had crossed a hard red line, and he was banned on 6 January.19 Spinzle had advised her to clean up Nightwork through gradual attrition. Adelynn instead began removing members deemed “toxic” and “dangerous” while allowing districts to grow within the rules, and set out a plan to equalize the membership of every district by June 2023.20

When Apollos revealed on 1 April 2023 that he had been running Waffle as “General Assailant,” Adelynn banned him for impersonation.21 Two days later she announced the Sponsorship Standard. New members needed an established member to vouch for them before gaining voting rights, sponsors shared responsibility for any alternate account they admitted, and candidates and staff had to pass voice verifications with their district general and with Adelynn or Grace.22

We do not seek to punish the innocent. This system is to make the risks of deception too high to sustain. … We seek to build a chain of trust. If the link breaks, we'll know where.

— Governor Adelynn, announcing the Sponsorship Standard, 3 April 2023

Many Nightians found the standard amusing, and Cat's effusive public endorsement of it was widely read as trolling. Within its first week two dozen alternate accounts were uncovered, and it swept away radical accounts on both the far right and the far left.22 Cat's strategist Pieban turned it to her advantage, however: by having her sleeper agents “catch” decoy accounts, he made them trusted sponsors, verified by the governor herself.23 On the advice of Raffaele and General Kay, Adelynn and Grace removed Meikaqu from the Preservate Apparatus as a “high risk.”24

In July 2023 she defeated the Orie General of Waffle, Kendall, who had turned down every debate request and accused her of a “saviour complex,” by 480 votes to 160 (75.0%). She carried every district, and the result remains the largest gubernatorial landslide in the Project's history.5

Second term

During her second term Vice President Meikaqu, working for Cat, contaminated Nightwork's public archive of moderation logs, kept under the Administrative Transparency Act, with concealed extremist text, then reported it to Discord's Trust & Safety team. Discord restricted the archive, and Adelynn suspended the act rather than alter its records, while the Ories accused her of a cover-up.25

The Original Party's ViaTea made the archive's collapse the centre of her campaign for governor in December 2023. The race was unusually cordial: Adelynn praised ViaTea's ambitions, and ViaTea called Adelynn “a gentle and enlightened spirit” but “naive.”6

We face malicious actors who weaponize our own rules against us. Accordingly, prudent moderation remains the final safeguard keeping Nightwork alive. … Trust is indispensable to a healthy community, but it cannot be extended without discernment when there are those determined to exploit it.

— Governor Adelynn, debating General ViaTea, 28 December 2023

She won with 351 of 651 votes (53.9%) on record turnout. In the same election Nightians chose to hold gubernatorial elections once a year, so her third term was to run for a full year.6

Third term

When Cat abolished elections in Ninzardia on 1 January 2024, Adelynn replied the next day that Nightwork would keep voting.26

To be clear, elections do not inherently foster cruel behavior; rather, they expose those who are prepared to act cruelly and provide the rest of us with an alternative that does not rely on the kindness of an individual.

— Governor Adelynn, 2 January 2024

Early in 2024, after General Rudolf had readmitted the former Scope general Lyman, Adelynn banned at least a dozen members of Parry, Lyman among them, for extreme and discriminatory rhetoric. The Ories compared her to a dictator and Parry's members threatened to leave Nightwork; she gave some of those banned a second chance while making clear that such rhetoric would not be allowed.27

The year brought the Catx3 Regime's violence close to home. After a member was kidnapped in April 2024, an incident in which Adelynn went in person to help, Cat threatened her and Grace directly, and Adelynn was doxxed. She came to regard the governorship as a moral duty to protect members from real-world harm.28,29

Because she was not up for re-election that summer, Adelynn followed a suggestion of Spinzle's and put her leadership to a plebiscite alongside the June 2024 district general elections, promising a snap election if fewer than half the voters backed her. Her party objected. Woofer argued that the December 2023 result was already her mandate, and General Matcha called her “emotionless” for risking the community to protect her integrity, while Deputy Governor Grace supported the vote. On 30 June, 325 of 580 voters (56.0%) rejected a snap election, though the Ories still accused her of dictatorship.30 Privately, she was overwhelmed with anxiety and guilt at having gambled the community's safety on her wish for “democratic legitimacy.”28

2024 election and defeat

Adelynn hesitated to seek re-election, but the Preservate Apparatus, whose other leaders feared becoming targets of Cat and of the Ories, persuaded her to stand again. Her opponent, Aceiron, promised Nexit, a complete break with Cat's Ninzardia. Adelynn called the plan “high risk, high reward” and warned that Cat's capacity for harm made the risk unacceptable. She defended a community of principles over one of mere activity and condemned the “Caleb Treatment” as a source of toxicity.31

She led the polls through the autumn. By the end of November, after weeks of round-the-clock vigilance, Grace was pleading with her to step back, and on 1 December 2024 her Discord account was banned. Adelynn said that a message she had quoted had been mistaken for her own, but a family emergency then took her away from the campaign.29,32 On 19 December a whistleblower revealed that Aceiron had his allies mass-report her messages out of context, and she returned on 21 December, the community's twelfth anniversary.32 In the final days both Spinzle and Apollos endorsed Aceiron, Spinzle describing Adelynn's Nightwork as “a lifeless spreadsheet of numbers.”33

She lost with 312 of 684 votes (45.6%), carrying only Main and Waffle. She conceded at once, asked her supporters to see Aceiron as “human” in spite of his flaws, and offered to help with the transition; he accepted a smooth handover but declined her guidance.7

Party leader (2025–2026)

Apollos's return

Adelynn remained in the Preservate leadership after her defeat. When the Catx3 Regime collapsed in August 2025, she joined Seandry, Grace and Woofer in arguing for Apollos over Cyntaku's objections, and persuaded the party to stand behind his candidacy.8 With Grace, Cyntaku and Woofer she formed the Blue Quartet, a close group of friends who met in person several times. Apollos, who apologized for having mocked her in the past, and Spinzle both grew close to her during his second presidency.12

Boss of the party

In the Dreadnought Debate of 12 November 2025 she warned that Apollos and Spinzle's plan to enlarge the party machinery would create “a singular point of failure” and foster a cult of personality, before conceding: “I merely felt a compulsive urge to articulate a response.”34 Two days later Apollos reappointed her as the officially recognized Boss of the party, though her unpopularity kept her out of the Capital itself. She privately opposed his controlled burn of the opposition but went along with it while looking after members' wellbeing, and habitually deflected praise for her own record.2

When Liberation Party raiders vandalized the Scope and Volley servers on the eve of the December 2025 elections, she told the party not to rush to repair them.35

We do not need a Discord channel to be a community. The Ninzard Project is not a list of permissions or a collection of bots; it is a network of people.

— Adelynn, Boss of the Preservation Party, 21 December 2025

During the Chud War of 14 February 2026 she and Spinzle stayed in contact with General Kay throughout the standoff.36 In Legislature XI's debate on the Charter she agreed with Snuck that it should regulate only what was lethal rather than define “dignity” for every district, and argued alongside Spinzle that its disciplinary rules would close the “Gray Zone” Cat had exploited. Part I passed with all fifteen votes.9

Injury and retirement (2026)

In the early hours of 13 March 2026, the day after her 23rd birthday, Adelynn was struck by a vehicle in Los Angeles and seriously injured. Unable to use her devices, she stepped down as Boss, and Grace resigned as General of Waffle to be with her and her family. Members sent farewell messages, and Aceiron's mocking response drew outrage even among the Ories.3

these last three years, adelynn has poured an immeasurable amount of her own time and sanity into keeping this community safe and shouldered burdens that most wouldn't want to look at, just to ensure the members here were protected. i consider her to be a person of immense virtue and honour.

— President Apollos, 13 March 2026

Woofer succeeded her as Boss on 14 March, and Adelynn was later named one of the party's Honorary Veterans.10 From hospital she continued to advise the party, counselling restraint in its dispute with General PoG on 25 April, and wrote to Main: “Hi. I'm slowly on the mend.”37 Still in a medical brace, she welcomed a Sacramento member who had long attacked the Preservate leadership to stay with her family, and brought her to the Holloway Summit at General Kay's home on 2 May 2026.38 Later that month she joined the senior leadership's discussion of the presidential nomination, proposing a vote among the party's operatives.39

Her message for Spinzle's 23rd birthday in June 2026 summed up their rivalry turned friendship: “Bon anniversaire, ma vieille. You are, against your considerable efforts, very difficult not to love.”40

Political style

Adelynn is identified with the Preservation Party's Logos tradition of rules-based, procedural government and the pursuit of democratic mandates. She dealt with opponents, Cat, Lyman and Rudolf among them, out of a belief in their capacity for redemption, or at least a wish to contain them without adopting their methods; her party found this frustrating, and critics such as ViaTea called her naive.1,6 Opponents described her governance as stifling: Aceiron called its “worst sin” the “sterilization of the community's soul and morale.”31 The archival record of 2026 credits her “exhibition of Christian principles” with keeping the ethics of the Logos tradition alive within the party.41
