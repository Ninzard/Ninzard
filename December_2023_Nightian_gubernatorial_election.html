The December 2023 Nightian gubernatorial election was held on 30 December 2023 to choose the Governor of Nightwork. Governor Adelynn of the Preservation Party and her deputy governor, Grace, were re-elected over General ViaTea of Waffle, the Original Party's nominee, and her running mate, former Vice President Kain, by 351 votes to 300 (53.9%). Adelynn carried four of the five districts; ViaTea carried only Parry.1,2

Six months after Adelynn's landslide over Kennie, the Ories had turned themselves into a competitive force, to the surprise of observers. ViaTea, a moderate who championed archiving and the community's memory, ran a cordial campaign that centered on the collapse of Adelynn's Administrative Transparency Act, whose public archive Discord had frozen after Vice President Meikaqu planted hidden extremist text in it. Turnout broke records.2,3

Voters also chose to elect the governor once a year instead of every six months, which set the next gubernatorial election for December 2024, while the district generals kept six-month terms. In the district general elections held at the same time, the Ories won more votes than the Preservates and took Volley from them.2,4

Background

In her second term Adelynn pressed on with her stabilizing agenda. Activity reached record highs by the end of 2023, the rules were clear and the chain of command clear-cut, and Kay, back as General of Capitalia, kept her informed about Cat's actions in Ninzardia. The restored Original Party, however, grew frustrated with her rigid order and lack of “fun”; members wanted looser joining rules, fewer restrictions and more community events, but Adelynn kept her restrictive policies and held events to a minimum.5

After Lyman lost Scope in July, he and Scalen founded the Sonnenrad Society, a fascist network spread across outside Discord servers and Telegram groups, some of whose members stayed in Nightwork while hiding their views.6 Adelynn's Administrative Transparency Act required every ban, mute and deleted message in Nightwork to be reproduced word for word in a public archive. Meikaqu built a modified Discord client that hid extremist propaganda in invisible zero-width characters, and Pieban spread it through the Sonnenrad Society's lower ranks. When Adelynn banned the members using it, the archive reproduced the hidden material, and Discord's Trust and Safety team restricted it. Minutes before Adelynn announced the suspension, Meikaqu, using an alternate account, told Orie channels that the governor was about to lock the archive and blame “invisible hate speech,” so that Adelynn's truthful explanation looked rehearsed. The Ories treated it as a cover-up, and the controversy consumed her administration through late 2023.3

On 4 November 2023, with the agreement of every general, Adelynn enacted the 2023 Election Reforms Act, which fixed elections on 30 December for the winter cycle, with terms from 1 January to 1 July, and on 30 June for the summer cycle, with terms from 2 July to 31 December. ViaTea, her rival, hailed it as a “humanist act.”1

Nominations

Original Party

ViaTea had succeeded Kennie as General of Waffle in July.7 Inspired by the Bethany Alliance, she founded what she called the “Liberation Movement” to support the governor's stand against Cat's corruption and her campaign of threats and doxxing against her own members. To ViaTea's surprise, Adelynn thanked her and agreed to take in several “Cat refugees,” reversing her own early opposition to expansion.7 The Original Party nominated ViaTea for governor, with Kain, the former vice president and former General of Parry, as her running mate.2

Preservation Party

Adelynn ran for re-election with Grace as her running mate.

Campaign

Issues

ViaTea differed from the old Original Party, which had cared only for expansion and freedom. She believed Ninzard should be understood and managed as a narrative: chats should be protected and archived, and the community should recount its history and discuss its culture so as not to repeat its mistakes. She agreed with Adelynn's limits on “free speech” but opposed her restrictions on growth, and urged her to make the community more vibrant, pointing to Volley, which had become one of the most inactive districts under its Preservate general, Molly, as an example of “stagnant” Preservate rule.7

The campaign was notably cordial. Adelynn praised ViaTea's efforts and ambitions, and ViaTea described Adelynn as embodying “a gentle and enlightened spirit” but “naive.” The collapse of the Administrative Transparency Act gave ViaTea, who had built her political identity on archival preservation, her central issue.2

Debate

In the gubernatorial debate on 28 December 2023, ViaTea took up the failure of the act without accusing Adelynn of corruption, aiming instead at the structural fragility of the Preservate system and its reliance on bots. Adelynn defended her record, pointing out that all five of Nightwork's Discord servers were still running.2

「Olivia, I recognize the concerns you have articulated, and I do not take them lightly. The temporary suspension of the ATA was essential to protect Nightwork from corporate dissolution. … We face malicious actors who weaponize our own rules against us. Accordingly, prudent moderation remains the final safeguard keeping Nightwork alive.」

— Adelynn, to ViaTea in the gubernatorial debate, 28 December 2023

The same night General Aceiron of Scope, seeing that polite debate was failing to energize the Orie base, broadcast an attack to his district, accusing ViaTea of “appeasing a tyrant” and Adelynn of locking the archives to conceal Preservate corruption. Watching anger and accusations of hypocrisy move voters as theoretical arguments could not, he laid the foundation for his takeover of the Original Party the following year.2

Adelynn won 351 of the 651 votes cast and ViaTea 300. Turnout broke records, as it had in July, a sign of growing participation under Adelynn's administration.2

Term of office

The voters of this election chose to hold gubernatorial elections once a year, discarding the six-month cycle, which many considered excessive. The next election for governor was set for December 2024; the district general elections stayed on the six-month cycle.2

Main

Main, the largest district, gave Adelynn 124 of its 181 votes (68.5%), her best result. General Woofer was re-elected over the Orie Ploutos, a well-liked member of the C-4 running as a moderate, by 100 votes to 81, her closest race until then; his candidacy caused mild conflict on the Main Staff Team, some of whom began preparing for her succession.2,4

Parry

Parry, where Kain had twice been general, was the only district ViaTea carried, by 83 votes to 38. General Rudolf easily defeated his challenger, the Preservate Gui, by 88 votes to 33.2,4

Scope

Scope voted for Adelynn by 72 votes to 53 while re-electing its Orie general, Aceiron, over the Preservate Eve by 73 votes to 52, a wider margin than in July.2,4

Volley

Volley was the closest district, 52 votes for Adelynn to 48. On the same ballot the former general AJ unseated the Preservate Molly by 51 votes to 49, returning to power in Volley after five years.2,4

Waffle

ViaTea lost her own district by 65 votes to 59. The Preservate Matcha succeeded her as general, beating the Orie Idodo by the same 65 votes to 59; ViaTea endorsed neither, since she was close friends with Matcha despite her political alignment with Idodo.2,4

Concurrent elections

In the district general elections the Ories won 352 of the 651 votes (54.1%) to the Preservates' 299 (45.9%) and three of the five generalships. AJ and Matcha, the two newly elected generals, took over their districts on 1 January 2024.4

Aftermath

When the June 2024 elections came, all five generals were up for re-election but the governor was not, and many who had voted for the one-year term said they regretted it. At Spinzle's suggestion Adelynn put her leadership to a plebiscite held alongside the general elections, and 56.0% of those who answered voted against a snap election.9,10

Aceiron, meanwhile, became Boss of the Original Party in June 2024 and its nominee for governor that August. In the 2024 election he and AJ defeated Adelynn and Grace with 54.4% of the vote, carrying Volley, which had voted narrowly for Adelynn a year before.11,12,13,14
