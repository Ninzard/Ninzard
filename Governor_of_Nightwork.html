The Governor of Nightwork was the supreme executive office of the Nightian Faction within the Ninzard Project. Established unofficially on 11 July 2015 following the reunification of Nightwork and Ninzardia, the governorship served as the regional counterweight to the Ninzard Presidency, commanding the five historic districts of Main, Parry, Scope, Volley, and Waffle.1,2

Across its ten-year existence, nine governors held the office across distinct operational eras: the foundational truce of Elusion; the Golden Era militarization of DegreeZ; the authoritarian one-month crackdown of PiggieMLG; the criminal expansionism of Spikester; the ironclad isolation of Syn; the emergency defense of Cyntaku; the clinical constitutionalism of Spinzle; the bureaucratic sanctuary of Adelynn; and the populist secessionism of Aceiron.2,3,4 The office was abolished following the execution of Nexit in 2025, when Nightwork subsumed the entirety of the community infrastructure.5
