Sophie Tremblay, known as SofT, is a Canadian member of the Parry Staff under General Kaimachi and a computer science student at McGill University in Montréal. She campaigned for Poka in Parry during the 2026 presidential election, which Poka carried in the district by 73 votes to 57.1

When Raffaele offered fifty dollars to members who submitted a résumé for review in May 2026, a bounty open only to Americans, she sent hers in anyway. With her consent, President Apollos posted it in NPC! as an example of what “a peak resume looks like,” and Poka marveled at her 3:34 marathon.2

Background

Tremblay lives in Montréal and speaks French as her first language and English fluently. She entered McGill University in September 2022 to study computer science, with a minor in statistics, and expects to graduate in December 2026; she was named to the Dean's Honour List in 2024 and 2025. Her capstone project was a peer-to-peer file synchronization prototype written in Rust. She is looking for a software engineering job in Montréal, Toronto or remotely from January 2027.

From 2021 to 2023 she worked as a hostess and online-order coordinator at a family-owned Vietnamese restaurant in the Plateau-Mont-Royal, often holding the floor alone during the weekday lunch rush. She was later a teaching assistant for McGill's introductory computer science course, from January 2024 to April 2025, and spent the summer of 2025 as a software engineering intern on a digital platforms team at TD Bank in Toronto, where she received a return offer for the following summer.

Her side projects include MetroPulse, a dashboard that flags Montréal buses running more than eight minutes off schedule and that some forty of her friends use on their commutes, and an open-source tool that uses a small language model to give structured feedback on essays. She co-leads mentorship for McGill Women in Computer Science, pairing about seventy first-year students with upper-year mentors each semester, and volunteers twice a month at the Moisson Montréal food bank. She runs marathons, finishing Montréal in 2024 in 3:42 and Ottawa in 2025 in 3:34, and has been training for Toronto in 2026.

May Day Resume Bounty (2026)

On 1 May 2026, to promote the Ninzard Career Initiative, Apollos announced that Raffaele would pay fifty dollars to every member who submitted a résumé for review before the end of 3 May. Because the money was sent by Venmo, the offer was open only to Americans. The résumés were reviewed by a committee of Raffaele, Poka, Hackman, Idodo, Ploutos and Apollos.2

SofT, graduating that December and frustrated with her campus career center, submitted hers anyway, asking the committee for honest feedback, above all on whether her project descriptions communicated what she had built. Apollos, himself a Canadian, shared it in NPC! the same day with her permission.3

2026 presidential election

In the 2026 presidential election SofT campaigned for Poka in Parry, a district where most voters were Ories.4 Parry gave Poka an upset win with 73 votes (56.2%) to ScottyAte's 57 (43.8%); the Parry Girls campaigned for her with fervor, and BadSpirit's endorsement added to her momentum.1
