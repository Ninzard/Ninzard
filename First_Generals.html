First Generals

The first generals of the Ninzard Project were the five leaders chosen in its first elections, held from 29 to 31 December 2012, to run the five legions that had founded the project on 21 December: Hokan in Capitalia, Eliza in Hybriden, Bray in Sacraday, Hyperio in Sacranight and Gabriel in Solorado.1,2 They took office on 1 January 2013 under Antareze, the first president, who led all five legions while each general hosted and ran a legion of their own.1,2

Each legion was a TeamSpeak server of 32 members, 160 in all, and each had grown out of a community of its own. Capitalia was Zarden, the first server Antareze and Eliza had made, renamed as the capital legion; Hybriden was the second, which Eliza hosted; Sacraday was Braykout, the server of Bray and Sacramento's popular crowd; Sacranight began as Knights, which Hokan helped UNO set up to harass Bray after Bray threw him out; and Solorado, the Colorado server of Gabriel and Faber, was the last to join, on 1 December 2012.1,3,4,5,6,7 No constitution was written. The generals gave the president full permissions on their servers, but because they hosted them, he could not abuse that power.1,8 At 12:21 a.m. on 1 January 2013 all five ratified Antareze's Capital Laws without opposition, and the same day they met in the new Legislature with trusted members of their legions.9,10 Hyperio and Gabriel took little part in it, and Sacranight and Solorado stayed the legions most detached from the rest of the community.10

By the end of 2014 all five had left their legions. In December 2013 Hybriden, tired of Eliza's ties to the establishment, elected Abby in her place, while Bray and Gabriel kept Sacraday and Solorado.11 Sacranight's vote ended in a tie between Hyperio and Elusion, and when a second vote in January 2014 tied again, Antareze took over the legion's server himself, ending Hyperio's generalship.12 Gabriel left Solorado on 14 February 2014 to become Vice President, and Faber succeeded him;13 Sacranight was disbanded on 26 April 2014, the day after Hyperio and Elusion left to found Hyperion and Nightwork.14 Bray lost Sacraday to Trixy in June 2014,15 and Hokan, the last of the five, held Capitalia until the end of the year, when he became Vice President.16
