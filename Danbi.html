Min-ji Yoon (Korean: 윤민지; born 13 September 1999), known as Danbi, is a former Ninzard politician who served as the 7th District General of Main from 21 May 2021 to 7 January 2022. A moderate Orie, she took Main from Grace in an upset, staffed the district from every party, and lost it to Geo in January 2022.1,2

One of the community's first members, she joined in 2012 and was later General of Capitalia. She left Ninzard on 1 July 2026, the day Apollos's presidency ended.3,4

Early years

A Korean-Canadian in British Columbia, Yoon belonged to Hokan's academic circle and took English lessons from him; he recruited her into the project in November 2012, and she became one of Capitalia's first members.3 She later succeeded Hokan as General of Capitalia. In 2016 she left the Alienova Party for the Ories and was re-elected there as an ally of Hokan and of the new President Apollos, before losing the district to Atria at the end of the year.5,6

General of Main (2021–2022)

In May 2021 the Ories nominated Danbi in Main against General Grace, one of Governor Syn's most important allies, and she won in an upset. She and Spinzle, elected in Scope the same day, were seen as moderates who opposed Syn's hardline approach and favoured expansion and friendly ties with Ninzardia. She appointed independents, Preservates and Ories alike to Main's staff, setting a precedent of bipartisan leadership in the district; among her administrators were Adelynn, Ploutos and Seandry.1 She was re-elected in August 2021.7

At the turn of 2022 she was among the establishment Ories incensed at Apollos for openly backing the Poopulist Snowby against his own party.8 In the January 2022 elections, a rout for the Ories, she lost Main to the radical Preservate Geo, who reversed many of her policies; she challenged him again that July and lost by 83 votes to 73.2,9,10

Later years

Cat had built a rapport with her by late 2021, but Danbi turned down the $111 that Cat's ledger records offering her.11,12 In 2025 she was counted among the Rationalists of Aceiron's Orie coalition.13 She left the community on 1 July 2026, alongside Grace and several other veterans.4
