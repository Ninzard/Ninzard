Natalia Whitaker (born 14 April 2007), known as Woofer, is a Ninzard politician who has served as District General of Main since 3 January 2023 and as Boss of the Preservation Party since 14 March 2026.1,2

Elected at fifteen as the youngest leader of the January 2023 election cycle, she unseated General Sundae and made Main, the community's largest district, its most stable, winning eight consecutive elections there; the last, in June 2026, gave her 90.4% of the vote, the widest margin in the district's history.3,4 A moderate of the party's Logos Flank and a member of the Blue Quartet with Adelynn, Grace and Cyntaku, she was among the Preservates who brought Apollos back in 2025.5,6

After Adelynn was injured in March 2026, Woofer was elected to succeed her as Boss and led the party through the final months of Apollos's presidency. Apollos had seen her as his ideal successor, but the party nominated her former Head Moderator, Poka, for president instead.2,7,8

Early life

Whitaker was born on 14 April 2007. Accounts of her childhood describe a quiet, self-reliant child for whom Ninzard became the first place where her actions visibly mattered to others. Friends describe her as calm, reserved and observant, the one who listens before she speaks.3,5 Club Korea nicknamed her 두부 (“tofu”); she has worked at learning Korean and is an enthusiast of K-pop. She lives in the Los Angeles area, near Adelynn and Grace.3,5

Early career (2022)

Woofer was appointed an administrator of Nightwork-Main under General Geo, and Mainians came to see her calm, which held steady through tragic events, as a source of stability. On 24 November 2022 she made what she called an “unlikely endorsement” of President Cat's re-election. She argued that the Original Party's expansionism under Hokan and Xorbit did more harm to the community, and objected, as a Christian, to Xorbit's courting of religious congregations for votes.3

General of Main (2023–present)

2023 election

Running for the Logos Flank against General Sundae, an Orie who secretly worked with Cat, Woofer was underestimated by Cat, who dismissed her as a “saddo” and a “nobody.” She won 113 of 188 votes (60.1%), carried by the quiet loyalty of Main's older members.3 She rebuilt the Main Staff Team around reliability rather than friendship, appointing Daka and reinstating Seandry, and tested her staff, cutting off those she did not judge “safe and qualified” without much explanation. She also set out to fill what she felt was Main's “empty culture.”3

Under Adelynn

Woofer was re-elected with 83.3% of the vote in July 2023, and in December 2023 survived her closest race against the moderate Orie Ploutos.9,10 In 2024 she opposed Adelynn's plebiscite on her own leadership, arguing that the December 2023 result was already her mandate and that asking again would make mandates look “arbitrary.” When General Matcha called Adelynn “emotionless,” Woofer defended her as merely “too naive.” Once the vote was called she urged Mainians to vote “No,” and Main rejected a snap election by 76.2%.11 That year her moderators, led by Poka, contained an attempt by Cat to seed grievances in Main by confining the compromised accounts to a single channel.12

Under Aceiron

Main changed less than any other district during Aceiron's governorship, and by 2025, with 260 members, it was the largest and most active in the Project. Her staff then included Seandry as Head of Administrators, Ploutos and PluPlu as administrators, and Poka as Head Moderator.1 She was re-elected in December 2024 and June 2025.13,14

After the fall of Cat in August 2025, Woofer asked to speak with Apollos before taking a side. After their call on 23 August she argued that he was the only leader capable of “bringing back a sense of home” for the community.6 Apollos and Spinzle came to see her as the best successor to his presidency and treated her as a little sister, though he kept his view quiet to deny the opposition a target.5 When Liberation Party raiders struck three districts in December 2025, Main's staff fended them off, and it was the only one left intact; Woofer was re-elected days later with 214 of 256 votes (83.6%) against HazyDaze.15

The Chud War

As the Chud War of 14 February 2026 escalated in Sacramento, Woofer told the belligerents: “This isn't ‘lore,’ guys.”16 After shots were fired at Aceiron's house that night, she issued a statement.17

That is a hole where a bullet went through a house where a family lives. … The fact that we created an environment where twenty people felt comfortable bringing weapons to a parking lot is our failure. All of us.

— General Woofer, after the Chud War, February 2026

When General PoG accused the Capital on 16 February of running a surveillance state, Woofer answered within the hour that the belligerents had been livestreaming their own locations, and that “when a bullet goes through a window near a mother and her son, the ‘right to be unknown’ ends.” She accused PoG of courting the far right by “defending the privacy of neo-Nazis and rioters.”17

Boss of the Preservation Party (2026–present)

Leadership election

When Adelynn was seriously injured on 13 March 2026, Apollos and Spinzle asked Woofer to succeed her. She was reluctant, still hoping that Adelynn might return, but agreed to stand.18 On 14 March she won the party's leadership election with 29 of 36 votes, against Cyntaku and Lesty.2

On 2 April she set out a new structure for the Preservate Apparatus. Adelynn, Grace and Snuck became Honorary Veterans; Ploutos, Seandry, Spinzle and Apollos formed its senior leadership; and district coordinators, among them Poka and PluPlu in Main, completed its inner circle. She also sought to bring more young men into the leadership, among them Laser.2

Dispute with PoG

On 25 April 2026, PoG suggested that no one could have survived “the inner circle of a paranoid predator” by being innocent, a remark aimed at Woofer's teenage years on Main's staff. While Poka and Cyntaku hit back, Woofer replied without anger.19,20

When I was 15, I didn't have a grand strategy. I was just a girl who [made] sure the Discord tickets got answered and the server didn't completely break down. That's all it was.

— General Woofer, 25 April 2026

2026 presidential election

Apollos hoped to be succeeded by Woofer, his ideal “boring & serotonin” successor, or by Poka.7 In the nomination talks at the end of May the senior leaders weighed the two.21 When the Ories named ScottyAte with Hackman as his running mate, Cyntaku warned that pairing Woofer with another technocrat would let the opposition paint the party as “the boring, sterile bureaucracy,” and the leadership chose Poka on 30 May.8

On 22 June 2026 Woofer was re-elected in Main with 235 of 260 votes (90.4%), her eighth consecutive victory and the widest margin in the district's history, and used her victory statement to celebrate the president-elect.4

I know people joke about the “circus” or the “brainrot,” but I know the real you. … We have the Charter. We have a beautiful community. We finally have peace.

— General Woofer, to President-elect Poka, 22 June 2026

Personal life

With Adelynn, Grace and Cyntaku, Woofer is one of the Blue Quartet. Though she is the youngest, the other three regard her as an anchor of emotional stability, and she is often the most realistic of the four.5 In March 2026 she and Representative Ploutos announced that they were in a relationship, known in the community as “NicNat”; the two spend much of their free time playing video games.22

Political style

Woofer is regarded as a moderate Preservate who seeks to balance stability with fun. Her hold on Main rests on a loyal staff, the PluPlu Army and what voters see as a safe haven, and she has backed leaders on pragmatic grounds, from Cat in 2022 to Apollos in 2025.3,4,6
