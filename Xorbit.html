Adam Kim (born 4 June 2002), known as Xorbit or AK, is a former Ninzard politician who served as 2nd District General of Main from 1 January 2017 to 27 June 2018, as 3rd Deputy Governor of Nightwork from 27 June 2018 to 29 February 2020, and as 7th District General of Parry (then known as BobaBitcoin) from 9 July 2022 to 3 January 2023.1,2,3

A childhood friend of founder Hokan and a prominent figure within Club Korea, Xorbit rose through the Original Party during the Golden Era and later served as Deputy Governor under three consecutive Nightwork administrations—DegreeZ, PiggieMLG and Spikester—before briefly acting as Governor during Spikester's ousting in February 2020.2,4,5 Though regarded as a stabilizing statesman during the Underground Era, his standing collapsed in early 2020 following relationship scandals with underage members.5

In June 2022 Xorbit was covertly placed on Cat's payroll under the “Bounty Protocol,” receiving $13,788 to unseat Aurase in Parry and flood the district with new recruits, including teenagers from a local synagogue.6,7,8 After contesting three offices simultaneously in the January 2023 elections and losing all three, he was exposed for running a paid protection racket on the Jewish members he had recruited and was permanently banned from the Ninzard Project on 6 January 2023.9,10,11

Early life and background

Kim was born on 4 June 2002 and grew up in California.1,6 Before the Ninzard Project existed, he was a real-life childhood friend of Isaiah Choi (Hokan). While Hokan treated him with condescension as a useful proxy to project his own influence, Xorbit was outgoing, ambitious and eager to win the same recognition in Ninzard that Hokan commanded.6,12 Well-liked among the Korean-American cohort, he also drew heavy backing from Kain's Los Angeles-based Club Korea network.1

General of Main (2017–2018)

Following Elusion's retirement, Xorbit assumed office as the 2nd District General of Nightwork-Main on 1 January 2017 under the Original Party banner, backed by Vice President Hokan, Governor DegreeZ and Club Korea, after defeating the secessionist Xsora.1,13

As a member of Legislature IX during the 2017 Capital Adjudicator Selection Crisis, Xorbit voted against President Apollos's first three nominees—Syn, Kain and Spinzle—before voting in favor of Kushay and finally confirming Raffaele on 26 August 2017.14,15 He was re-elected in Main in the December 2017 elections.16 In June 2018, following the deaths of Tiny2 and Holidae, Xorbit sat in Legislature X (“The Last Legislature”) and joined DegreeZ and Aceiron in criticizing Capitalia General Kay when she declined to assume the presidency.17,18

Deputy Governor of Nightwork (2018–2020)

Appointment and the RSN War

On 27 June 2018, three days before the outbreak of the RSN War, Governor DegreeZ appointed Xorbit to replace Aceiron as Deputy Governor of Nightwork—the first time a governor replaced his deputy without an election—as part of DegreeZ and Aceiron's covert plot to stage Aceiron's defection to the Rocket Squad Nation; Whyskitter was elected to succeed Xorbit in Main.2 During the war, Volley General Clayton criticized Xorbit for believing that “peace can resolve this,” though by the war's conclusion in August 2018 Xorbit had grown more popular than DegreeZ, whom many Nightians blamed for instigating the conflict.19,20

Under PiggieMLG and Spikester

After DegreeZ resigned in October 2018, Xorbit was retained as Deputy Governor by Governor PiggieMLG on 27 October 2018 and again by Governor Spikester on 27 November 2018.21,22 On 17 December 2018 he was one of the six members in a Discord call with Syn when loan sharks broke into Syn's Vancouver home.23

Throughout Spikester's Era of Progression, Xorbit was widely viewed as a steady statesman who kept Nightwork from collapsing, particularly during Spikester's unannounced two-week absence in December 2019. Unknown to the community, Xorbit privately coordinated with Hokan across all three administrations.5 When the Ninzard Council impeached Spikester on 22 February 2020, Xorbit publicly turned on the governor, pressured him to step down, and served as Acting Governor of Nightwork until Syn and Cyntaku took office at the end of the month.24,25

2020 scandals and 2021 gubernatorial run

Despite being cleared of involvement in Spikester's drug trade, Xorbit's reputation was derailed by his personal conduct. While dating Scope member Liver—then fourteen to his seventeen—in 2019, he used his office to engage in sexual calls with other female members, and in early 2020 he abruptly left Liver for ViaTea, also three years his junior.5 President Apollos told Xorbit he did not respect him and refused to consider him for higher office. Xorbit briefly challenged Apollos for the Original Party's 2020 presidential nomination, but suspended his campaign in March 2020 after ViaTea and other women released messages accusing him of manipulation and coercion.5

On 31 January 2021 Xorbit ran as the Original Party nominee for Governor of Nightwork against incumbent Preservate Governor Syn. Whereas Xorbit mocked Syn as a “shut-in loser” engaged in “cringe country roleplay,” Syn attacked him as a “drunkard” and a “loose cannon,” defeating him comfortably.26

General of Parry (2022–2023)

The Bounty Protocol and the Synagogue Strategy

In June 2022 Cat approached Xorbit with the “Bounty Protocol,” offering him milestone payouts to run against Poopulist General Aurase in Parry (then called BobaBitcoin) and rapidly expand the district.6 Around the same time, Xorbit casually mentioned to Cat in a voice call that Hokan had sold his prized Unusual Team Fortress 2 hat and was suffering from untreated health and financial troubles in Toronto, inadvertently giving Cat the leverage she used to place Hokan on a consulting retainer.12 In the 9 July 2022 election Xorbit defeated Aurase with 47 of 98 votes (48.0%) and took office on 10 July.3

Over the next six months Xorbit doubled BobaBitcoin's membership from 135 to 270 in an effort to eclipse Main, collecting $13,788 from Cat.7,8,27 Coached by Cat, he used his mandatory community service hours at a California Jewish community center and synagogue to merge Jewish teenagers' Discord groups into Parry, while Cat covertly stoked neo-Nazi and antisemitic harassment inside the server to ignite conflict; on 14 October 2022 Xorbit staged a public spat with Cat when she used the slur “BobaKikeCoin” in Parry chat.7,28

Oomfie honeypot scandal

On 21 November 2022 logs were leaked showing the twenty-year-old Xorbit—who had claimed to be nineteen—sexting two minors, including seventeen-year-old Oomfie, and proposing to meet her at a love hotel before banning her to protect his standing.29

It is unfortunate it had to turn out this way, but if the staff team has an inkling that I am a “pedo,” that will ruin my reputation forever. I am ashamed of myself.

— General Xorbit, to Oomfie before banning her, November 2022

After a failed attempt to pay a female member to honeypot Al Commando in retaliation, Xorbit issued a public apology. Governor Spinzle stripped him of his administrative permissions in Parry, though he was permitted to retain his title and seek re-election.29

2023 triple candidacy and expulsion

In the January 2023 snap election cycle, Xorbit ran simultaneously for three offices under the Original Party: Vice President as Hokan's running mate (secretly at Cat's instruction), Governor of Ninzardia against Roland, and District General of BobaBitcoin against Rudolf and Aurase.9,30 The move prompted Governor Spinzle to enact the “Seatbelt Standard” barring future multiple candidacies in Nightwork, while Roland mocked Xorbit's campaign boasting by filming an ad picking up garbage in the snow.9,31 On 24 December 2022, following Cat's script, Xorbit publicly praised Hokan for leaking Cat's 2014 Skype logs, setting up Meikaqu to retaliate by exposing Hokan's 2009 elementary school framing of a classmate.30

On 3 January 2023 Xorbit was defeated in all three races: he lost the vice presidency to Snowby with 25.4%, lost the Ninzardian governorship to Roland with 30.3%, and finished second in Parry with 33.5% behind Rudolf.32,33,34 Cat immediately cut off his funding, sent Hokan screenshots of Xorbit mocking Hokan as “poor” and “desperate” while negotiating his payouts, and released logs showing that Xorbit had been charging the Jewish teenagers he recruited from the synagogue a monthly fee for a “Protected Role” and private channel.10,11 Condemned by newly elected Governor Adelynn for real-world extortion, Xorbit was permanently banned from the Ninzard Project on 6 January 2023.11
