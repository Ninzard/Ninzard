Presidential elections in the Ninzard Project choose the President of the Ninzard Project, the head of its Capital Administration. The president is chosen by a vote of the whole community, counted district by district. Eight presidential elections are recorded in detail, from the narrow re-election of Antareze in 2014 to the landslide that made Poka president in 2026.1,2

Apollos has won more of them than anyone else. He was elected in 2016 at the age of twelve in one of the community's great upsets, won again in 2018 and 2020, and returned as a Preservate to win in 2025. In 2022 he led the count but resigned moments before the polls closed, and the runner-up, Cat, became president.3,4,5,6,7

The two most recent elections, held after the fall of the Catx3 Regime, were contests between the Preservation and Original parties across the community's districts: five in 2025, and six in 2026, when Zarden joined them. Both were won by Preservates, and Poka's 75.9% in 2026 is the largest share of the vote any candidate has won.6,2

How the president is elected

Members vote directly for the president, and the candidate with the most votes wins; in 2025 Apollos's victory was assured as soon as his votes passed half of the community's population. The count has always been reported district by district: by the four legions in 2014, across the four districts of Ninzardia and the five of Nightwork in January 2023, and across the five and then six districts of the reunited community in 2025 and 2026.2,4,8,9,1

In the early years the president, like the vice president and the Capital Adjudicator, was to be elected every year.10 By 2025 a term was two years, though Apollos cut his own second term short, to end on 1 July 2026.11,12 The vice presidency has at times been filled by a separate vote, as in 2021 and January 2023; in 2025 and 2026 the presidential candidates ran with running mates, and in 2026 both of them chose Hackman.13,14,6,1

The rules have bent with the times. In 2018 the Legislature kept Apollos in office rather than hold the election due that summer, until the community demanded a real vote; it was held on 12 August with a simple form telling members to “elect whoever you want,” and no party nominations.15,4 The 2022 election used approval voting, which is how Cat, the runner-up, came to be seated when the winner resigned.7 Since the Charter of the Ninzard Project was ratified on 11 May 2026, a candidate without a major party's nomination must be sponsored by 10% of members under its Section 6; in 2026 Lashae failed to reach it.16,17

District maps

Since the fall of the Catx3 Regime the whole community has voted as one set of districts. Each map draws every district in proportion to the votes it cast, so Main, which casts about a third of the vote, sits at the centre as the largest; Zarden, established before the 2026 election, lies offshore.
