Galen Joseph Gentry (born 31 August 2005), known as Gale, is a Ninzard troll and politician who represents Volley in the Legislature for the Preservation Party. He sat in Legislature XI, which drafted the Charter of the Ninzard Project, and was re-elected to Legislature XII in June 2026.1,2,3 A member of the party since November 2025, he serves in its Apparatus as a party operative for Volley.4,5

From Sacramento, Gale rose to notoriety as one of the “Saltz,” Volley's trio of trolls with Rickard and Shuffle, who helped turn the district against Mel in 2022.6,7 Known as a troll with “no limits” to what he would say, he was nonetheless credited by Cyntaku with helping her win Volley in November 2025.4,8 He was the only Preservate present at the parking-lot standoff of the Chud War in February 2026, and with Rickard wrote the parody song “Goonstitution,” which Apollos crumpled on camera before signing the Charter.9,10,11

Early life

Gentry was born on 31 August 2005 and grew up in Sacramento, where he belonged to the community's “Sacramento Diaspora.” Shuffle, who grew up nearby, knew him in person, and the two bonded over their shared experiences of the area.6 Known as “Gale,” “Joe” and by a string of “terrorist” alternate accounts, he was a notorious Minecraft griefer, banned from every Ninzard-related Minecraft server and event, and leaned into a shy, “simp” persona. Other Sacramento members mocked his weight; he leaned into that too, joking about food, while his detractors invented a creepy caricature of him.6

The Saltz (2022–2023)

The District of Molly

By 2022 Gale was the right-hand man of Rickard, the face of a trio of Volley trolls completed by Shuffle and known as “ReiBertAni” or “the Saltz.” All three were outcasts and capable actors, and like Rickard, Gale was widely seen as “malicious.”6 Loyal to the Poopulist and Orie camp but finding General Mel annoying, the trio began plotting to replace her with Molly, hyping her semi-ironically as a “savior” who would solve every problem known to man in a movement called “Molly4Volley,” or “the District of Molly.” Mel wrongly believed she still had the troll vote.7 In July 2022 Molly won Volley with 34 of 45 votes (75.6%), as the trio swayed a large bloc of Orie voters behind her.12

One of Gale's alternate accounts, styled after Osama bin Laden, sought the No More Anime Party's nomination in that month's presidential election.13

GamerGore

In May 2023 Molly banned Rickard after an account he had vouched for, “GamerGore,” revealed itself as Cat and flooded Volley with gore. Gale, Rickard's best friend, led a mass protest for his pardon, flooding the district with provocative posts about Molly to bait her into banning them all. Molly did not react, and Woofer let Rickard into Main instead.14

The Sactown Pound (2025)

In July 2025, after Cat's assault on Aceiron, Gale joined the Sactown Pound, a vigilante cell of about two dozen members, drawn mostly from the disaffected men of Parry and Volley, that ScottyAte formed to hunt Cat and Meikaqu in Sacramento, alongside AJ, Rudolf, Boygho, Snuck and Shuffle.15 In January 2026 he was among the nostalgic former members who tried with ScottyAte to reassemble the Pound, but most were not interested.16

The Preservation Party (2025–present)

Joining the party

On 17 November 2025 the Preservation Party voted to add Gale and Molly to its Apparatus to represent Volley, which until then only Cyntaku had represented. Molly was removed again within hours, but Gale stayed. The archive describes him as an amoral troll with “no limits” to what he said and a history of grotesque statements, whose philosophy was that if something was funny, he would say it, and his admission set off a debate within the party.4

Days later Cyntaku won the snap election in Volley against the far-right NGin, and in her victory statement singled out Gale for his work behind the scenes.8 During the party's GCM Matrix Debate of 17 December he was muted, and Al Commando asked Apollos to free him.17

The group chat

When NGin praised a non-white member in a voice memo in January 2026, Gale and other trolls asked whether NGin was sincere in his views at all.18 That month he, Al Commando, Moctezuma, Rickard and Shuffle used a private group chat to stage “nightmare blunt rotations,” adding rival members to provoke them and removing them when they were done; Gale and Rickard filled it with hornyposting. On 23 January Evhom, a Jewish member of Parry, complained of being harassed by Rickard and Gale for being Jewish.19

On 26 January Gale changed his name and picture to Apollos's and repeatedly added General Kaimachi of Parry to the chat, which she kept leaving. On 2 February Vice President Spinzle, who had decided with Apollos to bring Kaimachi inside the party, asked Gale to add her, and told Kaimachi that they needed a “wrangler,” someone who “speaks the language of the insane like Gale.”19 In the debate that followed, Spinzle argued that bringing provocative figures inside had worked with Apollos, with Al Commando and “kind of” with Gale, while Ixan complained that Gale's grotesque sexual comments went largely unchecked when Kaimachi was scrutinized; others answered that he was known to be trolling.20

The Chud War

On 14 February 2026, during the Chud War, a standoff broke out in a parking lot near Aceiron's home between fourteen members of NGin's Holy Iron Flank, eight Calebros, one Liberator and Gale, a Preservate bystander. As the only member of the Preservate Apparatus at “ground zero,” he gave the party a direct line to the belligerents, and that evening he rode with ScottyAte as he drove back and forth between the lot and Aceiron's house.9 When shots were fired at the house that night, he declared, “Bye Ninzard, this was fun while it lasted.”21

Legislature XI

When the party reorganized on 24 February 2026, Gale became a party operative for Volley in Apollos's Pragma Flank, a post he kept when Woofer restructured the Apparatus on 2 April.5,22 He was one of the fifteen representatives in Legislature XI when its debates on the Charter began on 28 February.23 At the end of March, as Scope and Volley quarrelled, he joined the Scopians taunting the Holy Iron Flank and helped hype talk of a “Second Civil War” to raise the community's alarm on purpose.24

Tired of the essays that Lless, Lyman and Liver kept posting in the Legislature, Gale and Rickard released “Goonstitution” on 16 April 2026, a parody rap with a video edit of AI-generated photos of NGin and Liver at a wedding. It was an instant hit.10 On 9 May he followed it with a profane, deliberately absurd voice memo parodying NGin's rants and declaring the Holy Iron Flank dead.25 Two days later Apollos opened his Charter ratification broadcast by unrolling a printed “Goonstitution” and crumpling it, and Gale was one of the eight representatives who voted for the joke motion to impeach him.11

Re-election

In the summer elections of June 2026 Volley re-elected Gale with 76 votes (58.5%), alongside Lunaix, who unseated Lyman.2 In Legislature XII, seated under President Poka, he and Lunaix represent Volley; every member but one is a Preservate.3

Reputation

The archive treats Gale as one of the community's most provocative trolls: Apollos summed up the contents of the party's chats as “gale hornyposts,” and the archive's glossary of Ninzard slang uses him in two of its examples.20,26 Those who knew him longer held that much of it was performance, and Cyntaku called him “scary smart underneath all the noise.”20,8
