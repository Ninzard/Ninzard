Athaniel Saliba (born 8 September 2004), known on Discord as AthanieLove, is an American Ninzard from Sacramento and a former member of the Holy Iron Flank, the far-right faction led by NGin. Born to Levantine parents, he took part in the Chud War of 14 February 2026 and is one of the boys holding up crowbars beside NGin in the war's infamous selfie.1 On 2 May 2026 he drove NGin and three others of the Flank to Los Angeles to confront Liver, a journey that ended at the Holloway Summit, where he was the first of them to put down his weapon; he is one of the Holloway Summit 30.2,3,4

Days later he went with Liver to the Flank's holdouts in Volley and told them how M had fed him, helping to win the last votes the Charter needed.5 Since July 2026 he has been close friends with Rhydeu, with whom he plays Nintendo Switch games, and since October 2026 he and NGin have attended an Orthodox mission.6

Early life and Holy Iron Flank

Saliba was born on 8 September 2004 in Sacramento, California, to Levantine parents. He joined the Holy Iron Flank, NGin's far-right faction, whose young men came largely from the District of Volley, from Sacramento and from the towns of the Central Valley.7,5 The archive describes him in 2026 as sharing NGin's hatred of Jews.3 He later said that he had joined NGin because NGin promised him power.5

Chud War (2026)

On 14 February 2026, after the Calebros had passed around NGin's home address and threatened to kill his dog, NGin told the Flank to “prepare for war,” and its members armed themselves with improvised blunt weapons.8,7 Athaniel marched with NGin that evening. Fourteen of the Flank faced Rudolf's Sactown Rivalists in a parking lot near Aceiron's house, and late that night they besieged the house itself, where Aceiron was holed up with AJ, Elle, Lyre, Rhydeu and ScottyAte.9,10

The siege ended without a fight between the two sides. ScottyAte stepped out and shook NGin's hand, and the Flankers and the Calebros were trading banter in the street when two shots were fired at the house from a passing car at 23:40. No one was hurt; the shooter was later identified as the Liberator Triste.10,11,12

In the days that followed, a selfie of NGin and his Iron Flank, the fourteen of them in the streets of Sacramento holding up crowbars, was memed relentlessly; Athaniel is one of the boys in it. AI versions of the picture recast the group as leprechauns, in a nod to NGin's ginger hair, as space marines on the moon and as Roman legionaries, and Al Commando posted one of them as a K-pop group.1

Holloway Summit (2026)

Drive to Los Angeles

On the evening of 1 May 2026 ScottyAte revealed that Liver, the Flank's ideological muse, had driven to Southern California to stay with Adelynn. NGin answered with a five-second voice memo, “It's time,” and gathered the Flank in a call to announce that he would go to Los Angeles; some of the boys demanded to come with him, and he let them. Early the next morning NGin, Athaniel, Chud Alessandro, Highlander and Cedario set off in Athaniel's car.2

Athaniel drove his rusted 2012 Honda Civic down Interstate 5 from Sacramento, with NGin in the passenger seat and the others in the back beside two tire irons, a flashlight and NGin's “Midwit Crusher” bat. He took a wrong turn on the way, keeping the twenty-three people gathered at the Holloway house waiting longer than expected.3 Before the boys arrived, M said grace over the meal and prayed for the young men on the highway, naming each of them.13,14

When they reached the house the boys sat frozen at the curb for five minutes. “Should we... should we honk?” Athaniel whispered. Kay, watching them on her security feed, then broke into the car's Bluetooth and spoke through its speakers, telling them to come up to the house, and the boys shrieked.3

At the Holloway house

At the door Kay told them to put their weapons in the umbrella stand and come and eat. Athaniel was the first to drop his tire iron in, and Highlander and Cedario followed; NGin kept his bat.3 Inside, AJ caught Athaniel's eye from his armchair and called for a fourth player in a race of Mario Kart 8; while Cedario took the controller, Athaniel wandered to the kitchen island, where M was carving the brisket. “Smells good, man,” he said quietly, and M handed him a loaded plate with mac and cheese and patted his shoulder. He stayed to ask shy questions about the seasoning.3,15

When NGin screamed at Cedario to put the controller down, Athaniel tried to calm him:3

「Ethan... chill, bro. It's just a game. The food's actually really good.」

— Athaniel, to NGin, 2 May 2026

NGin kicked Cedario in the head, and when Idodo and Sev restrained him, he tried to go after Athaniel.3 When at last his rage gave way and he collapsed weeping into M's arms, Athaniel, Cedario, Highlander and even Chud Alessandro sank to the floor around them, their heads bowed, while M prayed.3,16

Athaniel is one of the Holloway Summit 30, listed in the archive under his username, AthanieLove.4 As Aceiron, the last to arrive, sat down and embraced NGin, Athaniel exchanged quiet nods with AJ and Laser by the kitchen island. That evening he and AJ were seen sitting on the floor by the coffee table, eating brisket and cracking jokes, and late that night he and Chud Alessandro were among the boys who dissolved into giggles in the dark, like children at a sleepover.15,17,18

Holdouts in Volley (2026)

After the Summit the Charter still lacked the votes it needed, and some of the last were held by a group of the Flank's holdouts in Volley, who saw the reconciliation as a betrayal and twice turned Liver away from their apartment in Modesto. On her third visit she brought Athaniel, Highlander and Cedario.5 At the door Athaniel pressed a small wooden cross into the hand of one of the holdouts and told them what had happened in Los Angeles.5

「We lost our way. We fought a phantom enemy. … We sat on a sofa and we played video games. My hate evaporated.」

— Athaniel, Modesto, May 2026

He spoke of the father who had left his mother with nothing, of following NGin for the power he promised, and of watching M hold NGin as he wept: “I wanted a father to hold me like that.”5 When Liver called NGin to the apartment complex and he knelt on the wet pavement to ask the Flank's forgiveness, Athaniel, Highlander and Cedario rushed to his side and were among the first to embrace him, joined by dozens of the Flank who had gathered from Stockton, Citrus Heights and Elk Grove. On the evening of 7 May the holdouts cast their votes, carrying the Charter past the 640 it needed to 656.5

Personal life

In July 2026 Athaniel became good friends with Rhydeu, a long-time Orie and one of the “Calebros” who had been besieged in Aceiron's house during the Chud War, and the two often play video games together on the Nintendo Switch.10 At the Holloway Summit he had watched the Ninzards racing on the Switch, and AJ, Rhydeu's cousin, had called him over to play.3

After Hyperio's wedding in October 2026, he and NGin began attending an Orthodox mission in the suburbs.6 Several former members of the Flank have since become Orthodox converts.19
