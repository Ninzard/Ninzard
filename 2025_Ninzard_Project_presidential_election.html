The 2025 Ninzard Project presidential election was held from 20 to 22 September 2025, a month after the fall of Cat, to choose the 4th President of the Ninzard Project. Former President Apollos, the Preservation Party's nominee, and his running mate, former Governor Spinzle, defeated Aceiron, the Original Party's nominee and the community's de facto president, and his running mate, AJ. Apollos won 471 of 757 votes (62.2%) and four of the five districts.1,2

Aceiron called the election in August 2025, after the collapse of the Catx3 Regime left Nightwork as the only faction standing and its five districts became the districts of the Ninzard Project. The Preservates, unable to find anyone else willing to run, turned to their old adversary Apollos, who promised a single, shortened term devoted to dismantling what he called the “cult-environment of Ninzard.” Aceiron led the early polls, but he withdrew from a debate, broke down in a voice call on 10 September and then deleted the Nightwork-Main server, costing him the moderates of Main.3,1,4,5

Apollos carried Parry, long an Orie stronghold, by three votes and took 92% of the vote in Waffle; only Volley voted for Aceiron. Of the 780 members eligible to vote, 97.1% did so. Aceiron refused to concede, and Apollos was inaugurated on 22 September 2025, becoming the only president to serve non-consecutive terms.2,6

Background

On 20 August 2025, the day after the fall of the Catx3 Regime, the remaining infrastructure of Ninzardia was cleared away and its members were taken into Nightwork as “refugees” by Governor Aceiron. Nightwork became the Ninzard Project: Nightwork-Main, Nightwork-Parry, Nightwork-Scope, Nightwork-Volley and Nightwork-Waffle were renamed the Districts of Main, Parry, Scope, Volley and Waffle. Many former Ninzardians, and most of the member base, still identified as “Ninzard over Nightwork,” and from 20 August to 21 September the community had no elected Capital at all.3,1

Aceiron, who as Boss of the Original Party had won re-election as governor in June with 61.1% of the vote, effectively declared himself the de facto President of the Ninzard Project. On 21 August, with high approval ratings, he announced his candidacy, promising “new beginnings,” and set the election for 20–22 September, with the president-elect to be inaugurated on the last day. He also announced that the governorship of Nightwork would be abolished.1,3,7

Nominations

Preservation Party

The Preservates wanted a president who could lead the community's healing, and many worried that Aceiron's erratic and paranoid conduct made him unsuited to it. Yet no one stepped forward. Apollos was the unspoken option, but many in the party remembered his first presidency as a nightmare and were desperate to find “literally anyone else.” Former Governor Cyntaku was among the strongest opponents.8

「Let him back? Not just back, but as our presidential nominee? Are we collectively concussed?」

— Cyntaku, to the Preservate Apparatus, 22 August 2025

Admin Seandry, General Grace of Waffle, General Woofer of Main and former Governor Adelynn made the case for him. Grace doubted anyone else could beat Aceiron, and Adelynn said she trusted Seandry's and Grace's judgment. Woofer asked to speak to Apollos first, and after a call on 23 August she came away convinced that he was the only leader capable of “bringing back a sense of home” for the community. Cyntaku reluctantly fell in line.8

Apollos himself was uneasy. He urged Grace to run, which she refused, and then appealed to former Governor Syn, who also declined. Former General Matcha and the independent Power of Greed considered running but did not. On 24 August Apollos announced that he would decide by 30 August, and would run only if no one viable stepped up. If he ran, he said, it would be as a Preservate, with Spinzle as his running mate, for a single term that he intended to cut short.8

「My victory will be like the Donald Trump comeback, except it's actually good for people. If what I do can benefit 800 people, then I think it is worth it for the greater good.」

— Former President Apollos, 24 August 2025

He declared his candidacy on 29 August with a video that mixed clips of his younger self with a diss track aimed at Cat. The Preservation Party nominated him and Spinzle the next day with minimal opposition, and on 1 September he founded the Pragma Flank of the party. Spinzle made clear that she would leave office when he did and had no intention of succeeding him. Cyntaku accepted Apollos but could not come to terms with Spinzle's nomination.9

Original Party

Aceiron ran with AJ, his deputy governor. He was taken aback by the Preservates' choice and complained: “They're running a fucking Bethany Alliance against me.”9

Campaign

Issues

The two candidates agreed that the governorship of Nightwork would be abolished after the election. Apollos ran as a “moderate Preservate” on safety, stability and healing, putting “safety over activity” against the Original Party's “it's activity, stupid,” and promised tighter control over members joining and leaving. Aceiron cast him as an establishment figure who would let Ninzard stagnate, and blamed him for Cat's rise to power, a charge Apollos acknowledged as accurate and which some voters took as sincere. Voters also questioned Apollos's realignment with the Preservates: he was now running against the party he had built.9

Aceiron: Look, the choice is visible chaos versus hidden instability. The Orie doctrine accepts short-term disorder to prevent long-term existential fracture.

Apollos: i agree with you! but are you not the cause of instability? and neither have you regulated discord, but only fanned its flames. short-term disorder is actually my doctrine.

— Public announcements, 7 September 2025

The debate that never was

Apollos challenged Aceiron to debate from the day he declared, and members began calling Aceiron “chicken” for hesitating. In early September Aceiron was handed leaked photos of Apollos and friends posing in a sex store, but decided not to use them, suspecting a trap that would only make his rival look more human. On 4 September he agreed to a debate hosted by Ire, then failed to appear when it was due on the evening of 6 September, saying Apollos had tried to trap and slander him. By then he had pulled comfortably ahead in the polls, and he moved further ahead when Apollos caught the flu in the first week of September and had to hand much of his campaign to his team.9

On 8 September Aceiron accused the Main Staff Team of pro-Apollos bias and revoked some of its moderators' permissions, which pushed many undecided Mainians towards the Preservates. The next day he indirectly blamed Apollos for the deaths of three members; Apollos told his party to “civilly rebut or ignore” what he called “probably bait.”9

“TFIWWY”

The assassination of the conservative activist Charlie Kirk on 10 September 2025 hit Aceiron hard. He broke down in a voice call, and when members mocked him he lashed out at them, sobbing: “The fuck is wrong with y'all?!” As he spoke, others in the call played duck quacks and clapping sound effects. The recording became a meme that outlived the election, “TFIWWY,” with the 🦆 emoji as its symbol. Aceiron then deleted the entire Nightwork-Main server, which Woofer had to rebuild from scratch, and announced a “break.”4

Aceiron then recorded ads with AJ for supporters to edit and spread. The first used clips of his offensive language and erratic behaviour from 2018 to 2020 and called him unfit to lead. Apollos answered within five minutes with a clip of Aceiron crying, set to Ninja Tuna and closed by Al Commando's voiceover. Syn and Cyntaku found it petty, Adelynn and Matcha thought Aceiron's grief should not be mocked, and his hardcore supporters accused Apollos of mocking Kirk's death, which he denied; but to much of a community worn down by years of gloom, the ad was a welcome laugh.4

Final days

Generals Woofer, Ixan and Grace became the engines of Apollos's campaign in Main, Scope and Waffle, while Aceiron, suspicious of pro-Apollos activity in those districts, began interrogating members and examining their staff. Apollos left the Preservate districts to their generals and spent the last days of the campaign in group chats with voters in Parry and Volley, the Orie districts, winning over several, including former members of the short-lived “Sactown Pound.”9,4

Election day

On 20 September Apollos released an election-day video: a revised rendition of Arcturus Mengsk's inaugural speech from StarCraft, voiced by Apollos, Spinzle and other friends, followed by Apollos doing twelve pull-ups and challenging Aceiron to match him, and a “diss track” that ended by forgiving his opponent and quoting Matthew 5:44. Supporters acclaimed it; the Ories did not.2

AJ: Nahh, ts corny asf 💀

Rudolf: Cringevengers.

Mel: The secondhand cringe is lethal. Ew.

— Reactions to the election-day video, 20 September 2025

Apollos took the lead as soon as results came in, and by the end of the day he had passed 390 votes, more than half of Ninzard's population, which guaranteed his victory. He released a pre-recorded victory video urging Ninzards to “go outside” for their health. Many took it as arrogance, unaware that he had pre-recorded a losing speech as well, and some of Aceiron's supporters spun it into unfounded claims of fraud.2

Main

Main, the largest district, had been expected to lean towards Aceiron, who had won it in June and appealed to its moderates, while Apollos carried a long rivalry with the district and lingering conspiracy theories from the Cat years. Aceiron's deletion of its server on 10 September flipped some moderates, but many ordinary Orie voters stayed with him. The difference was the district's leadership: General Woofer backed Apollos, along with Seandry, Ploutos, PluPlu and Poka, the last two of whom had voted for Aceiron twice before, and Raffaele quietly brought over much of Main's Italian community. Apollos would have lost Main without Woofer's support.5

Parry

Parry produced the closest result of the election and its biggest upset. Even Apollos's most optimistic supporters had expected 40 to 45% there. But many Parryans were old friends of his and remembered the early days fondly, he campaigned hard in their group calls, and he was popular with the district's younger women. Even some members of the old Pound came round. Apollos had not expected to win Parry, and was flattered by its support.10

Scope

Aceiron's home district gave him only 24 votes, the result that hurt him most and the one he cited most often as “evidence” of fraud. Unknown to him, Raffaele had been recruiting Scopians for Apollos while publicly backing Aceiron. Scope also remembered its former governor Spinzle with devotion: her admirers called her “Queen Edith” and Apollos “King Reuben Apollos,” and the ticket won endorsements from Scopians as far apart as Lless and former Governor Spikester.11

Volley

Volley was the only district Aceiron won. It had turned from a polarized district into a right-wing one with ties to the alt-right, and most Volleyans distrusted Apollos. Even so, he drew a third of the vote there, more than expected, from voters looking for excitement.12

Waffle

Waffle gave Apollos 92% of its vote, the most lopsided result of the election. The district had become a Preservate stronghold in reaction to Aceiron, who had harassed many of its members, and many Wafflers also remembered Apollos as Assailant, the general who had rebuilt their district from scratch. Grace, Waffle's head of staff Rae Fox and its admins Shuffle and ViaTea drove turnout, and even the radical-left Liberation Front backed Apollos as the “lesser of two evils.”13

Aftermath

Refusal to concede

Aceiron refused to concede or to hand over the servers. When Apollos and Spinzle opened the Non-Partisan Community Initiative (NPC!) server and invited the opposition, he said he would concede only if he was given the Cat Files; Apollos declined, explaining that the files' release was being held back over legal threats and to keep it out of politics, and the exchange collapsed into insults. Aceiron told Mel not to cooperate with the new servers, which she defied, and tried to have his supporters “storm Parry” as it was rebuilt; Apollos set up decoy Parry servers and called Aceiron a “doofus” each time he joined one.2

Inauguration

Apollos and Spinzle recreated all five district servers in less than 24 hours, a show of competence that earned the new administration the trust of the Preservate Apparatus. On 22 September 2025 Apollos was inaugurated as the 4th President of the Ninzard Project at 21 years old, nine years after first taking office at twelve, with Spinzle as the 10th Vice President.2,6 He marked the occasion with a statement in the style of Donald Trump, the president Aceiron and many Ories supported.

「IN LESS THAN 24 HOURS, WE HAVE REBUILT THE ENTIRETY OF NIGHTWORK. … TOGETHER, WE SHALL MAKE NINZARD GREAT AGAIN!!! AND THIS TIME, WE'RE DOING IT THE RIGHT WAY!!!」

— President Apollos, 22 September 2025

For the cohort that had grown up in the Golden Era, the result was a “Welcome Home” victory, won barely three months after Aceiron's own 61.1% in June. Defeat split Aceiron's coalition between his loyalists, the Calebros, and NGin's far-right Holy Iron Flank, which branded him a coward. Apollos set his term to end on 1 July 2026, with the next election to be held alongside the June 2026 elections.6,14,3
