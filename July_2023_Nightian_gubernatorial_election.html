The July 2023 Nightian gubernatorial election was held on 4 July 2023 to choose the Governor of Nightwork. Governor Adelynn of the Preservation Party, facing re-election for the first time with her deputy governor, Grace, defeated General Kennie of Waffle, the Original Party's nominee, and her running mate, Atria, by 480 votes to 160 (75.0%) and carried all five districts, the biggest landslide in the history of the Project's gubernatorial elections.1,2,3

The election followed a spring of crisis. On 1 April 2023 Apollos revealed that he had been Waffle's General Assailant all along, and Adelynn answered with the Sponsorship Standard, a system of vouching meant to keep alternate accounts out of Nightwork. Kennie, elected in the Waffle snap election that followed, campaigned against Adelynn as naive for failing to unmask Assailant, but she was weighed down by a long feud with Roland, rejected every request to debate, and was suspected of being too close to President Cat.4,5,2

The Ories did better in the district general elections held the same day, winning Parry, Scope and Waffle, although the Preservates won more votes overall. In the concurrent vice-presidential election, Spinzle, the joint candidate of the Ories and the Preservates, swept Nightwork, but Cat's rigged count in Ninzardia handed the office to Meikaqu. After her defeat Kennie went over to Cat.6,7,8

Background

Adelynn had governed Nightwork since 3 January 2023.9 She began by clearing out members she considered toxic or dangerous and announced that every district would eventually have the same number of members. When forcing the cleanup proved more precarious than she expected, she moderated her plans and agreed to work by attrition, as Spinzle had urged, and that willingness to compromise won her the cooperation of four of the five district generals.10

In mid-March former President Apollos learned from Raffaele how he had helped build Cat's power and how Cat had paid Nightians to harass Apollos. On 31 March General Assailant of Waffle promised a “big April Fools' announcement,” and at midnight on 1 April 2023 he revealed himself as Apollos, to the shock and anger of many members. Adelynn banned the former president and publicly apologized for not recognizing an alternate account.4,11 In the snap election for Waffle that followed, Kennie, an Edith Gang member who already held a grudge against Apollos, rallied the angry district and was elected for the Original Party.4

Adelynn, Grace and the generals met to find a remedy, and on 3 April Adelynn announced the Sponsorship Standard: new members needed an established citizen to vouch for them, sponsors shared responsibility if their member proved to be an alternate account, and candidates and staff had to pass two private voice verifications. Many Nightians, especially Orie voters, found it amusing, and President Cat's endorsement of it struck nearly everyone as hypocritical.11,5

「It's quite reassuring to see Governor Adelynn demonstrating genuine maturity. I agree entirely. We ought to know who our neighbours are. I fully endorse this standard! :3」

— Cat, President of the Ninzard Project, 4 April 2023

Nominations

The Original Party nominated Kennie, who had held Waffle for three months.1,12 Her running mate was Atria, who had been Spinzle's deputy governor until January and, like Kennie, a member of the Edith Gang. Adelynn ran for re-election for the Preservation Party with Grace, her deputy governor, again as her running mate. At the same time the two parties were working together against Cat: the Ories nominated Spinzle for vice president against Cat's candidate, Meikaqu, and the Preservates joined them in the Bethany Alliance, the strongest opposition yet mustered against the Disco Party.13

Campaign

The Orie Apparatus explicitly asked Kennie not to campaign aggressively against Adelynn, so as not to damage the Bethany Alliance, but she did not comply. She portrayed Adelynn as naive for failing to detect Assailant's true identity, an argument that worked against the Ories, since it had been the Orie governor Spinzle who allowed Waffle to be created without knowing who Assailant was.2

Kennie's own drama hurt her more. After Roland insulted Soda's leadership of Scope, she quarreled with him, and the feud drew in Vice President Snowby: on 12 February 2023 an argument ended with Snowby kicked out of BobaBase, and by 28 March Roland's abuse of Snowby had turned crude and personal. Snowby and Kennie answered with false accusations against him, and by April the feud had left members exhausted.2

With Cat's shadow over Nightwork growing, members put a premium on a professional leadership that could handle both Cat and the district quarrels, which Adelynn provided by uniting the generals. Although Kennie insulted Cat in public, she still took part in Cat's events and was close to Cat's circle, including her best friend Sundaze and Snowby; the later discovery that Cat had been paying her confirmed the suspicion.2 Atria, unknown to almost everyone, was on Cat's payroll too.14 Adelynn tried to consult Kennie and asked repeatedly for a debate, but Kennie turned down every request and said the governor had a “saviour complex.”2 General Molly of Volley endorsed Adelynn despite their disagreements, believing Kennie to be far worse.15

Results

Adelynn won 480 of the 640 votes cast and Kennie 160, an exact three-to-one split and the biggest landslide in any of the Project's gubernatorial elections.2 Adelynn carried every district, with more than 60% in each.3

Main

Main, the largest district, gave Adelynn 176 of its 192 votes (91.7%), her best result and Kennie's worst. On the same ballot General Woofer was re-elected over the Orie Rhydeu by 160 votes to 32.3,6

Parry

Parry gave Kennie her best result, 60 of its 154 votes (39.0%). General Rudolf, who had joined the Original Party, was re-elected over the Preservate Gui by 85 votes to 69; Cat had planned to oust him, but the Assailant affair diverted her attention.3,6,15

Scope

Scope voted for Adelynn by 76 votes to 19, yet elected an Orie general the same day: Aceiron, who presented himself as a level-headed moderate who would keep Adelynn's popular policies while replacing Lyman's immaturity, unseated the Preservate by 50 votes to 45. Lyman's racist message after his defeat got him banned from Scope.3,15

Volley

Volley gave Adelynn 57 of its 77 votes. General Molly was re-elected over the Orie Sev with 50, notably fewer than Adelynn, as members grew frustrated with the district's stagnation.3,15

Waffle

Kennie lost her own district by 77 votes to 45. Wafflers had grown frustrated and suspicious of her drama-dominated leadership, which had damaged the friendly environment Assailant had built, and Kennie, barred from running for general while she ran for governor, was succeeded by the Orie ViaTea, who beat the Preservate Lashae by 67 votes to 55 as a moderate and a critic of Cat.3,15

Vice president

In what proved to be the last vice-presidential election in the Project, Spinzle, the Bethany Alliance's candidate, won 507 of Nightwork's 640 votes (79.2%) against Cat's candidate, Meikaqu. In Ninzardia only Capitalia's count was genuine, and Catsland and Lair, filled with alternate accounts and bots, returned near-unanimous votes for Meikaqu, who was declared the winner with 578 votes to 557. The obvious manipulation left Nightians uneasy, and after the election Cat abolished the separate election of the vice president.13,7,8

Aftermath

Kennie took her defeat bitterly. Cat offered her more money and prospects to abandon the Nightian Faction for Ninzardia, and she accepted.8 Nine days after the election, on 13 July, Atria was caught disparaging Apollos and Roland and exposed as a “snake” who could not be trusted; because she had been Spinzle's deputy governor throughout her term, the affair also led Nightians to judge Spinzle's governorship a failure that Atria had undermined.14 Cat's popularity in Nightwork, meanwhile, kept dwindling, and her shadow over the community became a more pressing issue for its voters.8

In her second term Adelynn pressed on with her stabilizing agenda, and activity reached record highs by the end of the year. The restored Original Party, however, grew frustrated with her rigid order and her lack of “fun.”17 The day after his defeat Lyman founded, with Scalen, the Sonnenrad Society, a fascist network whose members included former Nightians Adelynn had banned.18 In the December 2023 election Adelynn faced a far closer race against ViaTea.19
