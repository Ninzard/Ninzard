Elizabeth Kitten Dellinger (born c. June 2025), known as Eliza Kitten, is a black cat from Sacramento owned by ScottyAte and the unofficial mascot of the District of Parry. The Sactown Pound found her as a malnourished stray in a culvert near the American River in July 2025 and named her after Eliza, Cat's estranged older sister and one of Ninzard's founders, purely to spite Cat.1 She was the Pound's mascot and logo during its hunt for Cat, and after the group dissolved she stayed with ScottyAte as an ordinary pet, “a living artifact” of the month when the community came close to a killing.2

Since then she has been threatened in the Chud War, shown off in Legislature XI, credited by ScottyAte with endorsing his campaign for president, and accused by his opponents of being a campaign prop. Her name has also passed into Ninzard's slang.3,4,5,6,7

Discovery (2025)

On 20 July 2025 ScottyAte founded the Sactown Pound, a vigilante cell of about two dozen young men from Parry and Volley that set out to hunt President Cat in the Sacramento area.8 During one of its patrols near the American River, the group found a malnourished black stray kitten hiding in a culvert, abandoned by her mother, and ScottyAte pulled her out. She did not behave like other cats.1

In a unanimous decision driven by spite, the group named her Elizabeth, which became “Eliza Kitten.” The name was a calculated insult to Cat, invoking her estranged older sister Eliza, a founder of Ninzard, whom Cat famously despised.1 Her full name, Elizabeth Kitten Dellinger, carries her owner's surname. Her exact age is not known; from her size when she was found, she is estimated to have been born around June 2025.

Mascot of the Sactown Pound

Eliza Kitten became the mascot of the insurgency, and the Pound took a black cat as its official logo. She rode on the dashboard of ScottyAte's car while the group hunted for its targets, and its members treated her with an aggressive affection that contrasted sharply with their intentions toward Cat.1 Photos of her were posted to the District of Parry as taunts aimed at Cat, with captions such as “The only cat allowed in Sactown Pound” and “Eliza runs this city,” and the signs the Pound put up around Sacramento to frighten Cat were decorated with drawings of black cats.1

PoG, then one of Parry's senior staff, approved of the kitten, and General Rudolf's part in the Pound helped make her the unofficial mascot of the District of Parry.1 On 25 July 2025, when thirteen members of the Pound went to Hyperio's auto shop to offer him command of the unit, ScottyAte walked onto the shop floor holding her in his arms.9

Pet and mascot (2025–present)

Kay's evidence led to Cat's arrest in August 2025, and ScottyAte declared the end of the Pound on 20 August. Eliza Kitten was the one part of the operation that survived: ScottyAte kept her, and went on posting updates about her in Parry's chat. Once a symbol of violent defiance, she became a mundane pet.2

During the Chud War of 14 February 2026, one of the Liberators threatened on Discord to kill her and posted ScottyAte's address. He put her in his car, left her secretly at his grandmother's house, and drove off with a crowbar to look for them.3

When Part I of the Charter passed in Legislature XI in March 2026 with the votes of all fifteen representatives, ScottyAte marked it with “Eliza Kitten is purring. 🐈‍⬛ W.” and an offer:4

ScottyAte: Do you want to see my Cat?

Syn: No.

Al Commando: Yes

ScottyAte: [sends image of Eliza Kitten resting on chair]

2026 presidential election

When ScottyAte announced his campaign for president on 29 May 2026, he closed his list of promises with “Eliza Kitten endorses me.” PoG's endorsement of him the same day praised a man who “holds a creature small and fragile in his arms and he gives it the shelter,” and she signed it with a black cat.5

On 13 June Apollos sent the community a voice memo of his younger brother, who endorsed ScottyAte's opponent, Poka:6

Apollos's brother: He's pandering with the cat.

Apollos: Scotty is?

Apollos's brother: Yeah. I don't like cat supremacists.

The memo spread widely, and many members agreed that ScottyAte was using Eliza Kitten to win votes.6 In Parry, where she was the district's mascot, the Preservates Phalth and Aurase accused him of using her to pander to the “normie” voters.10 In Zarden, the smallest district, seven members voted for ScottyAte, some of them simply because they liked his cat.11

In Ninzard slang

Her name became a noun in Ninzard's slang. An “Eliza Kitten” is a chaotic, innocent variable that a group adopts purely to mock or spite an enemy.7

「Inviting his ex-girlfriend into the group chat wuz a total Eliza Kitten move.」

— example of the term in use, written in Parryan spelling
