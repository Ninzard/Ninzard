Samantha Donovan (born 2 November 2005), known as Sundaze, is a former Ninzard politician who served as the 9th District General of Main from 2 November 2022 to 3 January 2023, and as Governor of Ninzardia from 4 July 2023 until the faction collapsed on 19 August 2025. Presenting herself as an Orie, she secretly worked for President Cat, who installed her in Main after Geo's fall and in Ninzardia through a rigged election.1,2,3,4

She lost Main to Woofer after two months, was banned from Nightwork over the Giveawaygate gambling scheme, and was permanently banned from the Ninzard Project after Cat's arrest.5,6,7

General of Main (2022–2023)

On 11 October 2022, then one of Main's administrators, Sundaze wrecked the district's server on Cat's instructions, deleting almost every channel and banning almost every member.8 After Geo's removal she won the snap election of 2 November 2022, her seventeenth birthday, beating Grace by 110 votes to 57 with support from across the community, the Poopulists' included.1,2

As general she worked with Xorbit, the General of BobaBitcoin, to expand membership, and on Cat's orders endorsed Hokan and played the loyal Orie; Cat meant her eventually to lead the party.2 Her generalship was lively, but Main's older, quieter members resented the expansion, and many found her selfish and obnoxious. In January 2023 she lost to Woofer by 113 votes to 75.5

Governor of Ninzardia (2023–2025)

In June 2023 Giveawaygate exposed a gambling “giveaway” that Sundaze and Alaric had rigged with alternate accounts, and Governor Adelynn banned them both from Nightwork.6 Cat had more use for her. Nominated by Cat's Disco Party, Sundaze was elected Governor of Ninzardia on 4 July 2023, succeeding Rony, in a contest so plainly rigged that she took 444 of 495 votes overall but a single vote in Capitalia, the only district with honest results.3,9,10

With Cat she created the District of Lair and turned Ninzardia into a one-party community.11,12 On 31 May 2024 she staged a livestreamed “summit” with Adelynn, sobbing that Adelynn had broken her heart before turning to taunts, and afterwards spread a fabricated story of a secret romance between them, which the Preservates called “entirely false.”13 Cat's ledger records $54,992 paid to her, the third-largest sum.14

By July 2025 she had tired of Cat.15 Ninzardia was deleted on 19 August 2025, ending her governorship, and after Cat's arrest she was shunned by the community; she cooperated with investigators, and her case was dealt with through juvenile proceedings.4,16 On 21 February 2026 Minister of Justice Kay announced that she had been permanently banned from the Ninzard Project.7
