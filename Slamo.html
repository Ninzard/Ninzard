Marvin Andros (born 7 January 2003), known as Slamo, is a former Ninzard politician who served as the 5th Vice President of the Ninzard Project from 2 July 2018 to 17 May 2019, under President Apollos. One of Apollos's best friends, he was appointed by the Legislature in the aftermath of the deaths of 2018, on the same day it voted to keep Apollos in office without an election.1

A member of the Third Faction since 2017 who lives in Australia, Slamo helped the Capital cover the community's time zones.1 He served through the RSN War and into the Era of Progression, but the toxicity aimed at the Capital wore on him, and he resigned on 17 May 2019 at Apollos's encouragement; his predecessor, Hokan, was reinstated in his place.2 Campaigning against Apollos later that year, Quorey's supporters wrote that many considered Slamo the worst vice president, while casting him as the president's victim.3 He remained in the Original Party's inner circle, and took part in its dispute over Governor Navarch's bans in November 2020.4

Early life

Andros was born on 7 January 2003 and lives in Australia. He was invited to the Third Faction during the Golden Era in 2017, and became one of Apollos's best friends.1 Formed by Apollos and his friends, the Third Faction was the community's first break from the Ninzard–Nightwork binary: apolitical, lightly regulated and built around gaming events and Minecraft.5

Vice presidency (2018–2019)

Appointment

The vice presidency had stood empty since April 2018, when the Legislature removed Hokan over his absences. Apollos wanted an election to fill it, but put reforms first, and events soon overtook both.6 The deaths of Tiny2, leader of the breakaway Gold Road, and of Holidae left the community without direction, and Spinzle, as acting governor of Ninzardia, convened what became known as the Last Legislature to settle its affairs.7

Almost no one wanted to hold the presidential election due that summer. Rather than choose a new president, the Legislature voted on 2 July 2018 to keep Apollos in office and, at the same time, appointed his preferred pick, Slamo, as the 5th Vice President. His home in Australia was a factor in his appointment, since it helped balance the Capital across time zones. The community responded in a subdued manner: many disliked the president, but saw no alternative.1

The RSN War and the 2018 election

Slamo took office in the middle of the RSN War. Seandry's Rocket Squad Nation had declared war on 30 June 2018, two days before his appointment, and the conflict lasted until Nightwork destroyed the RSN's platforms on 7 August. The war left a more dangerous community behind it, as remote access trojans and petty hacking spread among members.8 Once it was over, the community demanded a real presidential vote, and on 12 August 2018 Apollos won 197 of 353 votes (55.8%) without campaigning.9 Slamo stayed on as his vice president.2

Resignation

From late 2018, as Governor Spikester's Era of Progression drew the Underground into the community, Apollos and his allies became frequent targets of slander.10 In May 2019 Slamo considered resigning because of the toxicity he had been enduring, but decided to stay for Apollos's sake. After he told the president how he felt about the role, Apollos encouraged him to step down, and he resigned on 17 May 2019.2

Apollos first hoped to replace him with Capital Adjudicator Raffaele and move Syn into Raffaele's post, a combination he called the “dream team,” but neither man believed it would work. Pressed by Governor Spikester, he instead reinstated Hokan, who became the 6th Vice President and the first to serve non-consecutive terms.2

Later years

Quorey's campaign

On 30 August 2019, during Quorey's campaign against Apollos for the 2020 presidential election, his supporters circulated a long attack on the president. One passage acknowledged that many considered Slamo the worst vice president in the community's history, but claimed that Apollos had used him throughout: that Slamo had wanted to resign for months, that the president had kept him in office by threatening to reveal his secrets, and that he had been made to create art for Apollos. The archive records the tract as character assassination.3

The Navarch bans

By November 2020 Slamo was part of the Orie Apparatus, the Original Party's inner circle. When Governor Navarch of Ninzardia permanently banned five trolls who had griefed the community's Minecraft server and harassed its female members, Slamo first asked why they had not simply been muted, and told the party it was taking the matter far too seriously.4

Toothpaste: that's the only punishment we could do that reu wouldn't just instantly reverse

Navarch: Reu gives them special treatment

Slamo: I mean you could always ignore it

Atria: How can you ignore it when they actively degenerate the culture?

— The Orie Apparatus, November 2020

Once Navarch's staff explained that Apollos had kept lifting the mutes, Slamo admitted he did not know why the president had done so and said he believed them, though he pointed out that they had been running Apollos down a moment earlier. Apollos pardoned the five trolls on 23 November 2020, and Navarch resigned in protest.4

The N16 Incident

In October 2022, as General Geo of Main unravelled after the N16 Incident, Lunaix said she wished Geo would resign, denounced Zylosted and the rest of BobaBase, and added that Slamo was “planning” too.11

Electoral history

Slamo never stood in a recorded election. The Legislature appointed him vice president on 2 July 2018.
