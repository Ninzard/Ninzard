Image codes

Every picture on the wiki is written as a short code instead of its link. A page asks for data-image="spinzle", and the link is looked up here, so replacing a picture means changing its link on this page only.

The pictures are kept on the site itself. Portraits go in Images/Portraits as Portrait_Name.jpg, and the pixel sprites in Images/Sprites as Name_32x40.png. To add a picture, upload it there, then add a row here with a new code (lowercase, no spaces) and the picture's link. The smaller copies the pages load are made automatically each time the site is published, so only the original is ever uploaded; sprites always load at their own size and are drawn with sharp pixels. The wiki's logo is the one exception: it is drawn by wiki.js rather than loaded as a picture.
