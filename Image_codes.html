Image codes

Every picture on the wiki is written as a short code instead of its link. A page asks for data-image="spinzle", and the link is looked up here, so replacing a picture means changing its link on this page only.

The portraits are kept on the site itself, in the Images folder. To add a picture, upload the photo to Images as Portrait_Name.jpg with its smaller copies in Images/160, Images/320 and Images/640, then add a row with a new code (lowercase, no spaces) and the photo's link. The wiki's logo is the one exception: it is drawn by wiki.js rather than loaded as a picture.
