Alessia Zorzi (born 11 September 2006), known as Poka, is a Ninzard politician serving as the 5th and current President of the Ninzard Project since 2 July 2026. A member of the Preservation Party, she previously served as the first Minister of Activity under President Apollos and as Head Moderator of Main.1

Poka joined the community at the age of ten in 2016, after her Club Penguin account was stolen, and that same afternoon helped lay the foundation of the PluPlu Army with her best friend, PluPlu.2 A protégé of Apollos, who trained her in the Theatrics of Theatrics, she became one of the loudest voices of the Capital during his second presidency.3

Nominated by the Preservates on 30 May 2026, she defeated ScottyAte of the Original Party with 75.9% of the vote, the most lopsided presidential election in Ninzard history.4 Her presidency has been defined by a light expansion of the districts, a calendar of community events, and the Bathos Address, in which she declared an end to the community's culture of mourning.5

Early life

Zorzi was born on 11 September 2006 into an Italian family and spent part of her childhood in Italy before moving to the United States. Still catching up in English and finding friendships at school exhausting, she took refuge in Club Penguin, where she kept a veteran account full of rare beta-era items, dozens of puffles and a carefully decorated igloo.2

Early Ninzard career (2016–2025)

The PluPlu Army

In late 2016 her Club Penguin account was stolen. Following the trail into a Ninzard server, the ten-year-old confronted the culprit, a young Raffaele, who confessed, apologized and took the full force of her shouting. She typed in the name Poka for the first time that day. She bonded instantly with Raffaele's cousin, PluPlu, and the foundation of the PluPlu Army was laid that afternoon.2

Raffaele went on to serve as a “Padrino” to both girls in Hybriden, teaching them the community's political landscape.6 Poka later joined the Third Faction, where Apollos, who had watched her grow up since 2017 and spent hours playing Minecraft with her during the Golden Era, promoted her to high staff roles.3

Main

By 2023 Poka was helping General Woofer shape the culture of Main, the community's largest district,7 and she served as its Head Moderator from 2024 to 2025.8 In 2024, when President Cat bought off several established Mainians to seed grievances inside the district, Poka quietly confined the compromised accounts to #general, where she and PluPlu buried their talking points under an avalanche of memes and all-caps penguin facts. When Cat messaged her directly on 16 May 2024 with a warning, Poka asked whether she knew penguins were dying of microplastics, mocked her light-up shoes, and blocked the president.9

Although she had voted for Aceiron of the Ories in December 2024 and June 2025, Poka backed Apollos's comeback in September 2025, a shift that carried real weight in Main.10

Minister of Activity (2025–2026)

Appointment

On 3 November 2025 Apollos split the Capital into three ministries and named Poka the first Minister of Activity, charged with keeping the community from dying out. Main theatrically demanded to know why he had “stolen” her, and Fogueteiro succeeded her as Head Moderator.11 Apollos instructed her to cause “mild havoc” that would draw the Ories into talking without letting drama derail the community. She was the only newcomer to the Capital during his second presidency.3

Apollos also trained her in the Theatrics of Theatrics, a self-aware meta-LARP that weaponizes absurdity and blurs sincerity with insanity. Poka mastered it almost immediately and, as an act of the craft itself, renamed it the Art of Queefmaxxing.3

The Charter and the career initiative

Before the full release of the Cat Files, the Capital walked Poka through the evidence. She cried, then helped send the files out to community leaders.12 With Minister of Health & Privacy 7orB she launched the Ninzard Career Initiative, which she staffed with mock interviews and peer encouragement.13

Ahead of the Charter referendum she spoke one-on-one with every Mainian she could while feeding meme after meme to the PluPlu Army, driving massive support for the Charter in Main.14 After Apollos signed the Charter over pineapple pizza, her joking call to impeach him inspired Representative Ploutos to file articles “for crimes against humanity.” Eight representatives voted yes; Poka's verdict was “LMAOOOOOOOO.”15

Feud with PoG

In spring 2026 Poka became the Capital's sharpest voice against General PoG of Parry, a leading Orie. She answered PoG's breathy French voice memos with a furious Italian tirade, and on 25 April branded her “Cat 2.0 but with a baguette,” a phrase that spread across the community and that Apollos himself later quoted on camera.16

2026 presidential election

Nomination

Apollos hoped to be succeeded by either Woofer or Poka.17 When PoG backed ScottyAte and the Ories named Hackman as his running mate, General Shuffle argued that a bored Ninzard would vote for a circus, and that Poka was “the healthy circus.” After a call with Apollos, Hackman agreed to stand on both tickets, and on 30 May 2026 the senior leaders of the Preservates unanimously nominated Poka with Hackman as her running mate. Her reaction: “Nah we are so cooked 😭”18

Results

Voting began on 21 June 2026. Poka won 616 of 812 votes (75.9%) to ScottyAte's 196 (24.1%), carrying every district except Volley: 92.7% in Main, 92.3% in Waffle, 80.7% in Scope, 74.1% in Zarden and an upset 56.2% in Parry, where the Parry Girls saw her as the spiritual successor of Apollos, their “Vascular King.”4

The Holy Roma Address

On 26 June 2026 the president-elect released a voice memo now known as the Holy Roma Address. It is regarded as the cultural closure to the trauma of the Catx3 Regime and the factionalism of the Chud War.19

Joy is not weakness. Laughter is not weakness! To laugh is the ultimate act of defiance against this world that wants to discourage you!

— President-elect Poka, the Holy Roma Address, 26 June 2026

Presidency (2026–present)

Transition and cabinet

In the final week of June, Poka met Apollos in person for the Succession Accords, an informal handover held during the 2026 FIFA World Cup. Between matches and a hike, they reviewed every server and how the newly ratified Charter would be enforced.19

Poka was inaugurated on 2 July 2026, the tenth anniversary of Apollos's first inauguration, with Hackman as the 11th Vice President. She named Raffaele Minister of Justice, Katdsm Minister of Health & Privacy and PluPlu her successor as Minister of Activity, drawing jokes about “Bellucci nepotism,” and reaffirmed Apollos's nomination of Meikaqu as GoA. Legislature XII seated eleven Preservates, one independent and no Ories.20

Light expansion and events

In her first month she pushed a “light expansion” of every district; by August 2026 the Ninzard Project had 837 members. Almost everyone supported it, though Representative Syn accused her of “dismantling a secure perimeter.”21 She hosted movie nights, marathons and gaming nights, often pouring in so much effort that she burned herself out.22

The Bathos Address

On 20 August 2026, the first anniversary of Cat's fall, the community began to reminisce about what it had survived. Poka refused to join in. Her Bathos Address rejected the expectation that members be “indestructible warriors” and called instead for a community that is silly, mundane and fully alive.5

There's truly nothing like it cause outta every miserable timeline we could have locked into, we chose… THIS ONE.

— President Poka, the Bathos Address, 20 August 2026

The Salon War

When Parry descended into a three-way war in August 2026, much of the Preservate leadership urged the Capital to step in. Poka overruled them all: “PARRY IS LITERALLY SUPPOSED TO BE OUT OF CONTROL… IO PUNTO TUTTO SU KAIA!!!!” (I'm betting everything on Kaia). She instead planned a strategy with General Kaimachi and Meikaqu, and Kaimachi won the district over on her own terms. Meikaqu's assessment named Poka's “real work” as “refusing to help.”23

Political style

Vice President Hackman describes the “Poka Preservates” as heirs to Apollos's Pragma Flank who use bathos to achieve the same results while keeping the ethics of the Logos tradition. Her Capital has declined the surveillance and private interrogations that threats from the Black Sun Fraternity seemed to invite.24 Her Christian faith runs through her addresses, which often close in prayer.25 The Ories cast themselves as the grounded opposition to an idealistic president presiding over the “LARPy” culture she inherited.26
