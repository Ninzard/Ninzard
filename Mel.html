Melody McCluskey (born 1 January 2005), known as Mel, is a former Ninzard politician who twice served as General of Volley for the Original Party, from 17 March to 9 July 2022 and from 1 January 2025 until her recall on 13 November 2025.1,2,3 Living in Pennsylvania, she stood apart from the Californians at the heart of the community.4,5

A Paleorie administrator in the Orie Apparatus and an outspoken defender of Apollos, she was one of the few Nightians Governor Clexas let into Ninzardia, and built a following of her own, MelMcCore, in imitation of Helen's HelenKore, sparking a rivalry between the two.6,7,8 She won Volley in a disputed snap election in 2022, but lost it within four months after it emerged that she had given the troll who wiped the district his permissions.1,9,10 She returned as general in 2025, succeeding AJ, and Volley was the only district to back Aceiron for president that September. That November a recorded tirade over her mass ban of thirty members led Volley to remove her by 100 votes to 23, and weeks later Aceiron cut her from the party.2,11,3,12

Early life

McCluskey was born on 1 January 2005 and lives in Pennsylvania. She is the younger sister of Malcolm McCluskey, and was known in Ninzard chiefly as his sister when she first rose to prominence.1 Much of the community lived on the West Coast, with its core in California, where many members knew one another offline; on the other side of the country, Mel came to Ninzard as an outsider to those circles.4,5

Early career (2020–2022)

Nightwork and the Ories

Mel began in Nightwork, and joined the Original Party in November 2020.13 Within days the party's inner circle, the Orie Apparatus, was arguing over Governor Navarch of Ninzardia, who had banned five trolls for harassing him, his girlfriend and his deputy, Clexas, only for Apollos to unmute them. Mel sided with the president, telling the party it was taking the matter too seriously and that “it's fucking Discord.” Navarch answered that her not caring did not mean no one else did, and Clexas rebuked her for backing Apollos when the trolls had not targeted her, pointing out that she was a Nightian who had held Orie membership for only a few days.13

Do not cry at me. It's not THAT big of a deal.

— Mel, to Clexas and Navarch, November 2020

Aceiron, then a fellow Orie, threw the line straight back at her. When Apollos pardoned the trolls on 23 November, Navarch resigned, and Clexas won the snap election to succeed him.13

Ninzardia and Governor Clexas

As the 7th Governor of Ninzardia, Clexas distanced Ninzardia from the Nightian Faction, whose quarrels she blamed for its falling activity, and admitted Nightians to its servers only with her administration's approval. Mel was one of the few let in, with Cubi and Atria.6 She became an administrator of BobaBase, Ninzardia's third district, as well as of BobaBitcoin and Nightwork-Volley, and when Governor Syn demanded that she stick to one district, BobaBase's members told him to mind his own business, since he was not even in Ninzardia.7

Mel and Clexas stood on opposite sides of the party. The governor led its moderate, establishment wing, which wanted rules against trolling and slurs, and her administration went as far as banning the word “poo” in Ninzardia. Mel belonged to the Paleories, the populist wing around Apollos that wanted the party to return to its Tomato Party roots and argued that such bans only made martyrs of the muted.6,7 Clexas did not seek re-election in August 2021, and Ninzardia passed to Rony of the Poo Party.14,15

Paleorie

As a constant defender of the president, Mel enjoyed the Capital's protection for her outlandish statements. She spread conspiracy theories and declared her opposition to vaccines, and on 15 June 2021 falsely claimed that Deputy Governor Cyntaku had voted for Joe Biden, then insulted her looks when Cyntaku replied that she had been too young to vote.7 That summer she endorsed AJ, the Orie challenger to General Yunin of Volley, and caused outrage with a racial slur that she defended as free speech; Yunin was re-elected.7,5

MelMcCore

Mel courted HelenKore, Vice President Helen's devoted following, and won Helen's support. She then built a following of her own in its image, MelMcCore, which drew many of HelenKore's members away and set off a rivalry between the two women.7,8

In August 2021 Governor Syn revoked her permissions in BobaBitcoin and Volley after she ignored his warnings about posts of herself that critics feared exposed her, at sixteen, to predatory attention.7 Soon afterwards older members of Volley who lived in Pennsylvania tracked down her workplace and harassed and filmed her there. She lost her job, and blamed the Syn Administration for the men who had found her; Syn called her “full of shit.”5 Looking back in 2026, the archive acknowledged that the community had laughed at her rather than hold those men to account.16

Feud with Helen

Nominated by the Ories against General Yunin in January 2022, Mel insisted she had “correctly” predicted that he was secretly working for the Preservates, and lost by a relatively narrow margin.17,8 After Helen lost the vice presidency in December 2021, Mel said she had been “never that competent to begin with,” hoping to bring the rest of HelenKore over to MelMcCore. Instead friends turned against her, Helen accused her of stealing her support, and the community dismissed the feud as a petty “mean girls” conflict.8

First generalship of Volley (2022)

Election

When Yunin resigned in March 2022, the Ories nominated Mel again, against Governor Cyntaku's hand-picked candidate, Molly. Cyntaku put the snap election to a Nightwork-wide vote, which ended in a 155–155 tie on 16 March. After Volley, the other generals and the Capital objected to Cyntaku's attempt to seat Molly, Mel took office as the 7th General of Volley on 17 March.1,18,19 Cat sent her a small gift, worth $38, which Mel accepted; Cat's ledger records $201 more that she refused.18,20

In office

With General Aurase she opposed Cyntaku's Electoral Reforms Act, arguing that its voting PINs made voting less accessible and spoiled the community's “fun.”21 In April she was among the leaders Apollos gathered to negotiate an end to the Four Core Issues.22

OneAgainstAll

Mel promoted Snuck to administrator because she found him funny, and on 5 May 2022, as “OneAgainstAll,” he banned the whole district and deleted every channel. She claimed she had “no idea” how he had got the permissions and dismissed Cyntaku's plan to rebuild Volley as a stunt to repair the governor's ratings, and Governor Rony of Ninzardia publicly took her side.9 On 8 May Cyntaku revealed that Mel herself had given OneAgainstAll his permissions. Mel then said she had “forgot,” and Volleyans turned on her.9

Defeat

Three trolls, Rickard, Gale and Shuffle, who found her performative and annoying, set out to make Molly general instead, and Mel wrongly believed she still had the troll vote.23 In July 2022 she lost Volley to Molly with 11 of 45 votes (24.4%), and left office on 9 July. Voters saw her as entitled and had not forgotten her lie about OneAgainstAll, the Preservates condemned her “irresponsible management” of the district, and her defeat frustrated the Orie establishment.10,24 Hokan responded by calling Volley's voters “vicious dopamine addicts.”10

Between generalships (2022–2024)

The Ories put Mel up for a rematch in January 2023, and Molly beat her again, by 57 votes to 10.25 Volley then swung back to the Ories under AJ, who unseated Molly in December 2023 and, as general through 2024, moved the district from the Preservates to the Original Party.26,27 When AJ became Aceiron's running mate for Governor of Nightwork, Mel won his open seat in December 2024 with 61 of 118 votes (51.7%), keeping Volley with the Ories.27,2

Second generalship of Volley (2025)

Under Governor Aceiron

Mel took office as the 10th General of Volley on 1 January 2025, the day Aceiron became Governor of Nightwork with AJ as his deputy.2 In June she was re-elected with 73 of 115 votes (63.5%), the only time she ever won re-election.28 By the presidential election of September 2025 Volley had become a right-wing stronghold, and it was the only district Aceiron carried, by 81 votes to 44.11 After Apollos won nonetheless, Mel defied Aceiron's demand that she obstruct the rebuilding of the district servers.29

Under President Apollos

Mel did not get along with the new president, but Capital Adjudicator 7orB persuaded her and General Rudolf to ease tensions, and the five generals at times worked together, though the Ories opposed any community-wide curfew.30,31 When two Volley members were hacked, she took up a suggestion from PluPlu and Poka that a former friend of Apollos was responsible and began spreading conspiracy theories, to their amusement.32 Following Aceiron's lead, she publicly asked whether the president was mentally sound.33

Meltdown and recall

On 8 November 2025, when Generals Woofer, Ixan and Grace announced they would close their districts for Thanksgiving, Mel declared that Volley would “never shut down for such an incompetently authoritarian reason.” Al Commando mocked her, Mel called him out, and members across the community, encouraged by Grace, switched off their invisible status to mock her too; at its peak a quarter of the community took part. When she claimed to be receiving hundreds of abusive messages, Apollos asked, “Which one, Mel?”3

As a third of Volley demanded she step down, some of the messages turned threatening and obscene. Mel, who had usually dealt with such abuse through timed mutes and had let her opponents face the same treatment, banned thirty members at once. Accused of hypocrisy and overreaction, she faced calls for impeachment from all sides.3 On 11 November she refused, in a call with Apollos and AJ, to accept that she had overreacted; AJ came out for impeachment, and when Spinzle joined, the president and vice president recorded the end of the call.3

I just think it's so fucking stupid how it's structured! Like, there is no fucking constitution, OKAY? LIKE, HOW IS THAT EVEN IMPEACHABLE WHEN I WAS BEING FUCKING HARASSED?

— General Mel, 11 November 2025

The video, which ended with Mel calling the pair “losers” and leaving, was received as an antagonist's rant. On 13 November 123 Volleyans voted in the recall, and 100 of them (81.3%) voted to remove her. Aceiron refused to endorse or defend her.3 Cyntaku succeeded her after defeating NGin in the snap election.34

Later activity (2025–2026)

Mel was among the Ories, disillusioned with the party's brand, whose defections contributed to Cyntaku's win over NGin.34 In December she endorsed AJ's bid to win Volley back from Cyntaku, an endorsement that hurt his campaign.35 Apollos later mocked her line that “there literally is no constitution” in a broadcast arguing that the community needed the Charter as a leash on its own leaders.36 As a moderator in Volley during NGin's speech of 25 February 2026, she shouted at the audience to let him finish.37 Counted among the Orie figures lacking the charisma to run for president in 2026, she and AJ endorsed ScottyAte, helping unite the party's Rationalist voters in Volley behind him.38,39

Relations with the Ories

The Orie Apparatus

Mel's career ran through the Orie Apparatus, the party's inner circle. She entered it in November 2020 as a newcomer from Nightwork defending Apollos against its moderates, and rose in 2021 as one of the Paleorie administrators, with General Snuck and Arcus.13,7 By early 2022 she was counted among the establishment Ories, with Helen, Aeromage, Snuck, Advy and Ploutos, who were incensed at Apollos for backing the Poopulist Snowby against his own vice president.40 The party put her forward for Volley five times between January 2022 and December 2024.8,1,10,25,2 Under Aceiron, who became the party's Boss in June 2024, she belonged to his “alt-lite” coalition as one of its ordinary ideological conservatives, until he cut her from the party.41,12

Aceiron

Aceiron mocked Mel as early as November 2020.13 In 2025 she governed Volley under him, and her district gave him his only win in the presidential election, but after his defeat she refused his order to obstruct the new servers.29,11 She copied his insinuation that Apollos was mentally unwell, yet when she faced recall he declined to stand by her.33,3 On 4 December 2025 he purged the Orie Apparatus and cut her from the party, saying her “hysteria” validated the Preservate view of the Ories as unstable children, and cited her “self-pitying rant” about harassment as proof of what “Rationalism” had brought the party.12 Syn argued that she and Boygho had broken with Aceiron because his volatility endangered their platforms, not out of any change of heart.42

AJ

Mel's career was bound up with AJ's. She endorsed him for Volley in 2021, and in 2025 inherited the Orie base he had rebuilt in the district as general through 2024.7,27,2 Cyntaku, who later held the seat, wrote that under Molly, AJ and Mel, Volley had “curdled” into a haven for people who thought Aceiron too soft.43 When AJ heard Mel out with Apollos on 11 November 2025, he lost sympathy for her despite their shared party and backed her impeachment.3 She endorsed his comeback in December all the same, to his cost, and in 2026 the two led Volley's centre-right Rationalist voters behind ScottyAte.35,39

Political style

Mel presented herself as a defender of free speech and an enemy of regulation, and the archive groups her with the Ories' ordinary ideological conservatives.7,41 Volleyans found her entitled and annoying, and the trolls who backed Molly described her stupidity as performative.10,23 Cyntaku, her longtime opponent, called her “a creature of attention.”43
