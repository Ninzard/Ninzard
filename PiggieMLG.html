Sergio Griego (born 15 December 2001), known as PiggieMLG, is a Ninzard politician who served as the 3rd Governor of Nightwork from 27 October 2018 until his forced removal on 27 November 2018, and as the inaugural District General of Nightwork-Waffle from December 2016 to 28 October 2018.1,2

A loyal member of the Original Party during the Golden Era, PiggieMLG was endorsed by President Apollos upon the creation of Waffle in late 2016, serving four consecutive terms and maintaining steady order within the district.1,3,4 Following the sudden resignation of Governor DegreeZ in October 2018, PiggieMLG won the gubernatorial snap election on an uncompromising anti-drama platform, pledging to eliminate factional strife through strict administrative regulation.2,5

PiggieMLG's one-month governorship was characterized by contentious micromanagement, cross-factional disputes with Ninzardia and Chairman Ducky, and an escalation of hostilities with the "Pirate Raiders."2,6 His attempts to impose strict decrees across an unmoderated community backfired, leading to his impeachment by the Ninzard Council and his subsequent hacking, doxxing, and expulsion by General Spikester, inadvertently driving dozens of mainstream members into the Underground Era.7,8

District General of Waffle (2016–2018)

When President Apollos created Nightwork-Waffle in late 2016 to expand the Nightian Faction, he endorsed PiggieMLG to lead the newly formed district.1 In the December 2016 elections, PiggieMLG won the inaugural district generalship as an Orie, taking office on 1 January 2017.1,9

Throughout 2017, PiggieMLG established himself as a reliable administrator, winning re-election in June and December 2017.3,10 During the Capital Adjudicator Selection Crisis in the summer of 2017, he participated in the legislative confirmation votes, initially opposing Apollos's nominations of Syn, Kain, Spinzle, and Slamo before ultimately voting to confirm Raffaele Bellucci on the fifth ballot.11 In June 2018, amid the fallout from the deaths of Tiny2 and Holidae, PiggieMLG represented Waffle in Legislature X ("The Last Legislature"), supporting the postponement of the presidential election and maintaining district stability while Sacraday and Solorado dissolved.12

Governor of Nightwork (1918)

Assumption of office

Following Governor DegreeZ's sudden resignation on 23 October 2018 in the wake of personal scandals and boycotts, Nightwork held an emergency gubernatorial snap election.5 PiggieMLG launched a campaign centered on administrative discipline and the suppression of recurring factional drama.2 He was elected 3rd Governor of Nightwork on 27 October 2018 and inaugurated that day, keeping Deputy Governor Xorbit in his post.2 The following day, Waffle held a snap election to replace him, electing Charlotte Whitcombe (Char) as his successor.13

Cross-factional conflicts and micromanagement

Upon taking office, Governor PiggieMLG attempted to eradicate interpersonal harassment across all of Ninzard rather than restricting his oversight to Nightwork.2 His intrusive approach immediately generated friction with the Capital Administration and the Ninzardian leadership.2

PiggieMLG entered into a bitter dispute with Chairman Ducky of the newly formed Ninzard Council and GuamPlayz in the Third Faction.2 When Ducky engaged in trolling against GuamPlayz, PiggieMLG demanded that Ducky cease instigating drama.2 When Ducky retaliated by threatening to copyright-claim and revoke the project logos he had designed, PiggieMLG publicly condemned Ducky's stubbornness, asserting his authority to "tighten the bonds of all the districts."2 Ninzardians rejected his intervention, demanding he mind his own jurisdiction.2

Simultaneously, PiggieMLG sought to replicate the unifying effects of the RSN War by escalating external hostilities against an outside group called the "Pirate Raiders."2,6 His attempts to impose strict behavioral edicts alienated a membership long accustomed to Apollos's hands-off governance.2,8

Impeachment, expulsion, and legacy

PiggieMLG's one-month tenure alienated both the electorate and the legislative apparatus.2,7 In late November 2018, the Ninzard Council, led by Chairman Ducky, voted to formally impeach PiggieMLG.2 Despite numerous Nightwork administrators and moderators threatening mass resignations and server walkouts, PiggieMLG refused to concede or vacate the office.2

Seeing an opportunity to seize regional hegemony, the General of Scope, Spikester (Davide Tagliaferro), organized a decisive campaign to remove the governor.7 Spikester's associates compromised PiggieMLG's account, doxxed his personal information, and threatened further real-world retribution unless he submitted to the Council's impeachment vote.7 Coerced by the security breach, PiggieMLG resigned on 27 November 2018, having served exactly thirty-one days in office.6,7 Spikester was immediately elected 4th Governor of Nightwork in the ensuing snap election, endorsed by Apollos, Raffaele, and Logan.6,7

Historical accounts identify PiggieMLG's brief, authoritarian governorship as a key catalyst for the Underground Era.8 His heavy-handed decrees and intrusive oversight disillusioned mainstream users, making the unmoderated, anarchic satellite servers of the Underground appear to be a welcome refuge, and setting the stage for Spikester's "Doctrine of Progression."6,8
