Mary Carmichael (née Abernethy; born 12 July 2003), known as Snowby, is a former Ninzard politician who served as the 8th Vice President of the Ninzard Project from 25 December 2021 to 4 July 2023, under Presidents Apollos and Cat. One of the two leaders of the Poopulist movement, she had earlier served as Deputy Governor of Ninzardia under Meikaqu in 2017.1,2,3

A Coloradan member of Solorado and of Spinzle's circle of friends, she rose to prominence in 2017 as one half of the “genki girl duo” with Meikaqu.2,4 Quietly backed by Apollos, she ran for the Poo Party in 2021 as a dark-horse candidate and unseated Vice President Helen.1 She defeated Helen again in July 2022, and in January 2023 was re-elected over Xorbit with 601 votes, the largest tally anyone in the Project had received to that point.5,6

Out of public view, Cat drew Snowby and her husband, Pieban, into financial dependence and then into laundering money for her, and Snowby passed her word of Apollos's secret plan to resign in 2022.7,8 She was the largest recipient in Cat's ledger. In December 2024 the family fled Colorado, and the Cat Files later reclassified her as a hostage of Cat's operations rather than an accomplice.9,10,11

Early life

Abernethy was born on 12 July 2003 and grew up in Colorado. She is the younger sister of Abbyndria “Abby” Abernethy, the 2014 presidential candidate and later General of Hybriden, from whom she became estranged.2,12,13 She belonged to Solorado's original cohort and to the Edith Gang, the circle of real-life friends around Spinzle that also included Aurase, Pieban, Soda, Kendall and Atria.4

Ninzardia (2017–2018)

Deputy Governor

In March 2017 Meikaqu, the Original Party's nominee to succeed Governor Gabriel, chose Snowby as her running mate against the Resistance Party's Daniel. Snowby was vibrant, outgoing and skilled at winning support, and the ticket won; Meikaqu became the 2nd Governor of Ninzardia, with Snowby as her deputy. The two, close friends online with many admirers, were dubbed the “genki girl duo,” and their leadership drew a new base of female members to the community.2

As deputy governor Snowby sat in Legislature IX, which spent the summer of 2017 rejecting President Apollos's nominees for Capital Adjudicator. She and Meikaqu were the only legislators to vote for all five of them, from Syn to Raffaele, who finally took office on 26 August.12,14

The 2017 governorship

With Meikaqu declining to seek re-election, Snowby announced her intention to run for the Original Party's nomination for governor in September 2017. When General Judah entered the race with Snuck's cousin Arcus as his running mate, she chose not to fight for the nomination, partly out of concern for Meikaqu, to the disappointment of many Ories. She had fallen out with the Resistance Party's nominee, Holidae, a real-life friend, and helped the Ories rally against her, but Holidae won in a landslide after Apollos endorsed her. Around the same time Snowby began dating Pieban.15

Grief

The deaths of Holidae and Tiny2 in June 2018 devastated the Coloradan members who had known Holidae. Snowby was overwhelmed by guilt and fell into a depression, during which Pieban was her only support.16

Vice presidency (2021–2023)

2021 election

After treatment for depression, Snowby had regained her energy by 2021 and returned to the community alongside Spinzle in Scope, where her magnetic energy and creative impulses made her popular with Ories and anti-establishment voters. After his break with Vice President Helen that autumn, Apollos secretly encouraged Snowby to run against her for the Poo Party. Working with Pieban and her friends, he helped her reach a large base of voters while holding back his own criticism so that she could chip away at Helen's support first. Meikaqu returned from years of quiet to help the campaign.17

The race had been expected to be a dull rematch between Helen and the Preservation Party's Grace, but by December Snowby had become a genuine threat, and Apollos openly endorsed her. The vote was delayed by a week after Snowby was banned from Discord earlier in the month and Grace fell ill. Held on 24 December 2021 amid widespread fatigue with the establishment, it gave Snowby the vice presidency; Grace barely passed 20%. She took office the next day as the 8th Vice President.1 Unknown to the candidates, Cat had been using bribes to spread slander and memes against Helen and Grace in support of Snowby's campaign.18

The genki girl queen

Snowby became, with Governor Rony, one of the two heads of the Poopulists, and was soon known as the “genki girl queen,” unapologetically edgy, offensive and cheerful. She memed, trolled and mocked the establishment. A Republican, she aired controversial views to provoke left-wing members and test the Preservates, and her rhetoric drew anti-establishment members on the right, some of them from the far-right fringe, to the Poo Party.1 She promoted a radical freedom-based ideology with no restrictions on what she called “petty stuff,” slurs included, and mocked the Preservates as “grassless losers.” Apollos, who found the Poo Party amusing, asked her to tone down her rhetoric, which she did, though a little late.1

She owned Apolleon, the main news platform read by Ninzardians, to which Cat gained access through Raffaele.19 In January 2022 she and Rony endorsed the Poopulist Runix for Governor of Nightwork.20 When Helen wrecked four districts at the end of that month and claimed to have been hacked, Snowby and Apollos initially believed her.21 In March 2022 Snowby called General Geo a “manchild” over his ban on the word “Geodude” in Nightwork-Main.22 Much of the Preservation Party despised her, the Gang of Five and the Poopulists alike,23 and she was among the most prominent voices of the far-right rhetoric that spread through Nightwork in 2022.24

2022 election

In July 2022 Helen, now an independent, challenged Snowby in a rematch. Of 556 votes, Snowby won 343 (61.7%) to Helen's 153 (27.5%), ending Helen's political career.5 That month her real-life friend Spinzle became Governor of Nightwork.25

Apollos's resignation

When Apollos decided to resign during the presidential election of July 2022, the only people he told were Snowby and Spinzle. Snowby pressed him for his real reasons, and he admitted that he felt unable to control his emotions and blamed Ninzard for it; she told him she did not accept that he was “fundamentally cursed.”8 By then she was deeply isolated and financially dependent on Cat, and dreaded governing alone or alongside someone as chaotic as Al Commando. She passed the plan to Cat, whom she saw as the only administrator capable of filling the void, not realizing what she was handing over.8 Apollos resigned moments before the polls closed on 19 July, and Cat, the runner-up, became president.26

Under Cat

Snowby stayed on as Cat's vice president and remained close to her, part of the inner circle in which Cat boasted of her schemes.27,28 In October 2022 she spread Al Commando's mocking video of General Geo's rant across the community's news platforms, taunting Geo as she did.29 In January 2023 she actively supported Rony, then a close friend, in his campaign for re-election as Governor of Ninzardia.30

In January 2023, running for the Disco Party as the Poo Party came to an end, she defeated Xorbit with 601 of 806 votes (74.6%), carrying every district. It was the largest raw vote total anyone in the Ninzard Project had received to that point, and she outperformed Cat, who beat former Vice President Hokan on the same day, by eight points, which irritated the president.6

Her friendship with Rony broke down in early 2023. An argument on 12 February ended with Snowby being removed from BobaBase, and the feud escalated into insults on both sides; she and Kendall spread false accusations about him, and by April the two were no longer on good terms.31 Now a mother, Snowby did not seek re-election, and Meikaqu succeeded her as the 9th Vice President on 4 July 2023.3

Cat's hostage (2022–2025)

Financial dependence

In early 2022 Snowby and Pieban faced a severe financial crisis, and she had returned to Ninzard partly as an escape. Cat, who had been monitoring her conversations, approached her in June 2022 under the guise of nostalgia and offered her a generously paid job as a “community consultant” for Catsland. Snowby married Pieban on 29 July 2022. That September Cat drew Pieban into a ticket-reselling scheme run through Colorado shell companies with names such as “Solorado Logistics,” whose bank accounts were in Snowby's name, and began mixing the proceeds of her other crimes into it.7

Struggling with debt and early parenthood, the couple turned to Spinzle, but she was absorbed in governing Nightwork and answered with cold, practical advice. Cat filled the gap, sending gifts, listening to Snowby's worries and casting herself as a generous aunt to the family; Snowby, who was estranged from her own sister, related to Cat's difficult relationship with hers.13,32 By July 2023 Pieban realized that the ticket business was a front for laundering Cat's money, but by then it paid for everything the family had.13 Cat's ledger records $96,789 in payments accepted by Snowby between March 2022 and April 2024, more than anyone else, and $5,193 that she refused.9

The break with Spinzle

In October 2024 Cat assaulted Snowby at her home in Colorado.10 Weeks later, fearing for her friend, whose name was on Cat's shell companies, Spinzle set up her Dead Man's Switch, and Cat forced her to stop it by threatening to ruin Snowby.33 Cat then leaked selected audio of Spinzle to persuade Snowby that she wanted her jailed. When Spinzle urged her to cut her ties with Cat, Snowby turned on her instead, refusing to leave the operation she saw as her only guarantee of safety.34

You don't care if I go to jail! You just want to beat her!

— Snowby, to Spinzle, 19 November 2024

The next day the Edith Gang shunned Spinzle, who left the community soon after.34 Over the following weeks Snowby's health deteriorated, and Pieban kept her away from the Ninzard chats.35

Escape

On 13 December 2024, after Meikaqu blinded Cat's monitoring of their accounts for a day, Snowby, Pieban and their two children fled Colorado for the Midwest, leaving their phones and computers behind.10

Aftermath (2025–2026)

When Cat fell in August 2025, Snowby was named as the largest beneficiary of her funding.36 Many members harassed the couple online, and before the Cat Files were released most believed they deserved to lose their children.37 Fearing exactly that, they drove back to Colorado on 28 August 2025 to confront Spinzle and demand that she delete the ledgers implicating them. She refused, but promised that the account given to law enforcement would stress that they had acted under duress, and the couple hired a criminal defence lawyer.38 Snowby and Meikaqu considered suing Apollos and the others preparing the files, which helped delay their release.39

Charged with money laundering in the District of Colorado, Snowby and Pieban argued successfully that they had acted under duress, citing credible threats against their children and the assault on Snowby. They accepted a plea deal of five years' probation, community service and the forfeiture of everything they had gained from the scheme, which left them bankrupt, and kept custody of their children under the supervision of social services.40 The Cat Files, released in January 2026, reclassified her from a willing accomplice to a hostage, trapped like Meikaqu in a cycle of financial dependence and blackmail.11

Political style

Snowby's appeal rested on her energy and humour; voters weary of the establishment saw her as a return to fun, and Meikaqu called her “a breath of fresh air.”1 Her opponents saw something else. Cyntaku, Governor of Nightwork for much of 2022, later wrote that the Poopulists' jokes hid a real threat to the community's safety measures:41

The Poo Party was not funny. That is what nobody understood. To the average member, it was just Rony and Snowby making jokes and posting memes.

— Cyntaku, recalling 2022
