Faith Fournier (born 20 February 2002), known as Phalth and as the Parry Admin, is a Canadian Ninzard administrator and Preservate who serves on the staff of the District of Parry. One of the first members of Nightwork to be invited online rather than through real-life friendships, she is one of the community's OGs. She had never held office in the Ninzard Project and was a moderator of Parry when President Apollos endorsed her for general in the snap election of October 2025, in which she finished second to Boygho with 43 of 130 votes.1,2

Under General Kaimachi, Phalth and her friend Elle, the district's Orie administrator, are the most senior members of the Parry Staff, and both enjoy high trust across the district; their partnership is credited with helping to build a bipartisan environment in a district where most voters are Ories.3 Phalth was also the Preservate Apparatus's District Coordinator for Parry from February to April 2026, and stood unsuccessfully for Parry's seat in the Legislature in March 2026.4,5

Early life and Nightwork

Fournier was born on 20 February 2002 and lives in Quebec. Nightwork, the community Elusion founded on 25 April 2014, drew its first members from his circle in Sacramento, many of whom he recruited in person in the city's parks.6,7 Phalth was one of the first members to be invited online instead, with no ties to those real-life networks.

When Nightwork merged with the Ninzard Project in July 2015 it was split into three legions, one of them Parry.8 Phalth made her home there, and for years held no position of power, belonging to the Preservation Party in a district dominated by the Original Party.1,3 In June 2024 she was among the Parryans in one of the district's late-night voice calls with Elle, Boygho and Kain.9 By October 2025 she was serving as a moderator of Parry.1

Parry snap election (2025)

On 22 October 2025 Parry's General, Rudolf, who had embraced Holocaust denial and aligned himself with NGin, lost his Discord account, leaving the district without a functioning leader. The next day Apollos called a referendum on removing him and endorsed Phalth as his successor.1 Parryans voted 97 to 21 to remove Rudolf on 26 October. Boygho, an administrator from the Original Party's Rationalist Flank, was the clear favorite in the snap election that followed, and Kain, angry that Apollos had called him unfit to lead and refused to endorse him, entered the race as an independent to spite the president, turning it into a three-way contest.2

During the two days without a general, Aceiron came into Parry's voice channels at 3 a.m. to campaign for Boygho, and Kain dropped in to scream at him. Kain had administrator permissions, so no one could mute him, and everyone left the call except Phalth and one other member. As the two men traded insults, Phalth answered with a sad-trombone sound effect and then spammed the soundboard; the other member's 28-second recording of the argument became famous.10

On 28 October Boygho won with 58 votes (44.6%), Phalth took 43 (33.1%) and Kain 29 (22.3%). She congratulated Boygho and wished him luck, while Preservates complained that Kain had sabotaged the campaign.2 Some in the party read the result as a good sign, since Boygho had fallen well short of a majority; others worried that her third of the vote showed fatigue with Preservate leadership in an Orie district. Apollos agreed with the second reading and pushed the party toward a new nominee in Parry, Burnette.3

Preservate Apparatus (2025–2026)

Phalth took part in the debates the Preservate Apparatus held that winter. During one of them, on 13 December 2025, she asked whether the Original Party ever held debates of its own.11

Grace: Edith should have an answer. I haven't been in that party for five years.

Phalth: I completely forgot you were once a member of the Original Party.

Grace: IIRC, the Preservates were not in existence until October 2020. I co-founded the party with Madie and Noah.

Phalth: It's been a long time. Wow. I'm old.

Four days later, when Adelynn joined a debate with Spinzle by voice recording, Phalth exclaimed, “I've never heard your voice before,” and Adelynn closed her next argument with “Bonjour.”12 When the Apparatus debated how to answer the “Parry Girl” phenomenon at the end of January 2026, Phalth summed it up in a single word: “Limerence.”13

When the Apparatus was expanded on 24 February 2026, with Adelynn as its Boss, Phalth became the District Coordinator for Parry, one of five liaisons between the party's leadership and the districts, with Burnette and Kaimachi as Parry's party operatives. Like Adelynn, she was counted with the Logos Flank, which held that the Charter should rest on coherent principles rather than be tuned to win over its opponents.4

In the snap election for Parry's seat in Legislature XI at the end of March 2026, held under approval voting, Phalth won the approval of 62 of 122 voters (50.8%), finishing third behind Kaimachi, who took the seat with 74, and Burnette, with 65, and ahead of the Orie BadSpirit.5 When Woofer, who had succeeded Adelynn as Boss, restructured the Apparatus on 2 April, Kaimachi became Parry's coordinator.14 After the Charter passed on the night of 7 May, Phalth celebrated in the Apparatus channel with a single word: “OUI!!!!”15

In the 2026 presidential election Phalth and Aurase accused ScottyAte, the Original Party's nominee, of using his cat, a mascot in Parry, to pander to the district's “normie” voters.16

Parry Staff

Parry's electorate leans heavily toward the Original Party.3 On the district's staff Phalth has worked closely with her friend Elle, an Orie who has been one of its administrators since 2024. Since Kaimachi became General in July 2026, the two have been the most senior members of the Parry Staff, and both enjoy high trust across the district.17 The partnership of a Preservate and an Orie in the district's day-to-day running helped to build a bipartisan environment there.

Personal life

Phalth lives in Quebec. She is not one of the Parry Girls, the loud and devoted circle of Parryan women who carried Kaimachi to the generalship, but she is friends with many of them.17 She is also a friend of Elle, the district's “Miss Protestant,” and enjoys watching her debate.
