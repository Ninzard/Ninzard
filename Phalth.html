Phalth, known as the Parry Admin, is a Ninzard administrator and Preservate who serves on the staff of the District of Parry. A veteran Parryan who had never held office in the Ninzard Project, Phalth was a moderator of the district when President Apollos endorsed the Preservate for general in the snap election of October 2025, in which Phalth finished second to Boygho with 43 of 130 votes.1,2

As an administrator of Parry, Phalth has worked closely with Elle, the district's Orie administrator, and is among the most trusted figures in the district, credited with helping to build a bipartisan environment in a district where most voters are Ories.3 Phalth was also the Preservate Apparatus's District Coordinator for Parry from February to April 2026, and stood unsuccessfully for Parry's seat in the Legislature in March 2026.4,5

Early years

Phalth is one of the community's OGs, its long-standing members, but for years held no position of power, belonging to the Preservation Party while making a home in Parry, a district dominated by the Original Party.1,3 In June 2024 Phalth was among the Parryans in one of the district's late-night voice calls with Elle, Boygho and Kain.6 By October 2025 Phalth was serving as a moderator of Parry.1

Parry snap election (2025)

On 22 October 2025 Parry's General, Rudolf, who had embraced Holocaust denial and aligned himself with NGin, lost his Discord account, leaving the district without a functioning leader. The next day Apollos called a referendum on removing him and endorsed Phalth as his successor.1 Parryans voted 97 to 21 to remove Rudolf on 26 October. Boygho, an administrator from the Original Party's Rationalist Flank, was the clear favorite in the snap election that followed, and Kain, angry that Apollos had called him unfit to lead and refused to endorse him, entered the race as an independent to spite the president, turning it into a three-way contest.2

During the two days without a general, Aceiron came into Parry's voice channels at 3 a.m. to campaign for Boygho, and Kain dropped in to scream at him. Kain had administrator permissions, so no one could mute him, and everyone left the call except Phalth and one other member. As the two men traded insults, Phalth answered with a sad-trombone sound effect and then spammed the soundboard; the other member's 28-second recording of the argument became famous.7

On 28 October Boygho won with 58 votes (44.6%), Phalth took 43 (33.1%) and Kain 29 (22.3%). Phalth congratulated Boygho and wished him luck, while Preservates complained that Kain had sabotaged the campaign.2 Some in the party read the result as a good sign, since Boygho had fallen well short of a majority; others worried that Phalth's third of the vote showed fatigue with Preservate leadership in an Orie district. Apollos agreed with the second reading and pushed the party toward a new nominee in Parry, Burnette.3

Preservate Apparatus (2025–2026)

Phalth took part in the debates the Preservate Apparatus held that winter. During one of them, on 13 December 2025, Phalth asked whether the Original Party ever held debates of its own.8

Grace: Edith should have an answer. I haven't been in that party for five years.

Phalth: I completely forgot you were once a member of the Original Party.

Grace: IIRC, the Preservates were not in existence until October 2020. I co-founded the party with Madie and Noah.

Phalth: It's been a long time. Wow. I'm old.

Four days later, when Adelynn joined a debate with Spinzle by voice recording, Phalth exclaimed, “I've never heard your voice before,” and Adelynn closed her next argument with “Bonjour.”9 When the Apparatus debated how to answer the “Parry Girl” phenomenon at the end of January 2026, Phalth summed it up in a single word: “Limerence.”10

When the Apparatus was expanded on 24 February 2026, with Adelynn as its Boss, Phalth became the District Coordinator for Parry, one of five liaisons between the party's leadership and the districts, with Burnette and Kaimachi as Parry's party operatives. Like Adelynn, Phalth was counted with the Logos Flank, which held that the Charter should rest on coherent principles rather than be tuned to win over its opponents.4

In the snap election for Parry's seat in Legislature XI at the end of March 2026, held under approval voting, Phalth won the approval of 62 of 122 voters (50.8%), finishing third behind Kaimachi, who took the seat with 74, and Burnette, with 65, and ahead of the Orie BadSpirit.5 When Woofer, who had succeeded Adelynn as Boss, restructured the Apparatus on 2 April, Kaimachi became Parry's coordinator.11 After the Charter passed on the night of 7 May, Phalth celebrated in the Apparatus channel with a single word: “OUI!!!!”12

In the 2026 presidential election Phalth and Aurase accused ScottyAte, the Original Party's nominee, of using his cat, a mascot in Parry, to pander to the district's “normie” voters.13

Administration of Parry

Parry's electorate leans heavily toward the Original Party.3 On the district's staff, Phalth has worked closely with Elle, an Orie who has been one of its administrators since 2024. The partnership of a Preservate and an Orie in the district's day-to-day running made Phalth one of the most trusted figures in Parry, and the two helped to build a bipartisan environment there.
