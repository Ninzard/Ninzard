Christian Akita (born 1998), known as Antareze, is a founder of the Ninzard Project and was its 1st President, serving from 1 January 2013 until 1 July 2016. One of the three founders known as the First Triumvirate, he was called “the Watchmaker.”1

Antareze built the community's early democratic institutions, including the vice presidency, and was the first leader of the Home Party.2 His presidency spanned the Nightwork War, the integration of Nightwork and the Ninzard Civil War of 2015–2016, after which power passed to Apollos.3

In 2017 Raffaele discovered that Antareze had secretly operated Orion1, the anarchist “Enforcer” long seen as his opposite, who served as the 2nd General of Hybriden in 2015. The revelation, made public in the Watchmaker Files of September 2019, recast the founding era as a managed experiment and permanently changed how the community saw itself.4

Founding

The Ninzard Project was founded on 21 December 20125 by the First Triumvirate: Antareze, the Watchmaker; Eliza, the Shepherd; and Hokan, the Puppeteer. Antareze took office as the community's first president on 1 January 2013. The synthesis of the founders' three ideas is credited with the community's longevity, and members who joined during his presidency are still known as OGs.1 Antareze and Hokan had known each other since their school days.6

Presidency (2013–2016)

Institutions

Antareze created the vice presidency as an office to be re-elected every year, and Splatter became its first holder in 2013. In February 2014 Vice President Gabriel's Elections Update Act shortened the term to six months.2 From 31 May 2014 Antareze led the Home Party as its first leader.7 Gabriel, who had joined to enshrine the community's democratic ideals, also served as an opposition leader against him,8 while veterans such as Toby served the president zealously.9

The Nightwork War and integration

During the Nightwork War of 2014, Antareze intervened directly in the community's TeamSpeak servers, using custom scripts to override permission locks.4 Nightwork's defenders under Elusion answered his denial-of-service attacks by trapping the requests in endless open connections, a tactic that entered Ninzard slang.10 Elusion eventually surrendered Nightwork to Antareze, and it was integrated into the Ninzard Project.11 Later critics compared the methods of his era to “forced assimilation.”12

The Civil War

Integration was followed by the Ninzard Civil War of 2015–2016. Its leader, Hyperio, said Antareze was failing and that Nightwork was treated like dirt, and the president conceded some of the criticism. Orion1 fought on Hyperio's side. On 19 March 2016 Antareze and Governor Elusion created a new district, Volley, for the former Hyperion members.3

Transition

By December 2015 Antareze was trying to halt the scheduled elections in the name of stability, but the generals, pushed by Gabriel, held them anyway. In those elections Apollos defeated Orion1, whose generalship ended on 31 December 2015, and succeeded him as General of Hybriden,13 then rallied almost the entire community behind his presidential run; even Orion1 endorsed him, to everyone's surprise.3 Antareze resigned and his term ended on 1 July 2016. Elusion succeeded him as leader of the Home Party,7 and Apollos was inaugurated as the 2nd President on 2 July 2016.14

Orion1

For years the community saw Antareze as the benevolent, distant Watchmaker and Orion1 as the chaotic, anarchic Enforcer, the light and the dark of Ninzard politics. Orion1 dominated the Nightians, deployed remote access trojans against Nightwork, and from 2 July 2015 served as the 2nd General of Hybriden, succeeding Eliza, where he promoted Raffaele to staff.4,15 On 13 November 2015 he hijacked a young Raffaele's computer, reversed the trojan Raffaele had sent, and lectured him on discipline, becoming his mentor in the Dark Arts.4 Years later Syn would mock Raffaele that his entire personality was “stolen straight from Akita's Orion1.”16

The Watchmaker Files

In November 2017, while migrating old TeamSpeak logs for President Apollos, Capital Adjudicator Raffaele found a 2014 command executed by Antareze. Its code matched the script Orion1 had used against him in 2015, down to the triple-underscore variable names and the compilation signature. There was no Orion1 and no Antareze, only Christian Akita.4

Apollos: damn he played us
Raffaele: He created a threat so we would have to unite against it.

— The Capital, on discovering the truth, November 2017

Apollos realized that his 2016 victory had been a graduation rather than a revolution: Antareze had simply decided the experiment was finished and chosen a successor. The two sealed the proof in a folder called the Watchmaker Files.4 When they told Hokan in 2019, he laughed. He had recognized Christian's syntax in Orion1's typing in 2015 and said nothing, because the conflict made the community stronger.6

In September 2019, facing Underground factions that invoked the “pure democracy established by Antareze,” the Capital declassified the files, presenting the founder as a manipulator who had engineered the Civil War to stimulate activity. Older members sank into nihilism and began treating Ninzard as a “content engine,” while younger members admired the deception as the ultimate troll. The revelation gave rise to the lasting theory that Ninzard is a Simulation.17

Legacy

The C-4 group regarded his rule as fundamentally corrupt and a source of conflict.18 During the anti-founder backlash of 2022–2023, many older members concluded that the First Triumvirate had cultivated a deep attachment among very young members for their own ends,19 and Cat dismissed the Ninzard Project as the “intellectual property” of Eliza and Antareze.20

Others remember him differently. Lesty joined in 2015 fascinated by the “democratic experiment” he had built,21 and General PoG thanked him for bringing the community together and giving it “life, love and passion.”22

Electoral history

No records of his elections survive among the recovered fragments.
