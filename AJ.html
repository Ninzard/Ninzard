Andre Jenkins (born 25 September 2003), known as AJ, is a Ninzard politician who served as the 8th Deputy Governor of Nightwork under Governor Aceiron, from 1 January 2025 until the office was abolished in September 2025.1,2 A member since 2014 from Sacramento, he was twice General of Volley for the Original Party, briefly in 2018–2019 and again through 2024, when he turned the district from the Preservates to the Ories.3,4,5,6

The best friend and twice the running mate of Aceiron, he belonged to the manosphere wing of his coalition, and in 2025 shared his defeat by Apollos and Spinzle.7,8,9 He broke with the Calebros in March 2026 after being accused of leaking Aceiron's address, and endured repeated racist abuse from NGin's Holy Iron Flank.10,11 Having apologized to the Preservates for his conduct, he was among the thirty guests at the Holloway Summit of May 2026.12,13

Early life

Jenkins was born on 25 September 2003. He grew up in Sacramento, in the same neighbourhood as Bray and Hyperio, and is African-American.3,14 He joined the Ninzard Project in late 2014, during the era of the Expansions Act, and was recruited into Sacramento's Sacranight Legion, known as “The Jungle.” Short in stature, he made up for it by playing a “street soldier” for the neighbourhood's rejects, helping coordinate real-life harassment of the “cool kids” in Sacraday; he saw Ninzard as an extension of the Sacramento streets and a place to assert himself.3

First generalship of Volley (2018–2019)

AJ was an early ally of Governor Spikester and served under him as his “Heavy,” backing his “Doctrine of Progression,” the belief that Ninzard had been at its best as an unregulated, expanding “Garrison State.” When enemy communities infiltrated Volley, he and his supporters dug up humiliating information on the infiltrators and counter-raided their voice calls.3

In Spikester's snap elections of 10 December 2018 he was the Orie nominee for Volley against General Clayton, whom Apollos had expelled from the party and who now ran for the Celestial Party as the favourite. AJ won by a slim margin and took office the next day as the district's 3rd general.3 He and General Whyskitter then invited new members en masse, and the backlash against the sudden elections forced fresh ones on 13–14 January 2019. AJ lost Volley to Cyntaku, an independent, and left office on 15 January.4,15 Cyntaku later remembered him as “fine” and “nice enough,” but part of a machine she ran against on a platform of “leave me alone.”16

Out of office (2019–2023)

In 2021 the Paleorie administrator Mel endorsed AJ, an Orie, to challenge General Yunin in Volley that August, in terms that caused outrage; Yunin was re-elected.17,18 By the summer of 2022, as women held the vice presidency, two successive Nightian governorships and several generalships, AJ was among the men, with Boygho and Rudolf, who began to question female leadership.19 Cat's ledger lists him among those who refused all of her money, turning down $357.20

Second generalship of Volley (2024)

In December 2023 AJ unseated the Preservate General Molly by 51 votes to 49, and on 1 January 2024 returned to power in Volley after five years.5 His upset was one of the few bright spots for Ories grown nihilistic under President Cat, and in June 2024 he was re-elected with 66 of 102 votes (64.7%).21,22 His Rationalist policies, set against what Volleyans saw as Molly's poor management, moved the district from the Preservates to the Original Party, though some were surprised by how far it shifted toward conservatism and the far right, and networks linked to the Sonnenrad Society began to coordinate within it.6

When AJ said he supported Donald Trump in the 2024 United States presidential election, the community's left taunted him for “selling out,” and he traded insults with his own members.21 Apollos later attacked Aceiron for doing nothing to stop Rudolf's Parryans from abusing AJ with racism.14

Deputy Governor of Nightwork (2025)

In 2024 the Ories nominated Aceiron, General of Scope, for Governor of Nightwork, with AJ as his running mate.6,23 The ticket won in December with 372 of 684 votes, defeating Governor Adelynn and Deputy Governor Grace. On New Year's Eve Adelynn spent an hour with the two in a cordial handover, and on 1 January 2025 AJ took office as the 8th Deputy Governor, while Mel succeeded him in Volley.1,2

That year AJ was one of the two dozen members of the Sactown Pound, a vigilante cell that spent a month hunting Cat and Meikaqu in Sacramento, where he was counted among its more “reasonable” voices.24,25 In the presidential election that followed Cat's fall, he was again Aceiron's running mate, recording campaign ads with him when his friend's spirits failed; the ticket lost to Apollos and Spinzle in September 2025, and Nightwork's offices were abolished as the new Capital rebuilt the community's servers.7,26,27

Opposition (2025–2026)

Mel and the Volley campaign

The archive counts AJ, with Rudolf, among the “redpill bros” of Aceiron's coalition.8 On 11 November 2025, after Mel's mass ban in Volley, he joined Apollos on a call with her, but lost sympathy when she refused to admit she had overreacted, and, despite their shared party, came out for her impeachment.28

When Aceiron purged the Orie Apparatus in December, he endorsed AJ, “his former Deputy Governor and his best friend,” to win Volley back from Cyntaku as a candidate of the Liberator–Orie Coalition, whose secondary leadership AJ joined.9,29 NGin then endorsed Cyntaku in openly racist terms. AJ called the endorsement racist, but when Cyntaku muted NGin for it he condemned the mute as a threat to free speech.30 He lost with 43 of 129 votes (33.3%), hurt by Mel's endorsement; his vote was what remained of the Orie base he had built in 2023–24.31 In January 2026, as the community reeled from the full release of the Cat Files, he apologized to Cyntaku and the Preservates for his toxic and demeaning behaviour.12

The Chud War and the Charter

On 14 February 2026, during the Chud War, AJ was among the Ories besieged with Aceiron in his house when NGin's followers gathered outside. At Aceiron's request he stepped out with Rhydeu and ScottyAte to keep the Capital from calling the police, and the Iron Flankers shouted racial slurs at him.10 Afterwards he worked with HazyDaze, Lyre and ScottyAte to make the Ories more reasonable, and when the Holy Iron Flank tried to seize the party, he helped re-establish the Orie Apparatus in a new server.32,33

Elected to Legislature XI for Volley in the snap elections, AJ took his seat on 28 February 2026.34 In the debate on the Charter he argued for letting each district govern itself, and with Raffaele and ScottyAte persuaded Aceiron to vote for the revised text.35 He condemned Snuck's use of an AI clone of Aceiron's voice as identity theft, and called BadSpirit's assault on Apollos “cowardly.”34,36

The Trvthnvkers

After Aceiron was attacked in March 2026, his brother Joshua accused AJ of leaking his address. AJ denied it, cut his ties with the Calebros, and took the party's “normie” conservatives and Rationalists into a splinter group, the Trvthnvkers. An attempted alliance with the Holy Iron Flank collapsed when its members met him with racist abuse in a voice call, and a feud of voice memos with NGin followed on 28–29 March.11 On 30 March AJ resigned his seat, and Lyman succeeded him.37,38

Later life (2026)

On 2 May 2026 AJ arrived with Channeling-4 at General Kay's home for the Holloway Summit, one of thirty guests drawn from every faction. When NGin turned on the guests with racist abuse, AJ set down his controller and shook his head rather than fight, and later, as Malachi Holloway spoke, he stood with tears running down his face.39,40,41,13 He spoke at the community's memorial for Soda.42 In the 2026 presidential election he and Mel endorsed ScottyAte, helping unite Volley's Rationalist Ories behind the party's ticket.43 By August 2026 he was one of the friends Poka counted as her Capital.44
