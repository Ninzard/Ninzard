The Original Party (OP), whose adherents are commonly known as Ories, is the oldest surviving political party in the Ninzard Project. Founded on 1 April 2014 as the Tomato Party by Kain and Apollos, it was restructured and rebranded in April 2015 as the populist vehicle of Apollos, who served as its Boss for seven years.1,2 Under his leadership, the Ories dominated community politics throughout the "Golden Era" following his historic presidential victory in July 2016.3 Since late 2020, the Original Party has served as one of the community's two primary political poles, locked in a bitter ideological rivalry with the Preservation Party.4

Historically, the Ories have prioritized community activity over administrative regulation, unfiltered free expression over moderation, and district autonomy over centralized control from the Capital, encapsulated by their informal doctrine: "It's activity, stupid."5 Following Apollos’s break with his own party establishment in 2021, the party entered a protracted series of crises, including the catastrophic electoral collapse of Hokan in January 2023, an aggressive, manosphere-populist takeover under Caleb Jensen (2024–2025), an attempted far-right coup known as the "Samson Switch" in February 2026, and an ideological pivot under its current Boss, Power of Greed.6,7,8 Following the ratification of the Ninzard Charter and the June 2026 general elections, the party was completely swept out of institutional power, holding zero seats in Legislature XII.9,10

History

Foundations and the Tomato Party (2014–2015)

In early 2014, growing polarization over Vice President Kain’s anti-establishment policies (such as the deletion of the ID database and the Expansions Act) triggered the formation of the community's first organized political parties1 On 1 April 2014—Kain’s 12th birthday—Kain and Apollos founded the informal Tomato Party.1 The moniker was adopted from the theatrical practice of throwing rotten tomatoes at stage performers as a form of heckling and mockery, embodying the faction's contempt for the older founding elite of Ninzard.1 Kain assumed the title of 1st Boss, while Apollos served as its primary propagandist.1 In December 2014, Danbi was elected General of Capitalia under the Tomato banner, becoming the party's first elected officeholder.11

Following the first anniversary of the party in April 2015, Kain moved to dissolve the faction to focus on Club Korea (CK) in Los Angeles.2 Apollos vehemently opposed the dissolution. On his 11th birthday, 4 April 2015, Kain formally ceded full leadership of the party to Apollos, who became the 2nd Boss.2 Together with Danbi, Kain, and defecting Home Party General Meikaqu, Apollos restructured the faction into the Original Party.2 The name reflected a desire to strip away bureaucratic statecraft and return Ninzard to its "original" purpose as an uninhibited, enjoyable digital playground.2 Supporters adopted the duck (🦆) as their mascot due to the phonetic similarity between "Ories" and the Korean word for duck (ori / 오리).2

The Golden Era and Hegemony (2015–2018)

In December 2015, Apollos achieved his first major executive victory by winning the Generalship of Hybriden against Orion1 (President Antareze in disguise).12 In the 2016 presidential election, Apollos capitalized on public fatigue with the Ninzard Civil War, scandals surrounding Breakout Party leader Bray, and the perceived elitism of Resistance Party leader Gabriel to score a monumental upset victory, becoming the 2nd President of the Ninzard Project.3

The Ories subsequently entered an era of total political and cultural hegemony known as the "Golden Era."13 The party favored minarchist, hands-off governance: legion/district generals were granted absolute autonomy, member caps were abolished, and the formal Legislature was largely sidelined in favor of gaming tournaments and Minecraft servers.13 In the June 2017 elections, the Ories achieved the largest single-party landslide in community history, capturing both governorships (Meikaqu in Ninzardia, DegreeZ in Nightwork), the Capital Administration (Vice President Hokan and Capital Adjudicator MMC), and almost every legislative and general seat.14

This hegemony fractured in May and June 2018 during the collapse of Gold Road, an anti-Orie breakaway server led by Tiny2. Following the sudden deaths of Tiny2 (from sickle cell anemia) and his partner Holidae (by suicide), the community plunged into a severe crisis of legitimacy.15 Critics blamed Apollos's hands-off tolerance of harassment for enabling the tragedies.15 Despite the fallout and the ensuing RSN War, Apollos was re-elected in August 2018 with 55.8% of the vote, largely due to a lack of viable opposition.16

The Underground Era and Progression (2018–2020)

Between late 2018 and early 2020, the Orie Party became tied to the "Doctrine of Progression," an aggressive expansionist strategy spearheaded by Nightwork Governor Spikester (Davide Tagliaferro) and covertly organized by Vice President Hokan.17 The party co-opted lawless satellite servers, integrating doxers, scammers, and Remote Access Trojan (RAT) operators into the official architecture to inflate activity metrics.17 Membership ballooned to nearly 1,400 unvetted users.18

By late 2019, the extreme criminal toxicity of the Underground forced Apollos and Adjudicator Raffaele Bellucci to reverse course. In the August 2020 presidential election, Apollos campaigned on the total annihilation of the Underground, defeating Revamp Party candidate Czulia in a 60.3% landslide.19 The subsequent Underground Cleanup Initiative (UCI) purged over 500 accounts and dismantled the OuterBand Faction.20

The Great Polarization and Establishment Collapse (2020–2023)

The aftermath of the UCI sparked the formal establishment of the Preservation Party (Preservates) on 1 October 2020, led by Syn, Cyntaku, and Grace Coolahan, who sought an indefinite border closure and total isolationism.4 This marked the beginning of Ninzard's permanent two-party era.4

Within the Original Party, a severe schism erupted between the "Establishment Ories" (who sought compromises with the Preservates and supported institutional rules) and the radical, populist "Paleories" (led by Kain, Mel, and Brutus, who rejected all moderation).21 In 2021, Apollos openly broke with his own party establishment. Infuriated by the moralizing elitism of Orie Vice President Helen and Ninzardian Governor Clexas, Apollos endorsed the emergent "Poo Party" (Poopulists), backing Snowby for Vice President in December 2021 against Helen.22

On 3 May 2022, Apollos formally stepped down as Boss of the Original Party, refusing to lead a fractured apparatus.23 On 13 May 2022, Hokan was selected as the 3rd Boss.23 While Orie General Spinzle captured the Nightwork Governorship in July 2022 in an Orie-Poopulist Coalition (OPC) landslide, Apollos abdicated the presidency days later, handing power to runner-up Catx3 of the Disco Party.24

Hokan sought to use the Original Party to challenge President Cat, mounting an ill-fated campaign in the January 2023 snap presidential election.25 Cat, who had covertly funded Hokan’s running mate Adam Kim (Xorbit) and exposed Hokan's private financial dependencies, crushed the Ories at the polls (Cat 66.6%, Hokan 33.4%).25 Disgraced and financially ruined by the FTX collapse, Hokan permanently left the community, ending the old Orie establishment.25 To prevent the party's dissolution, Raffaele Bellucci assumed leadership as 4th Boss on 3 January 2023.26

The Calebro Era and Nexit (2024–2025)

Under Raffaele, the Ories struggled to counter the moral technocracy of Preservate Governor Adelynn Leclaire (2023–2024) while quietly resisting Cat's criminal operations from Hybriden.27 In June 2024, Scope General Caleb Jensen leveraged a leaked recording of Kain to capture the Orie Boss-ship, ousting Raffaele.28

Caleb transformed the Original Party into the "Calebro" movement: an aggressive, redpill-aligned, anti-establishment faction that channeled deep male grievance against Ninzard's female-dominated leadership (the "Matriarchy").29 Caleb weaponized the "Caleb Treatment"—a practice of drawing political opponents into voice calls to subject them to severe, demoralizing personal degradation.29 In December 2024, Caleb defeated Adelynn (54.4% to 45.6%) for Governor of Nightwork, campaigning on "Nexit"—the total severance of Nightwork from Cat's corrupt Ninzardian Faction.30 Caleb executed Nexit on 1 January 2025.30

Caleb was re-elected in June 2025 against Matcha (61.1% to 38.9%).31 However, his coalition was heavily contaminated by an influx of far-right extremists from the Sonnenrad Society and the Holy Iron Flank (HIF), led by Ethan O'Neal (NGin).31

The Fall of Caleb and the Samson Switch (2025–2026)

Following the arrest of Catx3 in August 2025, Caleb declared himself de facto President and scheduled a September 2025 presidential election.32 In a shocking move, the Preservation Party nominated former President Apollos, who ran as a reformist Preservate on a platform of community healing and the "Controlled Burn."33 Sapped by personal scandals, a physical assault by Cat, and a publicized mental health breakdown ("The Fuck Is Wrong With Y'all" incident), Caleb was crushed by Apollos (62.2% to 37.8%).34

Caleb’s defeat shattered the party into two warring factions: the "Rationalists" (led by Boygho, Rudolf, and Danbi, who prioritized activity metrics and stability) and the "Holy Iron Flank" (led by NGin, who viewed the party as a racialist vanguard).35 In the December 2025 general elections, the Preservates scored an unprecedented 70.7% landslide, prompting the Orie Apparatus to force Caleb’s resignation as Boss on 22 December 2025.36 Danbi was appointed interim 6th Boss.36

On 25 February 2026, NGin attempted a hostile coup of the Orie Apparatus known as the Samson Switch.8 Utilizing compromised webhooks provided by Scopian traitor Soda, NGin threatened to trigger an automated flood of dark-web cartel gore into public channels if the Ories did not surrender to HIF control.8 Raffaele and Minister of Justice Kay outmaneuvered NGin by rotating the API tokens, dumping the payload into an isolated honeypot.8 The Orie leadership simultaneously migrated the authentic party apparatus to a new server, leaving NGin ruling an empty shell.8

Following the failed coup and the dissolution of the far-left Liberation Party in the wake of the Chud War, Parry General Power of Greed (Anaïs Lefebvre) stepped into the vacuum.8 On 22 February 2026, PoG was confirmed as the 7th Boss of the Original Party, bringing the former Liberators into the party to form the Romantic Flank.8

Charter Ratification and "Groundedness" (2026)

Between March and May 2026, the Ories under PoG, Caleb, and AJ actively negotiated the drafting of the Ninzard Charter with the Apollos II Capital.37 The Ories successfully fought to enshrine Section 3: District Autonomy and Section 4: Protection from Arbitrary Exile into Part I, preventing the Capital from micromanaging local district culture.37 On 11 May 2026, the Charter was ratified with an 82% community supermajority.38

Despite their constitutional victory, the Ories faced total electoral annihilation in the June 2026 summer elections.9,10 In the presidential race, Orie candidate Scott Dellinger (ScottyAte) was defeated in a historic 75.9% to 24.1% landslide by Preservate Minister Poka.39 In the district general elections, the Preservates swept all five districts, with Kaimachi famously unseating Orie Boss Power of Greed in Parry by two votes.40 In Legislature XII, the party secured 0 out of 12 seats.10

Following this total shutout, the Ories abandoned high-stakes political roleplay. By August 2026, the party converged on the philosophy of "Groundedness": an explicit rejection of Ninzard's self-important "nation-state" LARP, viewing the community simply as a collection of Discord servers and emphasizing real-world brotherhood, face-to-face meetups, and personal well-being over digital drama.41

Ideology and Factions

Core Principles

  • Activity Before Regulation: Often phrased as "It's activity, stupid," the founding premise of the party holds that digital communities die of stagnation and boredom, not conflict. Conflict, drama, and competition are seen as the natural "social metabolism" of the community.5,37
  • Anti-Bureaucracy & Anti-Constitutionalism: From its inception, the party fiercely rejected written constitutions, formal legalism, and administrative red tape, viewing them as tools employed by insecure elites to police culture and stifle fun.1,2
  • District Autonomy: The principle of local subsidiarity—enshrined in Section 3 of the 2026 Charter—stating that the tone, culture, and moderation of an individual district are the exclusive purview of its District General, free from Capital interference.37
  • Free Expression: Uncompromising defense of offensive, raw, and transgressive speech, rejecting corporate Terms of Service sanitization or "safe space" moderation.29,37
  • Groundedness (2026–present): The post-Charter doctrine emphasizing individual dignity, real-world connection, and physical actualization over obsessive digital lore and political roleplay.41
