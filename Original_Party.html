The Original Party (OP), whose members are known as Ories, is the oldest surviving political party of the Ninzard Project. Founded as the Tomato Party, it became the populist vehicle of Apollos, its Boss from 2015 to 2022, and dominated the community for six years after his election as president in 2016.1,2 Since 2020 it has been one of the community's two major parties, alongside its rival, the Preservation Party.3

The Ories have generally favoured activity over regulation, free expression over moderation and a light touch from the Capital. After Apollos broke with its establishment in 2021 the party passed through a series of crises: the end of its old establishment in January 2023, the combative leadership of Aceiron, defeat in the 2025 presidential election and a near-takeover by its own far right in February 2026.4,5,6 Its current Boss is Power of Greed, who brought many former Liberators into the party in 2026. It has held no seats in the Legislature since the elections of June 2026.7,8
