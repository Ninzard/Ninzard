Davide Tagliaferro (born 3 January 2001), known as Spikester, is a Ninzard politician and convicted felon who served as the 4th Governor of Nightwork from 27 November 2018 until his impeachment and arrest in late February 2020, and as District General of Nightwork-Scope from June to December 2018.1,2,3

The eldest brother of the influential Sacramento Tagliaferro family, Spikester entered high-level administration during the controversial June 2018 "Tone-Deaf Election," partnering with co-founder Isaiah Choi (Hokan) to seize control of Scope.4 After orchestrating the ouster of Governor PiggieMLG, Spikester ascended to the governorship and instituted the "Doctrine of Progression," an aggressive policy that integrated external satellite servers into Nightwork and established a digital hegemony over the Ninzard Underground.1,5

Behind his public persona as a cybersecurity administrator, Spikester operated an illicit prescription drug distribution network across the server to service his family's severe medical debts.1 His administration transformed Nightwork into a militarized "garrison state" marked by proxy wars, Remote Access Trojan (RAT) proliferation, and a bitter feud with General Syn.1,6 After falling victim to extortion by his client Quorey and facing investigation by Capital Adjudicator Raffaele Bellucci, Spikester was betrayed by his younger brother Ploutos and Seandry, culminating in his impeachment, RAT infiltration, and federal arrest in February 2020.3,7,8

Early life and family background

Davide Tagliaferro was born on 3 January 2001 into an Italian-American family in Sacramento, California.9 He was the elder brother of Niccolò Tagliaferro (Ploutos) and a cousin of Paolo Tagliaferro ("Tag") and Giada Tagliaferro (Jordan / OffBeat).9 During the pre-Ninzard Braykout era, Davide observed Paolo's logistical hustling, adopting his methods of securing imported goods, video game hardware, and niche Japanese media.9

In early 2018, his father suffered a catastrophic, debilitating stroke.1 Faced with over $50,000 in out-of-pocket medical debt and the imminent threat of home foreclosure, his mother worked double shifts while Davide took a job at an electronics repair store alongside Paolo.1 Realizing standard wages could not prevent financial ruin, Spikester established a discreet drug-routing network across Ninzard's Underground infrastructure, distributing Adderall and Vyvanse to college and high school students.1 He adhered to strict personal boundaries: handling only study aids, operating strictly through encrypted communications and cryptocurrency escrow, and keeping Niccolò entirely insulated from the operation.1

District General of Scope (2018)

Following the suicide of Holidae and the banning of General Alaric in June 2018, Scope entered an administrative vacuum.4 Former Vice President Hokan approached Davide, promising him an administrative platform in exchange for advancing Hokan's political agenda.4 Recognizing that Scope could provide infrastructure and digital capital for his real-world operations, Spikester agreed.4

Supported by Hokan and various "Dark Arts" operators who dismantled Alaric's network, Spikester won the June 2018 Scope snap election as an independent.4 His election, held amid community-wide grief, was widely condemned as the "Tone-Deaf Election."4 In July 2018, he represented Scope in Legislature X ("The Last Legislature") during the RSN War.10

Governor of Nightwork (2018–2020)

Ascent and the Doctrine of Progression

When Governor PiggieMLG was impeached by the Ninzard Council in late November 2018, Spikester mobilized his network.11 His supporters hacked PiggieMLG's accounts and doxxed his identity, forcing his resignation on 27 November 2018.1,11 Endorsed by President Apollos and Adjudicator Raffaele, Spikester was elected the 4th Governor of Nightwork.1,11

Spikester instituted the "Doctrine of Progression," declaring that Nightwork's long-term survival required the mass reintegration of the Underground.1 He granted amnesty and leadership ranks to known scammers, doxers, and RAT operators in exchange for their technical loyalty, integrating their infrastructure into the state.1 Though he falsely claimed to have single-handedly crushed the Pirate Raiders, the technical cyberattacks were conducted covertly by Hokan.1

Administrative turbulence and snap elections

Seeking to install loyalists across the faction, Spikester called unannounced snap elections on 10 December 2018.12 The arbitrary scheduling sparked immense unrest.13 In the January 2019 re-vote, his loyalist General Whyskitter was defeated in Main by Syn, while Cyntaku won Volley.13

Syn became Spikester's fiercest opponent, famously denouncing his expansionist policies as "the long con" and warning that the governor was commercializing the community and inviting cultural ruin.6 Spikester dismissed Syn as an emotionally unstable hypocrite.6 Despite fierce domestic pushback, Spikester was comfortably re-elected Governor of Nightwork in June 2019.14

Hegemony and the Quorey conflict

By mid-2019, Ninzard reached peak expansion, encompassing an estimated 1,300 to 1,400 members across its federated districts and the OuterBand.15 Spikester succeeded in paying off his family's $50,000 debt; however, he found himself trapped in his operation.16

His largest customer, Corey Walker (Quorey), an erratic sociopath leading the Celestial Party, became addicted to high doses of Adderall.16 Quorey began withholding payments, demanding larger shipments, and threatening the Tagliaferro family with violence, boasting of real-world gang connections.16,17 Operating as "Deadaxz" within the OuterBand, Quorey initiated proxy wars against Nightwork.17 Realizing Quorey represented an existential danger, Spikester brought the matter before the Ninzard Council, demanding his elimination.17 Quorey was eventually arrested in late 2019 on federal charges following an undercover sting operation.18

Impeachment, downfall, and legal consequences (2020)

Following a mysterious two-week absence in December 2019, Spikester narrowly won re-election.19 However, Capital Adjudicator Raffaele Bellucci uncovered indisputable evidence of Spikester's drug distribution ring.8 Disgusted by the corruption, Raffaele mobilized the community against him.8

On 22 February 2020, the Ninzard Council voted to impeach Spikester.3 Vice President Hokan declared his administration illegitimate, and Nightians staged widespread walkouts.3 To secure legal evidence, Seandry coordinated with Spikester's younger brother, Ploutos.3 Posing as a friendly contact, Seandry sent Spikester a malicious RAT payload, extracting his digital ledgers, chat logs, and delivery schedules.3

The evidence was transferred to the police.3 In late February 2020, law enforcement raided the Tagliaferro home in Sacramento.3,7 As officers handcuffed him, Davide looked at Niccolò and screamed in anguish: "Niccolò?! You did this?! How can you do this to your brother?!"3

Following his arrest, federal authorities executed civil asset forfeiture proceedings.7 Cash reserves, electronics, and vehicles bought with drug proceeds—including a computer given to Niccolò—were confiscated.7 While the original medical debt remained cleared, legal defense fees and the loss of Davide's income destabilized the household anew.7 Davide accepted a plea deal in early 2021, receiving an 18-month sentence, serving part in custody and the remainder under electronic home monitoring.7 He remained on probation until 2025.7

Legacy and later years

Spikester is remembered as the architect of Ninzard's Underground Era.1 His "Doctrine of Progression" dismantled the foundational firewall between the public server and the criminal internet, fostering a culture of RATs, scams, and commercialization that took years to expunge.1,15 His commercial networks were later exploited by Catherine Avalon (Cat) during her early financial hustles.20

Following the expiration of his probation in 2025, Spikester returned to Scope as an elder statesman.21 During the September 2025 presidential election, he surprised the community by endorsing Apollos and Spinzle, acknowledging that the restoration of institutional order was necessary to prevent another generation from repeating his mistakes.21
