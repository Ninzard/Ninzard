Rickard Bergman (born 19 November 2005), known in Ninzard simply as Rickard, is a Ninzard troll and politician who represented the District of Main in Legislature XI, which drafted the Charter of the Ninzard Project, from December 2025 until he lost his seat in June 2026.1,2,3 Elected for the Preservation Party, he serves in its Apparatus as a party operative for Main.4

Long one of the community's most notorious trolls, Rickard was the face of the “Saltz,” Volley's trio of trolls with Gale and Shuffle, and ran the alternate account “Buttersaltz.”5,6 In 2022 the trio helped turn Volley against its general, Mel, and in May 2023 he was banned from the district after an account he had vouched for revealed itself as Cat.7,8,9 A longtime Orie, he broke with the Original Party in late 2025 and helped swing the troll vote to the Preservates.6 In the Legislature he was known for trolling rather than legislating, and with Gale he wrote the parody song “Goonstitution,” which Apollos crumpled on camera before signing the Charter.10,2,11,12

The Saltz (2022–2023)

The trio

By 2022 Rickard was a member of Volley and the face of a trio of trolls known as “ReiBertAni” or “the Saltz,” completed by his right-hand man Gale and by Shuffle, who had bonded with him over the same video games.7,5 He operated the notorious “Buttersaltz” account, known for lengthy “hornyposts” and “schizoposts,” and was a leading ragebaiter in voice calls. He was widely seen as “malicious,” in part because the people he baited spread lies about him. Gale and Shuffle knew each other in person from Sacramento, but Rickard had no real-world connection to Ninzard, and members regarded him with more suspicion for it.5

The District of Molly

Loyal to the Poopulist and Orie camp but finding General Mel annoying, the trio set out in 2022 to replace her with Molly, hyping her half-ironically as a “savior” in a movement called “Molly4Volley,” or “the District of Molly.” Rickard privately assured Mel that he still supported the Ories, and told her she was “hot”; she wrongly believed she still had the troll vote.7 In July 2022 Molly won Volley with 34 of 45 votes (75.6%), as the trio swayed a large bloc of Orie voters behind her.8

In the vice-presidential election that month, Rickard was among the trolls who voted “for the lulz” for Joyza, a former vice president trying to win the office back.13 In the presidential election that followed, Buttersaltz sought the nomination of the Gusta Party, one of the troll parties of 2022.14

GamerGore

In early May 2023 an account called “GamerGore,” a Nightian who had joined six months earlier but for whom no established member had vouched, asked some of Volley's trolls to vouch for it. Rickard, who found the gore GIFs it sent him hilarious, vouched for it, giving it the vote and access to the chats. On 12 May GamerGore messaged him privately, revealed itself to be Cat, and warned that the staff would punish him if he exposed her. Rickard, amused, reported it to General Molly and the Volley staff. Cat then flooded Volley's chats with gore while Rickard laughed uncontrollably in a voice call, and Molly banned both GamerGore and Rickard.9

Rickard was well liked, and many Volleyans pressed for his pardon; Gale, his best friend, led a mass protest of provocative posts about Molly meant to bait her into banning them all. Molly refused, noting that Rickard had admitted to running Buttersaltz, one of Nightwork's most notorious troll accounts, and to knowing that GamerGore posted gore. Many still thought the ban unfair, and General Woofer let him into Main, though without the right to vouch for anyone.9 Governor Adelynn noticed that the gore had begun right after Rickard's report, a detail others had missed, but shared her suspicion only with Grace, so as not to set the staff against one another.9

Main and the break with the Ories (2023–2025)

Cat's ledger of the money she offered Ninzards between March 2022 and April 2024 lists Rickard among those who refused every cent: $1,117 in all.15

Rickard had long belonged to the Original Party, but he grew disillusioned with what he saw as the Ories' constant LARP, and their coalition with the Liberation Party completed his break with the party. In the elections of December 2025, when many trolls voted for the Preservates as the party that struck the right balance between order and humour, his backing was the most prominent, and it helped flip the troll vote.6

Legislature XI (2025–2026)

The “Low EE” insurgency

On 27 December 2025 Rickard was among the trolls elected as representatives with access to #charter-talk, the channel in NPC! where the Capital had opened debate on the new Charter, and the prototype of Legislature XI. With Snuck, he at once turned it into a stage for humiliating the defeated Original Party leadership. While Snuck flooded the channel with absurd images, Rickard turned the losers' old humiliations against them, dared Rudolf to earn a second ban, and joined Snuck in spamming pro-Israel slogans to provoke NGin, the leader of the neo-Nazi faction.1 The archive took the episode as the mark of a new order, in which the war against Cat had given way to the ironic bullying of anyone who took themselves seriously.1

The group chat

In January 2026 he, Gale, Al Commando, Moctezuma and Shuffle used a private group chat to stage “nightmare blunt rotations,” adding rival members to provoke them and removing them when they were done, while Gale and Rickard filled it with hornyposting. On 23 January Evhom, a Jewish member of Parry, which Rickard had by then been allowed to join, complained of being harassed for being Jewish by him and Gale. Rickard had demanded Evhom's views on Israel, and answered the complaint by denying only that he had used an antisemitic slur. Apollos and Power of Greed, added to the chat, advised Evhom to ignore the trolls.16

On 27 January, as Legislature XI chose its leader, Rudolf and NGin traded racist and antisemitic abuse; Rickard answered them with pro-Israel slogans, cast joke votes and told Rudolf to get off his computer. Shuffle was chosen as leader, and when Snuck began venting his distress later that night, Rickard was among those who tried to help him.10 When shots were fired at Aceiron's house during the Chud War on the night of 14 February, Rickard blamed NGin: “You ruined it for everyone.”17

The Charter

When the Preservation Party reorganized its Apparatus on 24 February 2026, Rickard joined it as an assistant for Main in Apollos's Pragma Flank.18 At the first public debate on the Charter, on 28 February, he mocked the Ories' draft until Aceiron moved to mute him. Shuffle put the motion to a poll, and the Legislature voted 8 to 3 to keep him unmuted; Al Commando pointed out that Aceiron had complained of censorship and then tried to censor Rickard.2 On 7 March he voted with every other representative for the revised Part I of the Charter, which passed 15 to 0.19 On 2 April Woofer, the party's new Boss, promoted him to party operative for Main.4

Goonstitution

Tired of the long essays that Lless, Lyman and Liver kept posting in the Legislature, Gale and Rickard released “Goonstitution” on 16 April 2026, a parody rap with a video edit of AI-generated photos of NGin and Liver at a wedding. It was an instant hit.11 On 11 May Apollos opened his broadcast ratifying the Charter by unrolling a printed “Goonstitution” and crumpling it, before signing the Charter and eating pineapple pizza off it. Rickard was one of the eight representatives who voted for the joke motion to impeach him that followed.12

After the death of the veteran member Soda on 18 May 2026, when Lless mocked those mourning him, Rickard's reply was brief.20

「Death doesn't care about the lore. Rest in peace, Liam.」

— Representative Rickard, 18 May 2026

Defeat

In the summer elections of June 2026 Main re-elected PluPlu, Ploutos and Syn and newly elected Seandry, and Rickard lost his seat.21,3 He took the defeat in character, mourning that the voters had rejected his “vascularity,” while Al Commando lamented the fall of “the SaltzMan.”3 When Mélanie, returning to the Preservate Apparatus, asked who he was, he answered with a string of grotesque, absurdist posts in the jargon of the video game Escape from Tarkov until he was muted.22

Later activity

In August 2026, when General Kaimachi told the Preservate Apparatus that Apollos had spent an hour playing worship songs on his guitar for her during a private call, Rickard was ready with a verdict.23

「Bro actually pulled out the acoustic guitar and played Wonderwall for the Lord. The youth group camp counselor rizz is absolutely unmatched.」

— Rickard, the Preservate Apparatus, August 2026

Reputation

The archive counts Rickard among the community's most notorious trolls, and in his Volley years he was widely seen as malicious; yet he was also well liked, enough for Volley to protest his ban in 2023.6,5,9 In the Legislature his trolling was mostly deadpan, and the stock line “I am hearing voices,” posted through many of its angriest debates, became his signature.10,24,2,3 Al Commando called him “the SaltzMan.”3,22
