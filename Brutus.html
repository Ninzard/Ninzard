Jacob “Jake” Baek (born 25 December 2004), known in Ninzard as Brutus, is a Ninzard troublemaker and former politician who served as the 5th General of Main from 1 March to 4 October 2020. A cousin and fellow Orie of Snuck, he succeeded Syn in Main when Syn became governor, defied Syn's lockdown of Nightwork alongside Snuck that September, and was voted out in favour of Grace.1,2,3

Early years (2014–2018)

Brutus first made his mark at the age of nine. In early 2014, without the community's knowledge, he used the account of his cousin, then Vice President Snuck, to ban members for trivial reasons; once he gave himself away, President Antareze moved to recall Snuck, who lost the vice presidency to Gabriel that February.4,5 In September 2017 General Judah picked him as his running mate for Governor of Ninzardia, and the Orie ticket lost in a landslide to Holidae and Spinzle after President Apollos endorsed them, to Judah's and Brutus's anger.6

In 2018 he, Snuck and his older brother Leon clashed with Governor DegreeZ over moderation and bullied Nightwork members they knew in real life.7,8 He was in the crowd that watched Syn beat Apollos in a park that year; when he asked Syn why he had stopped, Syn told him to “shut up or die,” seized him by the throat when he swung back, and let go only when he began to cry.9

General of Main (2020)

When Syn became Governor of Nightwork on 1 March 2020, Main elected Brutus to succeed him; he was considered “a man of the people.”1 He was re-elected in May 2020.

The lockdown

On 1 September 2020 he and Snuck, the General of Parry, ignored Syn's closure of Nightwork and invited about two dozen new members without warning, among them stranded former Wafflers and many of their own friends. Syn stripped their permissions. On the call that followed, Brutus joined the cousins' Korean shouting match with the governor and called Syn a slur in front of the president; Apollos nonetheless judged Syn “excessively controlling” and gave the permissions back.2 When Syn then moved all of Nightwork into one closed server, Brutus reluctantly went along, after Mainians had bombarded him with criticism, doxxing and death threats.10

Defeat

He endorsed Aceiron against Syn in the October 2020 election, but Main, angry that he had defied the governor, voted him out and elected Grace on 4 October.11,3

Later activity

Out of office he stayed on as an administrator among the Paleories, the populist wing of the Original Party, with Apollos, Snuck and Mel.12 Raffaele dismissed him and Snuck as “petty animals,” and in 2022 he sought the Ories' presidential nomination through an alternate account, AtUrMoms469.13,14
