Pierre Carmichael (born 4 January 2003), known as Pieban, is a former Ninzard politician who served as the 6th District General of Solorado in 2018 and as the 8th District General of BobaBase in 2023. A Coloradan of the old Solorado cohort and one of the eight known members of the Edith Gang, he is the husband of former Vice President Snowby.1,2,3,4

Struggling with debt, Pieban and Snowby were drawn into financial dependence on President Cat. He registered the Colorado shell companies through which her money was laundered and became her chief strategist, devising the “White Noise Protocol” that shielded her from investigation for more than a year.1,5 After Cat assaulted his wife in October 2024 he arranged the family's escape from Colorado, and after her fall he surrendered to the FBI, gave testimony that proved vital to the Cat Files and accepted a plea agreement of five years' probation on grounds of duress. He was permanently banned from the Ninzard Project in February 2026.6,7,8,9,10

Early life

Carmichael was born on 4 January 20031 and grew up in Colorado. Scalen, born in Fort Collins, was a childhood friend, and Pieban invited him into Ninzard's Solorado legion in 2012.11 Pieban belonged to the Edith Gang, Spinzle's circle of real-life friends, six of whose eight known members came from the old Solorado legion; the others were Spinzle, Aurase, Snowby, Soda, Kennie, Atria and Runix.4 In 2017 he began dating Snowby, another Coloradan member.12

General of Solorado (2018)

When Faber became Governor of Ninzardia on 1 April 2018, Pieban won the snap election to succeed him as the 6th District General of Solorado.2 He sat as an independent in Legislature X, nicknamed “The Last Legislature.”13

That spring the deaths of Tiny2 and Holiday devastated the Coloradan members who had been close to Holiday. Snowby, overwhelmed by guilt, fell into a depression, and Pieban and her older sister Abby were her only sources of support.14 With much of the old leadership gone, Legislature X met for the last time on 7 July 2018 and abolished Solorado, together with Sacraday, from the following day.15,16

Keeping the secret (2019–2021)

After Holiday's death Abby tried to persuade her sister and their friends to leave Ninzard, and told their mother.14 In 2019, while the Edith Gang quietly stayed involved behind Abby's back, Pieban deliberately gave Abby and Snowby's mother the impression that Snowby was no longer involved.17

Snowby was in her first year of college with Pieban when she was elected Vice President at the end of 2021. Friends of Abby told her, and she told their mother; the Abernethy family's trust in Pieban, who had been lying to them for Snowby's sake, broke down, and Abby distrusted him from then on.18

Marriage and Cat (2022)

In early 2022 the couple faced a severe financial crisis: Pieban's working hours had been cut, and they were struggling to pay down their debts.1 When Cat offered Snowby a generously paid job that June as a “community consultant” for Catsland, Pieban reviewed the first payments, concluded that Cat was merely a “rich kid” with more money than sense, and advised his fiancée to accept.1 They were married on 29 July 2022.1

In September Cat approached Pieban with a partnership in high-frequency ticket reselling, claiming to have a botting algorithm but not the American banking infrastructure to run it. Pieban saw bot scalping as an aggressive but victimless exploitation of a market inefficiency rather than a crime, and he registered limited liability companies in Colorado, among them “Solorado Logistics,” with business accounts in Snowby's name. Cat then began mixing the proceeds of her other crimes into the stream, and the sheer volume of money kept him from looking closely at where it came from.1,19 He had been wary of Cat at first, but her close partnership with Meikaqu, whom he respected, convinced him that she must have some legitimacy, and he believed that he, not Cat, was the one running the operation.19

When the couple turned to Spinzle, absorbed in governing Nightwork, she answered their troubles with cold, practical advice. Pieban came to see her as a “brilliant mind” trapped in a “failing administration” and Cat as a rising power with real resources, and the two slowly stopped confiding in their old friend.20 By the end of 2022 Pieban was part of Cat's inner circle with Meikaqu, Raffaele and Snowby.21

General of BobaBase (2023)

After the Poopulist General ME GUSTA resigned on 18 November 2022, Pieban and former President Apollos quietly ran BobaBase from 15 December. Cat nominated Pieban for general as the Disco Party's candidate, and Apollos endorsed him.3 His opponent, the former Orie troll SARZ, rallied trolls behind a “Mongol Empire Revolution,” flooded the server with joke accounts and called Pieban a “plant.” Pieban won by 50 votes to 27 (64.9%) and became the 8th General of BobaBase on 3 January 2023.3

On 14 February 2023 Cat took Pieban, Snowby and Meikaqu on a “business trip” to Italy. Raffaele joined them, and they dined with his family in Lazio, among them his young cousin PluPlu.22

At midnight on 1 April 2023 Waffle's General Assailant revealed himself as Apollos. Pieban drafted Cat's public response, in which she claimed to have “known all along” and to have been indulging Apollos's mental health crisis.23,5 Apollos then disconnected BobaBase from the rest of Ninzard, and it ceased to be one of its districts.24

Cat's strategist (2023)

Pieban had always regarded Raffaele, even while he dated Cat, as “significantly dangerous,” a chaotic operator who put emotional justice above structural stability. When the couple split and Raffaele turned on her, Pieban saw that Cat's position, and with it Snowby's safety and the family's income, had become precarious.5 He stepped out of the Edith Gang's shadow and made himself Cat's chief strategist. He was the only person she respected enough to listen to, largely because he treated her not as a queen or a sister but as a client.5

White Noise Protocol

Pieban foresaw that Governor Adelynn would answer the backlash against Cat with a strict policy to identify and purge alternate accounts, the future Sponsorship Standard. Cat wanted to fight or sabotage it; in June 2023 Pieban proposed instead to turn Adelynn's bureaucracy against her, arguing that fighting the cleanup would only make Cat look guilty. The plan, the White Noise Protocol, had three parts.5

First came a flood of false positives. Dozens of crude alternate accounts were sent into Nightwork to cause minor trouble and then “caught” and reported by Cat's sleeper agents, among them Atria and later Triste. Adelynn's administration felt that its system was working, and the agents, verified by the governor herself, became the trusted sponsors who controlled Nightwork's gates. Second, Pieban forbade Cat to give sensitive orders by direct message and had her payments to agents buried, with Meikaqu's help, in legitimate-looking trades of CS:GO skins, Valorant points and cryptocurrency, a camouflage that delayed General Kay's investigation by about fourteen months. Third, he coached Cat to drop her threats and play a clumsy but well-meaning ally, as in her response to the Assailant reveal, and she fed Adelynn fabricated intelligence about outside threats until the governor came to depend on her.5

The strategy kept Cat safe for more than a year.5 Its weak point was Kay, who by August 2023 had stopped reading chat logs and was watching the blockchain. Meikaqu found the giveaway in an audit of the botnet's gas fees, a task Pieban had been responsible for but had become too busy to do.25

Sonnenrad Society

After Lyman and Scalen founded the Sonnenrad Society, a fascist network on Ninzard's periphery, on 5 July 2023, Cat had Pieban renew his friendship with Scalen. Presenting himself as more radical than he was, he gradually won a measure of Scalen's trust.26 He then passed on a modified Discord client built by Meikaqu, sold as an “anti-surveillance patch” but secretly designed to plant hidden extremist propaganda in its users' messages, so that Adelynn's transparency archive would reproduce it word for word. Scalen refused the program, but Pieban told others in the network that he had “spoken with Scalen,” and it spread through the lower ranks until even Lyman used it.27 When the scheme brought Austrian police attention down on the Sonnenrad leadership, Scalen worked out that Pieban had used their Solorado friendship to deliver it, and the network came to hate the Cat Capital as deeply as it hated the Preservates.28

Hostages (2023–2024)

Pieban had worked as a project manager for a small logistics firm and as a remote consultant. In May 2023 he lost both jobs within two weeks, and Cat replaced his salary, doubled it and told him that the ticket operation now needed his full attention.19 In July, a meticulous record keeper, he noticed that the money coming in far exceeded anything the ticket sales could produce: the business was a front for laundering the proceeds of Cat's scams in Ninzard and beyond. By then it paid the mortgage and the groceries. Stopping would have cost the family its home, and going to the authorities would have shown the couple as the scheme's main beneficiaries, so he kept the full extent of the danger from his pregnant wife.19

He began quietly setting aside clean money, hoping to break with Cat once Kay's investigation cooled. In November 2023 Cat visited their home in Colorado and had Snowby sign an affidavit naming her as the sole owner of the shell companies; when Pieban reached to tear it up, Cat made veiled threats that social services would take their daughter away. Snowby signed, and Pieban understood that they were no longer Cat's employees but her hostages.29 The arson of Raffaele's home on Christmas Day was meant in part to show the couple what Cat was capable of.30 In 2024, when they threatened to go to the police, Cat reminded them that she had recorded everything and could end their custody of their children.31

On 14 October 2024 Cat assaulted Snowby at their home. When Pieban moved toward her, Cat stopped him by threatening to expose the laundered money in his wife's name and to have their children taken away.32 On 20 November, swayed by audio Cat had leaked and by Snowby's account of events, the Edith Gang turned on Spinzle: Pieban, Kennie and Soda joined an ultimatum accusing her of reckless endangerment over the Dead Man's Switch she had set to expose Cat, and they blocked her everywhere. Only Aurase refused to take part.33 In the weeks that followed Pieban adopted a strategy of complete compliance, running the scalping operations efficiently while trying above all to shield Snowby from Cat; he controlled her internet access, monitored her contact with what remained of the Edith Gang and acted as the only buffer between his wife and the president.34

Escape (December 2024)

The assault convinced Pieban that compliance had failed and that his children were in danger, and that any approach to the police would end with Snowby in prison. He resolved instead that the family would vanish. Cat watched their bank accounts in real time, tracked their phones and could hear through the smart devices in their home, so in early December he contacted Vice President Meikaqu through an encrypted chat inside an obscure mobile game she had made years earlier, and asked her for twelve hours of blindness.6

On 12 December Meikaqu slipped a fault into the botnet that monitored the couple's finances, suppressing withdrawal alerts for a day, and messaged him a single word: “go.” The next morning Pieban drained their personal accounts, took cash advances on every card and withdrew about $40,000 in cash from ATMs across Denver. The family left their phones, laptops and watches on the kitchen counter, set the lights on timers and left music playing to fool Cat's audio surveillance, and drove east with their two children in a used car bought with cash. They lived in short-term rentals in the Midwest under false names, and Pieban, months from finishing a certification that would have restored his career, gave it up.6

Cat did not notice they were gone for thirty-six hours. Their escape shattered her sense of control and drove her deeper into the paranoia of early 2025.6

Surrender and plea (2025–2026)

When Cat fell in August 2025, Nightians ostracized Pieban with Snowby, Sundaze, Kennie and Alaric and demanded their permanent removal.35 Still in contact with some members on Discord, the couple faced constant harassment, and before the Cat Files were released most of the community believed they deserved to lose their children.7 On 28 August they drove back to Colorado to confront Spinzle; Pieban threatened to sue her for defamation and invasion of privacy and demanded that she delete the ledgers implicating them. She refused, but promised that the account given to the authorities would stress duress.36

After Meikaqu assured them that cooperation was their only path to leniency, Pieban walked into the FBI field office on 3 September 2025 in a clean suit with his defense attorney, handed over an encrypted drive with the full ledgers of Solorado Logistics LLC and gave a testimonial deposition.37,7 Charged with money laundering in the District of Colorado, the couple argued successfully that they had acted under duress, citing the threats against their children and the assault on Snowby. They accepted a plea agreement of five years' probation, forfeiture of everything derived from the scheme, which left them bankrupt, and community service, and kept custody of their children under the monitoring of social services.9

「The financial structure implicated the victims. Immediate intervention would have incarcerated Mary and Pierre. I prioritized their safety.」

— Kay, reflecting on her investigation in the Cat Files

Pieban and Snowby had at first threatened to sue the Capital over the dossier, but he later became one of its contributors, and his testimony was vital to the Cat Files.8 Cat's recovered ledger for March 2022 to April 2024 lists him fourth among the recipients of her money, with $35,794 accepted and $2,926 refused.38

After the full release of the Cat Files in January 2026, NGin berated Pieban in a widely shared voice memo for failing to fight Cat when she attacked his wife. Many Preservates objected; Cyntaku noted that had Pieban done what NGin demanded, “Whatever future that family imagined would've ended right there.”39 The conditions of their federal probation took effect in early February, and on 19 February Minister of Justice Kay announced that they had been permanently banned from the Ninzard Project.10 That April, answering PoG's suggestion that no one survives the inner circle of a predator by being innocent, Poka named the couple among Cat's victims, “forced to register shell companies.”40

Personal life

Pieban married Snowby on 29 July 2022; both families disapproved of the marriage and refused to support them.1,19 The couple have two children.7 The archive contrasts the two in their years with Cat: Snowby acted out of fear and emotional subservience, Pieban out of cold, calculated pragmatism, seeing Ninzard's politics not as a community but as a hostile market that required risk management.5
