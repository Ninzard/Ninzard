The Ninzard Project

The Ninzard Project was founded on 21 December 2012 by the First Triumvirate: Antareze, Eliza and Hokan. This encyclopedia collects what has been recovered of its history from Phases II–V.

For the portraits of the community's members, see Faces of Ninzard. For the parties that have contested its elections, see Political parties of Ninzard, and for the elections themselves, Presidential elections in the Ninzard Project.
