The Disco Party, called the Disconic Party until 2022 and whose members were known as Disconics, was a political party of the Ninzard Project. Founded as a small expansionist party under Vesper, it was taken over in June 2022 by Cat, who led it to the presidency that July and made it the engine of her Meowsalute movement. It presented itself as a populist “third way” between the Original and Preservation parties.1,2,3

Under Cat the party absorbed the Poo Party and ruled Ninzardia through rigged elections until she abolished elections there altogether in 2024. It collapsed with her regime in August 2025.4,5,6
