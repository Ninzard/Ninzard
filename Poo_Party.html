The Poo Party, whose supporters were known as the Poopulists, was a populist political party of the Ninzard Project, founded in May 2021 by Rony, a real-life friend of President Apollos, in protest at Governor Clexas's ban on the word “poo” in Ninzardia.1 Anti-regulation, expansionist and deliberately unserious, it rode the “Poo Wave” of 2021 to the governorship of Ninzardia and, with Snowby, to the vice presidency.2,3

Its feud with the Preservation Party defined the polarization of 2022, and its nominee Al Commando ran third in that year's presidential election. The party came to an end in January 2023, when its members merged into Cat's Disco Party.4,5,6
