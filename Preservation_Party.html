The Preservation Party (PP), whose members are known as Preservates and which is sometimes called the Blue Party after its color, is the governing party of the Ninzard Project. It was founded on 22 September 2020 by Syn, Cyntaku and Grace as an isolationist opposition determined to protect the community from the toxicity of the Underground, and has been one of the community's two major parties ever since, alongside the Original Party.1,2

The party has passed through three eras: the isolationism of its founders; the “Logos” era of Adelynn, Governor of Nightwork from 2023 to 2024, who turned its norms into a moral and bureaucratic framework for governing; and the results-driven “Pragma” era that began when Apollos took it over in 2025.2 Under Apollos and his successor, Poka, it won the presidency twice, the Charter of the Ninzard Project and the largest victories in the community's history. Its Boss since 14 March 2026 has been Woofer.3,4,5

Symbols

Blue (#035AFC) is the party's color, and the Preservates are sometimes called the Blue Party because of it.6 Its accent color is gold, the same gold (#E08220) as the Resistance Party's, and its emblem is a plus sign inside a circle. Its mascot is Adelynn, the party's 4th Boss, whose moral principles became the core ideals of its Logos Flank.2 The party's logo shows her as a Preservate knight in a round blue badge ringed in gold: she stands before a gold-edged shield between gold scrollwork and white lilies, with the emblem at the bottom.
