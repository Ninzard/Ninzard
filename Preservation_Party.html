The Preservation Party (PP), whose members are known as Preservates and which is sometimes called the Blue Party after its colour, is the governing party of the Ninzard Project. It was founded on 1 October 2020 by Syn, Cyntaku and Grace as an isolationist opposition determined to protect the community from the toxicity of the Underground, and has been one of the community's two major parties ever since, alongside the Original Party.1,2

The party has passed through three eras: the isolationism of its founders; the “Logos” era of Adelynn, Governor of Nightwork from 2023 to 2024, who turned its norms into a moral and bureaucratic framework for governing; and the results-driven “Pragma” era that began when Apollos took it over in 2025.2 Under Apollos and his successor, Poka, it won the presidency twice, the Charter of the Ninzard Project and the largest victories in the community's history. Its Boss since 14 March 2026 has been Woofer.3,4,5
