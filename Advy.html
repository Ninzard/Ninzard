Advik, known as Advy, is a Ninzard politician and cybersecurity operator who served as 7th and 9th District General of Hybriden (2018, 2019–2020), as 4th District General of Capitalia (2022–2023), and as a Representative of Main in Legislature XII since July 2026.1,2,3,4

A co-founder of the California-based cybersecurity group Channeling-4 (C-4) with Seandry, Ploutos, and Hackman, Advy acted as a quiet institutional caretaker across Ninzardia.5 In June 2023, he built the honeypot marketplace script for Operation A1P that linked President Cat's Leeds IP address to her money-laundering wallets.6

General of Hybriden and Channeling-4 (2017–2020)

Endorsed by President Apollos as an independent, Advy unseated Abby in December 2017 to become 7th District General of Hybriden.1 After the Gold Road suicides in June 2018, Abby persuaded him to step down so she could resume office, though Advy continued helping manage Ninzardia through the summer crisis.7,8 In August 2018, he co-founded Channeling-4 (C-4) with Seandry, Ploutos, and Hackman, which investigated Quorey's crimes in late 2019.5,9

Following Abby's retirement, Advy was elected 9th General of Hybriden on 22 March 2019 and won re-election in September 2019 and February 2020, serving until Hybriden was temporarily abolished during the Underground Cleanup Initiative in August 2020.2,10,11,12

General of Capitalia and Operation A1P (2022–2023)

When General Kay fell ill prior to the January 2022 elections, Apollos asked Advy to run in Capitalia despite Advy belonging to the Orie establishment. Advy won and maintained Kay's strict 45-member ID system.3,13

In May 2022, Cat offered to buy Advy a video game as a belated birthday gift and proposed a $100 “discretionary war chest” for Capitalia. Advy replied that he did not “know you like that,” told her to give gifts to Capitalians directly, and rejected every cent of her $170 in attempted bribes.14,15 He voted “No” without comment on Governor Cyntaku's Anti-Bullying Amendment and won re-election in July 2022 and in January 2023, defeating Disconic challenger Strobe 34–11 (75.6%).16,17,18

When Kay returned in May 2023, Advy immediately resigned and endorsed her.19 On 4 June 2023, as part of Operation A1P alongside Kay, Raffaele, and Seandry, Advy coded a script that listed a high-value asset at 90% off for three seconds on a niche marketplace, successfully baiting Cat's local auto-buy bot forty times to correlate her Leeds IP with Wallet 0x7a.6

Later career (2025–2026)

In 2026, Advy helped rebuild the Original Party, volunteered for the Ninzard Career Initiative (NCI) and Psych Ward crisis tickets, and bridged ScottyAte and the Orie leadership during the 30 March 2026 Soft-Landing Protocol after Caleb's head injury.20,21,22,23 After losing the March 2026 Main representative snap election to Ploutos (165 of 237 votes, 69.6%) and attending the Holloway Summit on 2 May, Advy won a July 2026 snap election as an independent to succeed PluPlu in Legislature XII.24,25,4
