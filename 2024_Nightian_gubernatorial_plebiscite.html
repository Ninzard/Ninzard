The 2024 Nightian gubernatorial plebiscite was held on 30 June 2024, alongside Nightwork's district general elections, to decide whether to call a snap election for Governor of Nightwork. Governor Adelynn, elected in December 2023 to the first one-year term, called it at former Governor Spinzle's suggestion after many voters came to regret the longer term, and promised a snap election if fewer than half the voters backed her. Of the 580 members who answered, 325 (56.0%) voted No, and she served out her term until the December 2024 election.1,2

Adelynn's own party was divided over the vote. Woofer argued that the December 2023 result was already her mandate and Matcha that it gave the far right an opening, while Grace defended it as proof that Adelynn always sought the people's mandate. Once it went ahead, Woofer and Matcha urged their districts to vote No.1 Main and Waffle voted No by more than three to one and Scope narrowly; Parry and Volley, the Orie districts, voted Yes by nearly three to one.2

The Ories accused Adelynn of acting like a dictator. Every district general was re-elected the same day, and in August the Original Party nominated Aceiron and AJ to run against her in December.1,3

Background

Adelynn had governed Nightwork since January 2023. In December 2023 she was re-elected over ViaTea, the Orie nominee, by 351 votes to 300, and in the same election voters chose to hold gubernatorial elections once a year instead of every six months, which many had found excessive. The next election was set for December 2024, while the district generals kept their six-month terms.4 When the June 2024 elections came around, all five generals were up for re-election but the governor was not, which upset many voters; many who had voted for the one-year term now said they regretted it.1

Elections had meanwhile lost all meaning in Cat's Ninzardia, where Cat and Governor Sundaze ran a single-doctrine community whose referendums were rigged. Cat taught the members of Lair to look down on Nightians for devoting themselves to what she cast as a meaningless democracy, while nearly every Nightian looked on Ninzardia's leadership with disgust.1 On 31 May 2024 Sundaze drew Adelynn into a public voice call that broke down within minutes, and in June she spread fabricated claims of a secret romance between them, which the Preservation Party called “entirely false”; almost no Nightian believed her.5 Cat had also threatened Adelynn and Grace directly, and both had turned down large sums of her money.6,7

Proposal

At Spinzle's suggestion, Adelynn proposed a Nightwork-wide plebiscite held with the general elections. If fewer than half of those voting backed her, not counting abstentions, a snap election for governor would follow immediately, letting the community decide whether to remove her. Abstaining counted toward neither side.1

Preservate debate

Adelynn put the idea to the Preservate Apparatus first, and the party immediately asked why she would accept the risk of defeat in so precarious a climate.1

  • Woofer, General of Main, argued that the December 2023 result was itself Adelynn's mandate to serve the full year, that asking again would tell voters earlier mandates were “arbitrary,” and that Adelynn was taking community politics so seriously that she was risking the community's well-being to protect procedural norms.
  • Matcha, General of Waffle, rebuked Adelynn for “willingly” giving the far right an opportunity to take over the community, called her “emotionless,” and argued that letting the far right campaign against her was an unacceptable risk. Woofer accused Matcha of “twisting” things by assuming Adelynn's intentions: Adelynn was “too naive” about the problem, Woofer said, but “definitely not” what Matcha suggested.
  • Grace, the deputy governor, sided with Adelynn: the electorate would most likely back her anyway, and seeking a fresh mandate would secure future ones by showing that Adelynn always sought the people's will.

Summarized from the debate as the archive records it.1

After a heated debate Adelynn went ahead. Woofer and Matcha at once began telling the members of their districts to vote No to protect the community.1

Main

Main voted No by 125 to 39 (76.2%), with 10 abstentions. On the same ballot it re-elected Woofer over the Orie Lyre by 119 votes to 55.2,8

Parry

Parry gave Yes its strongest result, 78 to 29 (72.9%), and re-elected General Rudolf over Aurase by 84 votes to 28.2,8

Scope

Scope voted No by 64 to 51 (55.7%), while re-electing its Orie general, Aceiron, over the Preservate Ixan by only 65 votes to 54, a close margin put down to Ixan's popularity.2,8,1

Volley

Volley voted Yes by 64 to 24 (72.7%) and had the most abstentions, 14 of its 102 voters (13.7%). General AJ, whose Rationalist policies had turned the district from the Preservates to the Ories, beat Molly by 66 votes to 36.2,8,1

Waffle

Waffle gave No its strongest result, 83 to 23 (78.3%), and re-elected Matcha over the Orie Idodo by 62 votes to 52.2,8

Concurrent elections

The district general elections were, in the archive's words, “highly unremarkable”: every general was re-elected. The Ories won 322 of the 621 votes (51.9%) to the Preservates' 299 (48.1%), and held all three of their generalships. Woofer, Rudolf, Aceiron, AJ and Matcha became the five generals who shaped Nightwork's 2024.1,8 Aceiron had been named Boss of the Original Party just before the elections, after his allies circulated a compromising recording of his challenger, Kain.9

Aftermath

Despite the result the Ories accused Adelynn of a dictatorship. She answered that she had sought to respect the mandate voters gave her in December 2023 and that the plebiscite had confirmed it, an explanation the Ories turned into evidence of power-seeking.1

Privately the vote had left Adelynn overwhelmed with anxiety. Under Cat's threats she felt morally bound to stay on as governor to protect her members, and she confessed to Grace that she felt guilty for having gambled the community's safety on her need for “democratic legitimacy.” Grace told her she had protected the community as well as anyone could, and spent the summer visiting her in person.6

Aceiron came out of the June elections as the new face of the Original Party, and AJ's Volley drifted further right as servers linked to the neo-Nazi Sonnenrad Society began coordinating within its networks.1 In August 2024 the Original Party nominated Aceiron for governor with AJ as his running mate, and in the December 2024 election they defeated Adelynn and Grace with 54.4% of the vote.3,10
