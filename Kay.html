Mikayla Holloway, known in Ninzard as Kay, is a Ninzard politician and administrator who was the District General of Capitalia and the first Minister of Justice of the Ninzard Project, serving in the Capital of the Apollos II administration from 3 November 2025 until 2 July 2026.1,2 One of the last leaders of the community's Golden Era, she was known across Ninzard as “Mother Ninzard.”3

A survivor of abuse in the old Sacraday district, Kay joined the Original Party and was elected General of Capitalia in 2017, governing the small, insular district until illness forced her offline at the end of 2021.4,5,6,7 She returned in May 2023 and, over the following twenty-six months, led the counterintelligence operation that dismantled the criminal presidency of Catx3, compiling a 4.2-terabyte archive that became the basis of the Catx3 Dossier.3,8

Her near-total surveillance of Cat and her network earned her the nickname “the Eye of Sauron” and a reputation as the head of the so-called Ninzard Deep State.9 Appointed Minister of Justice when Apollos reorganized the Capital in November 2025, she managed the community's gravest real-world crises until she resigned with the rest of the administration in July 2026, deleting her surveillance apparatus on her way out.1,10

Early life and abuse

Holloway, who is Black, lives in Sacramento, California, with her older brother, Malachi.11,12 She joined the community as a teenager and was originally a member of the Sacraday district, which operated under the “protection” of General Bray.13 In 2014 she was among several young members sexually assaulted at a Sacraday party; the community at the time dismissed the victims as liars, and she was mocked afterward.13,14 The abuse became public again during the 2016 presidential campaign, when revelations about Bray's circle helped collapse his candidacy.14

General of Capitalia (2017–2022)

Transfer and election

With the permission of President Apollos and Governor Gabriel, Kay was transferred out of Sacraday to Capitalia, the quiet Ninzardian district also known as “the Silent Sky” and “District 1.”4,3 She joined the Original Party and, in the June 2017 elections, defeated the Resistance Party's Atria to become District General of Capitalia.4 She was re-elected that December and held the district through the turmoil of the following years.15

When the community reeled from two real-world deaths in 2018 and the Legislature postponed that year's presidential election, some members suggested that Kay step in as president. She declined flatly—“i have class. not spending my freshman year babysitting”—for which Governor DegreeZ and others accused her of self-interest.16 As Sacraday and Solorado fell defunct, Capitalia, under Kay, and Hybriden, under Advy, were among the last pieces of the old Ninzardian faction still standing.6

The Deadaxz campaign

In 2019 Kay became the fixation of Deadaxz, an alias of the white-supremacist troll Quorey, who used old Sacraday connections to obtain her Sacramento address and waged a campaign of anonymous terror against her, mailing death threats to her home and attempting to have her swatted.17 Late that year, when the group Channeling-4 uncovered the full scope of Quorey's crimes—which included serious offences against children and plans for a racially motivated attack—Kay helped have him swatted and arrested, and personally informed the parents of his young victims.18

Illness and departure

By late 2021 Kay had fallen gravely ill. Having expressed no interest in the Original Party's internal “civil war,” she went offline in December 2021 and did not seek re-election in January 2022; Apollos persuaded the veteran Advy to take Capitalia in her place.5,19 She had nearly died during her illness, an experience that drew her toward Christianity, and she would not return to the community until the middle of 2023.20,7

Return and “Mother Ninzard” (2023)

Kay returned to the Silent Sky in May 2023 to the delight of Capitalians, who had come to call her “Mother Ninzard” as one of the last surviving Golden-Era leaders. Advy resigned at once and endorsed her, and she won the snap election to reclaim the district unanimously.3 Because her return was so sudden, Cat—by then president—had no time to turn opinion against her, and, judging Kay impossible to bribe or manipulate, kept a wary distance.3 In the rigged July 2023 elections, Capitalia was the only district to return a legitimate result, and Kay was re-elected with all 45 of its individually verified votes.21,22

The investigation of Catx3 (2023–2025)

Within weeks of returning, Kay grew suspicious of the cult-like loyalty surrounding President Cat—feelings she described only as “hunches”—and began quietly speaking with members who reported serious personal abuse, relaying what she learned to the Nightwork leaders Adelynn, Grace and the administrator Seandry.3 What followed was a twenty-six-month counterintelligence operation that she led alongside Raffaele and Channeling-4, and which amassed a 4.2-terabyte archive of Cat's crimes.23,8

Operation A1P

Raffaele, who had reconnected with reality that spring, gave Kay the earliest evidence against Cat, and together they planned her tracking.24 In June 2023 Kay executed what Raffaele named Operation A1P, a traffic-correlation analysis run with Raffaele, Advy and Seandry: using bait listings that triggered Cat's automated laundering bots, she matched inbound connections from a residential address in Leeds against the timing of blockchain transactions, establishing with near-certainty that the money-laundering ring operated from that location.25 When Raffaele sacrificed his own position in a public “Last Stand” that led to the abolition of Hybriden, he baited Cat into a direct, unmasked connection, handing Kay a single clean digital fingerprint.24,26

The Phantom General

While the community saw Kay as a semi-retired figurehead who logged on only to ban the occasional alt, she was in fact dismantling Cat's digital life at a level the president—a social engineer—could not match.27,28 In December 2024 she compromised Cat's careless associate Blithem, and on 14 January 2025 Cat installed a poisoned update that granted Kay root access to her machines. Rather than plant a virus that Cat's compulsive wipes would erase, Kay booby-trapped the cloud backups Cat used to restore herself, giving her persistent “ghost access” to everything Cat did.28 She archived Cat's messages, mapped the laundering network and, with Seandry, waged a campaign of silent, gaslighting sabotage, leaving empty files in Cat's private drives named with her last login times and messages that read “i am in here. with you.”28

Capitalia itself kept an eerie silence. Cat sent agents to provoke the district, hoping Kay would lash out; Kay simply identified and banned them without a word, a flat affect that unnerved Cat more than any retaliation.29 When Cat sent an associate to photograph Kay's home in May 2025, he found signs she had planted around the house reading “Hi Catherine,” and a swatting Cat arranged against her in March 2025 had no effect at all.29,27

The Frankfurt Cache

Kay had arranged a “break glass” safeguard with Spinzle, who kept her own dead-man's switch on a server in Frankfurt. When Cat physically coerced Spinzle into halting the switch in November 2024, the alert reached Kay, who used her mirrored credentials to download the entire archive—ledgers, logs, wallet addresses and dates—into cold storage. The “Echo of Frankfurt” meant the evidence that would end Cat's presidency was safe even as Spinzle was forced into exile.30 A canary-token operation against the associate Alaric, Operation A3P, later confirmed that financial and operational control converged at the same Leeds address, tying it directly to Cat.31

Kay's work occasionally drew her into the physical world. After Cat lured Adelynn into a trap in April 2024, Kay met Adelynn and the victim, Tierra, in person and sheltered Tierra until her family returned.11 In August 2024 she received the former minister 7orB at her Sacramento home after Cat had terrorized him, though she declined to travel south with him.32

Exposure and collapse

In early August 2025 Kay uncovered evidence of the gravest of Cat's crimes, a pattern of coercion and self-harm she had demanded of her followers, including minors. Judging the discovery too urgent to withhold, she resolved to expose Cat with the dossier she had.33 On 19 August 2025 she sent Cat a private message telling her the torment was over, deleted Capitalia's platforms—having first moved every Capitalian safely into the District of Main—and, with Spinzle, Apollos, Snuck, 7orB and ViaTea, exposed the full extent of Cat's crimes to the community.33 The deletion of Ninzardia ended Cat's presidency, which had lasted from 20 July 2022 to 19 August 2025.8

Kay then surrendered herself to the FBI's Sacramento field office, accompanied by her brother as her proxy, presenting the Frankfurt Cache and her own archive and negotiating a Non-Prosecution Agreement to cover her unauthorized access.12 Cat was arrested in Leeds on 22 August 2025.34 Rather than trigger a dramatic sweep, Kay engineered a “controlled collapse,” framing the American accomplices as coerced, non-violent targets so that their children would not be taken, and letting psychological pressure force voluntary surrenders.35 She arranged for Elizabeth Avalon, Cat's estranged elder sister, to visit the hospitalized Mei in September 2025, a meeting that broke the victims' resistance to cooperating.36 She also ran an information-containment effort with Channeling-4 and Raffaele to keep the case out of the mainstream press.37

The investigation hollowed her out. Her brother and the member Sev watched her go days without sleep, flinch at every sound, and fall into a threat-detection state she could not switch off; the tremor in her hands grew so severe that by August 2025 she was hospitalized, and when Cat finally fell, Kay felt only anhedonia.20 She had become a Christian convert after her 2023 recovery, and reflected afterward that Cat's evil had no deeper meaning: “Evil is banal. A sequence of selfish choices made without regard for the outcome.”20,38

Minister of Justice (2025–2026)

Appointment

After returning to Ninzard, Kay became a central figure in Apollos's second Capital, helping him turn his sprawling instincts into workable strategy.39 On 1 November 2025 she publicly rebuked ViaTea during the fallout known as the “gayest breakup,” and two days later, when Apollos dissolved the office of Capital Adjudicator and split it into three ministries, he appointed Kay the first Minister of Justice—a post third in line after the president and vice president, charged with the application of the community's rules and the dignity and privacy of its members.40,1 As Minister of Justice she was the principal author of the Catx3 Dossier, the “Cat Files,” first released to leaders on 1 January 2026 and made public on 12 January.41

The controlled burn and the Deep State

With Apollos, Kay designed the “controlled burn,” a policy of allowing the Original Party's most toxic elements to expose themselves in a contained public space rather than suppressing them. “Suppression creates martyrs,” she wrote; “exposure creates evidence.”39 She met personal attacks, including a rape threat from the troll Rudolf, with cold dismissal—“yet here i remain. how tragic for you”—refusing the emotional reaction her opponents sought.39 Her command of the community's surveillance earned her and Channeling-4 the name of the “Ninzard Deep State,” though its members saw themselves as guardians rather than rulers.9 In the Charter debates of December 2025 she argued plainly for written law: “with no charter, every ban is a debate. with it, every ban is a reflex.”42

The Chud War

When shots were fired at Aceiron's house during the Chud War on 14 February 2026, Kay managed the response, instructing those present to say nothing of Ninzard to the police and moving Aceiron and his mother to a hotel.43 Over the following week she corroborated GPS telemetry, vehicle identification and message logs, and on 21 February 2026 she named the shooter as Tristen Mercer and designated the Liberation Party a criminal entity, closing the active phase of the crisis.44

Days later, during the “Samson Switch” coup of 25 February 2026, Kay worked with Raffaele to neutralize NGin's attempt to blackmail the community with a dead-man's switch wired to its servers; she reviewed the nullified payload and confirmed it held no child-exploitation material, and a member who had proposed including such material was permanently banned.45,46

The Soft-Landing Protocol

On the night of 29 March 2026, when Aceiron was critically injured at Snuck's Los Angeles apartment, Kay was brought into the crisis call and responded with chilling utility, pulling LAPD dispatch and EMS scanner traffic to confirm that Aceiron was alive and in hospital and that Snuck was in custody.47 Her subdued administrative update, part of a deliberate “soft landing” of the news through trusted intermediaries, kept the community from spiralling into conspiracy.47

The Holloway Summit

On 2 May 2026 Kay and her brother Malachi hosted the Holloway Summit at the family home, the gathering of thirty members from every faction that ended years of hostility in the community.48,49 From her monitors she watched the terrified Holy Iron Flank idle in their car in the driveway and, bridging her microphone to the vehicle's speakers, told them to “put the metal sticks in the ceramic umbrella stand and come eat.”48 As the reconciliation unfolded and the guests prepared to leave, the “Eye of Sauron” wept openly for the first time—the moment the community finally saw Mother Ninzard not as an architect of surveillance but as a woman who had carried its terror for too long.50

Death of Soda and final day

After the member Soda took his own life on a voice call on 18 May 2026, Kay issued the statement confirming his death and imposed a zero-tolerance ban on anyone who distributed footage of it.51 Throughout the administration's final months she continued to suffer health complications and remained an object of suspicion, especially among the far right.52 On 1 July 2026 she posted her final statement, announcing that she had permanently deleted her surveillance architecture—“the panopticon is closed. you have your privacy back”—and transferred her Capital permissions to Alessia and Gareth.10 Raffaele succeeded her as the 2nd Minister of Justice under President Poka on 2 July 2026.2

Character and methods

Kay was defined by an impenetrable flat affect. Cat, who controlled others by finding a “lever”—a fear, a secret, a need for validation—could find none in Kay, whom she privately described as worse than her own sister Eliza: Eliza was intellectually superior, but Kay was intellectually dangerous, and the only obstacle to her domination of Ninzard.27 Where Cat operated on the application layer of social manipulation, Kay operated on the network layer, with a technical literacy rooted in genuine computer engineering.28 She was, by her own account, not someone who cared to intervene for others; she was a quiet mover who acted only when she judged it necessary.3 Her critics saw something darker: the Scope member Liver called her “the Eye of Sauron” and a technological abomination who played God, even while acknowledging her capacity to enact justice against real evil.53
