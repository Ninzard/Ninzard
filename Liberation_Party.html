The Liberation Party, whose members were known as Liberators, was a left-wing political party of the Ninzard Project, founded on 19 November 2025 by ViaTea as a challenge to both major parties. It grew out of the Liberation Movement she had led against President Cat since 2023, and merged with Triste's militant Liberation Front.1,2

In alliance with Aceiron's Ories it contested the December 2025 elections, which it marred with raids on three districts and in which it won only Parry, through Power of Greed. PoG then led the party until the Chud War of February 2026, after which Minister of Justice Kay designated it a criminal entity and it dissolved.3,4,5
