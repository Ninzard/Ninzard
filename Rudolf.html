Tianhao Wu (born 11 April 2004), known as Rudolf, is a Ninzard gamer and former politician who served as the 8th District General of Parry from 3 January 2023 until Parryans voted to remove him on 26 October 2025. He had earlier been the 8th District General of Sacraday, from February 2018 until the district was abolished that July.1,2,3

An abrasive, openly toxic gamer, he won Parry as an independent in December 2022 on the district's weariness with Xorbit's runaway expansion, restored its name, and won six consecutive terms. After defying Governor Adelynn's bans of extremists in 2024 he became the Ories' de facto leader for a time, and in 2025 he joined the Sactown Pound, whose rebuff by Hyperio, aimed above all at him, gave Ninzard the retort “GSB.”2,4,5,6

Under President Apollos's second administration he drove the opposition's attacks on the Capital, threatened Kay with sexual violence and embraced Holocaust denial. Discord terminated his account on 22 October 2025, and four days later 97 of 118 Parryans voted to remove him. He went on to lead the Sactown Rivalists in the Chud War, lost his seat in Legislature XI for aiding NGin's Samson Switch, and in August 2026 led the “Sacback Room” in Parry's Salon War.7,8,3,9,10,11

Early years (2017–2018)

Rudolf joined Ninzard in 2017 through the Third Faction, where he became an administrator but found the community “too slow” and its members bad at video games. He was one of the few newcomers from the Third Faction allowed into Sacraday.1,2

General of Sacraday

In February 2018 Sacraday voted overwhelmingly for a snap election to replace its erratic general, Elsy, and on 14 February Rudolf won the generalship for the Original Party, endorsed by President Apollos, Governor DegreeZ, Deputy Governor Aceiron and Generals Kain, Alaric and Clayton. He made a scene from the first day and drove up the district's activity, which won him a personal following and the Ories' favor.1

After the death of Tiny2 later that year, Rudolf demanded that Governor Faber “step down,” dismissing the death in terms that outraged the Resistance Party; Apollos refused their demand to expel him from the Original Party. When former Governor Holiday died soon after, Sacraday descended into chaos that Rudolf could not control, and his public charge that Faber's negligence had led to her death was condemned on all sides, Apollos included.12 At the last meeting of the old Legislature he offered himself for the presidency and was dismissed at once; he then demanded the governorship of Ninzardia and was refused. Sacraday was abolished on 8 July 2018.13,14,2

Ninzardia (2018–2022)

Rudolf stayed on in the Ninzardian Faction as an administrator who enforced order through the fallout of Gold Road, the closing of Solorado and Sacraday and the RSN War. For all his aggression, he is credited with helping to steady Ninzardia at an uncertain time.15,2 On 22 September 2018 he lost the election for Governor of Ninzardia to Logan, who criticized him for staff abuse and emotional volatility.15

He later moved into Nightwork-Parry, then known as BobaBitcoin.16,2 A frequent Minecraft player, he was seen as an important presence in the community's gaming culture, and he moderated debates in several elections. In 2022 he was among the men who began to question the female leadership then holding the vice presidency, the Nightian governorship and several generalships.2,17

General of Parry (2023–2025)

2022 election

Rudolf had not wanted to run for general. Though formally an Orie, he despised Hokan's Original Party and its aggressive expansion, which had doubled BobaBitcoin from 135 to 270 members under Xorbit, and when Xorbit ran for three offices at once Rudolf entered the race as an independent to stop him. His reputation as a debate moderator helped him pass the threshold of support needed to stand.2,18 The Preservates nominated Aurase, whom Rudolf blamed for Xorbit's rise and attacked with homophobic slurs. Of 176 votes, Rudolf won 71, Xorbit 59 and Aurase 46.2

On taking office on 3 January 2023, Rudolf declared the name “BobaBitcoin” invalid, and the district's members became simply Parryans again, a signal against Orie expansionism and for the district's own identity within Nightwork.2 He appointed Atria as Parry's chief of administration on 9 January, and removed her on 16 October on suspicion of ties to Cat.19

Cat's courtship

In the presidential election of January 2023, Rudolf moderated a debate between Cat and Hokan, openly mocking Hokan, and admitted voting for Cat only because he hated her opponent.20 Cat took him for a natural ally, but her attempts to court him on Discord went nowhere. In February 2023 she flew to California and turned up at his home with her enforcer Toby, and arranged a date for him with one of her operatives, Calmsy, meant to bind him to her network. The date ended early, and a week of surveillance outside his home convinced Cat that he had nothing she could use against him.20 She resolved instead to fund a moderate Preservate against him in July, but the uproar over Apollos's unmasking as “General Assailant” took her attention, and Rudolf was re-elected without facing the challenger she had planned to fund.20,21

Under Adelynn

Rudolf blamed Xorbit for wrecking Parry and Governor Spinzle for allowing it, and shared Governor Adelynn's aim of equalizing the districts, though one of his stated reasons was antisemitic. With her support he cut Parry from 270 members to 160 by June 2023.18,22 Adelynn engaged with him as she did with Cat and Lyman, in the hope of redemption or at least of containing him.23 When Apollos revealed that he had run the “General Assailant” account in Waffle, Rudolf insulted him and proposed that every member be questioned individually.24

Early in 2024 he readmitted Lyman, the former General of Scope known for neo-Nazi rhetoric. Adelynn then banned at least a dozen members of Parry, Lyman among them, for extreme and discriminatory rhetoric, and Rudolf, his Parryans and the Ories compared her to a dictator.25 Standing up to her as a self-declared defender of free expression made him the party's de facto leader, until Aceiron moved to fill the vacuum at its head.5 In December 2024 Apollos attacked Aceiron for doing nothing to stop Rudolf's Parryans from racially abusing AJ, Volley's general; Rudolf answered with a torrent of personal abuse.26

He was re-elected in July 2023 against Gui with 55.2% of the vote, and then against Gui and Aurase four more times, reaching 84.8% in December 2024. By June 2025 he had won six consecutive terms.27,28,29,30,4 PoG served on his staff in a mid-level administrative post.31

Sactown Pound

In July 2025 Rudolf joined the Sactown Pound, about two dozen members, mostly men from Parry and Volley, who patrolled Sacramento believing that Cat and Meikaqu were hiding there.32 Being of Chinese descent, he became the main target of the group's racial mockery, and he answered in kind; his involvement also helped make ScottyAte's cat the unofficial mascot of Parry.33,34 On 25 July, when the Pound came to Hyperio's auto shop to recruit him, Rudolf insisted that Hyperio owed it to the community. Hyperio put a hand on his shoulder, walked all thirteen of them out, and sent them off with the words that, shortened to “GSB,” were thrown at Rudolf across Parry before becoming a stock retort throughout Ninzard.6 When the Pound broke up, Rudolf went back to his district.35

Downfall and removal

On election day in September 2025 Rudolf dismissed Apollos's election-day video as “Cringevengers.” When Apollos and Spinzle rebuilt the district servers, they handed Parry back to him only after the work was done, which he took personally; Apollos apologized and thanked him for his patience.36 Of the five generals, he and Mel got on worst with the new president. On 23 September, after Capital Adjudicator Idodo persuaded the generals to keep public discussion closed for a night, Rudolf joined in, in what the archive calls a rare display of solidarity that would never occur again.37

「I also endorse the idea that everyone should touch grass.」

— General Rudolf, 23 September 2025

By mid-October he was driving the polarization. In the Orie Apparatus he promised that “in 2026, we retvrn,” and on the community's open discussion server he insulted ViaTea, accused Apollos and Spinzle of neglecting the community for their relationship, and threatened Kay with sexual violence. Kay replied: “yet here i remain. how tragic for you.”38,7,8 On 22 October he openly embraced Holocaust denial and aligned himself with NGin, and that night Discord terminated his account.8

With Parry's general incapacitated, Apollos announced on 23 October a vote on whether to remove him, requiring two-thirds of Parryans, and endorsed the Preservate Phalth as his successor. The referendum opened on 24 October; on 26 October, 97 of 118 voters (82.2%) chose to remove him, leaving only 21 to keep him.8,3 Boygho, who won the snap election two days later, opened his victory statement: “I'm tired talking about Rudolf.”3 His removal cleared the way for what the archive calls a “Rationalist Restoration” in the Original Party.39

After the generalship (2025–2026)

Rudolf was soon back. When the community's trolls took over its new Charter forum in December 2025, Rickard dared him to post antisemitic memes and earn a second ban, and by early 2026 he sat in Legislature XI as one of Parry's representatives.40,10 On 27 January 2026 he unleashed a stream of slurs, threats and antisemitic abuse at other representatives, invited NGin to join his “counteralliance,” and protested that the point of a Charter discussion was discussion when Shuffle moved to mute him.41

Chud War

On Valentine's Day 2026 Rudolf led the Sactown Rivalists, a group close to Aceiron's supporters, who believed Cat's influence was to blame for Nina's death. They besieged the home of Meikaqu, then on probation, until the Sacramento police arrived, and circulated the addresses of Sacramento Liberators and of NGin.9 That night, as the Holy Iron Flank massed outside Aceiron's house, Rudolf messaged NGin that they had knives ready; minutes later Aceiron spoke in the negotiation channel, and Rudolf was among the dozen of his unarmed supporters who, at his direction, walked calmly toward NGin's men.42

Samson Switch

When NGin tried to seize the Orie Apparatus on 25 February 2026, he threatened to flood Main, Scope and Parry with gore through webhooks planted in their channels, the one in Parry with Rudolf's help. Raffaele defused the switch, and Rudolf and NGin were removed as representatives for their part in the coup.10 ScottyAte was elected to Parry's seat and took office on 28 February.43

Salon War

After Kaimachi became General of Parry in July 2026, the district's disaffected men, aligned with the Ories' Rationalist Flank, organized as the “Sacback Room” and raided the Parry Girls' events through August.44,45 Rudolf and BadSpirit tried to derail the Parry Trials Kaimachi called for 14 August.11 When she invited him to the stage that night, he opened with a string of slurs and insults, then accused her of ignoring the fifty men who found the district's devotion to Apollos “annoying.” She agreed with him, and then handed him the men's events.46

Kaimachi: Congrats. You run men's events now. For real. It's yours. Channel, event slot, staff seat, the whole thing. You've been yelling at me for a month that nobody plans anything for the Sacback Room. Okay. Now the guy planning it is you. Go.

Rudolf: The fuck? Are you being serious?

Kaimachi: Dead serious.

Giving her enemies real seats and channels, the Sacback Room's among them, ended the Salon War.46

Personal life

Rudolf is Chinese American and stands six feet tall. He lives with his mother in suburban California and rarely leaves the house.20 He plays Minecraft and Call of Duty, and is known for yelling erratically while he plays; the shouting matches with his mother that carry over his open microphone are well-known lore in Parry, where they passed into the district's slang.2,47
