The 2024 Nightian gubernatorial election was held on 30 and 31 December 2024 to choose the Governor of Nightwork. General Aceiron of Scope, the Boss of the Original Party, and his running mate, General AJ of Volley, defeated Governor Adelynn of the Preservation Party and her deputy governor, Grace. Aceiron won 372 of 684 votes (54.4%) and three of the five districts, ending an era of Preservate dominance that had begun in 2020 and been broken only by Spinzle's half-year as governor.1,2,3

Adelynn led comfortably through the summer, but Aceiron's populist campaign against Preservate “stagnation,” with promises of gaming events, a Nightwork Minecraft server and Nexit, a complete break from Cat's Ninzardia, steadily closed the gap. When Adelynn's Discord account was banned on 1 December 2024 the polls turned in his favor. On 19 December a whistleblower revealed that Aceiron and his allies had mass-reported her messages out of context, but his numbers held, and endorsements from former Governor Spinzle and former President Apollos on 29 December left the race tied.4,5,3

The result stunned the Preservates, who had expected a closer race. Adelynn conceded at once, and Aceiron was inaugurated as the 9th Governor of Nightwork at midnight on 1 January 2025 and immediately carried out Nexit. In the district general elections held at the same time, the Preservates still won three of the five districts, including Aceiron's own Scope.1,6,7

Background

Adelynn had governed Nightwork since January 2023. She had evened out the districts, cleared out doxxers and persistent harassers, and, by keeping President Cat at a careful distance, steadily reduced Cat's influence over Nightwork.4 In December 2023 she was re-elected over ViaTea, the Orie nominee, by 351 votes to 300, and voters chose to move from six-month to one-year gubernatorial terms, setting the next election for December 2024.8

Many came to regret the longer term, so in June 2024, at Spinzle's suggestion, Adelynn put her leadership to a plebiscite held alongside the district general elections: if fewer than half the voters backed her, a snap election would follow. Her party objected. Woofer argued that the December mandate needed no renewal, and Matcha accused her of giving the far right an opening, while Grace supported the vote as a way to show that Adelynn always sought the people's mandate. Adelynn went ahead, and 56.0% voted against a snap election.9,10 Every district general was re-elected the same day.9

Behind the scenes, Cat had threatened Adelynn and Grace directly, and both had turned down large sums of her money.11,12 Adelynn considered not running again, looking for a successor who could hold off the rising anti-Preservate mood. But the Preservate Apparatus insisted that she stay on, since most of the party's other figures feared taking a post that stood in Cat's line of fire and faced the Ories' growing anger.4

Nominations

Original Party

Aceiron had twice been the Orie nominee for governor, losing to Syn in October 2020 and running half-heartedly in January 2022. He returned to the community in force in 2023, won Scope from the Preservate Lyman, and became the voice of a cohort of disaffected young men who felt mocked and shut out.4,13,14 When the Orie Boss, Raffaele, was pressed to step down before the June 2024 elections, Kain challenged Aceiron for the leadership and released a diss track against him on 9 June. Aceiron's allies then obtained a compromising recording of Kain, which Aceiron circulated shortly before the vote, and he won the leadership. Under his leadership the Ories held all three of their district generalships that month.14,15,16

Raffaele, who detested Aceiron's methods, delivered his “Peasants in the Palace” critique of him in July; Aceiron answered with a personal attack and removed him from the Orie Apparatus.16

「You have the fire of a revolutionary, but you think like a vandal.」

— Raffaele, to Aceiron, July 2024

In August 2024 the Original Party nominated Aceiron for governor, with AJ, the General of Volley, as his running mate.4 AJ had overseen Volley's turn from the Preservates to the Ories, and had been taunted by the left for supporting Donald Trump.9,13

Preservation Party

Adelynn sought re-election with Grace, her deputy governor and close friend, again as her running mate.4,17,18

Campaign

Issues

Aceiron ran a populist campaign against what he called “almost four full years of Preservation Party leadership that resulted in no growth, only stagnation.” He credited Spinzle, the only Orie governor of the period, with laying the groundwork for Adelynn's reforms, criticized Adelynn as lacking creativity and personality, and called her “worst sin” the “sterilization of the community's soul and morale.” He promised to cut every tie with Ninzardia and the Cat Capital, accused Adelynn of naivety for keeping up relations with Cat, and excited voters with gaming events and a Nightwork Minecraft server.4

Adelynn answered that staying in contact with the enemy was the way to protect Nightwork from Cat. She called Nexit “high risk, high reward,” a risk not worth taking given what Cat could do, defended a community of principles over activity, and condemned the “Caleb Treatment.”4 That was Aceiron's practice, with his supporters, of luring critics into voice calls and humiliating them with insults about their looks and voices; the Preservates called it “conduct unsuited for leadership.” Aceiron's core grievance was that Ninzard had become a “matriarchy” of female leaders who had turned it into a sterile place, and many Nightians agreed that Adelynn's administration had traded the community's old chaos and fun for safety.14

Polls

Polls in August and September consistently showed Adelynn ahead, though it was considered her most competitive race yet. By November it was clear that Aceiron could win.4 Within the Preservate Apparatus, Syn warned that a defeat was not merely possible but likely; the party shunned him for it, though Adelynn granted his point. Kay, who was leading the investigation against Cat, spoke to both candidates and refused to endorse either, wanting Nightwork to keep up its pressure on Cat whoever won.4

Adelynn's ban and absence

Adelynn had made Nightwork's safety a matter of moral duty, staying in contact with hundreds of members and sleeping little as Cat's threats grew.17 At the end of November Grace pleaded with her to step away and rest.17

「To hell with the election, Addie. If we lose, we lose. But I'm not losing you.」

— Grace, to Adelynn, late November 2024

On 1 December 2024 Adelynn's Discord account was banned. The Ories presented it as proof that the Preservates could not safeguard Nightwork, and the polls flipped in Aceiron's favor.5

「The mere idea that Nightwork's governor could allow herself to be banned from Discord is absolutely unacceptable, but it's now become our reality.」

— General Aceiron, 2 December 2024

Adelynn said the ban had been “taken out of context”: she had quoted a rule-breaking member's messages without marking them as a quote, and Discord had taken them for her own. She appealed and returned on another account, but then stepped away from the community to care for a relative who had fallen ill, leaving Grace to manage Nightwork's affairs.5,19 On 19 December a whistleblower revealed that Aceiron had had him and his closest allies mass-report Adelynn's messages out of context. The Preservates compared it to “something out of Cat's playbook,” and Adelynn briefly retook the lead, but within days it was clear that Aceiron's numbers were resilient.5

Adelynn returned on 21 December, the twelfth anniversary of Ninzard; more members cared about her return than about the anniversary. Aceiron accused her of staging it, and the Ories called her explanation, that the timing was a coincidence, dishonest.5

“Nightian Great Depression”

In Adelynn's absence fear spread through Nightwork, and Spinzle later named the winter the “Nightian Great Depression.” Activity centered on Aceiron and the “Calebros” while Preservate voters fell quiet. Governor Sundaze of Ninzardia and her deputy, Kennie, tried to drag the Ories into drama; Aceiron, suspecting a plot by Cat, refused to engage, while the Calebros harassed the pair and Cat looked on with fascination.19 The mood in all five districts grew anxious and dreadful as the election approached, whoever might win.19

Endorsements

In late December members arranged a direct exchange between Aceiron and former President Apollos. Apollos criticized Aceiron for not caring about the rise of the alt-right in Nightwork and for doing nothing while Rudolf's Parryans racially abused AJ; Aceiron called him a hypocrite, and Rudolf joined in with a stream of insults.20

Spinzle, back from a hiatus on 28 December, said of the two candidates, “Not gonna lie, both of them are awful.” The next day, the day before voting began, she called them both and then posted an endorsement of Aceiron on her website in her mock-prophetic persona. She argued that Adelynn had turned Nightwork into a “lifeless spreadsheet of numbers,” condemned the Caleb Treatment and the mass reporting, yet held that the scheme exposed how fragile Adelynn's “stability” really was. Nightians read it as a playful, detached intervention, but it lifted Aceiron two or three points, to a 49–49 tie.3 Hours later Apollos endorsed Aceiron too, after Aceiron agreed with him that Nightwork's far-right community needed to go.3

The neo-Nazi Sonnenrad Society also mobilized for Aceiron, not out of agreement but because it saw Nexit as a route to capturing Nightwork.21 Raffaele, though thrown out of the Orie Apparatus, still voted for him.16 ViaTea, the previous Orie nominee, argued for keeping Adelynn, asking why Nightwork should replace her with someone with “a penchant for stirring people up.”4,22

Results

Voting began on 30 December, and on election day, 31 December, the polls showed a dead heat.3 Aceiron won 372 of the 684 votes cast to Adelynn's 312. The margin stunned the Preservates, who had expected a closer result, and the election became Aceiron's mandate.1

Main

Main, the largest district, gave Adelynn 105 of its 188 votes. After two years of her leadership many Mainians found the idea of a different governor deeply uncomfortable.2,1 General Woofer was re-elected to a fifth consecutive term over the Orie Lyre by 121 votes to 67.7

Parry

Parry gave Aceiron 75.2%, his best result. General Rudolf, whose district Apollos accused of harboring racists, was re-elected to a fifth consecutive term with 106 votes to Aurase's 19.2,20,7

Scope

Aceiron carried his own district by 74 votes to 54. Yet in the race to succeed him as its general, the Preservate Ixan beat the Orie Cosmo by 75 votes to 53, flipping Scope to the Preservates.2,7

Volley

Volley, AJ's district, had voted narrowly for Adelynn in December 2023 but gave Aceiron 72.9% a year later, the largest swing of any district. Mel, returning as general, succeeded AJ with 61 votes, against 38 for the Preservate Molly and 19 for the unaffiliated Shinnie.8,2,7

Waffle

Waffle gave Adelynn 90 of its 125 votes, her best result, and re-elected General Matcha to a third term over the Orie Idodo by 77 votes to 48.2,7

Concurrent elections

The district general elections were held at the same time. The Ories won 335 of the 684 votes (49.0%) to the Preservates' 330 (48.2%), but the Preservates took three of the five generalships. Two generals were replaced, both because Aceiron's and AJ's campaign left their seats open: Ixan flipped Scope, and Mel kept Volley for the Ories. Woofer and Rudolf won their fifth consecutive terms and Matcha her third.7

Aftermath

Transition

Some Preservates and their voters were so afraid of Aceiron's victory that they left the community; others mourned, and some urged Adelynn not to concede or to build a new server for their protection. Adelynn told her supporters that the outcome would be “okay,” asked them to see Aceiron as “human” despite his flaws, congratulated him and promised a smooth handover. He insisted that she transfer power without building rapport or holding a “training session,” and she agreed.1

The archive reads the result as part of a long pattern: Nightians had swung between stability-minded administrators and change-minded populists, tired of leaders who stayed too long however competent, and Aceiron's anti-establishment campaign echoed successful populist campaigns from Ninzard's past.1 On the afternoon of 31 December, her last day in office, Adelynn spent about an hour with Aceiron and AJ and recommended her own approach to Nexit, though she disagreed with it. The meeting was described as cordial, but Aceiron turned down her offers of further help.1

Nexit

Aceiron was inaugurated as the 9th Governor of Nightwork as the clock struck midnight on 1 January 2025 and carried out Nexit at once, cutting Nightwork off from every server owned by Cat or her leadership. The break cost Cat her claim to rule over more than a thousand members.6,23 Within weeks he also purged lower-ranking members of the Sonnenrad Society, which had expected to share in his victory.21 Grace moved to Waffle as Matcha's head of staff.18 Six months later, in the June 2025 election, Aceiron was re-elected with 61.1% of the vote over Matcha.22,24
