Lora is a member of the Parry Staff under General Kaimachi. She is best known as the former girlfriend of Aceiron, whom she left in 2023 as his undiagnosed mental illness took hold of him, after pleading with him to seek help.1,2,3 At the Parry Trials of 14 August 2026 she drew laughter from Kaimachi and the district by describing herself as someone who leaves “servers, people, Caleb.”4

Relationship with Aceiron

Lora dated Aceiron until 2023, when she left him because of what he was becoming. He was suffering from severe, undiagnosed post-traumatic stress and had fallen into obsessive rituals; he grew less communicative and spent less time with her, stopped going to church and spent hours a day reading Ninzard's chatrooms, fearful that President Cat would hurt the people he cared about.2 When she tried to intervene, pleading with him to get professional help for his agoraphobia and compulsions, he reacted with explosive rage and refused to accept that he had obsessive-compulsive disorder.3

Aceiron never spoke of the breakup, and his friends assumed he had been dumped. They noticed a colder edge in him afterward, and he went back from an active social life to the community's politics and to coding and game modding.1 His rivals used her against him for years: Kain's diss track of 9 June 2024 mocked him over her,5 and in a quarrel in Legislature XI in early 2026 Syn told him that Lora had left him out of pity and exhaustion, while Al Commando posted a screenshot of her being friendly with him.6

After his head injury in March 2026, Aceiron told friends over dinner in Seattle that the hospital had given him time to think about the things he had done and said, and about Lora. It was a name he had rarely spoken.7

「Lora just wanted a stable partner. I just pushed her away. By the end, I wasn't even looking at her. I thought if I accumulated enough control, it would compensate for the fact that she left. I blamed everyone but myself.」

— Aceiron, at dinner in Seattle, 2026

Parry Trials (2026)

On 14 August 2026, at the end of the Parry Trials, the public tribunal in which Kaimachi let her critics prosecute her generalship, Lora came to the microphone without a complaint.4

Lora: I'll be honest, I only came in for this because I heard it was going badly. Uh—I don't even have a complaint. I just leave things. Servers, people, Caleb. It's kind of my whole thing.

Kaimachi: Lora that's the most powerful thing anyone's said all night and you didn't even try.

Lora: I know.

Lora: You're funnier than they said.

Kaimachi: YES! THAT'S IT! THAT'S the review going on my tombstone. "Funnier than they said." Oh, Lora, don't leave THIS one, I just got here—

Lora: No promises.

Parry Staff

Lora stayed. She is a member of the Parry Staff Team under General Kaimachi.
