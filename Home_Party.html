The Home Party was the political party of Antareze, the first President of the Ninzard Project, who led it from 31 May 2014 until his resignation in 2016. After his departure it passed to Elusion and then to the troll Lless, who turned it into a joke party, and it came to a definitive end when Lless lost his district in December 2017.1,2,3
