Boygho, whose first name is Alex, is a Ninzard administrator and former politician who served as the 9th District General of Parry from 28 October to 31 December 2025. A Parry administrator from the Original Party's Rationalist Flank, which held that the community's survival depends on its activity, he won the snap election that followed Rudolf's removal and promised that Parry would govern itself.1

His short generalship was spent at odds with both the Capital and his own party's far right. When Aceiron purged the Orie Apparatus on 4 December 2025 he expelled Boygho, who withdrew from the December election and endorsed PoG, his eventual successor.2,3 Before entering office he had belonged to Seandry's Rocket Squad Nation and to the Sactown Pound; he returned to the public eye in August 2026 as one of the former generals who confronted Kaimachi at the Parry Trials.4,5,6

Early years

Boygho was a member of the Ninzard community until a falling-out with one of his best friends, DJNinja, after which he betrayed it and joined the Rocket Squad Nation, the rival community Seandry founded in 2017 out of a grudge against Ninzard.4 He later returned and settled in Parry. By 2022 he was among the men, with AJ and Rudolf, who had begun to question the female leaders then holding the vice presidency, the Nightian governorship and several generalships.7 In June 2024 he was in the Parry voice call where Kain proclaimed himself the “Yak King,” and told him to stop.8

Sactown Pound

In July 2025 Boygho joined the Sactown Pound, about two dozen members, mostly men from Parry and Volley, who patrolled Sacramento in the belief that Cat and Meikaqu were hiding there. Where Kain treated the hunt as “content,” Boygho and ScottyAte saw it as a crusade for justice.5,9 The two bombarded General Kay with demands for her “Kill List” of the addresses of Cat and her associates, calling her refusal cowardice.10 When the Pound broke up, he went back to Parry.11

Rationalist Flank

After Aceiron's defeat in the presidential election of September 2025, his coalition began to split between the Rationalists, who sought activity, and the Holy Iron Flank of NGin.12 In the Orie Apparatus Boygho predicted that Apollos's administration would let activity fade and that the president, never having wanted the burden, would grow bored, leaving 2026 open to Aceiron. When NGin answered with a tirade against “activity is purpose,” Boygho replied in one line.13

「I have no interest in being lectured about meaning by someone who has no employment, no social standing, and no education to speak of.」

— Boygho, to NGin, September 2025

General of Parry (2025)

Snap election

When Parry voted on 24–26 October 2025 on removing Rudolf, the Original Party could not even agree whether to back him, and Boygho emerged as its clear alternative. Outspoken, fairly hostile, but confident and reliable, he drew support from Ories and moderates alike, though his brash manner put some off, and his candidacy angered the party's extremists, who saw it as a return to the status quo.1 The Holy Iron Flank backed him in exchange for a greater voice in the party.14 President Apollos endorsed the Preservate Phalth, and Kain entered the race as an independent to spite the president.1 An attempt by Aceiron to campaign for Boygho in Parry's voice channels at 3 a.m. ended when Kain drove him out.15

On 28 October Boygho won 58 of the 130 votes (44.6%), to 43 for Phalth and 29 for Kain. Generals Woofer, Ixan, Mel and Grace, the president, Capital Adjudicator Idodo and Kay all congratulated him, and Phalth wished him luck; the Pragma Flankers of the Preservation Party mocked what they saw as his “moderate posturing.”1

「I'm not here to be your enemy, but Parry will govern itself. Only when it makes sense will we cooperate. We don't want to be told what's right or wrong. We will not be lectured on morality. My loyalty lies with this district and with the idea that Ninzard's existence relies on its activity.
My platform is easy to understand. A community that doesn't live, fight, or have purpose is already dead. The community of Ninzard was forged in conflict. Stagnation is a bigger problem than trolling. Drama is sometimes necessary.」

— General Boygho, victory statement, 28 October 2025

Some Preservates took heart that he had won less than a majority, a weak result for an Orie in Parry; Aurase pointed to it in the Dreadnought Debate as proof that the Ories could be beaten without enlarging the party.16

First weeks

On his first day PluPlu and Poka had their Preservate friends in Parry tell him, all at once, that the PluPlu Army was plotting a “civil war” against him. Boygho publicly demanded that the president investigate PluPlu. Apollos, after looking into it, replied that he was being trolled by some Italian girls; Boygho told him to stop acting like a child. To Apollos it was a test of how well the new general would put up with mild trolling.17

Following Aceiron's lead, Boygho and General Mel publicly asked whether the president was mentally sound, until Poka turned the rumor on its head with an obviously false announcement of the president's death.18 When the Orie nominee for Volley, NGin, released his openly Nazi campaign advertisement on 15 November, Boygho, who had endorsed him to hold the party together, came under heavy pressure to withdraw his support.2

Thanksgiving

Three of the five generals closed their districts for Thanksgiving on 27 November 2025; Boygho kept Parry open.19 Mainians invited onto Parry's new Minecraft server had their builds, some of them Italian-themed, destroyed by Parryans, and the next day the PluPlu Army raided the server, destroyed the builds of those who had harassed them and crashed it. Boygho asked publicly what the purpose of intentionally griefing a Minecraft server was; Apollos answered that the griefing had started on Parry's side.20

Expulsion

On 4 December 2025 Aceiron purged the Orie Apparatus. He expelled Mel, and he expelled Boygho, calling his Rationalism “a fallacy to cover spineless capitulation” and blaming it for “handing Parry over to the Capital” to keep activity high.3 Boygho withdrew from the coming election and endorsed PoG, the Liberation Party's nominee, calling Aceiron “a piece of shit.” Former Governor Syn judged that the purge had ended the prospects of the Orie most likely to succeed Aceiron, and Boygho admitted, to his own surprise, that he agreed.3 Syn added that Aceiron had humiliated him too publicly for him ever to drift back.21

On 13 December, when Apollos thanked General Ixan, who had stepped aside for Hyperio in Scope, for being “an earnest person without an ego,” Boygho retorted that having no ego was bad and that Apollos was one of the most egotistical people in the community. Two days later, arguing with Apollos, Cyntaku and Grace, he told them in crude terms that women could be loved and accepted without achieving anything while men could not. Apollos ended the exchange by posting a photograph of himself with his palms raised, which the community turned into a meme.22

His term ended on 31 December 2025. PoG, who had beaten the Preservate Burnette, succeeded him on 1 January 2026.23

Later years (2026)

When the Ories looked for a presidential candidate in 2026, the archive counted Boygho among the party figures who suffered from “charisma deficits.”24 PoG's success in Parry was later measured against his: where Generals Rudolf and Boygho had struggled to keep Parry's anarchic character without letting it fall into disorder, she channeled the pressure into her staff, and activity rose dramatically.25

At the Parry Trials on 14 August 2026, where General Kaimachi let her critics prosecute her generalship live, Boygho took the microphone and accused her of turning Parry into a fandom.6

Boygho: … And I look at it now and it's a Reuben shrine. You turned Parry into a fandom. That's the whole problem.

Kaimachi: 'Kay, no, first of all, Alex—Boygho, welcome back, everyone, it's the ghost of General's past—

Boygho: I'm not doing the bit.

Kaimachi: You're SO not doing the bit. You never do the bit, that's your entire personality. You're like a brick wall, bro. But no—you're right, okay? You are. … So genuinely, between the two of us? We could not run a lemonade stand. We're both disasters. Like you'd bore the customers and I'd marry the lemons.

Boygho: Fair.

Political style

Boygho is blunt and confident, and never, in Kaimachi's words, “does the bit.”1,6 As a Rationalist he holds that the community lives on activity and conflict: Parry should govern itself, cooperate with the Capital only when it pays, and refuse to be lectured on morality.1 NGin attacked this creed as a relic of Hokan's “activity is purpose,” and Aceiron as capitulation.13,3 Syn saw the Rationalists as transactional rather than loyal, valuing a stable platform above any party.21
