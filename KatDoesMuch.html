Katerina Johnson (born 10 October 2010), known as KatDoesMuch or Katdsm, is a Ninzard politician who has served as the 2nd Minister of Health & Privacy under President Poka since 2 July 2026. Elected for the Preservation Party as a Representative for the District of Waffle in March 2026, at fifteen she was the youngest elected member of Legislature XI, where the archive calls her “the ultimate reality check for the community's aging, traumatized establishment.”1,2,3

She joined in 2019 on a dormant account that had belonged to her older brother Judah, the former General of Sacraday. In 2023, at thirteen, she rebuffed President Cat's attempt to draw her in by simply leaving the server and blocking her. Deadpan and unimpressed by the community's dramas, she was re-elected to the Legislature in June 2026 before joining Poka's cabinet.1,4,3

Early life and joining

Johnson was born on 10 October 2010. On her fifteenth birthday Spinzle remarked on the date.1

Spinzle: Katerina, your birth date—10/10/10—is a statistical anomaly.

KatDoesMuch: EDITH UR SO WEIRD ITS A NUMBER U SOUND LIKE A FREAK

She joined the server in 2019 using an old, dormant account that had belonged to Judah, the “infamous” Orie General of Sacraday, whom she calls her “DUMBASS BROTHER.” For a time she claimed to have been born in 2008, because the older members treated her like a toddler.1 Asked what the “dsm” in her username stood for, she gave two answers. She told the Capital that her brother had added it to the account years earlier and that it stood for the Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders; to her friends she said it stood for “KatDoingSoMuch.” She is also known as KJ and KatNotCat.1

Unlike the “RadicaLs” elected around the same time, Lesty, Liver, Lless and Lyman, she had a stable, loving home life, which the archive treats as her defining advantage over all of them.1

Cat (2023)

In the summer of 2023, when President Cat was building her “Meowsalute” cult and targeting young, isolated and vulnerable members, the thirteen-year-old Kat was in Catsland. Cat singled her out, offering special roles and private voice calls and trying to isolate her, but Kat, close to her parents, found her creepy rather than compelling. She left the server, blocked Cat and went downstairs to eat dinner with her family.1 In an essay of 29 April 2026, Lesty held up her refusal as “the moral floor of this community.”5

Representative for Waffle (2026)

In the snap elections of March 2026, which followed a wave of resignations from Legislature XI, Waffle elected Kat and Lesty to replace Shuffle, who was elected General of Waffle at the same time, and ViaTea.6,2 Wafflers chose her because she was one of the few who treated the community like a joke without being a malicious troll.1 Invited into the Preservate Apparatus, she announced at once that she regretted joining, dismissed a 21-year-old member as a “boomer” and answered Syn's lament about the community's anti-intellectualism.7

Syn: This is exactly the kind of anti-intellectual, post-literate infantilization that allows the vandals to consolidate power. This community proudly flaunts its own ignorance.

KatDoesMuch: u type like u hold ur breath before u hit enter

Hackman asked the others to try not to traumatize “Judah's sister.”7 She took her seat on 2 April 2026, one of eleven Preservates among the fifteen representatives.8

In the chamber she was a steady deflator of long debates (“u guys have been yapping for an hour straight and accomplished literally zero”) and of grand quarrels.9 When PoG attacked Woofer's past under General Geo on 25 April, Kat called her “so weird” for “beefing with a minor's trauma response.”10 That night she joined PluPlu, Poka and Kaimachi in a clash with Liver, the traditionalist Catholic Representative for Scope. When Liver began typing a Latin exorcism into the channel, Kat observed that she was “literally typing out an exorcism in a discord server,” and Liver called her “the dead end of Western civilization.”11 Two days later the four were roasting Liver across her own district.12 When ScottyAte and Lless traded threats a few days later, Kat remarked: “boys will literally plot interstate homicide instead of getting a hobby 💀.”13 Of the Holloway Summit of 2 May she said only that she was “kinda mad” not to have been invited.14

After Soda's death on 18 May 2026 she posted a statement.15

「Lucca was a severely mentally ill guy who got stuck in an echo chamber with people who enabled his sickness. If you're one of the freaks laughing, you're also deeply unwell. This is a really tragic reality check of what happens when the internet becomes your entire world. If you are hurting, please talk to someone. Take care of each other cause we should be there for each other when we hit rock bottom. Rest in peace, Lucca.」

— Representative KatDoesMuch, 18 May 2026

Later that night, when Lless dismissed the tributes of PluPlu and others as a “pathological” bid for good-person points, she told him that people in the real world simply say they are sorry for someone's loss and check on their friends, called him “just a freak” and told him to log off. Lless did not answer. Closing the day, Woofer told the community: “Kat is right. You can't intellectualize a broken heart.”15

Minister of Health & Privacy (2026–present)

In June 2026 Waffle re-elected her first of its two representatives, with 105 votes (80.8%), alongside Lesty; it was the only district to return its delegation unchanged.4 Poka then nominated her Minister of Health & Privacy, the cabinet office Apollos had created in November 2025 to look after the community's well-being, from keeping members safe from harm and toxicity to encouraging good health in real life.16,17,3 She was confirmed with the rest of the cabinet and succeeded Idodo on 2 July 2026, and Rae Fox won the snap election for her seat in Waffle.3

During the Salon War in Parry that August she was among the members of the Preservate Apparatus, with Cyntaku, Aurase, Seandry, Syn and others, who pressed for the Capital to intervene. Poka overruled them all and told them to stand down, staking everything on Kaimachi.18

Personal life

Kat has a close relationship with her parents; her older brother is Judah.1 She writes in a deadpan, lowercase register, treats most of the community's grand dramas as absurd and is quick to tell older members to log off or go outside. Woofer once thanked her for “keeping us humble.”7,19
