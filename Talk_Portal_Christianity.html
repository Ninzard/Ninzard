  2. A word on the windows, since the whole page is built out of them. When Abbot Suger rebuilt Saint-Denis he had a line cut into the bronze doors: mens hebes ad verum per materialia surgit. The dull mind rises to truth through material things. That was the theology of the Gothic window. The glass does not make the light. It receives it, and it is only beautiful because it consents to be passed through.
  3. Aquinas says the same of grace: gratia non tollit naturam, sed perficit. Grace does not erase the colour of the window. It is what makes the colour visible. Crimson stays crimson, blue stays blue, and none of them is the source. Twenty-two windows, twenty-two colours, one Light.
