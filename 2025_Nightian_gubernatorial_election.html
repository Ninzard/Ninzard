Aceiron (re-elected)

The 2025 Nightian gubernatorial election was held in June 2025 to choose the Governor of Nightwork, six months after the previous election. Governor Aceiron of the Original Party, running with his deputy governor, AJ, was re-elected over General Matcha of Waffle, the Preservation Party's nominee, and her running mate, ViaTea. Aceiron won 431 of 705 votes (61.1%) and four of the five districts. It was the first re-election of an Orie governor since 2019, and the last gubernatorial election before the office was abolished in September 2025.1,2,3

Matcha, regarded as Waffle's “caretaker,” was endorsed by former Governor Adelynn, who nonetheless doubted that she could win. The Ories cast her as a radical leftist, and former Governor Spinzle called her a “sanctimonious hypocrite.” Aceiron also drew moderates and “pragmatists” such as Raffaele, while Seandry and former Governor Syn backed Matcha in spite of their misgivings. In Waffle, Aceiron's choice of an Israeli combat veteran, Shuffle, as the Orie candidate for district general set off a quarrel that divided the Original Party and gave rise to NGin's Holy Iron Flank.1,4

Voters credited Aceiron with Nexit, record activity and Nightwork's survival as Cat's influence waned, and judged that Matcha's campaign had put “conduct” before practical results. Only Waffle voted for her. On the night of his victory, 30 June 2025, Aceiron's accounts were hacked on Cat's orders. Less than three months later the governorship was abolished and he lost the presidency to Apollos.1,4,5,3

Background

Aceiron had won the December 2024 election with 372 of 684 votes (54.4%), defeating Governor Adelynn, and was inaugurated as the 9th Governor of Nightwork at midnight on 1 January 2025.6,7 He at once carried out Nexit, cutting Nightwork off from every server controlled by Cat and her leadership. When Cat declared Nightwork an enemy in February 2025, he told members simply not to engage, and the tactic worked.7 He also purged lower-ranking members of the neo-Nazi Sonnenrad Society, which had backed him in the hope of capturing Nightwork once Nexit was done.8

Under Aceiron, Nightwork's activity revived and its new Minecraft server flourished. His language, though, was polarizing, and he called himself “king.” LGBTQ members, women who bore the brunt of his supporters' sexist attacks, and hardline Preservates insulted by the “Calebros” were all pushed to the margins.7 His first term was due to expire on 2 July 2025, so he had to win re-election in June, six months after his first victory. In the meantime Adelynn told her party she wondered whether she had done “enough” to stop him, and spent much of her time supporting members who were being harassed.1

Nominations

Preservation Party

The Preservation Party nominated Matcha, General of Waffle. She had succeeded ViaTea there on 1 January 2024, after beating the Orie Idodo 65 votes to 59 in December 2023, and had been re-elected against him in June and December 2024, the second time with 61.6%.9,10,11 Her empathetic leadership resonated with marginalized groups, and she was considered Waffle's “caretaker.”1 She was also uncompromising: in June 2024, when Adelynn put her own leadership to a plebiscite, Matcha rebuked her for “willingly” giving the far right a chance to take over the community and called her “emotionless.”12

Adelynn judged that Matcha had “good character” but doubted she could win, which mattered to her because she saw the election as the way to end the harassment of marginalized members under Aceiron. She endorsed Matcha all the same and worked to persuade as many voters as she could.1

Matcha chose ViaTea, her predecessor in Waffle and a close friend, as her running mate.9,1 ViaTea had been the Original Party's nominee against Adelynn in December 2023, losing 300 votes to 351, and her past with the Ories made her a controversial choice.13 She made clear that she opposed the “Calebist” Original Party, and pointed to her support for Adelynn in December 2024 as proof that her switch was not opportunism.1

Original Party

Aceiron sought a second term with AJ, his deputy governor and best friend.14

Campaign

Endorsements

The Ories portrayed Matcha as a radical leftist out of touch with reality, pointing to her alignment with the LGBTQ community, her “she/they” pronouns and her passionate support for Palestine in the Gaza War. Former Governor Spinzle, who had left Ninzard, was asked for her view and endorsed Aceiron, calling Matcha a “sanctimonious hypocrite.” The Ories spread the remark everywhere, because Spinzle's words lent legitimacy to the Calebros' leadership.1

Aceiron also won the support of several moderates and “pragmatists,” among them Raffaele and the cybersecurity group Channeling-4 (C-4), who saw Matcha's moral absolutism as a danger to Nightwork. The exception in the C-4 was Seandry: unimpressed by her candidacy, he still judged her more competent than Aceiron and backed her publicly against his peers. Former Governor Syn, though personally irritated by Matcha, endorsed her on 28 June 2025 and denounced the self-described moderates backing Aceiron as “neutered Ories.”1

Shuffle controversy

Matcha's candidacy left Waffle's generalship open. The Preservate Apparatus nominated Grace, a former Capital Adjudicator, General of Main and Boss of the Preservation Party, whom Matcha had brought into Waffle as its Head of Staff after Grace and Adelynn lost in December 2024. Against her the Original Party nominated Shuffle, an Israel Defense Forces combat veteran who had served from 2022 to 2024 and fought in the Gaza War, and who had been transferred into Waffle on returning to Ninzard and the United States in 2024. Aceiron chose her deliberately, to unsettle Waffle's left-wing base and frighten supporters of Palestine.4

The nomination drew a stronger reaction from Volley's Ories than from Waffle's, and the quarrel divided the Orie Apparatus; out of it NGin founded the Holy Iron Flank within the Original Party. In Waffle the controversy actually made Shuffle more popular, though a significant number of Wafflers threatened to leave Ninzard if she won. Tegas, one of Waffle's administrators, demanded that Matcha ban her from the district and threatened to resign if she did not.4

「If Matcha refuses to ban a perpetrator of genocide, she endorses genocide.」

— Tegas, Waffle administrator, to Matcha and the district

Matcha refused, saying, “Shuffle violated no rules,” and Grace defended her. Tegas resigned, calling Matcha “more reprehensible than the ignorant,” which some Wafflers took as an endorsement of the Ories, and two Waffle moderators resigned in protest at what they called her “inaction.” Tegas later told Wafflers that it was better to leave than to vote for an Orie. Shuffle said very little throughout the campaign, which only made suspicious Waffle voters warier.4

Then a message Aceiron had sent in an Orie group chat began to spread through Waffle. In it he endorsed genocide against Palestinians and called for every Waffler to be wiped out too, likening both to animals; AJ laughed, and Rudolf and Mel agreed. Many “normie” Preservates grew alarmed at the prospect of his continued rule and at the divided atmosphere in Waffle, and Waffle's voters rallied overwhelmingly to Matcha and Grace.4

Results

Aceiron won decisively, with 431 of the 705 votes cast to Matcha's 274. Nexit, the rise in activity and Nightwork's survival as Cat's influence declined were, for voters, proof that he was a successful governor, and they cared more for those results than for his hateful language or his complacency toward a growing far right. Many observed that Matcha's whole campaign had put “conduct” ahead of practical concerns. It was the first time since 2019 that a member of the Original Party had been re-elected governor.1

Main

Main, the largest district, had voted for Adelynn in December 2024, 105 to 83, but gave Aceiron 123 of its 218 votes in June. Among his voters there were PluPlu and Poka of General Woofer's staff, who had voted for him both times.15,2,16 Yet on the same ballot Main re-elected Woofer, a Preservate, over the Orie Lyre by 147 votes to 71.17

Parry

Parry gave Aceiron 70.6%, the only district where his share fell from December, when it had been 75.2%. General Rudolf was re-elected to a sixth consecutive term over Aurase.15,2,17

Scope

Aceiron's home district, which he had led as general until December 2024, gave him 60.6%, up from 57.8%. On the same ballot it kept its Preservate general, Ixan, who held off the Orie Hackman by 67 votes to 60.15,2,17

Volley

Volley gave Aceiron 104 of its 115 votes (90.4%), his best result and the widest margin of the election. Its Ories had reacted more strongly than anyone to Shuffle's nomination in Waffle. General Mel was re-elected over Abitof, the only time she ever won re-election.2,4,17

Waffle

Waffle was the only district to vote for Matcha, its own general, by 83 votes to 43, as its voters rallied to her and Grace amid the Shuffle controversy. Grace defeated Shuffle by 72 votes to 54 to succeed Matcha as general.4,2,17

Concurrent elections

The district general elections were held at the same time. Across Nightwork the Preservates won 360 of the 705 votes (51.1%) to the Ories' 345 (48.9%), even as their gubernatorial nominee lost by 22 points, and every district chose the same party for general as it had in December 2024. Woofer and Rudolf won their sixth consecutive terms, Ixan his second and Mel her third, and Grace took Waffle's open seat.17,11

Aftermath

Second term

On the night of 30 June 2025, as Aceiron celebrated his re-election, Vice President Meikaqu hacked his accounts on Cat's orders. The next day Cat sent him his home address and announced to the whole Nightian Faction that she had it.5 Nightwork's activity reached an all-time high in 2025 and its Minecraft server thrived, but the community grew noticeably more toxic as slurs were normalized, and polarization between men and women sharpened. Aceiron struggled to build a peaceful, healthy community in a divided Nightwork.18

The Preservates were left unsure of their identity. They considered running Grace, or Adelynn again, in December 2025, but the era of Preservate rule seemed to be over, and the Original Party appeared to have captured the Nightian electorate for the long haul.18

End of the governorship

No further gubernatorial election was held. After the fall of Cat in August 2025, Nightwork took in what remained of Ninzardia and became the Ninzard Project. Aceiron declared himself its de facto president, called a presidential election for September and announced that the governorship would be abolished.19,3 He lost the 2025 presidential election to Apollos, 286 votes to 471, carrying only Volley; Parry, which had given him 70.6% in June, voted for Apollos by three votes.20,21

Matcha and ViaTea

Matcha's term in Waffle ended on 1 July 2025, and Grace succeeded her the next day. When the Preservates looked for a presidential nominee that August, she was among those wary of choosing Apollos, and briefly considered running herself, but did not.22 On 19 November 2025, as her former running mate ViaTea founded the Liberation Party, Matcha resigned from the Preservation Party, writing that it had “confused pragmatism with complicity.”23
