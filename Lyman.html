Linus Yngve Mandl (born 23 September 2004), known as Lyman, is an Austrian Ninzard politician, essayist, and controversial far-right theorist who served as the 6th District General of Scope from January to July 2023 and as a Representative for Volley in Legislature XI from April to July 2026.1,2

Joining during the COVID-19 pandemic in early 2020, Mandl approached Ninzard's political history with an anthropological fascination, memorizing its lore and frameworks while treating the community as a digital Roman Republic.3 Elected to lead Scope in January 2023 on a pragmatic Preservation Party ticket to counter the erratic tenure of Soda (Lucca Heckel), he was later ousted by Caleb Jensen in July 2023.1,4 Following his defeat, Mandl co-founded the esoteric fascist Sonnenrad Society alongside Scalen, orchestrating the administrative crisis surrounding the Administrative Transparency Act (ATA) before withdrawing in early 2026.5,6

A notorious "district hopper" who resided successively in Scope, Parry, and Volley, Mandl was elected as a Volleyan Representative in April 2026 and categorized alongside Lesty, Liver, and Lless as one of the community's "Four RadicaLs."2,3 Renowned for pairing radical traditionalist dogma, Julius Evola citations, and antisemitic rhetoric with dry Austrian humor, anime aesthetics, and philosophical treatises, Mandl abstained from ratifying the Charter of the Ninzard Project before being unseated in the June 2026 elections.7,8

Early life and arrival (2020)

Mandl was born on 23 September 2004 in Vienna, Austria, and was raised in a traditional Catholic household.3 During the onset of the COVID-19 pandemic in early 2020, seeking to practice his English and discuss European history, he joined Ninzard during the tail-end of the Spikester administration.3 Unlike casual members, Mandl treated the community's digital archives and political lineage as an anthropologist would study the classical Mediterranean, mastering the intricacies of past presidential administrations, factional splits, and founding mythologies.3

Though baptized Catholic, Mandl developed a cultural and philosophical alignment with Eastern Orthodoxy and Byzantine traditionalism, regarding it not as an emotional faith but as an aesthetic and metaphysical cudgel against modern Western liberalism.3 During the 2020–2022 period, he supported Apollos I on accelerationist and pragmatic grounds, calculating that Apollos's chaotic governance was slowly eroding the community's unhealthy foundational attachments.3

General of Scope (2023)

January 2023 election

By late 2022, the District of Scope had fallen into severe disarray under General Soda (Lucca Heckel), whose erratic moderation, violent rhetoric, and perceived nepotism under Governor Spinzle alienated the electorate.1 When Cosmo launched an independent bid on an anti-expansionist platform, Mandl declared his own candidacy.1

Approaching the Preservate leadership, Mandl struck an unexpected bargain with Grace and Adelynn: in exchange for the party's official nomination, he pledged to temper his extreme rhetoric, treat all members with basic dignity, and enforce Adelynn's equalization quotas.1 While Adelynn was initially reluctant—warning that "the ends do not justify the means"—Grace argued that Mandl was a man capable of honoring agreements, unlike the unhinged incumbent.1 On 3 January 2023, Mandl won the generalship with 30 of 58 votes (51.7%), defeating Soda (20 votes) and Cosmo (8 votes).1

Tenure and July 2023 defeat

Mandl honored his agreement with Governor Adelynn, accepting demographic transfers from Parry to equalize district populations and maintaining a structured, intellectual environment.1,9 However, he privately expressed resentment to General Assailant (Apollos's alt account), complaining that the transfer of Jewish members was "infiltrating rotten Jewish culture" into Scope—a claim Apollos dismissed as "schizophrenic."9

In the July 2023 elections, former Nightwork Deputy Governor Caleb Jensen challenged Mandl. Capitalizing on voter exhaustion with Mandl's rigid traditionalism and thinly veiled dog-whistles, Caleb promised a return to "fun" and gaming.4 Caleb defeated Mandl by 50 votes to 45.4

Following his defeat, Mandl abandoned his restrained persona, publishing a vitriolic, multi-paragraph antisemitic manifesto accusing Adelynn of betraying him to appease a "subversive demographic" and Scope of descending into cosmopolitan decadence.3 Caleb promptly banned him from Scope for "promoting hatred."4

Sonnenrad Society and the ATA crisis (2023–2026)

On 5 July 2023, following his expulsion from Scope, Mandl co-founded the Sonnenrad Society alongside former Solorado member Scalen.5 The organization operated as an insular, fascist network spanning peripheral Discord and Telegram servers, acting as an incubator for exiled far-right Nightians.5

The security in which the Preservate leadership believes itself to stand is nothing more than a fragile illusion, and we have made this visible. … Pieban has placed into our hands a poisoned chalice. You will purge your clients. All further communications are to be transferred into the secured channels.

— Lyman, addressing the Sonnenrad Society after Austrian police intervention

In late 2023, Vice President Meikaqu exploited the Sonnenrad network by engineering a modified Electron client embedded with zero-width Unicode characters containing extremist propaganda, circulating it through Pieban to Scalen and Mandl.5 When Sonnenrad members used the client, Nightwork's automated Administrative Transparency Act (ATA) bot copied the hidden strings into the public archive verbatim, causing Discord Trust & Safety to freeze the channel and provoking an investigation by the Austrian Bundeskriminalamt into Mandl's activities.5

Facing Austrian hate-speech statutes, Mandl purged the client and migrated the network to Telegram, resolving to operate through proxies like Caleb.5 Unknown to Mandl, President Cat systematically subverted the Telegram network using sleeper agents, transforming the Sonnenrad Society into an unwitting attack dog for Capital PSYOPs.6 Realizing the network had been compromised and disillusioned by the Capital's controlled burn, Mandl formally withdrew from the Sonnenrad Society on 17 January 2026.3

Legislature XI and the "RadicaLs" (2026)

After migrating from Scope to Parry, Mandl transferred to Volley in early 2026, earning a reputation as a chronic "district hopper."3 Following the impeachment of General Mel in March 2026, Mandl ran in the snap election for Volley Representative.2 Presenting himself as an articulate alternative to the neo-Nazi Holy Iron Flank (HIF) and the far-left, he finished first on 31 March 2026 with 71 of 119 votes (59.7%), taking his seat in Legislature XI.2

Alongside Lesty, Liver, and Lless, Mandl was dubbed one of the "Four RadicaLs" who influenced the final debates over the Charter.3

The "Moe Synthesis" and debate with Lless

On 4 April 2026, Mandl published his magnum opus essay, The Hyperborean Imperative and the Moe Synthesis, arguing that liberal egalitarianism was an ontological failure and proposing that the anime series K-On! served as an esoteric blueprint for an ethnostate.10

Houkago Tea Time operates entirely outside the corrupting influences of modernity, consumerism, and a relentless squabbling intrinsic to liberal democracy. … Yui Hirasawa is, in every meaningful sense, a Hyperborean ascetic operating upon a 5D astral plane. She has achieved total ego-death; she is completely detached from the grinding wheel of modern productivity. … The only character in the series who has successfully transcended the Promethean grinding of the modern materialist dialectic is Ayumu “Osaka” Kasuga. … Thus, voting rights in Ninzard are to be understood as a privilege strictly restricted to those who have ascended to an Osaka Hyperborean State.

— Lyman, The Hyperborean Imperative and the Moe Synthesis

Scope Representative Lless fiercely condemned the manifesto as "pathetic academic masturbation" and fascist escapism, sparking a multi-week intellectual feud.11 Mandl dismissed Lless's Marxist analysis as the work of an "unpaid, enthusiastic informant for the Federal Bureau of Investigation" and a "Bisexual Bolshevik."12

Reaction to Lucca Heckel's death

Following the public suicide of former General Soda (Lucca Heckel) on 18 May 2026, Lless celebrated the death as a "net positive for community safety."13 Mandl offered a solemn, traditionalist rebuke, calling Lucca a human being deserving of mourning while eviscerating Lless's cruelty.14

It is truly a marvel to witness the great Marxist-Leninist, the self-appointed vanguard of the post-left, stripped of his academic pretensions to reveal the absolute, shivering void beneath. … You resemble less a critic than a scavenger of ruins. A spiritually emasculated homunculus, so thoroughly steeped in irony and secular disenchantment that even the most elementary gestures of human sympathy have become inaccessible to you. … Your ideology is a graveyard. Auf Wiedersehen. Enjoy the void.

— Lyman, to Lless following the death of Lucca Heckel (Soda)

Charter abstention and departure

During the final legislative vote on the Ninzard Charter on 2 May 2026, Mandl was the sole member to abstain, declaring:7

The Charter is the ultimate triumph of the managerial state over organic, high-vibrational culture. I have no desire to violently obstruct the inevitable, but I shall not participate in the consecration of our own sterilization. I abstain.

— Lyman, abstaining from the Charter, May 2026

In the June 2026 elections, Mandl sought re-election as Volley Representative but was defeated, receiving fewer votes than Gale (76 votes) and Lunaix (69 votes), officially ending his legislative career.8

Political philosophy and style

Mandl's ideology was an idiosyncratic synthesis of traditionalist Catholicism, Russian cosmism, esoteric Hitlerism, and otaku subculture.3,10 He cited philosophers such as Julius Evola, René Guénon, Thomas Aquinas, and Savitri Devi, dismissing contemporary American conservatism (represented by Caleb Jensen) as "neocuckservatard" grifting devoid of metaphysical roots.3,15

Unlike the violent Holy Iron Flank led by NGin, whom Mandl openly mocked as "suburban American mutts" pretending to be Spartan warriors, Mandl was entirely non-violent in the physical world.3 When NGin led the armed march on Caleb's home during the Chud War of February 2026, Mandl remained detached in Vienna, ridiculing the participants from afar.3

In debate, Mandl favored archaic grammar, Latin theological phrases, and devastating ad hominem attacks masking a deeply cynical detachment. Despite his extremist beliefs, he maintained civil rapport with select political opponents, famously agreeing with Apollos's Ninzard Career Initiative on the grounds that it was an "Aryan duty to touch grass."10,16
