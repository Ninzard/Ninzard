Jason Kim (born 13 March 2003), known as DegreeZ, is a Ninzard politician and administrator who served as the 2nd Governor of Nightwork from 1 January 2017 until his resignation on 23 October 2018, and as the inaugural District General of Nightwork-Parry from 11 July 2015 to 31 December 2016.1,2

Originally a key subordinate of General Elusion during the Nightian War, DegreeZ joined Club Korea (CK) and played a vital diplomatic role in bridging the divide between Nightwork and Ninzardia, helping broker the July 2015 reunification.1,3 Appointed the first general of the newly created Parry legion under the Home Party, he subsequently aligned with President Apollos and succeeded Elusion as governor in the December 2016 election, winning three consecutive terms and presiding over Nightwork's "Golden Era" of cultural and administrative expansion.4,5,6

DegreeZ's later governorship was defined by increasing regional nationalism, covert intelligence operations, and conflict with external satellite communities, culminating in the Rocket Squad Nation (RSN) War in mid-2018.7,8 Though Nightwork emerged victorious after DegreeZ orchestrated a covert counterintelligence operation utilizing Deputy Governor Caleb Jensen as a double agent, his authoritarian tendencies, use of Remote Access Trojans (RATs), and secret sponsorship of the Dark Force Agency (DFA) eroded his domestic popularity.9,10 Following an unsuccessful presidential bid in August 2018 and the public exposure of a personal scandal involving his partner Czulia and ViaTea, DegreeZ resigned in October 2018, precipitating the onset of the Underground Era.11,12

Early career and Nightian War (2015–2016)

Jason Kim joined the Nightwork community during its initial separation from the Ninzard Project in 2014–2015, becoming one of General Elusion's most trusted operational subordinates.1 When former Vice President Kain established Club Korea (CK) in Los Angeles in 2015, DegreeZ joined the cohort, forming friendships with Kain, Brutus, and Apollos.1,3 He served as an essential diplomatic conduit between Elusion and the Korean diaspora, helping defuse the tensions of the Nightian War and paving the way for the historic merger of Ninzardia and Nightwork on 11 July 2015.1,2

Upon the merger, Nightwork was divided into three legions: Main, Scope, and Parry.2 DegreeZ was elected the 1st District General of Parry—a name chosen to symbolize repelling attacks—running under President Antareze's Home Party.2 During the Ninzard Civil War later that year, DegreeZ aligned with Elusion and the Capital to repel Hyperio's kinetic assaults in Sacramento, while Club Korea maintained backchannel negotiations.1,3 Following the civil war's conclusion in March 2016, DegreeZ and Apollos developed a close personal and political friendship, with DegreeZ supporting Apollos's populist victory in the July 2016 presidential election.3,4

Governor of Nightwork (2017–2018)

Golden Era administration

When Governor Elusion announced his retirement in December 2016, DegreeZ joined the Original Party and ran for Governor of Nightwork alongside Caleb Jensen (Aceiron) as his deputy governor.5 The ticket won decisively, taking office on 1 January 2017.5

DegreeZ's early administration coincided with the "Golden Era," a period characterized by record-high server engagement, low factional drama, and thriving gaming communities.4 DegreeZ actively supported content creators across Nightwork, providing video editing guidance, producing YouTube graphics, and maintaining a calm, approachable presence.11 He and Caleb were re-elected in June 2017 and December 2017 with comfortable majorities.6,13

During the protracted Capital Adjudicator Selection Crisis in the summer of 2017, DegreeZ served as an influential institutional voice in the Ninzard Legislature.14 He initially voted against Apollos's nominations of Syn, Kain, and Spinzle, questioning Spinzle's appointment due to her Resistance Party affiliation, before ultimately voting to confirm Raffaele Bellucci on the fifth ballot, appreciating Raffaele's ability to maintain cross-factional stability.14

Rising tensions and Gold Road

By late 2017, cracks began forming in DegreeZ's administration. He banned long-time member Xsora for targeted harassment, an action that led Xsora and exiled administrator Seandry to establish the Rocket Squad Nation (RSN), an external community dedicated to destroying Ninzard.8 In early 2018, DegreeZ clashed with General Kain, Brutus, and Leon over perceived moderation bias in Parry, instigating a localized "micro-war" before the dispute was temporarily resolved in May.8 Around the same time, Syn publicly condemned Nightwork's leadership for administrative incompetence, resulting in intense shouting matches between Syn and DegreeZ.15

In May 2018, when former Alienova leader Tiny2 died from sickle cell anemia, DegreeZ was among the few individuals informed in a private group chat alongside Holidae and Alaric.16 DegreeZ opted to keep the news confidential to avoid panic within the breakaway Gold Road community.16 However, Alaric broke the silence days later, lambasting DegreeZ as "spineless" and unjustly scapegoating Holidae, which contributed to her subsequent suicide in June 2018.16 The tragedy sent shockwaves through Ninzard and severely damaged DegreeZ's public standing.16

The RSN War (2018)

Following the deaths of Tiny2 and Holidae, the Rocket Squad Nation launched a sustained harassment campaign against Ninzard.7 Seeking to redirect the community's grief and foster "Nightian nationalism," DegreeZ seized on an RSN raid on 30 June 2018 to issue his famous declaration: "The war in which they awakened the sleeping giant."7,17

To prosecute the war, DegreeZ orchestrated a high-stakes intelligence scheme. On 27 June 2018, he appointed Mainian General Xorbit as acting Deputy Governor, replacing Caleb Jensen.17 On 16 July 2018, DegreeZ publicly announced that Caleb had "betrayed" Nightwork by leaking internal intelligence to the RSN.17 In reality, DegreeZ and Caleb had staged the defection to allow Caleb to infiltrate the RSN high command as a double agent.17 Caleb successfully discovered that RSN leader Seandry was compiling a dossier of real-life addresses for a mass-swatting operation against Nightwork members.9 On 7 August 2018, Nightwork executed a coordinated counterstrike, wiping RSN's servers and exposing their plans.9

While DegreeZ held a victory celebration, the war's conclusion damaged his administration.10 Although DegreeZ released private logs proving Caleb had acted on his orders, the psychological fallout of the staged betrayal caused deep resentment among Nightians.9 DegreeZ subsequently distanced himself from Caleb for political self-preservation, permanently alienating his former deputy.9 Critics accused DegreeZ of instigating the war to salvage his own popularity while contributing little to its tactical resolution.9

2018 presidential campaign

Following the RSN War, the Capital Administration scheduled a community-wide presidential election on 12 August 2018.18 DegreeZ launched a campaign challenging incumbent President Apollos.18 He was decisively defeated, receiving 85 of 353 votes (24.1%) against Apollos's 197 votes (55.8%).18 DegreeZ carried only Nightwork-Volley, losing his home district of Parry as well as Main, Scope, and Ninzardia.18

Boycotts, scandal, and resignation

In late August 2018, DegreeZ and Apollos permitted refugees from the dissolved Sacraday and Solorado districts, as well as the Third Faction, to migrate into Nightwork.19 Native Nightians, led by Syn, violently objected to what they characterized as forced assimilation.19 Facing open revolt, DegreeZ abruptly halted all admissions, but the reversal failed to mollify either side.19

Simultaneously, DegreeZ's reliance on covert surveillance was exposed. To suppress internal dissent, DegreeZ had secretly sponsored Xudex (the undercover alias of Hokan) and the Dark Force Agency (DFA), utilizing Remote Access Trojans (RATs) to monitor and intimidate critics.11 When these ties were revealed, Nightians organized widespread boycotts, refusing to engage in chat channels whenever DegreeZ was online.11

The final blow to his governorship occurred on 22 October 2018, when First Lady Czulia publicly dumped DegreeZ after uncovering evidence that he had been cheating on her with Olivia Park (ViaTea), who was thirteen years old at the time.11,20 The scandal destroyed whatever moral credibility remained of his administration.11 On 23 October 2018, DegreeZ resigned as Governor of Nightwork, citing personal complications, and withdrew from public life.11 A chaotic snap election ensued four days later, electing PiggieMLG as his successor.11,21

Political style and legacy

DegreeZ is remembered as one of the most polarizing executives in Ninzard history. During his early tenure, he was widely regarded as a gentle, patient, and creative administrator who professionalized Nightwork's media operations and helped unite the community during the Golden Era.4,11,22

However, the psychological weight of the 2018 tragedies and the RSN War drastically altered his behavior.11,22 In late 2019, General Kain reflected on DegreeZ's trajectory, noting that he had started as an orderly, unifying leader before deteriorating into an impulsive, angry executive who relied on fear, DFA cyber-espionage, and militaristic rhetoric to maintain control.22 His use of RATs and covert proxy networks significantly accelerated the server's descent into the lawless Underground Era under his eventual successor, Governor Spikester.10,11 In her 2025 Liberation Party manifesto, ViaTea explicitly cited DegreeZ's predatory manipulation during her early adolescence as a foundational example of systemic abuse by Ninzard's male leadership.20,23
