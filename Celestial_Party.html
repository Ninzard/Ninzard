The Celestial Party, whose supporters were known as Celestials, was an expansionist political party of the Ninzard Project, founded in April 2018 by Tiny2, the former leader of the Alienova Party, and formed out of the old supporters of the Breakout Party.1,2 After Tiny2's death it was seized by Quorey, who used it as a cover for predatory and extremist activity until his arrest at the end of 2019. Its last leader, Clenzy, ran for president in 2020 and won 7.3% of the vote, after which the party collapsed.1,3,4
