Fabricio Fragoso (born 7 May 2000), known as Faber, is a Coloradan former politician of the Ninzard Project who served as the 4th Governor of Ninzardia from 1 April to 15 August 2018. The best friend of Gabriel, with whom he brought the Colorado cohort into the project and founded the Resistance Party, he was twice District General of Solorado (2014–2016 and 2017–2018) and the first Deputy Governor of Ninzardia.1,2,3,4,5

Earnest and moralistic, Faber argued that Ninzard put activity before humanity, a view his rivals mocked; Raffaele called him a “second-rate Gabriel.” His governorship was overtaken within weeks by the deaths of Tiny2 and Holiday, and on 30 June 2018 he announced his resignation, then took two weeks off instead. He returned to oppose the RSN War, and his tearful video address of 22 July 2018, asking Ninzards to “love one another,” was mocked across the community and drew a furious answer from President Apollos. He did not run again, and handed the governorship to Logan on 15 August 2018.5,6,7,8,9

Early life and Solorado (2012–2014)

Faber grew up in Colorado, where his closest friend was Gabriel. When Gabriel's family broke apart after his father left home, Faber was the one he confided in, sharing feelings he hid from everyone else, and the two grew very close. Faber loved video games, and Gabriel played with him; when one of Faber's Xbox 360 friends insisted that they join his TeamSpeak server, both did, and that server became a prototype of what would be known as Ninzard.10

By mid-2012 the two were members of Hybriden and were hosting a TeamSpeak server of their own, meant to bring friends together, settle conflicts between the cliques at their school and talk about outer space. When the cliques they had united outgrew the 32-member limit of a single server, Antareze suggested that Gabriel and Faber connect their new server, “Solorado,” to the Broader Project of Zarden he was planning. Gabriel was persuaded, and on 1 December 2012 Solorado joined as the project's final legion.1 Three weeks later the Ninzard Project was founded, and Gabriel was elected the first General of Solorado.11,10

Faber and Gabriel knew Abby in person, as fellow Coloradans. When Abby, newly elected General of Hybriden, backed Vice President Kain's deletion of the member IDs in January 2014 and accused Antareze's circle of treating members as test subjects, the two took her charges seriously but rebuked her for making them without evidence.12

General of Solorado (2014–2016)

When Gabriel won the recall election against Kain and became Vice President on 14 February 2014, Solorado held an election to replace him on 15–16 February. Faber won, and took office as the 2nd General of Solorado on 17 February 2014.2 During Gabriel's vice presidency Faber, who considered her reliable, made Spinzle a moderator of Solorado; she went on to sit in the Legislature in line with the Resistance Party's positions.13

As the community divided into parties in the spring of 2014, Faber and Gabriel founded the Resistance Party to resist what they saw as the community's increasingly erratic developments. It formed in the same weeks as the Tomato Party of Kain and Apollos, Bray's Breakout Party and Antareze's Home Party, as the community's first political factions took shape.3 In the first cycle to feature parties, in June 2014, Faber was re-elected as a Resistance candidate, part of a swing toward the party that reflected a wish for normalcy and a less political atmosphere.14 He was re-elected in December 2014, when Solorado also gave Abby, the opposition's presidential nominee, 50 of its 60 votes against Antareze, the most lopsided result of any legion,15 and again in June 2015.16

With Gabriel, Faber was one of the faces of the Resistance Party in the summer of 2015, when both opposed Antareze's plan to absorb Nightwork as a subordinate legion after the Nightian War; Gabriel called it a disguised conquest and argued for an autonomous Nightwork in a looser alliance with Ninzard.16 When the Ninzard Civil War broke out that October, Gabriel and Solorado stood back from the fighting.17

In the June 2016 cycle, which made Apollos president, Faber unexpectedly lost Solorado to Idodo of the Original Party. Everyone had expected Gabriel's political machine to re-elect his best friend with ease.18

Deputy Governor (2016–2017)

Apollos's electoral reforms of 2016 made the governorships of Ninzardia and Nightwork official six-month offices, each elected together with a deputy governor.19 Faber became Gabriel's deputy, the first to hold the post in Ninzardia, and the two were re-elected by a slim margin in December 2016 against the Original Party's ticket of Danbi and Meikaqu. Afterward Gabriel said he would almost certainly not run again.4

Gabriel endorsed Faber as his successor in the gubernatorial election of 29–31 March 2017, surprising many who had assumed Faber would step down with him. Faber chose his girlfriend, Maria Lui, as his running mate, which some voters resented as nepotism. Against him the Original Party ran Meikaqu, with Snowby as her running mate and the enthusiastic backing of President Apollos, who judged that her energy would work well against the “dull” deputy governor. The race was treated as a test of which party was stronger. In the debates Meikaqu was widely judged to have outperformed him, and she won, becoming the 2nd Governor of Ninzardia with Snowby as her deputy.20

In the June 2017 cycle the Resistance Party nominated him for vice president against Hokan, who kept the office by a close margin. The same cycle saw Spinzle unseat Idodo in Solorado; Idodo had lost support there through his constant conflict with Gabriel, Faber and the party's leadership.21

In July 2017 Faber and Gabriel, by then trusted older figures among the Soloradans, organized the travel for a large get-together of the Solorado Cohort and the Edith Gang, who spent the day playing beach volleyball by the ocean. It was the last time the cohort would all meet in person.22

Return to Solorado (2017–2018)

When Spinzle was elected Deputy Governor of Ninzardia on Holiday's ticket in September 2017, Faber was elected to succeed her in Solorado, returning as its 5th District General.23 He was re-elected in December 2017.24

Holiday's governorship faltered that winter. In February 2018 she withdrew from the community almost entirely, leaving Spinzle to keep Ninzardia running behind the scenes, and in March she announced she would not run again and endorsed Faber to succeed her.25

Governor of Ninzardia (2018)

March 2018 election

Faber was the Resistance Party's nominee in the gubernatorial election of March 2018. He criticized the Ories for putting activity ahead of humanity and condemned their leading figures as agents of chaos, blaming the culture they had cultivated for the community's toxicity.5

At Apollos's suggestion, Raffaele, the new leader of the Alienova Party, entered the race on a platform of “Ninzardian restoration.” He attacked Faber's fixation on morality as the wrong path, argued that an online community was right to put activity first, and said that if Faber cared so much about the “human cost” he should never have joined Ninzard. He also accused him of hypocrisy for pandering to the Orie base and called him a “second-rate Gabriel.” The attacks cut Faber's lead sharply and made the Alienova Party a credible contender for the first time. The Original Party ran MMC, an administrator from Capitalia known for his balance and common sense, who dismissed Faber as a moralistic panderer without genuine principles.5

Faber answered that Raffaele's proposals were “ridiculously confusing,” but otherwise rarely attacked his opponents. He moved toward the center, as Holiday had, promoting legionism and freedom, opposing a return of the member-ID system and dropping the call for a written constitution, which the Ories regarded as taking online politics too seriously. He and MMC both wanted Spinzle to stay on as deputy governor, while Raffaele proposed abolishing the post in favor of a separately elected directorate. As voters tired of the negativity between Raffaele and MMC and came to see Faber as a source of stability, he recovered, and he won with a plurality ahead of Raffaele and MMC. When Apollos blamed MMC's “repulsively petty interference” for Raffaele's defeat, the governor-elect suggested that the two of them calm down.5

Faber took office as the 4th Governor of Ninzardia on 1 April 2018, and Pieban won the snap election to succeed him as General of Solorado.5

Removal of Hokan

Critical of the Original Party's unchecked rule, Faber urged the Legislature to replace “unqualified” officials and demanded the removal of Vice President Hokan, of whom the Resistance Party had grown suspicious. Apollos, despite his own feud with Hokan, was angered and questioned whether it was necessary, but legislators pointed to Hokan's many absences and his lack of contributions outside the Ninzardian Faction. Faber led the effort, and the Legislature voted to remove him; Hokan's vice presidency ended on 14 April 2018. Apollos was not required to act on the vote, but abided by it, and amid what members called political fatigue the vice presidency was left vacant for a time.26

Deaths of Tiny2 and Holiday

Within weeks of Faber's election, Tiny2 died in May 2018, and Holiday's death was revealed to the community on 18 June.27,28 At first Faber kept his composure and led Ninzardia through its grief and its doubts about whether the community should exist at all, listening to members' fears and to President Apollos, whom many were blaming for Tiny2's death.6

But activity in Ninzardia was subdued, and members who wanted to move on found his leadership obnoxious. Rudolf, the General of Sacraday, demanded that Faber “step down,” dismissing Tiny2's death in terms that outraged the Resistance Party, and Faber asked Apollos to “do the right thing” and expel Rudolf from the Original Party. Apollos refused, and called Faber a hypocrite for not denouncing the members of his own party who blamed the president for Tiny2's death; Faber apologized.6

Faber had known Holiday personally. Grieving himself, he spent more than a week giving emotional support to the community's mourners before he no longer felt able to go on. Ninzardians railed against what they called his weak leadership and his refusal to take action over Gold Road, Tiny2's breakaway community. When Rudolf publicly declared that Faber's negligence over Gold Road had led to Holiday's death, he was condemned on all sides, Apollos included, but Sacradians already tired of the Faber Administration used the charge against the governor.6

Resignation and the Last Legislature

On 30 June 2018, the day Nightwork declared war on the Rocket Squad Nation, Faber announced his resignation. Abby, angry, told him that the older members had a responsibility to stay, but in the conversation that followed he expressed such despair that she was sorry she had been angry. When members denounced him for fleeing, Apollos defended him, saying anyone in his position would have quit because it was “extremely heartbreaking,” and the outrage gradually shifted onto the president.7

After talks with his deputy, Spinzle, Faber took two weeks off rather than resigning outright, though he gave up the leadership of the Resistance Party for good, and Spinzle became acting governor.7 He was listed as governor, but not online, when Legislature IX, later called the “Last Legislature,” first met that evening.29 On 7 July the Legislature closed Sacraday and Solorado, Faber's old legion, from the following day. Gabriel left the community for good, and Spinzle disbanded the Resistance Party and left as well.30

Opposition to the RSN War

Faber returned on 16 July 2018; in his and Spinzle's absence, Generals Kay and Abby had managed Ninzardia's affairs. With Sacraday and Solorado gone, he now governed only Capitalia and Hybriden. Dismayed by the RSN War, he demanded that Governor DegreeZ of Nightwork end it. DegreeZ asked whether he was trolling, and when Faber said he was not, told him he might as well leave, since his presence was “useless.” Faber also criticized Apollos, saying publicly that the community was “behaving badly” and needed to get its act together. He lamented its support for the war and its “lack of moral clarity,” and accused the Orie leaders under Apollos and DegreeZ of encouraging people to become “mean-spirited.”8

On 22 July 2018 Faber posted a video address to all Ninzards. He asked, “What are we without love?” and urged them to “love one another,” then, holding back tears, turned to the two leaders he blamed.8

「Two friends we buried together. Two friends are gone. And two friends we have here, who are encouraging mean-spirited acts. … Let's tell our two friends tonight to stop their unkindness. … Jason, Reuben, where is your humanity?」

— Faber, video address, 22 July 2018

The video was mocked, memed and screenshotted throughout the community, and pictures of his crying face were posted everywhere; Kain gave him a mocking nickname, which became the name of a new emoticon of the crying face. Faber did not respond in anger. On 23 July he asked the community why it could not be nice for even a moment.8

The question enraged Apollos, who called Faber “idiotic” and told him to expect such reactions to cringe videos. When Faber replied that there was no reason to be harsh, Apollos told him he “hardly understood anything” about what members went through in their personal lives, and Faber answered that he had been grieving Holiday's death. Apollos's reply, laced with profanity, accused him of knowing nothing about inner mental-health struggles, and many in the community agreed with the president.8

「You only think you're a better person than everyone else because you were privileged enough to be born lucky with enough natural serotonin production. But you're not better than anyone else for something you didn't even earn.」

— Apollos, to Faber

The exchange later weighed on Apollos. In the archive's reading, Faber had warned that the community's culture was unsafe, and Apollos, who had mocked him as “privileged” and “lucky,” came to see that he himself had been the lucky one: depressed and chaotic, he had survived, while Tiny2 and Holiday had not.31

Handover (2018)

Faber did not stand in the gubernatorial election of 12–14 August 2018, and promised a clean transfer of power to his successor.32,9 Logan, the District 3 Administrator, won as an independent with 83 of 155 votes (53.5%) against Rudolf, and took office as the 5th Governor of Ninzardia on 15 August 2018.9 The archive does not record Faber in the community after the handover.
