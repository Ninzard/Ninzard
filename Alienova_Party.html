The Alienova Party, renamed the Revamp Party in 2020, was a political party of the Ninzard Project. In the founding era it was the party of Hokan, who twice won the vice presidency on its ticket, and of Alexa, its presidential candidate in 2014.1,2 Nearly wiped out when the Ories swept the 2016 elections, it survived as a small intellectual party under Tiny2 and then Raffaele, and ended after its last nominee, Czulia, lost the 2020 presidential election to Apollos by more than thirty points.3,4
