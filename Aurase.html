Adrian Keller, known as Aurase, is a Ninzard politician who served as the 6th District General of Parry, then called BobaBitcoin, from 7 January to 9 July 2022. Elected for the Poo Party, he remains the only Poopulist ever to win office in Nightwork.1,2

A member of Spinzle's circle of real-life friends, the Edith Gang, Aurase defied Governor Cyntaku's Nightian-Ninzardian Assembly and was the one general whom President Cat tried and failed to buy. She then backed Xorbit, who unseated him by three votes.3,4,5,6 He joined the Preservation Party and stood for Parry against Rudolf four times without success.7,8,9,10

A voice of the party's principled Logos Flank, he argued in November 2025 against enlarging the Preservate Apparatus, sat in its senior leadership under Adelynn in early 2026, and in August 2026 was among the Preservates who wanted the Capital to step into Parry's Salon War.11,12,13

General of Parry (2022)

The district general elections of January 2022 went badly for the Original Party. In Main the Preservate Geo unseated Danbi, and in BobaBitcoin Aurase unseated the Orie general Kain, becoming the only Nightian ever elected under the Poo Party's banner.1 He took office on 7 January. On 14 February he joined Generals Spinzle and GoodChinese in voting to expel Helen from the community, a decision some saw as a power grab by President Apollos.14

Nightian-Ninzardian Assembly

Minutes after that vote, Governor Cyntaku announced the Nightian-Ninzardian Assembly, in which the votes of Nightwork's generals would decide its affairs and the governor would break ties. With two Preservate generals, one Orie and one Poopulist, the arrangement favored her party.4 The next day she put forward the Anti-Bullying Amendment, which would let the governor override any district's own rules in the name of fighting discrimination. Aurase and Spinzle voted against it, citing its vagueness and the power it gave the governor; Geo and Yunin voted for it, and Cyntaku broke the tie.4

Spinzle chose to comply, but Aurase refused to recognize the Assembly or any override of BobaBitcoin by the governor, and Parry became the one district where the amendment never took effect. When Cyntaku put the amendment to a community-wide vote of the generals, where it failed, Aurase gave his reason in one line.4

「The ABA undermines your right to dissent.」

— General Aurase, voting on the Anti-Bullying Amendment, February 2022

When Volley's election ended in a tie in March 2022 and Cyntaku named Molly the winner, Aurase and Spinzle insisted on a new election; the snap election gave Mel a narrow victory.15 With Mel, Aurase opposed Cyntaku's Electoral Reforms Act, which made voters register and enter a six-digit PIN, arguing that it made voting less accessible and spoiled the community's “fun.”16 He was among the leaders Apollos gathered on 23 April 2022 to negotiate an end to the Nexit crisis, and that summer he sought the Poo Party's nomination for president.17,18

Refusing Cat

By April 2022 Cat had tried to bribe every general in Ninzard except Aurase, assuming a Poopulist would be a natural, chaotic ally. Then he condemned her doxxing of Geo: Geo's behavior had been “reprehensible,” he said, but her campaign against him was “equally, if not more, inexcusable.” Her boyfriend Raffaele told her that some people held “unreasonably rigid” moral standards, and that “People like Adrian would punish the paramedic who violated protocol to save a life.”5

Cat put it to the test. Instead of a bribe she offered “political synergy”: money for BobaBitcoin's Minecraft server, Nitro giveaways and large events, which would have made the district a supercenter, if it aligned itself with her and looked away from the doxxing. Aurase refused. Tying the district's livelihood to Cat's wealth, he argued, would trade its longevity for short-term vitality; when she promised the money would see him re-elected, he answered that he had no business knowledge and no interest in turning BobaBitcoin into a business. In private Cat came to despise him and resolved to replace him, as much for her own satisfaction as for strategy.5 Her ledger, recovered after her fall, lists him as having refused $960 and accepted nothing.19

Defeat

Cat found her weapon in Xorbit, whom she offered large sums for every milestone of Parry's growth. In July 2022 Xorbit, the Orie nominee, won 47 of 98 votes (48.0%) to Aurase's 44 (44.9%), with 7 for the Preservate Gui. Aurase's term ended on 9 July.6,2

Preservate (2022–present)

In the fall of 2022, when Apollos was being stalked at his university, Aurase helped him gather information and evidence, using Apollos's account to trace the routes the stalkers were likely to have taken.20 That December the Preservates nominated him, a former Poopulist, for Parry against Xorbit and the independent Rudolf. Rudolf blamed him for Xorbit's rise, disliked the Edith Gang and attacked him with homophobic slurs. Of 176 votes, Rudolf won 71, Xorbit 59 and Aurase 46.7

He challenged Rudolf again in June 2024, December 2024 and June 2025, winning between 15% and 27% of the vote each time.8,9,10

Logos Flank

After Cat's fall in August 2025, Aurase was among the party operatives who doubted the wisdom of making Apollos the Preservates' presidential nominee.21 In the Dreadnought Debate of 12 November 2025 he argued against enlarging the party's leadership: a bigger Apparatus would invite retaliation from the Ories, feed an “inescapable cycle of political factionalism,” and risk turning the party into the very cult it had been formed to fight. He pointed to Boygho's recent win in Parry with less than half the vote as proof that the Ories could be beaten without it.11

「The expansion of the Preservation Party does not equate to fighting the Original Party, nor does their growth correspond to better political results.」

— Aurase, Dreadnought Debate, 12 November 2025

Grace and Burnette sided with him, and Adelynn shared “Adrian's primary apprehension” that the party must not pursue political gain in ways that undermined its principles. Spinzle and Apollos answered that the party could grow without sacrificing them, Spinzle comparing the larger Apparatus to the Dreadnought, which changed naval warfare through its integrated systems rather than its size.11 Five days later, in the Iron Flank Debate, he agreed that NGin's victory in Volley had to be prevented.22

By 24 February 2026 he sat in the four-member senior leadership of the Preservate Apparatus under its Boss, Adelynn, counted with Grace as a member of the Logos Flank.12 In the structure Woofer set out on 2 April, after Adelynn was injured, he was one of Parry's two party operatives.23 In June 2026 he and Phalth accused ScottyAte of pandering to Parry's “normie” base with his cat, the district's mascot.24

Salon War (2026)

When Parry fell into the Salon War in August 2026, Aurase was among the members of the Preservate Apparatus, with Cyntaku, Seandry, Syn and others, who pressed for intervention in the district. President Poka overruled them all and left General Kaimachi to handle Parry alone.13 At the Parry Trials on 14 August, where Kaimachi let her critics prosecute her generalship live, Aurase took the microphone to object to the very premise.25

Aurase: If I may interject, I find the framing here fundamentally flawed. The presupposition that so-called authenticity constitutes a governing philosophy is, frankly, a category error. A district requires structural coherence. Charismatic abdication of responsibility masquerades as vulnerability, which merely externalizes the labor of stabilization onto—

Kaimachi: I need you to know I stopped understanding you at “presupposition.”

Aurase: It's a common word.

Kaimachi: It is NOT a common word. You talk like—like a BOT! …

Aurase: Point taken. Stop yapping.

Kaimachi: See, that's growth. You said one whole sentence I understood. We're healing.

Kaimachi's approach ended the war. Meikaqu, writing for the archives, named him among the Apparatus members she hoped would not take the wrong lesson from it and congratulate themselves.25,26

Personal life

Keller is one of the eight known members of the Edith Gang, Spinzle's group of real-life friends, with Snowby, Pieban, Soda, Kendall, Atria and Runix.3 When the group collapsed on 20 November 2024 and the others blocked Spinzle on every platform, he alone refused to shun her, opening a permanent rift between himself and the rest of the circle.27 He had planned to speak for Soda, his former friend, at his memorial in Scope on 23 May 2026, but was too ill with strep throat to speak.28 In July 2026 Apollos confided in him privately after his relationship with Spinzle ended.29

Political style

Aurase is known for unbending principles and a formal, academic way of speaking. Raffaele's verdict, that he would punish a paramedic for breaking protocol to save a life, was meant as a warning to Cat about how rigid he was; his refusal of her money bore it out.5 As a Preservate he has argued against concentrating power in the party's leadership, much as he once refused the governor's power over his district.4,11
